This article by M.K. Narayanan (former NSA, IB Director, and Governor of West Bengal) examines how Artificial Intelligence and cyber threats are converging to create unprecedented global security challenges.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Core Thesis</mark>

The digital ecosystem has evolved from a limited interconnected world into a complex web of pervasive dangers, where AI and cyber threats now reinforce each other like a "double helix." AI has transitioned from being a mere operational efficiency tool to becoming absolute infrastructure, while cyber threats have grown more sophisticated, harder to detect, and difficult to mitigate.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Key Cyber Threat Developments</mark>

- AI-powered malware can adapt and evolve dynamically in response to its environment, defeating traditional anti-virus detection methods.
    
- Malicious autonomous agents are undermining "Zero Trust" security protocols, worsening insider threat vectors.
    
- Global digital interconnectedness has raised the stakes of cyber attacks across every level of security.
    
- Governments face sophisticated adversaries and are building security architectures blending innovation, policy coherence, and strategic realism, yet still lag behind.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">The New Disruptive AI Era</mark>

Large Language Models (LLMs) are penetrating business enterprises without adequate training or human interface evolution, while "Agentic AI" (distinct from Generative AI) is emerging as a more autonomous and complex force. A key catalyst of concern is Anthropic's AI system "Mythos," which demonstrates an uncanny ability to detect "Zero-day" vulnerabilities across major operating systems, effectively testing the limits of legacy security systems.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">AI in Modern Warfare</mark>

AI has already transformed warfare, evident in the Russia-Ukraine conflict and the US/Israel-Iran war.

- AI-powered smart systems detect, track, and predict missile trajectories with remarkable accuracy.
    
- AI processes diverse intelligence sources — photos, text, radio, and electromagnetic signals — to deny adversaries tactical advantage.
    
- Autonomous munitions like "Goalkeeper" and "Whiplash" can independently identify and strike targets.
    
- Nations, particularly the US, have not shown adequate sagacity in wielding this power safely.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">The AI Power Shift</mark>

|Aspect|Details|
|---|---|
|Corporate dominance|Western firms like Anthropic and Palantir behave as though they own tomorrow's world, holding keys to the mightiest technology|
|Global rivalry|China is believed to have its own comparable AI capability, offering little comfort globally|
|Rogue actor risk|Advanced AI gives "rogue groups" capabilities once reserved for nation-states|
|Agentic shift|As Agentic AI displaces Generative AI, autonomous action increases, raising threat levels further|
|WEF warning|AI will strengthen cyber defences but also enable more sophisticated automated attacks, potentially neutralizing current "Zero Trust" protocols|

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Risks and Limitations of AI Systems</mark>

Despite their seemingly god-like capabilities, modern AI models are prone to "hallucinations," creating scope for distortions and misleading outputs. Judgments derived from AI tend to be subjective, and inherent biases can creep into AI-driven decisions, amplifying dangers of unrestricted reliance. Experts also caution against "algorithmic radicalisation," where AI platforms could push opinion-makers toward extreme views.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">US-China AI Rivalry</mark>

A notable flashpoint is the US allegation that China's "Moonshot" AI stole from Anthropic's sophisticated LLM "Fable" to develop its own AI version, a charge China has rejected. This dispute underscores the intensity of the technological race between the two powers, especially since Anthropic claims its latest model surpasses "all but the most skilled humans" in finding and exploiting security vulnerabilities across operating systems and cryptographic software.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">The Way Forward</mark>

The world has shifted from concerns over stagnant economic growth to grappling with "doomsday technologies," demanding serious introspection rather than mere altruism. As AI increasingly enters defence sensing and warning systems, there is a critical need to keep machines under human control and ensure AI-generated warnings do not outrun genuine understanding of reality. The author suggests a cautious, incremental approach ("cross the river by feeling the stones") is preferable to either blind prejudice against or unadulterated endorsement of this transformative technology.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Key Terms</mark>

- **Zero Trust protocol**: A security model assuming no implicit trust for any user or system, verified continuously.
    
- **Agentic AI**: AI systems capable of autonomous action and decision-making, distinct from Generative AI which primarily creates content.
    
- **Zero-day vulnerability**: A software flaw unknown to the vendor, exploitable before a patch is developed.
    
- **Algorithmic radicalisation**: The phenomenon where algorithms push users toward increasingly extreme viewpoints.
