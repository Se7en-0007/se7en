## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">GS 1</mark>

- **SASTRA Deemed University (Thanjavur) Ruby & Silver Jubilee**: Vice President **C. P. Radhakrishnan** stated **NEP 2020** is a guiding pillar "from KG to Ph.D." He noted India's **Higher Education Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)** is expected to rise from **30% to 50% by 2035**. Launched **six new schemes**: TRUST, SASTRA.AI, MAITRI, STRIDE Fund, DRISHTI, and SMILE; also highlighted the **SASTRA–Tata Communications** cybersecurity partnership.
    
- **Central University of Tamil Nadu, Thiruvarur (11th Convocation)**: **1,103 students** graduated; **616 were women**; **50 Gold Medals** awarded, of which **37 went to women**. VP stressed the **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill** and urged a **"Nation First – Rashtra Pratham"** mindset for **Viksit Bharat @2047**.
    
- **Beyond the Numbers – UDISE+ 2025–26 Report (School Education)**: Covers **14.67 lakh schools, 1.03 crore teachers, 24.72 crore students**. **GER**: Secondary **71.7%**, Middle **89.6%**. **Pupil-Teacher Ratio (PTR)**: Overall **24:1** (within NEP 2020 benchmark of 30:1). **Dropout rates** declined to **1.8% (Preparatory)** and **7.0% (Secondary)**. **Retention rates**: Foundational **98.5%**, Secondary **51.9%**.
    
- **Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme**: Provides **₹2.5 lakh** incentive to promote social harmony and equality (case study of Ashok & Sabita).
    
- **SEED Scheme** (De-notified, Nomadic & Semi-Nomadic Communities): Implemented by **DWBDNC** (constituted Feb 2019) under Ministry of Social Justice; provides support in **education, healthcare, livelihoods, and housing**.
    
- **SMILE Scheme** (Support for Marginalised Individuals for Livelihood and Enterprise), launched **12 February 2022**: **42,589 beggary-affected persons surveyed**, **12,710 rehabilitated**; **23 Garima Grehs** across 17 States/UTs sheltering **1,041 transgender persons**; **33,303 identity cards** issued to transgender persons; **27 Transgender Protection Cells** and **30 Transgender Welfare Boards** operational.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">GS 2</mark>

- **PM's Ex-Gratia Announcements**: **₹2 lakh** from **PMNRF** for next of kin of deceased and **₹50,000** for injured, announced separately for the **Birbhum, West Bengal fire tragedy** and the **Lakhisarai, Bihar stampede**. PM also condoled/expressed grief over both incidents and met **Maharashtra CM Devendra Fadnavis**.
    
- **CEC Gyanesh Kumar – Electoral Literacy**: **Election Commission of India** launched **Electoral Literacy Clubs (ELC 2.0)** at Patna with tagline "Learn Democracy. Lead Democracy," integrated with the **ECINet** digital platform. Targets nearly **15 lakh schools (~25 crore students)** and **~1,500 universities** across **70,000 institutions (4.33 crore students)**.
    
- **Central Information Commission (CIC)**: Launched upgraded **"AppCoMS 2.0"** (Appeal and Complaint Management System) for processing Second Appeals/Complaints under the **RTI Act, 2005**; introduces individual user accounts and **Digital Signature Certificates (DSCs)**.
    
- **Pension Reforms (DoPPW)**: **Dr. Jitendra Singh** conferred the **9th National ANUBHAV Awards** (over **2,000 nominations**, highest ever); highlighted shift to **Digital Life Certificate & Face Authentication**, the **_Bhavishya_ portal**, and simplified family pension rules.
    
- **Modi Government destroys ISI-backed Shahzad Bhatti Network (SBN)**: **253 people detained across 14 states** on **12 August 2026** (UP-62, Haryana-52, Delhi-51, Punjab-44, etc.); over **200 arrests** and **80+ FIRs** under **UAPA, BNS, Arms Act, NDPS Act**; recoveries included **IEDs, grenades (Pakistan Ordnance Factory marked), pistols**. Described as reflecting the government's **"zero tolerance" policy** on cross-border terrorism.
    
- **SSB Family Quarters**: MoS Home **Bandi Sanjay Kumar** inaugurated **48 new family quarters** at the SSB campus, Ibrahimpatnam, Hyderabad, reaffirming MHA's commitment to CAPF personnel welfare.
    
- **India's BRICS Presidency 2026** (theme: _"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"_):
    
    - **7th BRICS ICT Working Group Meeting**, Pune – discussed AI, next-gen communications, Industry 4.0, EMF exposure.
        
    - **BRICS Environment Working Group meeting**, New Delhi – four priorities: sustainable lifestyles, afforestation/disaster resilience, circular economy, adaptation; **12th BRICS Environment Ministers' Meeting** scheduled **18 August 2026**.
        
    - **BRICS Youth Council Meeting & Youth Summit** (19–20 Aug, Gandhinagar) and **BRICS Youth Ministers' Meeting** (22 Aug, Visakhapatnam), centred on **"Yuva Shakti."**
        
- **Qatar–India Postal Remittance Service**: Launched **15 August 2026** via **PosTransfer** (powered by **UPI**), a collaboration between Qatar Post, India Post, **UPU-IP**, and **NIPL**; transaction limit **QAR 10–4,000**, capped at **₹1,00,000**, with **QAR 15** service charge.
    
- **PSB Confluence 2026** (DFS): Day 1 themes – Deposit Mobilisation, Banking for Youth, Investment Cycle, Global Capability Centres; FM **Nirmala Sitharaman** announced an upcoming **"High-Powered Committee on Banking Reforms for Viksit Bharat."**
    
- **Skill Development – 'Kaushal Manthan' Regional Conference**, Kolkata: Focus on **PM-SETU scheme** to transform ITIs; **SOAR (Skilling for AI Readiness)** enrolled **over 2.10 lakh learners**; new international skilling programme for **600 OBC youth from MP** for jobs in Japan/Germany.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">GS 3</mark>

- **PMAY-G Rajasthan**: **2,89,355 pucca houses** worth **₹3,473 crore** delivered; **₹340.59 crore** approved for agriculture/PM-RKVY; eligibility reforms include raising women's income ceiling to **₹15,000/month**; **Lakhpati Didi target doubled** to **32 lakh** for Rajasthan (national target: **3 crore → 6 crore**); urea kept at **₹266/bag** despite actual cost of ~₹3,000.
    
- **Kharif Crop Coverage (as on 14.08.2026)**: Total area sown **1037.52 lakh hectares** vs **1016.57 lakh hectares** last year; **Rice** down by **14.37 lakh hectares**; overall decrease of **20.95 lakh hectares** compared to 2025.
    
- **NICDIT/NICDP** (chaired by FM Sitharaman): **BHAVYA scheme** (100 plug-and-play industrial parks) received **87 applications** against a target of 20; **20 approved projects across 13 States/7 Corridors**; **469 plots allotted (~5,348 acres)**; investment potential **₹2.21 lakh crore**; employment potential **~1.29 lakh persons**; **4 smart cities** (Dholera, Shendra-Bidkin, Greater Noida, Vikram Udyogpuri) in production stage.
    
- **ECMS (Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme)**: **106 projects approved**, investment **₹69,548 crore**, projected production **₹5,34,101 crore**; **38 plants operational, 16 in advanced construction**; expected **74,628 direct + 2.5 lakh indirect jobs**; self-reliance achieved in Anode material (~110%), Optical Transceiver-SFP (350%), Relays (200%).
    
- **MoD–General Atomics Contract**: Ministry of Defence signed a **₹1,943 crore** contract with **General Atomics Aeronautical Systems Inc. (GA-ASI)** to lease **two MQ-9B Sea Guardian HALE RPAS** for the **Indian Navy for 30 months**, boosting **Maritime Domain Awareness/ISR** in the Indian Ocean Region.
    
- **8th IN–USN EOD Exercise 2026** concluded at **Southern Naval Command, Kochi** (14 Aug 2026) - covered IED threats, render-safe procedures, mine neutralization.
    
- **Lokayan 2026 – INS Sudarshini** concluded port call at **Ponta Delgada, Azores (16 Aug 2026)**, strengthening India–Portugal naval cooperation.
    
- **India–Thailand Exercise MAITREE-XV**: Indian Army contingent (**85 personnel, mainly 9 Gorkha Rifles**) departed for the **15th edition**, to be held **18–31 August 2026** at Vibhavadi Rangsit Camp, Surat Thani.
    
- **India's First-Ever Guide to Grasslands & Open Natural Ecosystems (ONEs)**: Launched at **UNCCD COP17, Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia**; emphasizes science-based conservation of grasslands/savannas/deserts (often misidentified as "wasteland"), tied to India's **Land Degradation Neutrality (LDN)** targets.
    
- **Fisheries & Blue Economy** (80th Independence Day): India is the **world's 2nd-largest fish producer** (8% of global output); **~3 crore fishers/fish farmers** supported; **₹39,272 crore invested since 2015** via Blue Revolution, FIDF, PMMSY; **PM-MKSSY** outlay **₹6,000 crore** (FY24–FY27).
    
- **PLFS Monthly Bulletin (July 2026)**: **LFPR** up to **55.4%** (Rural 58.0%, Urban 50.4%); **WPR** up to **52.5%**; **Unemployment Rate (UR)** down to **5.1%** (Rural 4.5%, Urban 6.7%).
    
- **Bharat Taxi**: India's first **cooperative-led ride-hailing platform** (est. 6 June 2025 under Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002); **~8 lakh registered drivers, 41 lakh registered customers** (as of 25 July 2026); operates on **zero-commission, member-owned model** - "Saarthi Hi Maalik"; **20% earnings credited as Sarathi capital, 80% distributed** based on kilometres travelled; integrated with **e-Shram, Ayushman Bharat (₹5 lakh medical cover)**, and **DigiLocker/UMANG/API Setu**; includes **"Sarathi Didi"** and **"Bike Didi"** women-inclusion initiatives.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">GS 4</mark>

- **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill**: Referenced by the Vice President at both SASTRA and Central University of Tamil Nadu events as reflecting the government's **ethical commitment to fairness and transparency** in public examinations; VP stated **"bad elements in society must be punished to protect the interests of good people,"** linking examination integrity to social justice.
    
- **Anti-Narcotics Appeal**: VP C. P. Radhakrishnan urged students to **"say no to narcotic drugs"** and called on security agencies to take strict action against drug peddlers, framing it as a **collective moral responsibility** toward youth welfare.
    
- **Zero Tolerance Policy on Terrorism**: Home Minister Amit Shah's framing of the **Shahzad Bhatti Network crackdown** as reflecting a **"zero tolerance" ethical stance** against cross-border terrorism and anti-national activities - an assertion of state integrity and resolve in national security governance.
    
- **RTI Transparency & Accountability**: The CIC's launch of **upgraded AppCoMS 2.0** reflects a governance ethic of **transparency, accountability, and citizen-centric grievance redressal** under the RTI Act, 2005, with features enabling applicants to track timelines and access orders directly - reducing information asymmetry between citizen and state.
    
- **Pension System Ethics**: Dr. Jitendra Singh's emphasis on **reducing litigation by resolving grievances at source** (via Pension Adalats) reflects a governance value of **empathy and efficiency** in dealing with retired citizens, alongside the **ANUBHAV** initiative's ethic of **valuing institutional memory and experience** of retired employees.
    
- **Cooperative Model Ethics (Bharat Taxi)**: The **"Saarthi Hi Maalik"** (driver-as-owner) principle embeds an ethic of **worker dignity, ownership, and equitable earnings distribution**, contrasting with conventional aggregator-driven gig models - reflecting a values-based approach to platform economy governance.
    
- **Women & Social Inclusion Ethics**: Multiple releases (SASTRA's women medal toppers, PMAY-G's women income-ceiling revision, SMILE's transgender welfare provisions, Bharat Taxi's "Sarathi Didi") collectively reflect a governance ethic of **substantive equality and inclusive empowerment** rather than mere formal equality.
