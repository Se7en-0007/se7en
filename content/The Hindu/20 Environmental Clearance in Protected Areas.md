## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Environmental Clearances in Protected Areas</mark>

- 20 infrastructure projects/activities were granted environmental clearance (EC) in 2024-25 and 2025-26.
- Project sites were located wholly or partly in a protected area, eco-sensitive zone (ESZ), or eco-sensitive area (ESA).
- Clearances followed the applicable EC process.
- Informed by Union Minister of State for Environment, Forest and Climate Change Kirti Vardhan Singh in the Rajya Sabha.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Forest Land Diversion Proposals</mark>

- The Advisory Committee under Section 3 of the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980 considered 775 proposals for diversion of forest land for non-forest purposes between July 2023 and May 2026.
- Of these, 378 proposals (48.77 per cent) were recommended for in-principle (Stage-I) approval.
- Note: Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam is the renamed/amended Forest Conservation Act, 1980 — important for Polity/Environment linkage.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Air Pollution in Delhi-NCR</mark>

- The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) in NCR and Adjoining Areas identified major polluting sectors.
- Basis: 2018 source apportionment study "Source Apportionment of PM2.5 and PM10 of Delhi NCR for Identification of Major Sources."
- Supreme Court order (January 6, 2026) directed CAQM to identify major causes of air pollution.
- NCR states prepared short- and long-term action plans covering vehicular pollution, road/construction dust, industrial emissions, waste management, and stubble burning.
- Government stated a "whole of government" and "whole of society" approach is needed.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">El Niño and the Indian Monsoon</mark>

- IMD predicted El Niño development during the 2026 southwest monsoon season.
- Classification (weak/moderate/strong/very strong) is based on sea surface temperature anomalies over the equatorial central Pacific Ocean.
- Conditions were weak in June 2026, intensified to moderate in July 2026.
- Likely to strengthen further during October-December 2026, possibly reaching "very strong" category.
- Actual impact on India depends on coupled ocean-atmosphere conditions, of which El Niño is only one factor.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Afforestation Programmes and Climate Change</mark>

- Green India Mission: plantation/eco-restoration over 1,84,467 hectares via community participation; species selection based on site-specific ecology, favoring indigenous species.
- Nagar Van Yojana: 19,348.59 hectares approved (2020-21 to 2025-26), including 3,712.36 hectares in 2025-26 alone.
- MISHTI (Mangrove Initiative for Shoreline Habitats and Tangible Incomes), launched 2023: restoration of 9,536 hectares of degraded mangroves.
- Compensatory Afforestation: 2,00,407.56 hectares afforested across states/UTs (2019-20 to 2024-25), supported by CAMPA (National Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority).

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Tyre Pyrolysis Units</mark>

- India has 695 tyre pyrolysis units; 440 comply with the standard operating procedure for recycling waste tyre scrap into pyrolysis oil, pyro gas, and char.
- Data sourced from CPCB and 36 State Pollution Control Boards/Committees.
- Of 255 non-compliant units: 25 self-closed, 33 closed by regulators, and action initiated against remaining 197.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Electric Vehicles (EVs)</mark>

- EV penetration in India rose from 0.71 per cent (2019-20) to 8.26 per cent (2025-26), per VAHAN data.
- Cited by Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways Nitin Jairam Gadkari in the Lok Sabha.
- Growth attributed to lower operational costs and zero tailpipe emissions versus internal combustion engine vehicles.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Riverbank Erosion in Assam (Brahmaputra)</mark>

- Brahmaputra underwent erosion of about 252.6 sq km and deposition of about 118.6 sq km between 2003-05 and 2008-11.
- Based on a morphological study by the Central Water Commission conducted through IIT Guwahati.
- Cited by Union Minister of State for Jal Shakti Raj Bhushan Choudhary in the Lok Sabha.
