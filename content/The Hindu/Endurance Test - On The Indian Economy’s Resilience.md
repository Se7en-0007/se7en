- **India’s real economic growth in the April-June 2026 quarter was 7.8%**, exceeding economists’ expectation of a slowdown to about **6%-7%** due to the **West Asia crisis**.
- The growth was **not a statistical anomaly**: it was supported by strong performances in both **manufacturing** and **services**.

## Manufacturing And Capital Creation

- **Manufacturing grew 9.2%**, its **highest growth in three quarters**.
- The sector likely benefited from the **Goods and Services Tax rate cut** implemented in **September 2025** and the **RBI’s cumulative 125-basis point interest-rate cuts through 2025**.
- Companies may also have **front-loaded output** in anticipation of uncertainty relating to **inflation**, possible future **rate hikes**, and other headwinds.
- **Capital creation appears to have picked up**, though the respective shares of the **government** and **private sector** are unclear.
- Overall capital creation has a **welcome multiplier effect on the economy**.
- The **services sector** has also continued to register **robust growth**, strengthening the economy.

## Hormuz Uncertainty

- The economy is **not out of the woods**, and replicating this robust performance in the coming quarters will be more difficult.
- **Hormuz uncertainty** is likely to keep oil prices above **$80 a barrel**.
- This is especially difficult for India, which imports **85%-90% of its oil requirement**.

## Trade Deficit And Services Exports

- The appeal to Indians is to **buy local**, avoid **non-essential foreign travel** and **weddings abroad**, and stop **unnecessary purchases of gold**.
- The task of maintaining a reasonable **trade deficit** has so far rested on **India’s services exports**.
- While this has served India reasonably well so far, the **global economy is stuttering**.
- The growth of **Artificial Intelligence services elsewhere**, combined with global economic weakness, could hurt demand for **Indian services exports**.

## Rural Demand And Inflation

- The economy has yet to face the full impact of a **deficient monsoon** and its effect on **rural demand**.
- **Consumption-related data for July** in the **Index of Industrial Production** already indicates **sluggish rural demand**.
- Inflation remains within the **RBI’s comfort zone**, but it has been increasing.
- The RBI expects inflation to reach **5.9% in October-December 2026**, which would further hurt demand.
- Manufacturing front-loading will, by definition, **peter out over the next few quarters**.

## The Tougher Stamina Test

- The Indian economy’s resilience was tested in **Q1**, and it **passed admirably**.
- However, the **much tougher stamina test remains**.
