## Background of The Review

- This is India's **first review by the U.N. Committee on the Elimination of Racial Discrimination (CERD)** since **2007**.
- India **ratified the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination in 1968**.
- CERD has **elicited an expression of "grave concern"** about reports of the **law enforcement apparatus resorting to violence** - physical and otherwise - against **minority ethnic and ethno-religious groups, Dalits, and non-citizens** over the years.
- The Committee noted India has **not provided detailed and updated information** about the state's **inquiries and sanctions** arising from allegations of such violence.

## Issue of Caste and Race

- The **government has told CERD that caste bias falls outside Article 1 of the convention** because **caste is not synonymous with race**.
- **CERD has allowed all forms of discrimination based on inherited status** to fall within its purview.

## Key Concerns Raised by CERD

- The practice of **manual cleaning of sewers** despite a **law banning it**.
- **Hate speech against**, **poor living conditions of**, and the **mass refoulement of Rohingya Muslims**.
- The **mass deprivation of citizenship via the National Register of Citizens (NRC)**.
- **Large-scale deletions from the Special Intensive Revisions of electoral rolls**.
- The **government's use of the FCRA, UAPA, AFSPA, and PMLA instruments** to **hamper the work of civil society organisations**.
- The **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita not expressly criminalising racist hate speech**.
- The **Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act exempting "national security" projects** from **consultative decision-making**.

## Data and Administrative Gaps

- There is a **lack of suitably disaggregated data on disenfranchised communities**, including due to a **much-delayed Census** and the **slow release of NCRB data**.
- This lack of data has **transcended the bounds of administrative lethargy** to actively **impeding independent checks of India's claims** about fighting **caste- and faith-based discrimination**.
- India has been **implementing "special measures" based on 2011 Census data**, which **could mask a regression in the socioeconomic status of Scheduled Tribes and Adivasis**.

## Concerns Regarding The NHRC

- The **Global Alliance of National Human Rights Institutions recommended the downgrade of the NHRC last year**.
- This recommendation followed the **government appointing police officers to investigative roles** and a **lack of pluralism** within the institution.
- **CERD is effectively stepping into a vacuum** created by these institutional weaknesses.

## The Road Ahead (Recommended Measures)

- **Restoring the NHRC's independence**.
- **Criminalising hate speech**.
- **Supportive affirmative action**.
- **Collecting and publishing disaggregated data**.
- **Improving the transparency of the electoral roll revisions**.
- **Strengthening enforcement overall**.

## Government's Response

- The government has attempted to use a **technicality to keep the issues of 200 million Dalits a "domestic matter"** and **avoid international accountability**.
- The government has **dismissed CERD's concerns as "politically motivated"**.

## Conclusion of The Article

- Given the government's stance, **the task continues to fall to civil society** to:
    
    - **Exert influence in multilateral fora**.
    - **Sustain political, legal, and administrative resistance** to government choices.
- The overall aim is to **resist the normalisation of India's "sadly widespread culture of discrimination of all sorts"**.
