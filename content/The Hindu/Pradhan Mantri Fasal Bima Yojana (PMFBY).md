Affordable crop insurance scheme providing comprehensive coverage against natural calamities, with ₹12,200 crore allocated for 2026-27 and over 92.46 crore farmer applications insured since 2016.

## Basic Overview

- Launched: 18 February 2016, aims to bring maximum farmers under crop insurance coverage.
- Nodal Ministry: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.
- Coverage span: Pre-sowing (prevented/failed sowing) → mid-season (widespread adversity) → localized calamities (individual land parcels) → post-harvest losses.
- Budget 2026-27 allocation: ₹12,200 crore.
- Perils covered: droughts, floods, cyclones, hailstorms, pests, diseases, prevented sowing, localized calamities, inundation, unseasonal rainfall, specified post-harvest losses.

## Farmer Eligibility & Categories

- Inclusive coverage: tenant farmers and sharecroppers eligible (subject to prescribed conditions - insurable interest, valid land documents/tenure agreements/sowing certificates, timely application).
- Non-loanee farmers: those without crop loans or with non-standard KCC-linked loans; enroll voluntarily (avg. 50% of all farmers in last 10 years are non-loanee).
- Loanee farmers: those with active/standard seasonal crop loans/KCCs; premium auto-deducted by banks.
- Since 2018, cumulatively more than 1.44 crore tenant/sharecropper farmers enrolled.

## Case Illustration

Anwar Hussain, a farmer from Chankhala village, Nagaon district, Assam, paid a nominal premium of Rs 100 under PMFBY and received ₹50,600 compensation after heavy rains damaged his crop, helping him repay debt and invest in the next season.

## Risks Covered vs Excluded

|Category|Details|
|---|---|
|Yield losses (standing crops, area-based)|Drought, dry spells, floods, inundation, cyclones, hailstorms, lightning, pests, diseases|
|Prevented sowing|Claims up to maximum 25% of sum insured|
|Post-harvest losses|Crops in "cut-and-spread" condition covered up to 14 days after harvest against cyclonic/unseasonal rain|
|Localized calamities|Hailstorms, landslides, inundation, cloud bursts, damage by natural fire (individual farm-level)|
|Excluded risks|War, nuclear risks, riots, theft, specified post-harvest conditions, other preventable risks; also non-notified areas and losses outside covered crop lifecycle|

## Premium Structure

- Kharif foodgrain/oilseed crops: maximum 2% premium.
- Rabi foodgrain/oilseed crops: maximum 1.5% premium.
- Commercial/horticultural crops: maximum 5% premium.
- Remaining premium subsidized by Centre & State in 50:50 ratio (90:10 for North-Eastern & Himalayan States/UTs).

## Key Statistics (Progress & Achievements)

- Implemented by 25 States/UTs in Kharif 2026.
- Total since inception: 92.46 crore farmer applications insured; 26.33 crore applications paid claims exceeding ₹2.06 lakh crore.
- Kharif 2025: 229.77 lakh farmers covered, 269.38 lakh hectares; claims worth ₹9,837.61 crore paid to 60.89 lakh farmers.
- Kharif 2026 (as of 27 Aug 2026): 241.38 lakh farmers insured, 278.12 lakh hectares — already exceeding Kharif 2025 levels.
- All-time high enrolment: 2024-25, with 15.23 crore farmer applications, 4+ crore farmers, 623+ lakh hectares.

## State-Wise Trends

### Rejoining States (growing confidence in PMFBY)

- Andhra Pradesh — rejoined Kharif 2022.
- Jharkhand — rejoined Kharif 2024.
- West Bengal — rejoined Kharif 2026.
- Bihar — returning from Rabi 2026-27.

### Universalisation Discontinuation Impact

- Andhra Pradesh & Maharashtra had adopted "universalization" (2023-24): State bore 100% premium, causing very high Kharif 2024 enrolment.
- Discontinued in 2025, causing enrolment drops:
    
    - Andhra Pradesh: dropped 77% (31.92 lakh → 7.43 lakh) in Kharif 2025; recovered to 16.22 lakh by 28 Aug 2026 (108% increase over Kharif 2025).
    - Maharashtra: dropped 40% (76.59 lakh → 45.99 lakh) in Kharif 2025; reached 42.55 lakh by 28 Aug 2026 (92% of Kharif 2025 level); dip also linked to exclusion of certain add-on claims.

## Digital & Technology Initiatives

- National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP): digital enrolment, subsidy administration, claim calculation, electronic claim transfer.
- Land Records Integration: validation via state e-land records in MP, Chhattisgarh, AP, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Haryana, HP, UP, Odisha (~85% insured area validated).
- Digiclaim Module (since Kharif 2022): transparent claim calculation/settlement via NCIP, paid through PFMS; over ₹55,000 crore claims processed digitally.
- CCE-Agri App: digital capture of Crop Cutting Experiment yield data for actual yield calculation.
- YES-TECH (Yield Estimation System based on Technology): remote sensing-based yield estimates; launched for paddy/wheat (Kharif 2023), soybean (Kharif 2024); weightage increased from 30% to up to 50% in certain states.
- WINDS (Weather Information Network and Data System): Automatic Weather Stations/Rain Gauges at Block/Gram Panchayat level; supports weather-based insurance, YES-TECH, disaster management, forecasting.
- CROPIC: geo-tagged photos for crop health validation, damage assessment, yield estimation (pilots initiating).
- Krishi Rakshak Portal & Helpline (KRPH): toll-free helpline 14447 launched Jan 2024; 26.12 lakh grievances resolved with 99.66% resolution rate.
- Learning Management System (LMS): stakeholder knowledge/training platform.
- AIDE (App for Intermediary Enrolment): doorstep enrolment of non-loanee farmers via intermediaries.
- CLAP (Crop Loss Assessment App): digital recording of localized calamity crop loss at farm level.

## Restructured Weather-Based Crop Insurance Scheme (RWBCIS)

- Complements PMFBY; addresses weather-specific risks using weather parameters as "proxy" for crop damage (not actual yield loss).
- Crop life cycle divided into phenological phases; sum insured allocated per vulnerability in each phase.
- Operates on "Area Approach" within Reference Unit Areas (RUAs).
- Payouts triggered by deviation of weather variables (rainfall deficit/excess, dry spells, extreme temperature, humidity, wind speed) from pre-defined thresholds at notified weather stations.
- States/UTs may add farm-level cover for hailstorms/cloudbursts.
- Same premium structure as PMFBY (1.5%-5%).
- Popular for Fruit, Vegetable, Plantation crops: 25.95 lakh farmer applications, 12.31 lakh hectares in Kharif 2026.
