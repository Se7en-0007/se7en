## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Context and Launch</mark>

- e-Shram Portal completed 5 years on 26 August 2026 (launched 26 August 2021).
    
- Launched by Ministry of Labour and Employment.
    
- Developed by National Informatics Centre (NIC).
    
- Created country's first Aadhaar-authenticated National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW).
    
- Aligned with vision of Viksit Bharat — inclusive, technology-driven social security ecosystem.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Who is an "Unorganised Worker"</mark>

- Home-based worker, self-employed worker, or wage worker in unorganised sector.
    
- Also includes organised-sector worker who is NOT a member of ESIC/EPFO/Government employee.
    
- Sectors covered: agricultural labourers, construction workers, domestic workers, street vendors, migrant workers, gig and platform workers.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Key Features / Objectives</mark>

- Provides every registered worker a unique 12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN).
    
- Improves delivery of social security services via convergence of schemes across Ministries/Departments.
    
- Facilitates secure data-sharing with government agencies through APIs (supports evidence-based policymaking).
    
- Enables nationwide access and portability of benefits, especially for migrant and construction workers.
    
- Creates a comprehensive national database for planning and response during emergencies/crises.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Evolution: One-Stop Solution (Milestone)</mark>

- e-Shram One-Stop Solution launched on 21 October 2024.
    
- Transformed portal from a mere worker registry into a unified service delivery platform.
    
- Integrates welfare, employment, skilling, and pension services.
    
- Aligned with Union Budget 2024-25 vision of delivering labour services via single digital platform.
    
- Registrants can check eligible schemes and view benefits already received.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Integrated Schemes and Services</mark>

- Brings together 15 Social Security and Welfare Schemes on one platform.
    
- Key schemes: PM-SYM (Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-Dhan Yojana), PMSBY (Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana), PMJJBY (Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana), NSAP (National Social Assistance Programme), ONORC (One Nation One Ration Card), PMAY-G (Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana–Gramin).
    

|Scheme|Benefit|
|---|---|
|PMSBY|₹2 lakh (accidental death/permanent disability); ₹1 lakh (partial disability)|
|PMJJBY|₹2 lakh life cover (death due to any cause)|
|PM-SYM|Minimum assured pension of ₹3,000/month after age 60|

- Linked to National Career Service (NCS) for jobs.
    
- Linked to Skill India Digital Hub for skilling/apprenticeship.
    
- Linked to myScheme for identifying eligible government schemes.
    
- Bhashini platform powers multilingual functionality in 22 languages, introduced in 2025.
    
- Captures family details of migrant workers.
    
- Shares construction workers' data with States/UTs for BOCW (Building and Other Construction Workers) Board registration.
    
- Provides identification/registration platform for gig and platform workers — around 11.5 lakh platform workers registered so far.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Registration: Eligibility and Process</mark>

- Eligibility: Age 16 years and above, engaged in unorganised sector.
    
- Must NOT be an income tax payer or member of EPFO/ESIC.
    
- Coverage: construction, migrant, gig/platform, street vendors, domestic, agricultural, and other unorganised workers.
    
- How to register: Online (register.eshram.gov.in), Common Service Centres (CSCs), State Seva Kendras, or State Government field officers at district/sub-district level.
    
- Cost: Completely free — no fee to any agency or service provider.
    
- Documents required: Aadhaar number (for Aadhaar eKYC) and mobile number (preferably Aadhaar-linked).
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Registration Statistics (as of 18-20 August 2026)</mark>

- Total registrations: 31.89 crore.
    
- Gender: Women 54.28%; Men 45.72%.
    
- Age profile: 16-18 yrs (0.08%), 18-40 yrs (55.21%), 40-50 yrs (24.18%), above 50 yrs (20.53%).
    
- Top occupational categories: Agriculture, Domestic and Household Workers, Construction, Apparel.
    
- Top States: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra.
    
- Mode of registration: Mostly via CSCs, followed by self-registration.
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Significance / Way Forward</mark>

- Created a nationwide digital identity and database for unorganised workers.
    
- Enabled more targeted, transparent delivery of government support.
    
- Positioned as a key digital pillar for an inclusive, future-ready workforce ecosystem.
