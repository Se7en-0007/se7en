##### <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">DRI Uncovers Large-Scale Illegal Use of SAFTA Agreement in Areca Imports</mark>

- **Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI)** busted a syndicate mis-declaring South-East Asian areca nuts (from **Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia**) as Bangladeshi-origin to fraudulently claim **SAFTA duty exemption**.
    
- **Revenue loss to exchequer: over ₹2,500 crore**; **9 arrested**; **₹75 lakh cash** and **~160 MT areca nuts** seized; customs broker licence suspended.
    
- Modus operandi: routed goods through a **Bangladesh Export Processing Zone (EPZ)** to swap documents, bypassing **100% Basic Customs Duty**; used **hawala channels and dummy entities**.
    
- **Impact**: distorts domestic market prices, harms Indian **areca growers**, undermines **border economic security** - key for **trade policy/FTA misuse and customs enforcement** topics.
