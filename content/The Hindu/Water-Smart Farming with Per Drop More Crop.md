## Basic Overview

- PDMC promotes micro-irrigation to improve water use efficiency and agricultural productivity.
- Over 115 lakh hectares brought under micro-irrigation as of July 2026 (~8.11% of India's net sown area).
- Centrally Sponsored Scheme; supports drip and sprinkler irrigation systems.
- Implementation uses direct benefit transfer (DBT) and digital tools for transparency.

## Rationale

- More than 80% of India's water resources are used for agricultural irrigation, yet only ~50% of net sown area has irrigation facilities.
- Micro-irrigation delivers water directly to crops with minimum losses, making it central to improving water use efficiency.

## Financial Assistance Structure

- Assistance covers up to 5 hectares per beneficiary.
- Small and marginal farmers: 55% subsidy.
- Other farmers: 45% subsidy.
- Some states provide additional top-up subsidies from own budgets.
- Central assistance released: ₹8,123.24 crore over last three years.
- Beneficiaries can reapply for same land after 7 years.
- FY 2025-26: ₹8,957.72 crore released/sanctioned for PM-RKVY, of which ₹3,226.36 crore for PDMC specifically.

## Evolution Timeline

|Year|Development|
|---|---|
|Jan 2006|Started as CSS for Micro Irrigation|
|2010-11|Upgraded to National Mission on Micro Irrigation (NMMI)|
|FY 2014-15|Merged into National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA), under "On Farm Water Management" (OFWM)|
|FY 2015-16 to 2021-22|Named PDMC; core component of Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)|
|Since 2022-23|Implemented under Pradhan Mantri Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana (PM-RKVY)|

## PM-RKVY (Umbrella Scheme)

- Original RKVY launched 2007-08 for holistic agriculture development.
- Restructured as PM-RKVY in October 2024.
- Umbrella scheme merging nine schemes, including PDMC, agri-mechanization, soil health management, crop diversification, and accelerator fund for agri-startups.
- Key feature: states can re-allocate funds between components based on local needs.
- Aims: climate resilience, crop productivity, value chain development.

## Strategic Focus Areas of PDMC

- Precision water management.
- Fertigation - applying fertilizers with irrigation water directly to the root zone.
- Priority to water-scarce/groundwater-stressed regions.
- Integration with tube wells, river-lift irrigation, and solar-powered irrigation.
- Convergence with other government programmes.
- Promotion of scientific advances in micro-irrigation for agriculture/horticulture.

## Benefits of Micro-Irrigation

- Reduces wastage; delivers water to plant root zone.
- Lowers use of water, energy, fertilizer, and labour.
- Improves crop growth, productivity, and cropping intensity.
- Brings marginal lands into productive use.
- Encourages horticulture cultivation.
- Strengthens food and nutritional security via cost reduction and higher returns.

## Progress Highlights (2025-26)

- Top states by micro-irrigation coverage: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Rajasthan.
- 12.30 lakh farmers benefitted.
- ~20% of beneficiaries were women.

## Case Study: Andhra Pradesh Farmer

- LAM Srinivasa Rao, Palnadu district, switched from flood irrigation to drip irrigation on 2-acre acid lime orchard.
- Yield increased: 90 → 105 quintals/acre.
- Cultivation cost declined: ₹3.0 lakh → ₹2.4 lakh.
- Net income increased: ₹1.5 lakh → ₹2.85 lakh (additional ₹1.35 lakh).

## New Flexibilities Introduced

- States/UTs empowered for micro-level water storage/conservation (diggis, water harvesting) under "Other Interventions" component - for individual or community use.
- Previous funding caps removed:
    
    - Earlier: 20% cap for general states/UTs; 40% for NE states, Himalayan states, J&K, Ladakh.
    - Now: states/UTs can exceed these caps based on local requirements.

## Types of Micro-Irrigation Systems

### Drip Irrigation

- Water delivered slowly/directly to root zone via emitters on lateral tubes.
- Types:
    
    - On-line drip irrigation: drippers installed per plant location; suits uneven spacing.
    - In-line drip irrigation: drippers built into pipe at fixed intervals; even distribution.

### Sprinkler Irrigation

- Water discharged under pressure through nozzles, simulating rainfall; suited for high plant density crops (cereals, pulses, spices).
- Subtypes:
    
    - Portable sprinklers - pipes shiftable; suits plains and undulating terrain.
    - Micro sprinklers - low radius; used for leafy vegetables, nurseries; alters micro-climate near plant.
    - Mini sprinklers - larger radius than micro; used for groundnut, potato, onion, ginger; also frost protection.
    - Semi-permanent sprinklers - main/lateral pipes buried; nozzles on movable riser pipes.
    - Large Volume Sprinklers (Rain-Guns) - discharge 10,000–32,000 litres/hour; throw radius 24–36 m; for large areas in short time.

## Institutional Framework

- National level: National Stewardship Council (NSC) - strategic direction.
- State level (three-tier):
    
    - State Level Sanctioning Committee (SLSC) - approves plans.
    - Inter-Departmental Working Group (IDWG) - coordinates planning/implementation.
- District level: District Level Implementation Committee (DLIC) - oversees execution, inter-departmental coordination.
