Bharat Taxi is India's first cooperative-led ride-hailing platform, established to combine digital mobility with a member-owned, driver-centric model under the Ministry of Cooperation's vision of "Sahkar se Samriddhi."

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Basic Facts and Legal Foundation</mark>

- Full nature: India's first cooperative-based ride-hailing platform
    
- Date of establishment: 6 June 2025
    
- Legal framework: Registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002 (governs cooperatives operating across more than one state; enables voluntary formation, democratic functioning, institutional autonomy, self-help and mutual assistance)
    
- Guiding vision: "Sahkar se Samriddhi" (Prosperity through Cooperation)
    
- Core ownership principle: "Saarthi Hi Maalik" (Driver is the Owner)
    
- Context: Cooperatives collectively account for more than one-fourth of the world's cooperatives; they operate across agriculture, credit, banking, housing, and women's welfare in India

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Cooperative Ownership and Financial Model</mark>

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Zero-Commission Structure</mark>

- No convenience fees, platform fees, or surge pricing charged
    
- Riders pay only trip fare (based on distance, time, market conditions)
    
- Subscription-based model lets drivers retain full fare earnings
    

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Earnings Distribution</mark>

|Component|Share|
|---|---|
|Credited as Sarathis' capital|20%|
|Distributed among Sarathis (based on km travelled)|80%|
|Service charge (only at airport prepaid booths)|7%|

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Ownership Mechanism</mark>

- Drivers called "Sarathis"
    
- Become member-owners by purchasing shares valued at Rs. 100 each
    
- To gain full partner/ownership rights: purchase shares worth Rs. 500
    
- Reserved seats for Sarathi members on the Board of Directors ensure driver representation in governance
    

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Growth and Geographic Footprint</mark>

- Registered Sarathis (as of 25 July 2026): nearly 8 lakh
    
- Registered customers (as of 25 July 2026): around 41 lakh
    
- Currently operational in: Delhi-NCR, Gujarat, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Mumbai, Jaipur, Kanpur, Pune
    
- Jaipur specific data: over 30,000 Sarathis, nearly 7 lakh customers
    
- Planned expansion cities: Ranchi, Patna, Guwahati, Bhopal, Kolkata, Indore, Nagpur
    
- Target: Nationwide presence by 2029 (phase-wise rollout)

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Women-Focused Initiatives</mark>

- **Sarathi Didi**: Allows women passengers to specifically choose women drivers for rides
    
- **Bike Didi**: Initiative to boost women's participation as drivers; over 150 women drivers had joined by February 2026
    

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Safety Features</mark>

- Safety-by-design architecture with police integration and control-room monitoring
    
- Mandatory Know Your Customer (KYC) verification, periodic background checks, safety training for drivers
    
- Live ride tracking, SOS support, driver ratings
    
- Regular review of safety ratings to retain only trained/verified drivers

|Platform|Function|

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Social Security for Sarathis</mark>

- Facilitated registration on the **e-Shram portal** (Ministry of Labour and Employment) — creates national database of unorganised workers, Aadhaar-seeded, for targeted welfare delivery
    
- Eligibility for **Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Aarogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)**: free medical treatment up to Rs. 5 lakh (world's largest public healthcare scheme, launched 2018)
    
- Personal accident insurance of Rs. 5 lakh via IFFCO Tokio at nominal rates
    
- Group health insurance for drivers and families via Paytm partnership
    
- Advisory support for long-term driver welfare and insurance planning
