PRAXIS is a NASA NIAC-funded mission concept that will use a bio-inspired, AI-driven robotic explorer to directly sample particles from planetary rings for the first time.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">What is PRAXIS</mark>

- Full form: Planetary Rings Autonomous EXploration with In-situ Sampling
    
- Selected under NASA's Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) program for 2026 Phase I funding
    
- Currently a mission concept, not yet an approved full mission
    
- Involves a bio-inspired robotic explorer controlled by an artificial intelligence (AI) model

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Scientific Motivation</mark>

- Guided by open questions flagged in NASA's Planetary Science Decadal Survey
    
- Key unanswered questions: how planetary rings form, how they evolve over time, and what ring particles are actually made of
    
- No previous mission has attempted direct, in-situ sampling of ring particles

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Mission Design and Method</mark>

- Avoids flying through the ring plane directly, since that poses collision hazards from moving particles
    
- Instead, the spacecraft will "graze" the ring plane, staying at a safe distance
    
- Uses a touch-and-go sampling technique: once a target particle is identified, a long deployable boom performs the sample collection
    
- After sampling one region, the craft moves to another region or gap in the rings to collect further samples
    
- Small onboard instruments snag free-floating particles once a sample site is reached

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Role of AI in the Mission</mark>

- The AI model helps analyze collected particles by measuring: particle size, particle porosity, particle composition
    
- AI-driven autonomy is central to the "bio-inspired robotic explorer" concept, allowing it to make sampling decisions in a dynamic ring environment

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">NIAC Funding Stages</mark>

|Stage|Status/Activity|
|---|---|
|Phase I|Current stage — funds simulations and system design studies|
|Phase II|Next possible stage — would fund building a physical prototype|

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Broader Applications</mark>

- Developers state the PRAXIS sampling system architecture is not limited to one planet
    
- Design intended to be adaptable for missions studying multiple ringed planets in the solar system (e.g., Saturn, Uranus, Neptune)
