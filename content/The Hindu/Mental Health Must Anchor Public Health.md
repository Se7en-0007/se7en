- **India’s vision of Viksit Bharat 2047**, under the **Healthcare for All** pillar, prioritises **strengthening Ayushman Bharat**, **expanding primary healthcare**, **reducing out-of-pocket expenditure**, and **promoting preventive care**.
- **Mental health** cuts across these ambitions and determines their success; it must move from the margins of public policy to its centre.
- Mental health is **not a niche concern**. It influences **educational attainment**, **workforce participation**, **social cohesion**, and **physical health outcomes**.
- A **healthier and more productive India** cannot be built without placing **mental well-being** at the centre of public health.

## Major Implications

- **Mental illness affects one in seven Indians**, with nearly **200 million people** living with a **diagnosable mental health condition**.
- The contribution of **mental disorders** to India’s **total disease burden has doubled** over the past **three decades**.
- The national **treatment gap is 84.5%**: **more than four out of five** people requiring care do not receive it.
- India has only **0.3 psychiatrists per 1,00,000 population**, along with significant shortages of **clinical psychologists**, **psychiatric social workers**, and **psychiatric nurses**.
- These shortages reflect decades in which mental health received **insufficient attention**, in India as well as globally.
- **Mental disorders** significantly increase the risk of **chronic diseases**, while chronic diseases increase the risk of **mental illness**.
- **More than 60%** of people attending **primary care facilities** have a **diagnosable mental disorder**.
- **Anxiety and depression** are substantially higher among people living with **diabetes**, **hypertension**, **tuberculosis**, and **HIV/AIDS**.
- Ignoring mental health undermines efforts to control **non-communicable diseases** and reduce **healthcare expenditure**.
- A **2025 modelling study** by researchers from **PGIMER** and **NIMHANS** estimated that integrating **universal depression screening** into India’s primary healthcare system could generate annual **net savings of ₹291 billion to ₹482 billion**.
- This is equivalent to as much as **0.32% of GDP**.
- Mental health is not only a **social welfare issue**: it is a **human capital investment** and an **economic growth strategy**.
- More than **1.73 lakh sub-centres and primary health centres** have been converted into **Ayushman Bharat Arogya Mandirs**, with mental healthcare included in the **essential service packages**.
- The **District Mental Health Programme** covers more than **90% of districts**.
- **Tele-MANAS**, launched in **2022**, has expanded across **all States and Union Territories**.

## Three Main Priorities

### Empower Frontline Health Workers

- The task is not to start from scratch, but to build on the existing foundation **at scale**.
- India’s **one million ASHA workers** constitute the world’s largest **community health workforce**.
- Evidence from **Madhya Pradesh** and other States shows that, with appropriate **training and supervision**, ASHAs can successfully **identify**, **support**, and **refer** individuals with **common mental disorders**.
- Scaling this model through **validated screening tools**, **digital learning platforms**, and **performance-linked incentives** could expand access to care.

### Invest More Systematically In Community-Based Care

- Models such as **Zimbabwe’s Friendship Bench**, **Atmiyata in India**, and programmes implemented by **The Live Love Laugh Foundation** show that trained community workers can bridge the gap between **awareness and treatment**.
- They are particularly useful for **rural and underserved populations**.
- These approaches improve access, reduce costs, and deserve wider **adoption and funding**.

### Fully Realise The Promise Of Financial Protection

- **Outpatient mental health benefits** should be extended under the **Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana**.
- Implementation of **insurance parity**, as provided under the **Mental Healthcare Act, 2017**, should be strengthened.
- These steps would make mental healthcare affordable for **millions** who currently go without care.
