- **August 15** has a shared resonance: **India marked 79 years of freedom from British rule**, while **South Korea marked 81 years of liberation from Japanese rule**.
- The central question is whether the two countries can convert their promising relationship into a **substantially more significant strategic partnership**.
- The partnership is taking shape in India’s industrial corridors: **Chennai’s car plants, Sriperumbudur’s electronics lines, Bengaluru’s research labs, Sri City’s South Korea enclave, Hazira’s howitzer programme, and Thoothukudi’s shipyard ambitions**.
- Its future is being built in **India’s industrial south**, rather than merely announced at summits.

## Hyundai’s Journey In Tamil Nadu

- **Hyundai** has manufactured in **Tamil Nadu since 1996**.
- It reaffirmed Chennai’s importance with a **₹45,000-crore commitment through 2032**.
- Tamil Nadu has been formally declared **Hyundai’s flagship EV hub** for India.
- The plant that once assembled the **Santro** for a newly liberalising India now anchors Hyundai’s **electric future**.
- **Tamil Nadu accounts for over one-third of India’s automobile output**.
- **Samsung’s Sriperumbudur facility**, established in **2007**, has expanded alongside a wider network of suppliers and manufacturing partners.

## Bengaluru’s Research Labs

- **Samsung’s R&D institute in Bengaluru** is the company’s **largest outside South Korea**.
- **Over a thousand engineers** build the **AI and software** used in **Galaxy phones sold worldwide**.
- The institute is supported by **two decades of university research partnerships** across Bengaluru.
- This represents **South Korean innovation jointly built in India**.

## Sri City’s Korea Enclave

- **LG, Samsung, Hyundai Mobis, and Seoul Semiconductor** already operate in **Sri City, Andhra Pradesh**.
- The State directly pitched South Korean firms in **Seoul and Busan**.
- The proposed **“Korea Enclave”** is a **ready-to-use industrial township** built specifically for South Korean manufacturers seeking alternatives to **China-based supply chains**.

## Thoothukudi’s Emerging Shipyard Ambitions

- **HD Hyundai**, South Korea’s shipbuilding giant, has signed an agreement with the **Tamil Nadu and Union governments** for a proposed **$4-billion shipyard** on the Thoothukudi coast.
- The project is explicitly linked to India’s goal of ranking among the world’s **top shipbuilding nations by 2047**.

## Negotiations To Upgrade CEPA

- **President Lee Jae-myung’s state visit to India in April** was the **first visit by a South Korean President in eight years**.
- The visit produced a pronouncement to **accelerate negotiations to upgrade CEPA**.
- Bilateral trade offers considerable economic motivation for both countries.
- However, the headline trade figure masks an important imbalance: **India’s trade deficit is considerable**, though it is likely to shrink through continued negotiations and better understanding between the two governments and their trade.

## The Journey Is No Longer Only A Trade Story

- In **October 2025**, the **Indian and South Korean Navies exercised together for the first time**.
- In **May 2026**, the two Defence Ministries signed their **first cybersecurity pact**.
- In the same month, an **Indian howitzer jointly built with a South Korean firm in Gujarat** demonstrated the potential of defence co-production.
- An Indian diplomat chaired the **UN commission** that oversaw **Korea’s first elections in 1948**, three years before the **Korean War** began.

## What Is Missing From The India-South Korea Relationship

- What is missing is **not substance but visibility**.
- Despite sharing an **Independence Day calendar** and a fast-growing industrial partnership, the two countries have not told this story loudly enough to their own people or to the world.
- **Indians and South Koreans can create dreams together** based on **mutual strengths and shared vision**.

## Co-Builders Of Each Other’s Future

- India and South Korea are no longer only **trading partners**.
- From **Chennai’s assembly lines** to **Thoothukudi’s drawing boards**, they are becoming **co-builders of each other’s future**.
- The partnership needs recognition that something substantial has already been built and is worth building further, rather than another summit announcement.
- Both countries understand the value of **sovereignty, resilience, and the ability to determine their own future**.
- Their potentially powerful convergence lies in building the next generation of **technology, industry, and strategic capability** together.
