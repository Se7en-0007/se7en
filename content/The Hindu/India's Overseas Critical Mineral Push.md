## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Background</mark>

India's overseas critical mineral acquisition strategy centers on KABIL (Khanij Bidesh India Ltd), incorporated in 2019 as a joint venture of National Aluminium Company Ltd (NALCO), Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL), and Mineral Exploration & Consultancy Limited (MECL). Its mandate is to identify, explore, and acquire overseas critical mineral assets to meet India's growing strategic resource demand. Despite this institutional setup, KABIL has struggled to expand its footprint beyond limited progress in Argentina, with proposed investments in Australia, Vietnam, Mali, and Chile either stalling or falling through entirely.

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Key Structural Challenges Identified</mark>

- High asset valuations amid intense global competition for critical mineral projects
    
- Volatile mineral prices, particularly lithium, with significant variation in long-term price forecasts across different market intelligence agencies
    
- Financial constraints limiting India's public sector undertakings (PSUs) in competitive bidding
    
- Socio-political risks and instability in resource-rich host countries
    
- Absence of a domestic value chain to process end products (e.g., spodumene concentrate), weakening the investment case
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Country-Wise Progress</mark>

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Argentina: The Success Story</mark>

KABIL has acquired five lithium brine blocks in Catamarca province (acquired January 2024) and is evaluating seven additional greenfield lithium blocks in the same province. Discussions are also underway for two more lithium projects in Jujuy province, making Argentina India's most successful overseas mineral venture so far.

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Australia: Lost to POSCO</mark>

|Stage|Details|
|---|---|
|Initial bid (Dec 2024)|Consortium of KABIL, Coal India Ltd (CIL), Oil India Ltd (OIL), ONGC Videsh Ltd (OVL) submitted non-binding offer of $184 million for 10% stake each in Mt Marion and Wodgina lithium mines (20% stake in parent Mineral Resources Ltd)|
|Revised bid (Sep 2025)|Raised to $233 million for 20% stake each in both mines (40% stake in Mineral Resources Ltd)|
|Outcome|South Korean firm POSCO won with $765 million for 15% stake each (30% stake in Mineral Resources Ltd)|
|Ongoing evaluation|KABIL, along with OIL, CIL and NLC India Ltd, is jointly evaluating two other projects — Mt Holland and Andover|

Lessons flagged to the Parliamentary panel included lack of domestic processing capacity, price volatility, and valuation mismatches.

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Chile: Two Missed Opportunities</mark>

- A lithium brine project involving high-value investment: KABIL signed a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) in October 2025 and got initial "data room" access, but could not submit a bid in time due to delayed due diligence by consortium partners (CIL, OIL, OVL)
    
- ENAMI (Chile's state mining company) opportunity: KABIL signed an NDA to evaluate the full lithium value chain but handed the opportunity to Coal India Ltd, citing limited financial capacity given its existing Argentina commitments
    

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Vietnam: Deal Withdrawn</mark>

KABIL's proposed acquisition of a 5% stake in rare earth element (REE) projects in Lai Chau province did not materialise, as the seller withdrew the opportunity before due diligence could be completed.

## <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">Africa: Socio-Political Instability</mark>

- Mali: In June 2025, KABIL signed an NDA with Uranium One Group (a Rosatom/Russia subsidiary) to jointly evaluate third-country critical mineral projects. Due diligence began on an exploration-stage hard-rock lithium project in Mali but was put on hold due to socio-political instability
    
- Malawi: KABIL has initiated discussions with the Malawi Mining Investment Company (a Malawi government entity) for joint investment opportunities
    

## <mark style="background: #CACFD9A6;">Other Countries Under Evaluation</mark>

KABIL is also exploring potential opportunities in Brazil, Canada, Russia, and Indonesia.
