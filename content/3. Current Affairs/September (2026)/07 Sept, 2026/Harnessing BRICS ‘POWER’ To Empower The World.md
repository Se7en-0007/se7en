- The **18th BRICS Summit** is to be held in **New Delhi on September 12-13, 2026**.
- China expresses support for India’s **BRICS presidency** and calls for a blueprint for the **high-quality development of a greater BRICS**.
- The proposed framework of **BRICS POWER** comprises five commitments: **Principle, Openness, Win-Win, Engine, and Responsibility**.

## P For Principle

- The world is undergoing an **accelerated transformation unseen in a century**, marked by **deepening multipolarity**, **economic globalisation**, and the **collective rise of the Global South**.
- At the same time, **hegemonism and power politics** are becoming more rampant, while lingering geopolitical conflicts generate recurring crises and challenges.
- As a force for **world peace and development**, BRICS should uphold the **purposes and principles of the United Nations Charter** and basic norms of international relations:
- **Sovereign equality**
- **Non-interference in internal affairs**
- **Peaceful settlement of disputes**
- This is necessary to prevent a return to the **law of the jungle where the strong prey on the weak**.

## Upholding International Principles

- **Building a community with a shared future for humanity** and four global initiatives, including the **Global Security Initiative** and the **Global Governance Initiative**, are presented as solutions for addressing common human challenges and building a better world together.

## O For Openness

- The world economy faces a **sluggish recovery** alongside growing **protectionism**.
- Certain countries have undermined the international economic and trade order through **tariff barriers**, attempts at **decoupling**, and disruption of **supply chains**.
- **Economic globalisation** is described as an **inevitable trend of history**.
- BRICS should:
- Uphold the **multilateral trading system** with the **World Trade Organization** at its core.
- Safeguard WTO principles such as **most-favoured-nation treatment**.
- Resist **all forms of protectionism**.
- Deepen cooperation in **energy and mineral resources**, **infrastructure**, and **industrial and supply chains**.
- Maintain an **open global economic landscape**.
- An **open global economy** should enable countries to **share opportunities in openness** and achieve **common development**.

## W For Win-Win

- BRICS should adhere to the **Spirit of Openness, Inclusiveness and Win-Win cooperation**.
- Development should remain at the **centre of the BRICS agenda**.
- BRICS should:
- Accelerate implementation of the **UN Sustainable Development Goals**.
- Strengthen dialogue and build consensus on formulating the **post-2030 global development agenda**.
- Ensure that **Global South countries** participate in international cooperation **as equals** and share the fruits of development.

## Economic And Financial Cooperation

- Greater BRICS should pursue **mutual benefit and win-win results** to consolidate economic cooperation.
- Required measures include:
- Stronger coordination on **macroeconomic policies**.
- More **trade and investment facilitation measures**.
- Cooperation on **local currency and cross-border payments**.
- Improvement of the **financial safety net** to strengthen the ability to ward off **external risks**.
- In response to global **energy and food security challenges**, BRICS should build a more **resilient and sustainable agriculture and food system**.

## E For Engine

- BRICS countries account for:
- **Nearly half of the world’s population**.
- **Around 30% of global economic output**.
- **One-fifth of global trade**.
- BRICS also possesses **major natural resources**, **key manufacturers**, and **vast markets**.
- According to international agencies such as the **International Monetary Fund**, **greater BRICS growth** will be **nearly three times faster than the G-7 by 2028**.
- This reinforces BRICS’ role as an **engine of world economic growth**.
- BRICS should leverage its respective advantages and deepen practical cooperation in:
- **Business**
- **Finance**
- **Science and technology**
- **Digital economy**
- **Smart manufacturing**
- **New industrialisation**
- It should promote the integration of **artificial intelligence** with **science and technology, health, agriculture, and education** to inject fresh impetus into world economic growth.
- China has established:
- The **China-BRICS AI Development and Cooperation Center**.
- The **China-BRICS New Quality Productive Forces Research Center**.
- All parties are invited to participate actively.

## R For Responsibility

- BRICS countries are described as the **main force in safeguarding fairness and justice** and the **backbone of promoting unity and cooperation**.
- BRICS must shoulder responsibility for maintaining **world peace and development**.
- **India and China** will successively hold the **BRICS rotating presidency** over this year and the next.
- Sound coordination between India and China is considered conducive to the **steady and sustained progress of greater BRICS cooperation** and to safeguarding the common interests of **developing countries**.

## China-India Ties

- Since the beginning of the year, China-India relations have maintained momentum of **improvement and development** under the strategic guidance of **President Xi Jinping** and **Prime Minister Narendra Modi**.
- The two sides have strengthened dialogue at all levels and promoted exchanges and cooperation in various fields, producing positive results.
- **Five direct flight routes** between the Chinese mainland and India have been resumed.
- **Border trade** has been reopened after a **six-year suspension**.
- China seeks to translate the leaders’ consensus into concrete actions, become **good-neighbourly friends and partners that help each other**, and advance China-India relations in a **sustained, healthy, and stable manner**.
