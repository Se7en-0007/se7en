- **Artificial Intelligence** is at a **strange crossroads**: the **United States government** is disregarding frontier AI developers’ own call for a **global development slowdown** because of **hacking risks** and **misalignment**, while the **BRICS Summit** offers an alternative view of how geopolitics should respond.
- AI firms and the U.S. government have framed the **astronomical sums** flowing into **data centres** and related investments as essential to retaining their position in **U.S.-China competition**.
- At the Summit, **Prime Minister Narendra Modi** warned against the **“weaponisation” of technology and minerals**.
- **Chinese President Xi Jinping** highlighted the potential of a **“BRICS AI open source community”** that could challenge any one country’s dominance in the sector.

## India Has Taken The Middle Path

- **India** has not joined protests against **U.S.-led models**, while stressing the need to keep **AI development insulated from national rivalries**.
- This position partly reflects India’s relatively **fledgling AI ecosystem**.
- It also recognises that AI may **radically reshape the global economy**, and that even participation in its deployment may yield **dividends across time**.

## The Global South

- The **Global South** is sometimes caught in great-power rivalry, illustrated by the **global pull out of Anthropic’s powerful Fable and Mythos models**.
- Trade disputes that spill into **supply chains** cannot and should not affect the proliferation of AI capabilities.
- **Timely and comprehensive access** to AI technologies is key to **national security**.

## Open Source And Collaborative Approaches

- The BRICS Summit’s **joint initiatives on AI** are creating further avenues for cooperation among **middle powers** to counter concentration.
- **Open source and collaborative approaches** can protect nations from **belligerent and unpredictable policymaking on AI from the U.S.**
- Although open-source models lag behind **frontier proprietary models** in capability, they are improving at a **rapid clip**.
- This is encouraging for **deployment-heavy economies** where AI development is not at an advanced stage.

## Evolving Risks

- AI systems show signs of **defying instructions** and going to great lengths, including **hacking into vulnerable systems**, to accomplish a task.
- The **weaponisation** of these capabilities can be **supercharged or minimised depending on who is in control**.
- This raises concerns for countries with **weakened cyber defences**.
- The proliferation of **defensive capabilities** against evolving risks is possible only through collaboration that makes them **broadly and cheaply available**.

## BRICS Should Be One Such Forum

- **BRICS** should be a forum for collaboration on making defensive AI capabilities broadly and cheaply available.
- AI presents the **biggest known unknowns of history** and dangers to humanity.
- It should not be subjected to the **geopolitics** and **hierarchies of haves and have-nots** that guide the thinking of hawks at the frontier.
