- In an increasingly **complex and uncertain international landscape**, meaningful **international cooperation** should be judged by its capacity to deliver **stability, resilience and shared prosperity**.
- Since becoming a **full BRICS member in January 2024**, the **UAE** has pursued the principle that **economic cooperation should create tangible opportunities for countries, businesses and people**.

## A Diversity That Creates Opportunities

- **BRICS’ strength** partly arises from the diversity of its members' **economic structures, resources, capabilities and development experiences**.
- Members include **major producers and consumers**, **sources of capital**, **investment destinations**, **energy exporters** and **manufacturing centres**.
- Such differences generate **complementarity** by connecting **markets, capital, capabilities and ideas** for the benefit of members and, by extension, the **Global South**.
- The UAE's BRICS participation reflects its commitment to **multilateralism, constructive dialogue and diversified international partnerships**.
- It seeks to build bridges between **economies and regions** and supports an **open international system** advancing **peace, stability, sustainable development and shared prosperity**.
- BRICS’ value ultimately lies in **implementation**: dialogue should result in **easier trade, stronger investment flows, more resilient supply chains, better connectivity and greater business opportunities**.

## India’s 2026 BRICS Chairship

- **India’s 2026 BRICS Chairship** is centred on **resilience, innovation, cooperation and sustainability**.
- Amid rising **trade restrictions, supply-chain disruptions and uncertainty**, which increase costs for **businesses and consumers**, the response should be not retreat from **global economic integration**, but making it more **resilient and inclusive**.
- BRICS can produce tangible benefits by:
    
    - Strengthening the **multilateral trading system**
    - Facilitating **trade and investment**
    - Improving **connectivity**
    - Supporting **resilient global value chains**
    - Promoting **sustainable development**

## Development Finance

- The **New Development Bank** has approved **more than $40 billion in financing** since its establishment for **infrastructure and sustainable development** across member countries.
- The UAE's engagement with the NDB before joining BRICS reflects its commitment to mobilising capital for **productive investment and long-term growth**.

## On UAE-India Ties, The UAE’s Advantages

- The **UAE-India Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement** has created a more enabling environment for trade and investment between two **highly complementary economies**.
- In **2025**, **non-oil bilateral trade** grew by **17%** to exceed **$76 billion**.
- The UAE and India aim to increase bilateral trade to **$200 billion by 2032**.
- The relationship extends beyond trade through **investment, innovation, education, tourism**, and long-standing links among **businesses, institutions and people**.
- Strong economic partnerships require not only government agreements but also lasting connections across societies, creating **trust, opportunity and resilience** for commercial ties to deepen.
- **Education, research, tourism, cultural exchange and engagement of younger generations** build the trust and networks necessary for longer-term economic relationships.
- BRICS should deepen the quality of connectivity among members by connecting not only **economies**, but also **institutions, ideas and people**.

## Connectivity Lies At The Heart

- **Connectivity lies at the heart of the UAE’s economic model**.
- **Non-oil sectors** accounted for almost **79% of UAE GDP in 2025**.
- This is supported by **world-class infrastructure**, **trusted financial institutions**, **advanced logistics and energy capabilities**, and an **open investment environment** that enables businesses to **scale-upwards and outwards**.
- UAE **ports, airports and logistics networks** connect markets across **Asia, Africa, Europe and beyond**.
- Its financial centres connect **international capital** with regional opportunities, while its **universities, research institutions and innovation ecosystem** link **talent, knowledge and ideas**.
- With **sovereign wealth assets exceeding $2.9 trillion** and **38 concluded Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreements**, the UAE has the capacity to connect **capital, markets and economies at scale**.
- Connectivity is not merely moving goods between markets; it creates conditions for **capital, knowledge, technology, talent and people** to interact and create opportunities.
- The UAE seeks to build economic cooperation around **connectivity**, not **division**.

## The Path Under India’s Leadership

- India’s 2026 leadership offers an opportunity to move towards delivery by:
    
    - Building **resilience without closing markets**
    - Supporting **innovation while widening opportunity**
    - Strengthening links among the **businesses, institutions and people** of the UAE and India
- The UAE looks forward to **China’s BRICS Chairship in 2027** to build on India’s momentum and continue **openness, connectivity and practical cooperation**.
- The success of BRICS cooperation will be measured by the **opportunities it creates**.
- As India hosts the **2026 BRICS Summit on September 12-13**, the UAE intends to continue translating cooperation into **shared prosperity**.
