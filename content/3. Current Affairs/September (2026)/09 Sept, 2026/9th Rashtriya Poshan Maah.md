## A Month-Long Celebration Of Nutrition

- **Rashtriya Poshan Maah (National Nutrition Month)** is observed every **September** to mobilise communities and reinforce nutrition, health and caregiving behaviours among **pregnant women, lactating mothers, children, adolescents and their families**.
- The **9th Poshan Maah 2026**, launched on **9 September 2026**, highlights the importance of **Anganwadi Centres** and promotes awareness of **balanced, protein-rich nutrition** for pregnant and lactating women.
- Successive Poshan Maah campaigns have strengthened awareness and positive behaviour change on **maternal nutrition**, **Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) practices**, **growth monitoring** and **hygiene**.

## Background

- **Malnutrition** remains a major public-health challenge affecting the **health, growth, learning and productivity** of women and children across the life cycle.
- The Government of India launched **POSHAN Abhiyaan** in **March 2018** as a **multi-ministerial convergence mission** that places nutrition at the centre of the development agenda.
- In the **Union Budget 2021-22**, interventions were integrated under **Mission Poshan 2.0**, India’s unified **National Nutrition Mission**, anchored by the **Ministry of Women and Child Development**.
- **Mission Poshan 2.0** consolidates:
    
    - **POSHAN Abhiyaan** - technology-driven monitoring, **Jan Andolan** mass mobilisation and behavioural change.
    - **Anganwadi Services/Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)** - supplementary nutrition, hot cooked meals and early childhood care.
    - **Scheme for Adolescent Girls** - nutritional support for out-of-school/adolescent girls.
- **Rashtriya Poshan Maah**, observed every September, spreads awareness about the objectives of **Mission Poshan 2.0**.
- The **9th Rashtriya Poshan Maah** was launched at the **Rudraksh International Cooperation and Convention Centre, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh**.
- **Anganwadi Centres** are described as the **world’s largest childcare network**, providing essential care and support even in remote communities.
- The central theme is **“Hamari Anganwadi Hamari Jimmedari”**, calling on every citizen to take shared ownership of Anganwadi Centres.

## 9th Rashtriya Poshan Maah

- Under **“Hamari Anganwadi, Hamari Jimmedari”**, Poshan Maah 2026 seeks to place **Anganwadi Centres** at the centre of community-led action for nutrition, care and early childhood development.
- It aims to strengthen **collective ownership and accountability for Poshan** through five priorities:
    
    - Promotion of **dietary diversity**, adequate protein intake, diverse food groups and local foods.
    - Promotion of fresh, diverse **Hot Cooked Meals (HCM)** under the **Supplementary Nutrition Programme (SNP)** through menu rotation, hygiene and quality assurance.
    - Strengthening community ownership and accountability through newly constituted **Anganwadi Management Committees (AMCs)** and display of **SNP Menu Boards**.
    - Promoting **cognitive development** and adequate nutrition as core pillars of **Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE)**.
    - Commemorating **10 years** of **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)**, highlighting maternal nutrition, health-seeking behaviour, income support during pregnancy and care during the **first 1,000 days of life**.

## Dietary Diversity & Adequate Protein

- A diverse diet including **cereals and millets, pulses, fruits and vegetables, nuts and seeds, milk and milk products**, and adequate protein from **plant and animal sources** is essential to meet nutrient requirements, support healthy growth and development, and prevent all forms of malnutrition.
- Dietary diversity across the life course, especially during the **complementary feeding period**, improves nutrition outcomes and encourages healthy eating habits from an early age.
- Proposed activities:
    
    - **Healthy and Diverse Recipe Demonstrations** at Anganwadi Centres using affordable, locally available ingredients from different food groups, including cereals, millets, pulses, vegetables, fruits, nuts, seeds, milk products and eggs.
    - **Poshan Melas** showcasing local nutritious foods through interactive displays and recipe demonstrations, with counselling on balanced and diverse meals for every age group.
    - **‘Protein-Rich Foods’ Campaign** on plant and animal protein sources such as pulses, legumes, nuts, seeds, milk, milk products and eggs.
    - **Showcasing Poshan Vatika Produce and Local Foods** to promote their use in household meals and complementary feeding for young children.

## Fresh Hot Cooked Meals (HCM) At Anganwadi Centers

- Consistent provision of fresh, diverse and nutritious **Hot Cooked Meals (HCM)** is central to improving the quality, utilisation and effectiveness of the **Supplementary Nutrition Programme (SNP)** and encouraging regular attendance at Anganwadi Centres.
- Freshly prepared, hygienic meals made with locally available ingredients, including **millets, pulses and seasonal vegetables**, promote dietary diversity and healthier household eating practices.
- Poshan Maah 2026 seeks to improve the quality and acceptability of HCM through **weekly menu rotation, community participation and quality assurance mechanisms**.
- Proposed activities:
    
    - **Weekly Menu Rotation** using locally available ingredients, millets, pulses and seasonal vegetables.
    - **Hot Cooked Meal Demonstrations** as per SNP guidelines, focusing on ingredients, hygiene and balanced meal composition.
    - **Mission-Mode Hot Cooked Meal Quality Testing** nationwide in collaboration with **FSSAI** to reinforce food-safety and quality standards.
    - **Community Hot Cooked Meal Tasting Sessions** involving parents, Panchayats, Self-Help Groups and Anganwadi Management Committees.

## Community Ownership & Accountability

- **Anganwadi Management Committees (AMCs)** were constituted under Ministry guidelines issued on **23 June 2026**.
- Chaired by the **Anganwadi Supervisor**, AMCs are intended to promote participatory oversight, community engagement and improvement in the quality and effectiveness of service delivery at Anganwadi Centres.
- Poshan Maah 2026 aims to operationalise AMCs through their participation in awareness generation, beneficiary mobilisation, and oversight of nutrition and early-childhood-care services.
- Universal display and use of **SNP Menu Boards** at Anganwadi Centres can improve transparency by making beneficiary entitlements and daily menus visible to communities.
- AMCs and Menu Boards can increase community monitoring of **menu adherence, meal quality and service regularity**, while improving awareness of supplementary nutrition, beneficiary entitlements and responsive childcare practices.
- Proposed activities:
    
    - **Activation of AMCs** at all Anganwadi Centres, including at least **one AMC meeting during Poshan Maah**.
    - Display of **SNP Menu Boards** at all Anganwadi Centres for awareness of day-wise **HCM/THR menus**, transparency and community monitoring.
    - Involvement of AMCs and **Mothers’ Committees** in monitoring meal quality, quantity, menu adherence and periodic social audit of the Supplementary Nutrition Programme.

## Nutrition And Early Stimulation For Childhood Development

- This theme reflects the direction of the **Chief Secretaries’ Conference** that cognitive development and adequate nutrition are fundamental to **Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE)**.
- It emphasises the inseparable link between **what a child eats** and **how a child learns**.
- Good nutrition and responsive caregiving during the **first 1,000 days** are essential for optimal brain development.
- Undernutrition during this period can have lasting effects on **cognitive, motor and socio-emotional development**.
- The revised **Home Visit Scheduler** contains a structured package of **34 developmental milestones** for children aged **0-3 years** and supports Anganwadi Workers in caregiver counselling and early stimulation.
- Proposed activities:
    
    - Accelerated rollout of the revised **Home Visit Scheduler** for coordinated counselling and follow-up during the first 1,000 days and early childhood.
    - Implementation of **Navchetna** and **Aadharshila** curricula for age-appropriate learning, development and school readiness.
    - Promotion of referral pathways and awareness for identifying and referring children with developmental delays to health systems and **RBSK teams**.
    - Mass awareness on adequate nutrition and early childhood stimulation for optimal brain and cognitive development.

## Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY): Cash Transfers For Maternal Health

- **PMMVY** completes **10 years** as India’s flagship maternity benefit programme.
- It supports **maternal nutrition**, promotes health-seeking behaviours and provides **income support during pregnancy**.
- Poshan Maah 2026 aims to expand coverage among all eligible beneficiaries, strengthen citizen awareness and mark a decade of progress in maternal and child health.
- Proposed activities:
    
    - **100% Saturation Drive** to identify and register all eligible pregnant and lactating women; review pendency; expedite verification, approvals and **Direct Benefit Transfers (DBTs)**; and monitor progress through PMMVY dashboards.
    - **Citizen Awareness Campaign** on eligibility, benefits, instalments and the application process, including beneficiary testimonials and success stories through social and mass media.
    - Launch of a **PMMVY Toolkit** for field implementation by States and Union Territories, with localisation and dissemination in regional languages.

## Community Engagement: Nutrition As A Jan Andolan

- The defining character of **POSHAN Abhiyaan** is its positioning of nutrition improvement as a **Jan Andolan** - a genuine people’s movement rather than a government-administered programme.
- Lasting behavioural change in **infant feeding practices, dietary diversity, antenatal care, hand hygiene and institutional health-seeking** requires active community ownership and ground-level participation.
- **Poshan Maah** and **Poshan Pakhwada** are among the largest nutrition-centric community-mobilisation campaigns, conducted annually in designated months to promote practices that nurture health, wellness and immunity.

## Poshan Pakhwada (April 2026)

- **Poshan Pakhwada** has been observed annually since **2018** as a two-week intensive outreach campaign in **April** under POSHAN Abhiyaan.
- The **8th Poshan Pakhwada** was held from **9 to 23 April 2026**.
- More than **85% of brain development** occurs by the age of **six years**, with the most rapid growth occurring during the **first 1,000 days**.
- The first 1,000 days are crucial for optimal brain growth, physical development and holistic well-being.
- Its theme was **“Maximizing Brain Development in the First Six Years of Life”**.
- It sought to shift the narrative from **“nutrition for health”** to **“nutrition and early childhood stimulation for optimal brain development”**.

## 8th Rashtriya Poshan Maah

- The **8th Rashtriya Poshan Maah** was launched on **17 September 2025** from **Dhar, Madhya Pradesh**, along with the **Swasth Nari, Sashakt Parivar Abhiyaan (SNSPA)**.
- It was jointly implemented by the **Ministry of Women and Child Development** and the **Ministry of Health and Family Welfare**.
- Its focus was **nutrition literacy and healthy lifestyles**.
- Key themes included **maternal nutrition**, **Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF)**, **Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE)**, and reduced **sugar and oil consumption** to address obesity.
- A total of **14.33 crore activities** were conducted nationwide in convergence with **more than 20 Ministries**.

## The Poshan Tracker: A Digital Platform For Nutrition Governance

- Launched on **1 March 2021**, the **Poshan Tracker** is the primary governance tool for monitoring infrastructure and service delivery.
- It enables near real-time data collection for Anganwadi Services, including:
    
    - Opening of Anganwadi Centres.
    - Daily attendance of children.
    - ECCE activities.
    - Growth monitoring.
- The system monitors **pregnant women, lactating mothers, children up to 6 years of age and adolescent girls**.
- As of **31 July 2026**, India’s nutrition infrastructure consisted of:
    
    - Nearly **13.99 lakh Anganwadi Centres**.
    - **13.30 lakh Anganwadi Workers**.
    - Nearly **8.94 crore beneficiaries**.
- Children aged **6 months to 6 years** accounted for **over 7.2 crore beneficiaries**.
- Pregnant women and lactating mothers together numbered nearly **1.1 crore**, reflecting focus on maternal health during the **first 1,000 days**.

## Towards Healthier Families And Nation

- **Rashtriya Poshan Maah** combines **technology-driven real-time tracking** with large-scale **Jan Andolan** community mobilisation.
- Operating under **Mission Poshan 2.0**, it seeks to bridge service-delivery gaps, promote dietary diversity and prioritise the critical early years of a child.
- The campaign aims to build a **healthier, malnutrition-free India**.
