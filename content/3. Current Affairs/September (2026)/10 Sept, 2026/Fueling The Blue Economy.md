### Six Years Of Empowering India’s Fisheries Sector Under PMMSY

- **The Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** has strengthened India’s fisheries sector.
- The Government allocated a record **₹2,500 crore in BE 2026-27** under **PMMSY**.
- **Fish production** increased from **141.64 lakh tonnes in 2019-20** to a record **197.75 lakh tonnes in 2024-25**.
- **Fisheries exports** rose from **₹46,663 crore in 2019-20** to **₹73,890 crore in 2025-26**.
- The scheme created employment opportunities for **58 lakh persons** in fisheries and aquaculture-related activities.
- **₹2,797 crore** was approved for **cold-chain and marketing infrastructure**.
- **2,195 Fish Farmers Producer Organizations (FFPOs)** were supported through projects worth **₹544.86 crore**.

## Strengthening India’s Blue Economy

- India’s fisheries sector sustains **nearly three crore livelihoods**, particularly among **marginalised and coastal communities**.
- As a recognised **Sunrise Sector**, fisheries has recorded consistent growth through policies promoting **production, exports, infrastructure and technology adoption**.
- **PMMSY** was introduced to foster **inclusive and sustainable growth** and address critical gaps across the fisheries value chain, building on the **Blue Revolution scheme**.
- Completing **six years on 10 September 2026**, PMMSY has contributed to **modernisation, sustainability and prosperity** in India’s fisheries sector.
- Since its launch in **2020**, the scheme has built infrastructure, opened opportunities, and provided support to fishers and fish farmers.
- PMMSY has been implemented since **2020-21** with a total outlay of **₹20,750 crore**.
- As of **11 August 2026**, fisheries and aquaculture projects worth **₹21,394.88 crore**, including a **Central share of ₹9,510.89 crore**, were approved during **2020-21 to 2025-26** on proposals received from **States, Union Territories and various implementing agencies**.
- PMMSY focuses on enhancing **production and productivity**, modernising the **value chain**, generating **livelihood opportunities**, increasing **incomes**, boosting **exports and GVA**, strengthening **fishers’ security and welfare**, and improving **fisheries management and regulation**.

## Success Story: From Ice Plant To Greater Prosperity

- In **Porbandar, Gujarat**, **Pravinbhai Babulal Masani**, who had spent over two decades building his fishing enterprise, needed support to establish an ice plant.
- In **2021-22**, he received a **₹48 lakh subsidy** under PMMSY and established a **30 MT/day ice plant**.
- The facility produces high-quality ice blocks for fishing boats, enabling them to carry sufficient ice, keep fish fresh and reduce spoilage during fishing operations.
- The support strengthened his fishing business, improved earnings and supported a better livelihood for his family.

## Transforming India’s Fisheries Sector Through PMMSY

- PMMSY is an umbrella scheme comprising **Central Sector (CS)** and **Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS)** components.

### Central Sector (CS)

- The **Central Sector** is **fully funded and implemented by the Central Government**.
- It supports **genetic improvement, innovation, technology demonstrations, training, aquatic quarantine, harbour modernisation, disease surveillance and fisheries data systems**.
- It strengthens the **National Fisheries Development Board (NFDB)** and fisheries institutions, **fisher safety**, **FFPOs**, **cooperatives** and **SHGs**.
- It promotes **certification, traceability, accreditation and labelling**.
- **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY)** is a **Central Sector sub-scheme** under PMMSY and complements its overarching objectives.

### Pradhan Mantri Matsya Kisan Samridhi Sah-Yojana (PM-MKSSY)

- **PM-MKSSY** is operational across all **States and Union Territories** for **four years from 2023-24 to 2026-27**.
- It has an estimated financial outlay of **₹6,000 crore**.
- It facilitates structural transformation through **formalisation**, expanded **insurance coverage**, improved access to **institutional finance**, and **quality assurance and traceability** across the fisheries value chain.
- It aims to enhance **financial resilience**, **risk mitigation** and **market integration** for fishers, aquaculture farmers and allied stakeholders.

### Centrally Sponsored Scheme (CSS)

- The **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** is partially funded by the Central Government and implemented by **States and Union Territories**.
- **Enhancement Of Fish Production And Productivity** focuses on technology infusion and improved fisheries practices across **inland fisheries, marine fisheries, mariculture, seaweed cultivation, fisheries in Himalayan and North-Eastern States/UTs, and ornamental fisheries**.
- **Infrastructure And Post-Harvest Management** supports **fishing harbours, fish landing centres, post-harvest facilities, cold-chain infrastructure, deep-sea fishing, integrated modern coastal fishing villages, markets and marketing infrastructure**.
- **Fisheries Management And Regulatory Framework** promotes **monitoring, control and surveillance**, insurance for fishing vessels and fishers, fisheries extension and support services, safety and security of fishers at sea, and livelihood and nutritional needs of fishers.

## PMMSY: Strengthening Production, Infrastructure And Livelihoods

- **Fish production** increased from **141.64 lakh tonnes in 2019-20** to **197.75 lakh tonnes in 2024-25**.
- PMMSY created direct and indirect employment opportunities for **58 lakh persons** in fisheries and aquaculture-related activities.
- **Fisheries exports** increased from **₹46,663 crore in 2019-20** to **₹73,890 crore in 2025-26**.

## Expanding Modern Aquaculture And Fisheries Infrastructure

- **12 Integrated Aquaparks** were approved at a total cost of **₹713.80 crore**.
- During **2020-25**, projects worth **₹198.17 crore** were approved for **seaweed cultivation and allied activities**.
- **52,058 reservoir cages** and **23,285.06 hectares of pond area** were approved for inland aquaculture.
- PMMSY supported **12,081 Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS)** and **4,205 Biofloc units**.
- **2,672 ornamental fish rearing and integrated ornamental fish units** were supported.

## Strengthening Fisheries Infrastructure And Value Chain

- During the last five years, **₹3,741.89 crore** was approved for **28 fishing harbour development/modernisation projects**, **25 fish landing centre projects**, and **23 dredging projects**.
- **₹2,797 crore** was approved for cold-chain and marketing infrastructure.
- As of **11 August 2026**, approved projects included:
- **28,489 fish transportation units**.
- **1,394 live fish vending units**, **6,018 fish kiosks**, and **117 retail fish markets**.
- **775 cold storage and ice plants** and **24 wholesale fish markets**.
- These interventions strengthen **post-harvest management, cold chains, transportation and market access** for inland fisheries produce.

## Empowering Communities Through Collective Action, Resilience And Technology

### Promoting Collective Empowerment Through FFPOs

- PMMSY provides financial assistance to establish **Fish Farmers Producer Organizations (FFPOs)** to economically empower fishers and fish farmers and strengthen their bargaining power.
- From **FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26**, projects worth **₹544.86 crore** were approved to establish **2,195 FFPOs** nationwide.
- The support covers **incubation, management costs and equity grants**.

### Building Climate-Resilient Coastal Communities

- **100 coastal fishermen villages** have been identified as **Climate Resilient Coastal Fishermen Villages (CRCFV)**.
- The initiative aims to strengthen climate resilience and make the villages economically vibrant.
- It is implemented under the **Central Sector component** with a **unit cost of ₹200 lakh per village**, fully funded by the Government.
- It supports **resilient infrastructure, livelihoods and post-harvest facilities** for coastal fishing communities.

### Technology-Driven Aquaculture

- PMMSY promotes high-density and water-efficient aquaculture systems that enhance productivity while optimising land and water use.
- **Recirculatory Aquaculture Systems (RAS)** and **Bio-floc technology** improve output, water quality, resource efficiency and environmental sustainability.
- **Recirculatory Aquaculture System (RAS)** filters and recirculates water, removes waste and impurities, and enables intensive fish farming with minimal land and water.
- **Bio-floc Technology** recycles nutrients in water using beneficial microbes, which form **bio-floc** that serves as natural feed and helps clean water.
- Bio-floc requires little or no water exchange, supports high-density fish farming with minimal resources, and is known as **“green soup”** or **heterotrophic ponds** in aquaculture circles.

## Digital Transformation Of Fisheries

- The **National Fisheries Digital Platform (NFDP)** was launched under **PM-MKSSY** in **September 2024**.
- It aims to advance **digital governance and formalisation** in the fisheries and aquaculture sector.
- NFDP creates **work-based digital identities** and a comprehensive stakeholder database.
- It functions as a **single-window digital system** for access to **institutional credit, aquaculture insurance, traceability mechanisms and performance-linked incentives**.
- The platform also supports fisheries cooperatives, training and capacity-building initiatives.
- As of **8 September 2026**, NFDP recorded over **37.01 lakh individual registrations**, while total registrations crossed **37.23 lakh**.

## Contribution To SDG 14: Life Below Water

- PMMSY promotes **sustainable and responsible fisheries and aquaculture** and supports sustainable use of aquatic resources.
- It promotes sustainable fish production methods and modern aquaculture practices, including **Bio-floc, RAS, cage culture and mariculture**.
- These interventions contribute to **SDG 14 - Life Below Water** through sustainable fisheries and aquaculture management and aquatic resource conservation.

## Towards Sustainable Fisheries And Blue Economy Growth

- Fisheries contributes to **livelihoods, food security, employment and exports** in India’s agricultural and rural economy.
- PMMSY strengthens **production, infrastructure, technology, livelihoods and sustainability** in India’s fisheries sector.
- Its investments in modern infrastructure, cold-chain facilities, technology-driven aquaculture and digital platforms strengthen the fisheries value chain.
- Continued support for fishers, fish farmers and coastal communities can create opportunities for higher incomes and improved market access.
- PMMSY has laid the foundation for a **modern, competitive and sustainable fisheries sector** and will continue to support livelihoods, food security, exports and responsible utilisation of aquatic resources.
