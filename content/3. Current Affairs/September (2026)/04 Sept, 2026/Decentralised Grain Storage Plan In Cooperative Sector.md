## **Strengthening India’s Decentralized Grain Storage**

- **The Decentralised Grain Storage Plan in Cooperative Sector**, launched in **2023**, aims to improve **storage, processing, procurement, and distribution systems** through **Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS)**.
- It follows an **integrated, cooperative-led approach** by converging existing government schemes, supported by **institutional coordination** and **financial support mechanisms**.
- It began with pilot projects in **11 states/PACS**, creating **9,750 MT** of storage capacity.
- As of **July 2026**, godown construction was completed in **313 PACS**, creating more than **1.80 LMT** of storage capacity.
- The framework emphasises **quality standards, transparency, and operational viability**.

## **Building Storage Capacity For A Growing Agricultural Economy**

- India’s grain storage system combines **traditional storage practices**, **decentralised local systems**, and **modern infrastructure**.
- Traditional methods include **earthen bins, underground pits, and mud plastered storage structures**, alongside warehouses and **silo-based infrastructure**.
- Rising agricultural production and food-security requirements have increased the need for **modern, decentralised storage facilities**.
- According to the **Third Advance Estimates (2025-26)**, total foodgrain production is estimated at **376.563 million tonnes**, nearly **18.8 million tonnes** or **5.3 percent** higher than the previous year’s **357.732 million tonnes**.
- The **National Food Security Act, 2013** covers about **80 crore people**.
- While storage managed by the **Food Corporation of India (FCI)** and State agencies remains important, decentralised facilities are essential for:
    
    - **Reducing transportation costs** between procurement centres
    - **Minimising foodgrain wastage**
    - Supporting **food security**
- The **Ministry of Cooperation** has highlighted the need for storage closer to **production centres**.

## **Decentralised Grain Storage Plan In Cooperative Sector**

- The Plan was rolled out as a **Pilot Project on 31 May 2023**.
- It seeks to:
    
    - **Reduce foodgrain wastage** and strengthen **food security**
    - Give farmers flexibility to avoid **distress sales** and secure better **price realization**
    - Reduce transportation costs between **procurement centres, warehouses, and Fair Price Shops**
    - Expand the role of **PACS** through diversification of agricultural activities
    - Support higher income opportunities for farmers
- **PACS** are village-level institutions that provide **short-term credit and financial services** to rural borrowers and facilitate the **collection of repayments**.

## **Integrated Infrastructure And Convergence Framework**

- The Plan seeks to create integrated agricultural infrastructure at the **PACS level** through convergence of existing schemes.

|**Scheme**|**Support Provided**|
|---|---|
|**Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) Scheme**|Financing and **interest subvention** for creation and upgradation of **post-harvest infrastructure**|
|**Agricultural Marketing Infrastructure Scheme (AMI)**|**Back-ended capital subsidy** for construction of foodgrain storage facilities|
|**Sub Mission On Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM)**|Establishment of **Custom Hiring Centres** and access to agricultural machinery|
|**Pradhan Mantri Formalisation Of Micro Food Processing Enterprises Scheme (PMFME)**|Formalisation and growth of **micro food processing units**|

- Convergence enables PACS to evolve into **integrated rural service hubs**, strengthening agricultural infrastructure, value chains, and farmers’ incomes.

## **Key Facilities And Services Under The Decentralised Grain Storage Plan In Cooperative Sector**

- **Custom Hiring Centres** enable PACS to procure agricultural machinery for rental to farmers, including **tractors, harvesters, tillers, and other farm machinery**.
- **Fair Price Shops** facilitate grain distribution and sale of processed food products within local communities.
- **Primary processing units** conduct grain cleaning, sorting, and drying to improve produce quality and marketability.
- **Procurement centres** enable PACS to procure grain for **State Agencies and FCI**.
- The Plan provides **silo-based storage infrastructure** with capacities of **50 MT, 100 MT, and 200 MT** under the cooperative storage model.

## **Selection Of PACS And Institutional Framework**

- Under the **“Margdarshika” (Standard Operating Procedure)**, PACS are identified and approved by **District Cooperative Development Committees (DCDCs)**.
- PACS are selected in areas with **storage demand**.
- PACS should preferably have their **own land**.
- Land should not be **waterlogged** or located in **forest areas**.
- Consent of PACS members is required for land utilisation.
- For leased land, the lease period should be **longer than 20 years**.

## **Implementation Mechanism**

- The **National Cooperative Development Corporation (NCDC)** is the implementing agency.
- At the national level:
    
    - The **Inter-Ministerial Committee (IMC)** monitors overall implementation.
    - The **National Level Coordination Committee (NLCC)** supports operational coordination among participating institutions and agencies.
- At the State and district levels:
    
    - **State Cooperative Development Committees (SCDCs)**
    - **District Cooperative Development Committees (DCDCs)**  
        support implementation and coordination.

## **Financial Support And Project Viability**

- The subsidy under the **AMI scheme** was enhanced from **25 percent to 33.33 percent**.
- The margin money requirement was reduced from **20 percent to 10 percent**.
- Cost norms for godown construction were revised.
- Under the **AMI scheme**, **NABARD** serves as the subsidy-channelizing agency for eligible projects.
- **State Cooperative Banks** and **District Central Cooperative Banks** are the primary lending institutions for PACS and sanction and disburse credit.
- **NABARD’s special refinance facility** reduces the financial burden on participating cooperatives.
- Combined with the **3 percent interest subvention** under the **AIF**, the effective loan interest rate for PACS is reduced to **1 percent**.
- **FCI** is authorised to provide assured hiring of eligible godowns, subject to prescribed conditions, improving financial viability and encouraging PACS participation.

## **From Pilot To Expansion**

- Under the pilot phase, godown construction was completed in **11 PACS across 11 states**, creating **9,750 MT** of storage capacity.
- Pilot projects were implemented in:
    
    - **Maharashtra**
    - **Uttar Pradesh**
    - **Gujarat**
    - **Rajasthan**
    - **Madhya Pradesh**
    - **Uttarakhand**
    - **Tamil Nadu**
    - **Telangana**
    - **Assam**
    - **Karnataka**
    - **Tripura**
- The pilots demonstrated the feasibility of local storage infrastructure at the cooperative level.
- As of **July 2026**, **1,012 PACS or cooperative societies** had been identified under the Plan.
- As of the same date, godown construction was completed in **313 PACS**, creating more than **1.80 LMT** of storage capacity.

## **Cooperative Storage In Action: Nerpingalai PACS**

- **Nerpingalai PACS, Amravati, Maharashtra**, constructed a **3,000 MT warehouse** under the Plan.
- The project received support under the **AMI** and **AIF** schemes.
- With **NABARD refinance support**, the effective loan interest rate was reduced to **1 percent**.
- Around **300 farmers** are storing nearly **32,000 bags of soybeans** in the warehouse.
- The society has provided advances of about **Rs. 4.50 crore** against stored produce, helping farmers access liquidity and reduce distress sales.
- The PACS is expected to earn about:
    
    - **Rs. 7.68 lakh annually** through rental income
    - Nearly **Rs. 54 lakh** through annual interest income
- The warehouse improved storage access for surrounding farmers, strengthened the cooperative’s income base, and created local employment opportunities.

## **Quality Standards, Branding, And Transparency**

- Every storage structure must display the approved **grain storage logo** and prescribed **colour scheme**.
- The purpose is to create a recognisable identity and strengthen awareness among stakeholders.

### **Quality Assurance**

- Warehouses should comply with **Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (WDRA)** norms and suit local environmental conditions.
- Construction must use **durable, corrosion-resistant materials** and proper **ventilation systems** to prevent spoilage and maintain grain quality.
- **Regular inspections** and **quality audits** are required to ensure compliance with design specifications and safety standards.
- PACS members are expected to monitor project progress.
- **Project Management Consultants (PMC)** share updates on construction activities and expenditure to strengthen **transparency and accountability**.
- These measures seek to ensure infrastructure remains **durable, accountable, and aligned with prescribed quality standards** over the long term.

## **Strengthening India’s Decentralized Grain Storage Network**

- The Plan links **storage, processing, procurement, and distribution** at the **PACS level**, strengthening foodgrain management closer to production centres.
- It reinforces **food security**, reduces wastage, and strengthens **rural cooperative institutions**.
- Its phased expansion is supported by the convergence of existing schemes, institutional coordination, and financial support mechanisms.
- The framework reflects a **cooperative-led approach** to strengthening decentralised agricultural infrastructure and long-term foodgrain management systems.
