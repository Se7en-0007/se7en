## **India’s BRICS Presidency Comes At A Difficult Time**

- **Forging a joint statement at a major multilateral conference is difficult amid global conflicts involving member countries.**
    
- By hosting the **BRICS Summit in Delhi**, India is shouldering such a responsibility for the **second time in recent years**, following the **G-20 Summit in 2023**.
    
- When India previously hosted the coalition of emerging economies in **2012, 2016, and virtually in 2021**, BRICS was a smaller grouping of **five major powers**, making consensus easier to manage.
    
- BRICS expanded in **2024** with the addition of **Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE**. **Indonesia was inducted in 2025**.
    
- Although the expansion has changed **BRICS’ identity**, a **common focus remains elusive**.
    

## **Global Conflicts Involving BRICS Members**

- The number of **global conflicts involving BRICS members** has increased.
    
- The **war involving Iran** has intensified the challenge:
    
    - **Iran** was a victim of the **U.S.-Israel strikes**.
    - The **UAE** was subsequently attacked by **Iran in retaliation**.
    - Both countries are BRICS members and have been **unwilling to agree to a common narrative on the problem**.
- **India’s position on Israel** also conflicted with that of other BRICS members earlier in the year, as the grouping has traditionally adopted a **much more critical line on Israel’s actions**.
    

## **The Largest Share Of New Delhi’s Problems**

- The largest share of New Delhi’s problems as host comes from the **United States**.
    
- **President Donald Trump** has accused BRICS of **plotting to overthrow the U.S. dollar’s domination of the global economy**.
    
- He has threatened **tariffs on all BRICS members** over plans to increase **intra-BRICS payments and trade**.
    
- The **Modi government** is seeking to restore ties with the **Trump administration** on **trade** and its **Indo-Pacific strategy**.
    
- Consequently, hosting BRICS risks invoking **America’s ire**, however irrational.
    

## **A Big Win For Indian Diplomacy**

- Successfully reconciling these different strands would be **a shot across the bow for the grouping** and **a big win for Indian diplomacy**.
    
- BRICS was first conceptualised **25 years ago**, and its first summit was convened in **2009**.
    
- Since then, BRICS countries have risen in **global stature** and **economic heft**, benefiting **intra-BRICS trade**.
    

## **Global Stature And Economic Heft**

- BRICS now accounts for:
    
    - **Half of the world’s population**
    - **Two fifths of the global economy**
    - **A fourth of global trade**
- The grouping includes many of the world’s **top energy producers** and **biggest consumers**.
    
- Although BRICS prefers not to be called **anti-western**, it is a **powerful counter to the G-7 and western-led thinking**.
    

## **India’s Ambitions On The Global High Table**

- Membership of BRICS gives India **more heft in advancing its ambitions on the global high table**.
    
- India’s presidency has ensured it **prominence on the world stage**.
    
- Leaders and representatives of **11 BRICS countries**, including **Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin, and Masoud Pezeshkian**, are beginning deliberations on **Saturday at the Bharat Mandapam**.
    
- Their discussions will focus on the **future of global governance**.
