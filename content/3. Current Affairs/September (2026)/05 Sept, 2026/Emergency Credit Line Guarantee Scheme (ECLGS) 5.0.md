## Strengthening Business Resilience Through Timely Credit Support

- **ECLGS 5.0** provides **targeted credit guarantee support** to businesses affected by **external disruptions**.
- It is implemented by the **National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC)** and enables eligible lending institutions to extend **additional working capital** with reduced credit risk.
- The scheme supports **MSMEs**, **eligible non-MSME businesses**, and **scheduled passenger airlines** through **government-backed guarantees**.
- It builds on earlier ECLGS phases introduced during the **COVID-19 pandemic** and strengthens access to institutional credit, business continuity, liquidity, employment preservation, supply chains, and sustained economic growth during global uncertainty.

## Supporting Enterprises During External Economic Shocks

- Evolving **geopolitical developments** can affect **supply chains**, increase **logistics costs**, and create **liquidity pressures** across sectors.
- The Government approved **ECLGS 5.0 on 5 May 2026**.
- The scheme provides **government-backed credit guarantees** to **Member Lending Institutions (MLIs)** for extending additional working capital to eligible business borrowers.
- It aims to facilitate an **additional credit flow of up to ₹2.55 lakh crore**.
- It supports **uninterrupted domestic production**, **resilient supply chains**, preservation of employment, sustained economic activity, and fulfilment of short-term operational and financial commitments.

## Evolution Of ECLGS

- **ECLGS** was launched in **2020** under the **Aatmanirbhar Bharat Package** to help businesses overcome financial stress caused by the **COVID-19 pandemic**.
- It enabled MLIs to extend additional credit with **100% Government-backed guarantee support**.

### ECLGS 1.0

- Covered **MSMEs**, **business enterprises**, **Mudra borrowers**, and **individual business loans**.
- Eligible borrowers had outstanding loans of **not more than ₹50 crore** and **days past due up to 60 days as on 29 February 2020**.

### ECLGS 2.0

- Expanded coverage to **26 stressed sectors** identified by the **Kamath Committee** and the **healthcare sector**.
- Covered borrowers with outstanding loans of **above ₹50 crore and up to ₹500 crore**, with **days past due up to 60 days as on 29 February 2020**.

### ECLGS 3.0

- Extended support to the **hospitality**, **travel & tourism**, **leisure & sporting**, and **civil aviation sectors**.
- Eligible borrowers had credit facilities with lending institutions and **up to 60 days past due as on 29 February 2020**.

### ECLGS 4.0

- Focused on strengthening **healthcare infrastructure** during the pandemic.
- Covered **hospitals**, **nursing homes**, **clinics**, **medical colleges**, and manufacturers of **liquid oxygen** and **oxygen cylinders**.
- Eligible entities had credit facilities with lending institutions and **up to 90 days past due as on 31 March 2021**.
- From **ECLGS 1.0 to 4.0**, **1.19 crore guarantees** amounting to **₹3.68 lakh crore** were issued.
- These schemes concluded on **31 March 2023**.

## Salient Features Of ECLGS 5.0

- ECLGS 5.0 is operational until **31 March 2027**, or until guarantees amounting to **₹2.55 lakh crore** are issued, whichever is earlier.
- It covers **MSMEs**, **eligible non-MSME business borrowers**, and **scheduled passenger airline companies**.
- Borrowers must satisfy eligibility conditions prescribed by **NCGTC**, including account status and applicable lending norms.
- Credit assistance is available through **Scheduled Commercial Banks**, **Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks**, **Financial Institutions**, and eligible **Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)**.

## MSMEs And Eligible Non-MSMEs

### Eligible Borrowers

- Eligible borrowers must have **existing working capital facilities from MLIs as on 31 March 2026**.
- Their loan repayments should not be overdue by more than **60 days**.
- Borrowers who have already availed additional credit under the **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Exporters (CGSE)** are not eligible under ECLGS 5.0 up to the amount already availed under CGSE.
- The scheme is available to **MSMEs across all sectors**.

### Excluded Sectors

- For eligible **non-MSME borrowers**, excluded sectors include:
- **NBFCs**
- **Power** - generation, transmission and distribution
- **Telecom service providers**
- **Sugar and ethanol**
- **Information technology companies**
- **Paper and paper products**
- **Educational institutions**
- **Beverages**, excluding tea and coffee
- **Tobacco**
- Where a borrower operates in both eligible and ineligible sectors, the lending institution determines eligibility based on the proportion of turnover from eligible sectors during **FY 2025-26**.

### Credit Guarantee Coverage

- **100% guarantee** for loans extended to eligible **MSMEs**.
- **90% guarantee coverage** for loans extended to eligible **non-MSMEs**.
- **No guarantee fee** is payable by MLIs.

### Member Lending Institutions

- **MLIs** are lending institutions registered under ECLGS 5.0 to extend additional credit facilities to eligible borrowers.
- They include **Public and Private Sector Banks**, **Small Finance Banks**, **Foreign Banks**, **Co-operative Banks**, **Regional Rural Banks**, **NBFCs**, and **Finance Institutions**.

### Additional Credit Support

- Additional credit of up to **20% of the peak fund-based working capital outstanding during the fourth quarter of FY 2025-26** is available.
- The credit is subject to a ceiling of **₹100 crore per borrower**.

### Interest Rate Of Credit

- For **MSMEs**, the interest rate is based on **EBLR**.
- For eligible **non-MSMEs**, the interest rate is based on **MCLR**.
- Lending institutions may charge up to **0.75% above the benchmark**, subject to an overall ceiling of **9% per annum**.
- For loans extended by eligible **NBFCs**, the interest rate cannot exceed **13% per annum**.

### External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR)

- **EBLR** is the benchmark interest rate used by banks to determine floating interest rates on eligible retail and micro and small enterprise loans.
- The benchmark is set by the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.

### Marginal Cost Of Funds-Based Lending Rate (MCLR)

- **MCLR** is an internal reference rate for banks fixed by the **RBI**.
- It helps banks define the minimum interest rate on different types of loans.

### Loan Tenure

- The loan has a **5-year tenure** from the date of first disbursement, including a **1-year moratorium**.

## Scheduled Passenger Airlines

### Eligibility Condition

- Business enterprises in the scheduled passenger airline sector are eligible if they had outstanding **fund-based and non-fund-based credit facilities** from MLIs as on **31 March 2026**.
- Such credit facilities must be classified as **Standard**, excluding **SMA-2**, on that date.

### Credit Guarantee Coverage

- A **90% credit guarantee** is available for loans extended to eligible scheduled passenger airlines.
- **No guarantee fee** is payable by MLIs.

### Additional Credit Support

- Additional credit of up to **100%** is available, subject to a ceiling of **₹1,500 crore per borrower**.
- Any amount beyond **₹1,000 crore** and up to **₹1,500 crore** requires a proportionate equity contribution from the **promoters/owners**.

### Interest Rate Of Credit

- The lending institution determines the interest rate according to its **Board-approved policy**.

### Loan Tenure

- The loan has a **7-year tenure** from the date of first disbursement, including a **2-year moratorium**.

## ECLGS 5.0 In Action

- As on **20 August 2026**, **6,73,979 guarantees** had been issued under ECLGS 5.0.
- The **guaranteed amount** reached **₹2,50,024 crore**.
- **MSMEs accounted for 97.3% of all guarantees issued by number** and **80.79% of the total guaranteed amount**.
- Eligible borrowers can access the scheme through the **Jan Samarth Portal**.

### Phase 1: Outreach Campaigns

- Outreach campaigns were conducted across **9 locations** between **20 May and 6 June 2026**.
- Campaigns were organised through **State Level Bankers' Committees (SLBCs)**.
- **NCGTC**, **PSB Alliance**, banks, industry associations, and enterprises participated.

### Phase 2: Outreach

- Outreach is continuing across **10 additional locations**, of which **4 have already been completed**.
- Banks and NBFCs ensure broad geographical and sectoral coverage.
- MLIs continue to contact eligible borrowers through multiple communication channels.

## Sustaining Growth And Enterprise

- ECLGS 5.0 strengthens access to institutional credit through **government-backed guarantees**.
- It enables enterprises to continue operations, preserve employment, and maintain supply chains.
- By enhancing **business resilience**, ECLGS 5.0 contributes to sustaining **India's economic growth momentum**.
