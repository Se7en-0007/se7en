- **Women’s health care is often focused entirely on the reproductive age group.**
- **Menopause is a natural transition phase**, yet women with **severe and debilitating symptoms** seldom have access to health care and are often forced to **“just endure”**.
- There is a **huge care gap** in women’s mid-life health care.

## Perimenopause/Menopause Policy

- **Tamil Nadu and Karnataka** announced moves towards a **perimenopause/menopause policy** within a short period of each other in **August**.
- The policy can retrieve **women’s mid-life care from the neglect bin** and make their **physical and mental-health needs** part of **routine government health care**.
- This is expected to make **access to care easier**.

## Tamil Nadu

- **Tamil Nadu** announced a **perimenopausal care policy** in the Assembly on **August 17**.
- Its aim is the **early identification of physical and mental-health needs of perimenopausal women**.
- It proposes **screening, counselling and treatment services** through **PHCs and higher centres**.

## Karnataka

- On **August 4**, Karnataka introduced **‘Ruthu Thare’**, described as the country’s **first dedicated women’s health policy** with a strong focus on **perimenopause and menopause**.

## Menopause And Perimenopause

- **Menopause** is the **permanent end of menstrual periods and reproductive ability**.
- It is confirmed after **12 consecutive months without a menstrual cycle**.
- It occurs when the ovaries naturally slow down and produce lower levels of **oestrogen** and **progesterone**.
- **Perimenopause** is the transition period leading up to menopause and may bring **irregular periods** and **bodily changes over several years**.

## Common Symptoms

- **Hot flashes**
- **Sleep trouble**
- **Extreme mood changes**
- **Joint pain**
- **Debility**
- **Fatigue**
- For many women, these symptoms can be **disruptive**.

## Planned Measures

- Both States plan:
- **Outreach screening and counselling at the community level**
- **Women’s-health clinics**
- **District-level well-women screening**
- **Community outreach**
- **Referrals to district hospitals and government medical colleges**

## Implementation And Awareness

- The measures are **progressive** and can give **legitimacy to the complaints of menopausal women**.
- However, their effectiveness will be known only through **implementation**.
- Menopausal symptoms are not treated with adequate seriousness even by the **medical community**.
- Therefore, **adequate awareness generation** is necessary among **medical professionals**, **beneficiaries**, and their **families**.
