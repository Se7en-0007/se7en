- **India will host the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi on September 12 and 13.**
- **BRICS is an informal grouping of 11 major emerging markets and developing countries:** **Brazil, China, Egypt, Ethiopia, India, Indonesia, Iran, Russia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa and the United Arab Emirates.**

## BRIC

- **BRIC originally referred to Brazil, Russia, India and China**, which came together on the sidelines of the **United Nations General Assembly in New York in 2006**.
- **South Africa joined in 2010**, after which the grouping was renamed **BRICS**.
- The grouping sought to provide a platform for the concerns of emerging economies and counter the overwhelming domination of industrialised and developed economies, especially the **G7**, in international institutions.

## A Dragon In The Room

- **Key economic data shows how much China dwarfs the rest of the BRICS members.**
- **Economic might** is ascertained by the overall size of the economy, measured by **Gross Domestic Product (GDP) in US dollar terms**.
- **Economic prosperity** is measured through **GDP per capita**, which indicates how rich an average citizen is in a country.

## Overall Size Of The Economy

- **Even in 2006, China was the largest economy among the five countries after which the grouping is named.**
- For ease of analysis, **data for South Africa is taken from 2006**.
- In **2006**, **China’s overall GDP was less than the combined GDP of the other four members**.
- Such was the pace of China’s economic growth that, by the **inaugural summit meeting in 2009**, China had overtaken the combined economic size of **BRIS - BRICS minus China**.
- **Since 2009, the gap has continued to increase every year.**
- **As of 2026, China’s GDP alone is more than double that of the other four BRICS members combined.**

## Country-Specific GDP Trajectory

- Between **2006 and 2015**, **India did better than the other BRICS members apart from China**.
- India overtook the others to become the **second-biggest economy within BRICS**.
- However, **China’s rise dwarfs all the rest**.

## All The G7 Countries

- Since **2009**, China has opened a similarly massive gap even against **all G7 countries, barring the US**.
- The **Great Financial Crisis of 2008** dragged back most other developed countries.
- China grew rapidly during this period and became the **second-largest economy in the world**.

## GDP Per Capita

- In **2006**, an average **Russian, Brazilian or South African** was **between 3 and 3.5 times richer** than an average Chinese.
- Two decades later, an average Chinese is **richer than a Brazilian** and **twice as rich as a South African**.
- An average Chinese is also **closing the gap with an average Russian**.
- Compared with an average Indian, an average Chinese was about **three times richer in 2006** and is **five times richer now**.

## In The Process

- China has **dwarfed not just all its BRICS siblings but almost all the erstwhile big economies**.
