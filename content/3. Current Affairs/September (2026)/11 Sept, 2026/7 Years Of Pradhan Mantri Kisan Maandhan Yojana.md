- **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY)** was launched on **12 September 2019** for eligible landholding **Small and Marginal Farmers (SMFs)**, both male and female.
- It assures a **steady monthly pension from the age of 60 years**.
- On **12 September 2026**, the scheme completes **seven years**.

## Impact: Expanding The Social Security Footprint

- PM-KMY recorded **24,96,252 enrolments** by **06 February 2026**.
- **Haryana** leads with around **5.75 lakh enrolled farmers**, followed by **Bihar** with over **3.46 lakh**.
- **Jharkhand** and **Uttar Pradesh** have each crossed **2.5 lakh enrolments**.
- **Chhattisgarh** has recorded over **2 lakh enrolments**.
- **Odisha, Jammu and Kashmir, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra** further strengthen the scheme’s national footprint.
- Since **2019**, **₹540.66 crore** has been utilised nationwide under PM-KMY as of **February 2026**.

## A Pension Shield For Farmers' Golden Years

- PM-KMY is a **Central Sector Scheme** administered by the **Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare**.
- It is implemented in partnership with the **Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)**.
- It is a **voluntary and contributory old-age pension scheme** for small and marginal farmers.
- Eligible subscribers receive a **minimum assured pension of ₹3,000 per month** after attaining **60 years of age**.

### Family Pension Support

- If a subscriber dies while receiving pension, the spouse receives a **family pension equal to 50% of the subscriber’s pension**.
- The family pension amounts to **₹1,500 per month**.
- This benefit is available **exclusively to the spouse**, provided the spouse is **not already a beneficiary of PM-KMY**.
- If a subscriber dies before attaining **60 years**, and has made regular contributions, the spouse may:
    
    - **Join and continue the scheme** by making prescribed regular contributions, or
    - **Exit the scheme** under the exit and withdrawal provisions.

## Who Can Benefit From PM-KMY ?

- The scheme is open to **small and marginal farmers throughout the country** with cultivable landholdings of **up to two hectares**.
- Farmers must be between **18 and 40 years of age** at enrolment.
- Their names must appear in the land records of the respective **States or Union Territories as on 1 August 2019**.
- They must not fall under the scheme’s **exclusion criteria**.

## Exclusion Criteria: Who Is Not Eligible For PM-KMY ?

### Farmers Covered Under Other Social Security & Pension Schemes

The following are not eligible:

- Subscribers to statutory schemes such as the **National Pension Scheme (NPS)**, **Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) scheme**, or **Employees’ Fund Organization scheme**.
- Beneficiaries of the **Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan Yojana (PM-SYM)**.
- Beneficiaries of the **Pradhan Mantri Laghu Vyapari Maan-dhan Yojana (PM-LVM)**.

### Categories Of Higher Economic Status

The following are excluded:

- **Institutional Landholders** - all institutional landholding entities.
- **Constitutional Post Holders** - former and present holders of constitutional posts.
- **Public Representatives** - former and present Ministers or State Ministers, Members of Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha, Members of State Legislative Assemblies or Councils, Mayors of Municipal Corporations, and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
- **Government & Public Sector Employees** - serving or retired officers and employees of Central or State Government Ministries, Departments or field units, Central or State Public Sector Enterprises, attached or autonomous institutions, and regular employees of Local Bodies.
- **Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS), Class IV and Group D employees** are exempted from this exclusion and remain eligible.
- **Income Tax Payers** - all individuals who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- **Registered Professionals** - Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants and Architects registered with professional bodies.

### Verification & Self-Declaration

- Eligibility verification relies primarily on **self-declaration by the applicant**.
- **State or Union Territory Governments** certify eligibility based on these self-declarations.
- If an applicant submits an **incorrect or false declaration**:
    
    - The applicant becomes **ineligible for financial benefits**.
    - The **Central Government’s matching contribution is stopped**.
    - The subscriber’s contributions are **refunded without interest**.

## Enrolment: A Simple Digital Process

- Eligible SMFs can enrol through the nearest **Common Service Centre (CSC)** through a **simple, paperless enrolment process**.
- Required documents and details:
    
    - **Aadhaar card**
    - **Bank account details**
    - **Mobile number for OTP verification**
- At the CSC, the **Village Level Entrepreneur (VLE)** verifies details and completes **online registration**.
- The farmer signs the mandate form and the **first contribution is processed digitally**.
- After successful registration, the farmer receives a **Pension Account Number** and **pension card**.
- Details are forwarded to **LIC**, which manages the **pension fund and pension payouts**.
- Subsequent contributions are **auto-debited** from the farmer’s bank account.
- Farmers can choose **monthly, quarterly, four-monthly or half-yearly** contribution options.
