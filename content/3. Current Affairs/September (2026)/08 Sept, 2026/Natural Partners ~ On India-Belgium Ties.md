- **Belgium is not among India’s most important partners in terms of size and engagement**, but the Belgian Prime Minister’s visit to Delhi forms part of the larger picture of India’s ties with European countries.
- These ties have gained importance for India in **economic relations, defence relations and strategic ties**.

## India-European Union Free Trade Agreement

- A key agenda item in talks between **Prime Ministers Bart De Wever and Narendra Modi** was **bilateral trade opportunities after the India-European Union Free Trade Agreement is signed**, which is **expected in December**.
- **India-Belgium trade is about $13 billion**, with **one-third** arising from **over a century of collaboration in the diamond industry**.
- **Antwerp** is a global hub for diamond trading, while **Mumbai and Surat** provide the largest cutting and polishing services.
- Following the EU FTA, India and Belgium expect collaboration in areas such as **renewable energy** and **food processing**.
- The Prime Ministers agreed on a target of **doubling bilateral trade in the next five years**.

## High-Tech Defence Industry

- Though surrounded by friendly European allies, Belgium has a robust **high-tech defence industry**, including **small arms, drone and counter-drone technology, ammunition and naval defence**.
- Belgium is keen to enter the Indian market, while India seeks to **diversify its procurement beyond global powers**.
- The two sides agreed on an **MoU for co-development of military hardware** and signed a **letter of intent on defence cooperation**.
- **India will appoint a resident defence attaché to Brussels**, complementing Belgium’s move.

## Strategic Autonomy

- The leaders’ statements indicated that the visit extended beyond bilateral matters to a **geopolitical framing of strategic ties**, both between India and Belgium and between India and Europe.
- At a business event, **Bart De Wever** said Europe was recognising the validity of India’s warnings about **strategic autonomy from global powers** and **overdependence in trade and industrial production**, particularly with reference to **China**.

## Democratic Values, Market Economy, And People-To-People Ties

- **Narendra Modi** stated that **democratic values, a market economy and people-to-people ties** make India and Belgium **“natural partners”**.
- This is a theme promoted by Modi during the past year, alongside increased visits to Europe and the hosting of several European leaders in Delhi, ahead of his **Brussels visit later this year for the FTA**.

## Russia-Ukraine Conflict

- De Wever’s visit came in the same week that New Delhi was preparing to welcome **Russian President Vladimir Putin** for the **BRICS summit in Delhi**.
- Engagement with Belgium demonstrates a **new maturity for both European countries and India**.
- It reflects sensitivity to their respective positions on the **Russia-Ukraine conflict**, showing that bilateral ties outweigh **Europe’s enmity** and **India’s deep friendship with Moscow**.
