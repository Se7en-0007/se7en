- **All humans must be treated equally** - a **moral and constitutional position**, not a scientific one, because **science has no moral or constitutional authority**.
- The normative system in the **Manusmriti** rests on the principle that a person’s **birth reveals something essential** about them. It assigns people born into particular social categories a position in the **division of labour**, along with permitted relationships and social rights.
- **Modern constitutional thinking** is radically different. From the **Magna Carta** and the **English Bill of Rights** to present constitutional democracies, individuals possess rights that depend on more than their social-hierarchical position.
- The **fundamental rights of the Indian Constitution** attach to **persons and citizens**, not to **inherited social functions**.

## The Authority Of Science

- Some defenders of the caste system claim it rests on **inherited biological differences**, but the system is actually the **cause of differences rather than their inheritor**.
- The **human organism** develops through interaction between **genes and environment**. **Caste-based discrimination** affects those environments.
- Studies of the **social determinants of health** show **caste-related inequities** in healthcare and access to healthcare.
- **Social epigenetics** finds that social circumstances can induce biological changes through mechanisms that affect how genes are regulated.
- **People of Black African heritage** and **Indigenous peoples in Asia** have non-identical genomes for this reason. Scientific methods show that such differences do not justify a **caste-like system**.
- Rather, differences in their historical social circumstances - including participation in societies practising a **hereditary, hierarchical system** - affected how they developed.

## Endogamy And The Genome

- A landmark **2009 study** found that most present-day Indians descend from **two differentiated ancient populations** that mixed extensively before **endogamy** took root.
- Present Indian groups contain mixtures of these ancestries. However, there are substantial genetic differences among communities today because of a long history of **endogamy**.
- Other studies have reported strong **founder effects** due to endogamy. A founder effect occurs when a new population emerges from a small group and inherits its lack of genetic diversity.
- The genome does not proclaim that people were ordained to marry one another; it records that they elected to do so for reasons beyond **scientific wisdom**.

## Plasticity And Human Development

- The **human nervous system** is highly **plastic**: it constantly rewires itself in response to experience throughout life.
- Not everyone is good at everything, but neural plasticity means that the neurological system develops with each person’s **environment, interests, temperament, and individual choice**, producing varied human abilities.
- Science cannot determine whether all humans are equal, because equality is a **political and ethical proposition** that science cannot test or prove.
- But science does establish that **all humans belong to one species**, their development is shaped by their experiences, and **social environments can become embodied**.

## Caste And Human Capacity For Culture

- Caste constrains both the **plasticity of the human brain** and the **human capacity for culture**.
- **Homo sapiens** can accumulate knowledge and transmit it, so that other humans do not need to begin learning from scratch.
- **Caste-based discrimination** restricts the spread of knowledge and narrows the social range across which this defining human capacity can operate.
- A student’s ability to understand **thermodynamics** or solve a **differential equation** has nothing to do with the **ritual status of their ancestors**.
- This conflict between caste and the possibilities of science makes the appearance of a message reinforcing the caste system at a centre of scientific learning **particularly tragic**.
