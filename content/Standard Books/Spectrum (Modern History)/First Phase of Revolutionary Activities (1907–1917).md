## Why The Surge Of Revolutionary Activities
*   **Byproduct of Militant Nationalism**: Swadeshi & Boycott Movement decline (1907) → Youth disillusioned → Surge of revolutionary heroism.
    *   *Phase I (1907–1917)*: Fallout of Swadeshi Movement.
    *   *Phase II (post-1920s)*: Fallout of Non-Cooperation Movement.
*   **Leadership Failures**: Extremists called for sacrifice but failed to:
    *   Build effective organizations.
    *   Find new political methodologies to channel youthful energy.
*   **Government Repression**: Peaceful protest avenues blocked → Youth concluded British must be ousted physically by force.

## The Revolutionary Programme
*   **Impractical Options (at that stage)**: Violent mass revolution or subverting army loyalty.
*   **Chosen Model**: **Russian nihilists** & **Irish nationalists** methodology:
    *   **Individual heroic actions**: Assassinate unpopular officials, traitors, and informers.
    *   **Swadeshi dacoities**: Conducted to raise funds for revolutionary work.
    *   **Military conspiracies** (during WWI): Sought help from Britain’s enemies.
*   **Core Objective**: Strike terror in rulers → Arouse people → Remove fear of authority from minds → Inspire youth to drive British out.
*   **Ideological Vacuum**: Extremists failed to highlight difference between a mass-based revolution vs. individual violent acts → Violent individualism took root.

## A Survey Of Revolutionary Activities

### Bengal
*   **1870s**: Calcutta student secret societies formed (remained inactive initially).
*   **1902**: First active groups:
    *   *Midnapore*: Under **Jnanendranath Basu**.
    *   *Calcutta*: **Anushilan Samiti** founded by **Promotha Mitter** (included **Jatindranath Banerjee**, **Barindra Kumar Ghosh**); limited to physical/moral training.
*   **April 1906**: Inner circle of Anushilan (**Barindra Kumar Ghosh**, **Bhupendranath Dutta**) started weekly ***Yugantar*** → led to abortive 'actions'.
*   **Media Advocacy**: Post-police brutalities at **Barisal Conference (April 1906)**, *Yugantar* wrote: *"Force must be stopped by force" (30 crore people must raise 60 crore hands)*.
*   **Secret Network**: **Rashbehari Bose** & **Sachin Sanyal** organized secret societies across Punjab, Delhi, and United Provinces; **Hemachandra Kanungo** went abroad for military/political training.
*   **Key Incidents & Conspiracies**:
    *   **1907 (Sir Fuller Attempt)**: Abortive attempt on former Lt. Gov. of Eastern Bengal & Assam by *Yugantar* group.
    *   **Dec 1907**: Attempted derailment of Lt. Gov. **Sir Andrew Fraser’s** train.
    *   **April 1908 (Muzaffarpur Bomb)**: **Prafulla Chaki** & **Khudiram Bose** threw bomb at carriage of sadistic judge **Kingsford** → killed 2 British ladies instead → Chaki shot himself dead; Khudiram Bose hanged.
    *   **Alipore Conspiracy Case** (*Manicktolla/Muraripukur Bomb Conspiracy*): Entire Anushilan group arrested.
        *   **Aurobindo Ghosh** & **Barindra Ghosh** tried for "waging war against the King".
        *   **C.R. Das** defended Aurobindo → acquitted.
        *   Barindra Ghosh & **Ullaskar Dutt** (bomb-maker) → death penalty (commuted to life transportation).
        *   **Narendra Gosain** (approver) → shot dead in jail by co-accused **Satyendranath Bose** and **Kanailal Dutta**.
    *   **Feb 1909**: Public prosecutor shot dead in Calcutta.
    *   **Feb 1910**: Deputy Superintendent of Police shot dead leaving Calcutta High Court.
    *   **1908 (Barrah Dacoity)**: Organised by Dacca Anushilan under **Pulin Das** to raise funds.
    *   **Dec 1912 (Delhi Bomb)**: **Rashbehari Bose** & **Sachin Sanyal** threw bomb at **Viceroy Hardinge** during entry into Chandni Chowk, Delhi → Hardinge injured.
        *   *Delhi Conspiracy Trial*: **Basant Kumar Biswas**, **Amir Chand**, and **Avadh Behari** executed.
        *   Rashbehari Bose evaded arrest (escaped in disguise).
*   **Bagha Jatin (Jatindranath Mukherjee)**: Leader of Western Anushilan Samiti (*Jugantar/Yugantar*); revitalized links between Calcutta, Bihar, and Orissa.
    *   **The "German Plot" / "Zimmerman Plan"** (WWI): Arranged import of German arms via sympathizers abroad. Jatin tasked Rashbehari Bose with Upper India insurrection.
    *   **Funds**: Raised through **taxicab and boat dacoities**.
    *   **Plan**: Guerrilla force uprising, seize Fort William, and military mutiny.
    *   **Failure**: Leaked by a traitor → Jatin cornered at **Balasore (Orissa coast)**.
    *   **Sept 1915 (Balasore Shootout)**: Jatin Mukherjee died hero's death. Quote: *"We shall die to awaken the nation."*
*   **Journals**: ***Sandhya*** & ***Yugantar*** (Bengal), ***Kal*** (Maharashtra).
*   **Limitations in Bengal**: Hindu overemphasis kept Muslims aloof; encouraged quixotic heroism; lacked mass involvement; narrow upper-caste base → crumbled under State repression.

### Maharashtra
*   **1879 (Ramosi Peasant Force)**: Organised by **Vasudev Balwant Phadke** to disrupt communications and start an armed revolt → suppressed prematurely.
*   **1890s**: **B.G. Tilak** propagated militant nationalism via Ganapati/Shivaji festivals and journals ***Kesari*** and ***Maharatta***.
*   **1897**: Disciples **Chapekar brothers** (Damodar & Balkrishna) assassinated Poona Plague Commissioner **Rand** and **Lt. Ayerst**.
*   **1899**: **Savarkar** & brother (Ganesh) organized **Mitra Mela** (secret society) → merged with **Abhinav Bharat** in 1904 (inspired by Mazzini's 'Young Italy').
*   **1909**: Abhinav Bharat member **Anant Lakshman Kanhere** assassinated Nasik Collector **A.M.T. Jackson** (also an indologist).
    *   *Nasik Conspiracy trial*: 38 arrested; Savarkar identified as brain → sentenced to transportation for life and forfeiture of property.

### Punjab
*   **Extremist Triggers**: Frequent famines, land revenue & irrigation tax hikes, 'begar' (forced labor) by zamindars, and Bengal partition events.
*   **Key Actors**:
    *   **Lala Lajpat Rai**: Published ***Punjabee*** (motto: self-help at any cost).
    *   **Ajit Singh** (Bhagat Singh's uncle): Formed **Anjuman-i-Mohisban-i-Watan** (Lahore) with journal ***Bharat Mata***; organized Chenab colonists & Bari Doab peasants against revenue/water rates.
    *   **Others**: Aga Haidar, Syed Haider Raza, Bhai Parmanand, Lalchand 'Falak'.
*   **Crackdown (May 1907)**: Ban on political meetings + deportation of Lajpat Rai & Ajit Singh → Punjab extremism died down quickly.
*   **Post-Deportation Revolutionaries**: Ajit Singh, Sufi Ambaprasad, Lalchand, Bhai Parmanand, Lala Hardayal.
*   **WWI Connection**: Rashbehari Bose & Bagha Jatin planned an 1857-type all-India armed rising → failed → Bose escaped to Japan in 1915 (later founded INA).

## Revolutionary Activities Abroad
*   **Drivers**: Need for safe shelter, publishing literature immune to British Press Acts, and procuring arms.
*   **London (India House)**:
    *   **1905**: **Shyamji Krishnavarma** started **Indian Home Rule Society** ("**India House**"), scholarship schemes, and journal ***The Indian Sociologist***.
    *   *Members*: Savarkar, Lala Hardayal.
    *   **1909**: **Madanlal Dhingra** assassinated India Office bureaucrat **Curzon-Wyllie**.
    *   *Outcome*: Savarkar extradited (1910) → transported for life; London base collapsed.
*   **Paris & Geneva**: **Madame Bhikaji Cama** (Parsi revolutionary, brought out ***Bande Mataram***, worked with French socialists) & **Ajit Singh**.
*   **Berlin**: **Virendranath Chattopadhyaya** made Berlin his base after 1909 Anglo-German relations worsened.

### The Ghadr
*   **Structure**: Revolutionary group based in US & Canadian Pacific coast cities; HQ in **San Francisco (Yugantar Ashram)**; organ was weekly ***The Ghadr***.
*   **Members**: Ex-soldiers and peasants migrated from Punjab in search of employment.
*   **Pre-Ghadr Activities**: Carried on by **Ramdas Puri**, **G.D. Kumar**, **Taraknath Das**, **Sohan Singh Bhakna**, and **Lala Hardayal** (arrived 1911).
    *   *Early setups*: **Swadesh Sevak Home** (Vancouver) & **United India House** (Seattle).
*   **Establishment**: **1913**.
*   **Key Figures**: Lala Hardayal, Ramchandra, Bhagwan Singh, Kartar Singh Saraba, Barkatullah, Bhai Parmanand.
*   **Programme**: Assassinate officials, publish anti-imperialist literature, work among overseas Indian troops, procure arms, and incite simultaneous revolt in all British colonies.

### Komagata Maru Incident and The Ghadr
*   **The Incident (1914)**:
    *   Japanese ship ***Komagata Maru*** carried 370 prospective Punjabi Sikh & Muslim immigrants from Singapore to Vancouver.
    *   Canadian authorities denied entry for 2 months (under British influence).
    *   Ship returned → anchored at **Budge Budge (Calcutta) in Sept 1914**.
    *   Clash with police after passengers refused Punjab-bound train → **22 died**.
*   **Armed Revolt Attempt**:
    *   Ghadrites (inflamed by Budge Budge & WWI outbreak) decided to launch violent ouster.
    *   **Kartar Singh Saraba** & **Raghubar Dayal Gupta** dispatched to India.
    *   Bengal leaders (**Rashbehari Bose**, **Sachin Sanyal**) asked to lead the military insurrection.
    *   *Funds*: Raised via dacoities (Punjab cases in Jan–Feb 1915 targeted moneylenders and debt records).
    *   **Revolt Date**: Fixed for **February 21, 1915** in Ferozepur, Lahore, and Rawalpindi garrisons.
*   **Betrayal & Failure**: Plan leaked by a traitor → immediate British pre-emption.
    *   *Suppression*: Rebellious regiments disbanded, leaders arrested, **45 hanged**, Sachin Sanyal transported for life, Rashbehari Bose fled to Japan.
    *   **Defence of India Act (March 1915)**: Passed primarily to smash Ghadr; allowed detention without trial and special courts.
    *   *Pan-Islamists interned*: **Ali brothers**, **Maulana Azad**, **Hasrat Mohani** interned for years.

## Evaluation of Ghadr
*   **Achievement**: Deeply ideological; preached militant nationalism with a completely **secular approach**.
*   **Failures**:
    *   Failed politically and militarily.
    *   Lacked organized, sustained leadership.
    *   Underestimated organizational, ideological, financial, tactical, and strategic preparations.
    *   Lala Hardayal was unsuited for the practical role of an organizer.

## Revolutionaries in Europe
*   **Berlin Committee for Indian Independence (1915)**: Established by **Virendranath Chattopadhyay**, **Bhupendranath Dutta**, **Lala Hardayal** under the **"Zimmerman Plan"** (aided by German Foreign Office).
    *   *Objective*: Mobilize settlers, send volunteers/arms, incite troops, organize armed invasion of British India.
*   **Missions**: Dispatched to Baghdad, Persia, Turkey, and Kabul.
*   **Kabul Mission**: **Raja Mahendra Pratap Singh**, **Barkatullah**, and **Obaidullah Sindhi** set up a **"Provisional Indian Government"** with crown prince Amanullah's help.

## Mutiny in Singapore
*   **Date**: **February 15, 1915**.
*   **Participants**: Punjabi Muslim **5th Light Infantry** & the **36th Sikh Battalion** under **Jamadar Chisti Khan**, **Jamadar Abdul Gani**, and **Subedar Daud Khan**.
*   **Outcome**: Crushed after fierce combat; 37 executed, 41 transported for life.

## Decline
*   **WWI Aftermath Respite**: Passages cooled down due to:
    1.  Release of political prisoners held under Defence of India Rules.
    2.  Conciliation atmosphere following **Montagu’s August 1917 statement** and discussions of constitutional reforms.
    3.  Arrival of **M.K. Gandhi** with a novel promise of non-violent non-cooperation.
