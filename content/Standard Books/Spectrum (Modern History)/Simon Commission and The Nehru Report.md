## Appointment Of The Indian Statutory Commission

*   **Government of India Act, 1919 Provision** → Appointment of constitutional review commission after **10 years** (scheduled **1929**).
*   **Early Appointment (November 8, 1927)**:
    *   **Reason** → Conservative Government (PM **Stanley Baldwin**) feared defeat by Labour Party in upcoming British elections → did not want India's future in "irresponsible Labour hands".
    *   **Constitutional Crisis/Failure** → Dyarchy failed to create stable imperial control by mid-1920s → prompted preceding enquiries.
*   **Preceding Commissions/Committees**:
    *   **Lee Commission (1923)** (Royal Commission on Superior Civil Services) → Recommended immediate establishment of the Statutory Public Service Commission.
    *   **Muddiman Committee (1924)** (Reforms Enquiry Committee) → Investigated 1919 Act/dyarchy. Majority proposed minor structural adjustments; **Minority (non-official Indians)** condemned dyarchy → demanded immediate abolition + democratic constitution.
    *   **Linlithgow Commission (1926)** (Royal Commission on Agriculture) → Submitted report (**1928**) recommending agrarian reforms as welfare foundation; famously proposed improving Indian cattle quality by importing superior foreign bulls.
*   **Secretary of State Lord Birkenhead** → Challenged Indian politicians to produce a consensus constitutional scheme.
*   **Simon Commission Structure**:
    *   **7-member** all-white **Indian Statutory Commission** (no Indian representation).
    *   Jointly chaired by **Sir John Simon** and **Clement Attlee** (future Labour PM).
    *   Members: 4 Conservatives, 2 Labourites, 1 Liberal.

## Indian Response

*   **Core Grievance** → Exclusion of Indians → Violated self-determination principle + insulted Indian self-respect.
*   **Congress Response (Madras Session, December 1927)**:
    *   President: **M.A. Ansari**.
    *   Decision → Boycott commission "**at every stage and in every form**".
    *   **Snap Resolution** (spearheaded by **Jawaharlal Nehru**) → Declared **complete independence** (Purna Swaraj) as official Congress goal.
*   **Other Groups Supporting Boycott**:
    *   **Liberals** (Hindu Mahasabha).
    *   **Muslim League (Jinnah Faction)** → Formally split Muslim League in 1927:
        *   **Jinnah Faction (Calcutta session)** → Anti-Simon Commission/Boycott.
        *   **Muhammad Shafi Faction (Lahore session)** → Supported Government/Commission.
*   **Groups Opposing Boycott**:
    *   **Unionists** (Punjab).
    *   **Justice Party** (Madras).
*   **Public Protest Dynamics**:
    *   **Landing Date**: **February 3, 1928** (Bombay) → Nationwide hartal + mass black-flag rallies.
    *   **Catchy Slogan**: "**Simon Go Back**" → Coined by **Yusuf Meharally** (Mayor of Bombay/Congress Socialist Party) [some attribute to Lala Lajpat Rai].
    *   **Impact on Youth** → Sparked mass political activation → Youth leagues & conferences proliferated.
    *   **New Leaders** → **Jawaharlal Nehru** and **Subhas Chandra Bose** emerged as primary youth icons.
    *   **Radical Germination** → Prepared fertile ground for socialist groups (**Punjab Naujawan Bharat Sabha**, **Workers' and Peasants' Parties**, **Hindustani Sewa Dal** in Karnataka).

### Dr Ambedkar and The Simon Commission
*   **Appointment** → Selected by Bombay Legislative Council to collaborate with the Commission.
*   **Key Constitutional Arguments (October 1928)**:
    *   Demanded **universal adult franchise** for both men and women (ahead of most European nations).
    *   Advocated provincial autonomy in provinces + dyarchy at the Centre.
*   **Safeguards for Depressed Classes** (via **Bahishkrita Hitakarini Sabha** memorandum):
    *   Asserted zero link between depressed classes and Hindu community → demanded recognition as a **distinct, independent minority**.
    *   Stressed extreme vulnerability (educational backwardness, poverty, social enslavement, unique political disabilities) → required greater protection than other minorities.
*   **Electoral Demands**:
    *   Demanded representation equal to the Muslim minority.
    *   Demanded **reserved seats** under universal adult franchise.
    *   Demanded **separate electorates** if universal franchise was denied.
    *   Demanded constitutional safeguards or Governor's instrument of instructions to guarantee education and public service entry.
*   **Outcome** → Simon Commission granted reserved seats subject to Governor's competence endorsement → Ambedkar dissatisfied → Report remained a dead letter.

## Police Repression

*   **Tactics** → Draconian lathi charges against peaceful demonstrators.
*   **Major Assaults**:
    *   **Lucknow** → **Jawaharlal Nehru** and **Govind Ballabh Pant** severely beaten.
    *   **Lahore (October 1928)** → **Lala Lajpat Rai** received fatal blows to his chest during anti-Simon march → Died on **November 17, 1928**.
    *   **Lala Lajpat Rai's Famous Declaration**: *"The blows, which fell on me today, are the last nails driven into the coffin of British Imperialism."*

## Impact Of Appointment Of Simon Commission On The National Movement

1.  **Stimulated Radical Forces** → Infused militancy, popularized demands for complete independence and socialist economic reforms.
2.  **Forged Political Unity** → Provided a unifying rallying point for a dormant Congress; Birkenhead's challenge forced diverse political factions to collaborate on a constitution.

## The Simon Commission Recommendations

*Published in two volumes (May 1930); quickly overtaken by events:*
*   **Provincial Level**:
    *   Abolish **dyarchy** → establish representative, autonomous provincial governments.
    *   Enlarge provincial legislative councils.
    *   Governor granted discretionary powers over internal security and protection of minority communities.
*   **Central Level**:
    *   **Rejected** parliamentary responsibility at the Centre.
    *   Governor-General retained absolute cabinet-appointment authority.
    *   Government of India granted absolute control over High Courts.
*   **Electoral & Federal Structure**:
    *   **No universal franchise**.
    *   **Retained separate communal electorates** (extended to other communities) until Hindu-Muslim communal tensions ceased.
    *   Federalism accepted only as a distant goal → proposed a **Consultative Council of Greater India** (including British provinces and princely states).
*   **Territorial & Military Reforms**:
    *   Local legislatures + central representation for **NWFP** and **Baluchistan**.
    *   **Sindh** to be separated from Bombay.
    *   **Burma** to be separated from India (not naturally part of sub-continent).
    *   Indianization of army recommended while retaining British troops.

## Nehru Report

*   **Genesis** → Consensus answer to Lord Birkenhead's challenge.
*   **Drafting Sub-committee**:
    *   Appointed by **All Parties Conference** (February 1928).
    *   Chairman: **Motilal Nehru**.
    *   Key Members: **Tej Bahadur Sapru**, **Subhas Chandra Bose**, **M.S. Aney**, **Mangal Singh**, **Ali Imam**, **Shuaib Qureshi**, **G.R. Pradhan**.
*   **Finalization**: **August 1928** (First major indigenous effort to draft a constitutional framework).
*   **Core Ideological Split** → Recommendations unanimous except on the national goal: majority wanted **Dominion Status**; younger section (Nehru/Bose) demanded **Complete Independence**.

---

## Main Recommendations

*Envisaged a federal union linking British India and Princely States:*
*   **Form of Government** → **Dominion Status** on the lines of self-governing dominions (infuriated younger radical nationalists).
*   **Electoral System**:
    *   **Rejected separate electorates** (the traditional colonial reform base).
    *   Proposed **joint electorates** with reservation of seats for Muslims *only* in provinces where they were a minority (no reservation in majority provinces like Punjab and Bengal), in proportion to population, with a right to contest additional seats.
*   **Provinces** → Created on a linguistic basis.
*   **Fundamental Rights** → **19 rights** (equal rights for women, right to form unions, universal adult suffrage).
*   **Responsible Government at Centre**:
    *   **House of Representatives** (500 members, adult suffrage, 5-year tenure).
    *   **Senate** (200 members, elected by provincial councils, 7-year tenure).
    *   **Governor-General** → Appointed by Crown, paid out of Indian revenues, must act on advice of Central Executive Council responsible to Parliament.
*   **Responsible Government in Provinces** → 5-year tenure councils, headed by Governor acting on provincial executive council advice.
*   **Religious Safeguards**:
    *   Full protection to cultural and religious interests of Muslims.
    *   **Complete dissociation of State from religion**.

## The Muslim and Hindu Communal Responses

*Communal divergence quickly sabotaged the initial All Parties consensus:*

### Delhi Proposals of Muslim League (December 1927)
*Four joint demands formulated by Muslim leaders, initially accepted by Congress Madras Session:*
1.  **Joint electorates** instead of separate electorates, with reserved seats for Muslims.
2.  **One-third** Muslim representation in the Central Legislative Assembly.
3.  Muslim representation in **Punjab and Bengal** proportional to their population.
4.  Formation of **three new Muslim-majority provinces**: Sindh, Baluchistan, and NWFP.

### Hindu Mahasabha Demands
*   Vehemently opposed new Muslim-majority provinces and reservation of seats in majority provinces (Punjab & Bengal) → feared Muslim legislative domination.
*   Demanded a strictly **unitary political structure**.

### Compromises
*   **Gridlock** → Motilal Nehru trapped in dilemma: satisfying Muslim demands alienated Hindu communalists, and vice-versa.
*   **Concessions to Hindu Communalists in the Final Nehru Report**:
    *   Joint electorates proposed everywhere; reservation for Muslims restricted *only* to minority areas.
    *   Sindh detachment from Bombay postponed until *after* Dominion Status was achieved, subject to weightage for Hindu minority.
    *   Broadly **unitary structure** established (residual powers vested with the Centre, not provinces).

## Amendments Proposed by Jinnah

*Presented at Calcutta All Parties Conference (December 1928); all rejected:*
1.  **One-third** Muslim representation in Central Legislature.
2.  Muslim reservation in Bengal and Punjab legislatures proportional to population until universal adult suffrage was established.
3.  **Residual powers** to be vested in the provinces (not Centre).

---

## Jinnah’s Fourteen Points

*Drafted in **March 1929** (collaborating with the orthodox Shafi faction) as the baseline for future Muslim League propaganda:*
1.  **Federal Constitution** with residual powers vested in the provinces.
2.  Provincial autonomy.
3.  No central constitutional amendment without state concurrence.
4.  Adequate Muslim representation in all legislatures without reducing Muslim majorities in any province to minority/equality.
5.  Adequate Muslim representation in services and self-governing bodies.
6.  **One-third** Muslim representation in central legislature.
7.  **One-third** Muslim representation in central and provincial cabinets.
8.  **Separate electorates**.
9.  No bill/resolution to be passed in any legislature if **three-fourths** of a minority community's members oppose it as anti-interest.
10. Territorial redistributions must not affect Muslim majorities in Punjab, Bengal, and NWFP.
11. Separation of **Sindh from Bombay**.
12. Constitutional reforms in **NWFP and Baluchistan**.
13. Full religious freedom.
14. Protection of Muslim rights in religion, culture, education, and language.

---

## Nehru Report Found Unsatisfactory

*   **Widespread Rejection** → Opposed by Muslim League, Hindu Mahasabha, and Sikh communalists.
*   **Internal Congress Rift** → Younger radicals (**Jawaharlal Nehru** and **Subhas Chandra Bose**) rejected the compromise goal of Dominion Status as a major step backward.
*   **Swarajist Conflict Response** → Nehru and Bose actively rejected the Congress' modified goal and jointly founded the **Independence for India League** to lobby for Purna Swaraj.
