## Attlee’s Statement Of February 20, 1947
* **British House of Commons Declaration** = Intention of leaving Indian subcontinent announced by British PM **Clement Attlee**.

### Main Points of Attlee’s Statement
* **Deadline for Power Transfer** = **June 30, 1948** (fixed irrespective of constitutional agreement).
* **Modes of Devolution** (if Constituent Assembly not fully representative/Muslim provinces boycott):
  * British would relinquish power to a **central government** or in some areas to **existing provincial governments**.
* **Princely States** ⇒ British paramountcy & obligations lapse (not transferred to any successor government).
* **Viceroy Replacement** ⇒ **Lord Mountbatten** replaces Wavell.
* **Significance** ⇒ Clear hint of partition & balkanisation; reversion of **Cripps Offer** terms.

### Why A Date Fixed By Government For Withdrawal
* **Shock Therapy** ⇒ To force Indian political parties into agreement on the main question.
* **Crisis Management** ⇒ To avert the rapidly developing constitutional crisis.
* **Demonstrate Sincerity** ⇒ To convince Indian leaders of genuine British intentions.
* **Wavell's Assessment Validated** ⇒ Irreversible decline in British administrative authority.

### Congress Stand
* **Acceptance of Multi-Centre Power Transfer** ⇒ Acceptable as existing assembly could frame constitution for represented areas → offered way out of deadlock.
* **Muslim League Reaction** ⇒ Boldened by statement → launched civil disobedience movement in **Punjab** to overthrow coalition.

## Independence And Partition
* **Communal Riots + Coalition Failure (Congress-League)** ⇒ Acceptance of partition became thinkable.
* **Hindu & Sikh Groups (Bengal & Punjab)** ⇒ Most insistent demand for partition to escape compulsory grouping in Pakistan.
* **Hindu Mahasabha (Bengal)** ⇒ Feasibility study for a separate Hindu West Bengal province.
* **March 10, 1947 (Nehru)** ⇒ Partition of Punjab & Bengal = only real alternative to Cabinet Mission Plan.
* **April 1947 (Kripalani to Viceroy)** ⇒ "rather than have a battle, we shall let them have their Pakistan provided you allow Bengal and Punjab to be partitioned in a fair manner."

---

## Mountbatten as The Viceroy
* **Powers** ⇒ Firm & quick decision-making; informally granted extra powers to decide on the spot.
* **Mandate** ⇒ Explore unity vs. division until **October 1947** → advise British on transfer form.
* **Discoveries** ⇒ Cabinet Mission Plan = dead horse; Jinnah obstinate on a fully sovereign state.
* **Strategy** ⇒ Preferred to woo both sides rather than support unified forces & counter partitionists.

## Mountbatten Plan, June 3, 1947
* **Menon Innovation (V.P. Menon)** ⇒ Transfer on basis of **Dominion Status** (with secession right) → bypassed waiting for CA agreement.
* *Alternative Forces to Independence (R.C. Mazumdar View)*:
  * No direct success from civil disobedience alone (ended 14 years prior).
  * Cumulative impact: German help to revolutionaries (WWI) + Subhas Bose & INA campaigns (WWII) + Hitler & Japan's indirect war actions.
  * **INA Trials** ⇒ Convinced British they could no longer rely on Indian sepoy loyalty to maintain authority.

### Main Points
* **Punjab & Bengal Assemblies** ⇒ Meet in two groups (Hindus vs. Muslims) to vote on partition (simple majority → partition).
* **Partition Outcome** ⇒ Two dominions + two constituent assemblies.
* **Sindh** ⇒ Take its own legislative decision.
* **NWFP & Sylhet (Bengal)** ⇒ Referendums to decide destiny.
* **Congress Concerns Met** (due to conceding unified India):
  1. Independence of princely states ruled out (must join India or Pakistan).
  2. Independent Bengal ruled out.
  3. Hyderabad accession to Pakistan ruled out (supported by Mountbatten).
  4. Freedom Date ⇒ **August 15, 1947**.
  5. **Boundary Commission** to be established.
* **Formula** = Divide India but retain maximum unity.

### Why Congress Accepted Dominion Status
* **Peaceful & Quick** ⇒ Guaranteed smooth, immediate transfer of power.
* **Control of Chaos** ⇒ Allowed Congress to assume authority & curb explosive communal situation.
* **Stability** ⇒ Ensured continuity in army & bureaucracy.
* **British Objective** ⇒ Keep India within the Commonwealth to secure trade, investment, and defense interests.

### Rationale For An Early Date (August 15, 1947)
* **Double Objective** ⇒ Secure Congress agreement to dominion status + escape British responsibility for escalating communal riots.
* **Plan Implementation**:
  * Bengal & Punjab Assemblies voted for partition.
  * **Sylhet Referendum** ⇒ Incorporated into East Bengal (Pakistan).
  * **Boundary Commissions** ⇒ Two commissions constituted under **Radcliffe**.
  * **NWFP Referendum** ⇒ Favored Pakistan (Provincial Congress boycotted/refrained).
  * **Sindh & Baluchistan** ⇒ Acceded to Pakistan.

## Indian Independence Act
* **Timeline** ⇒ Passed by British Parliament: **July 5, 1947**; Royal Assent: **July 18, 1947**; Implementation: **August 15, 1947**.
* **Key Provisions**:
  * Created two independent dominions (**India & Pakistan**) from Aug 15, 1947 (Pakistan independent on Aug 14, India on Aug 15).
  * Each dominion led by a **Governor General**.
  * **Constituent Assemblies** ⇒ Assumed complete legislative powers; Central Legislative Assembly & Council of States dissolved.
  * **Interim Governance** ⇒ Governed under **GoI Act, 1935** until new constitutions were adopted.
  * **Leadership** ⇒ Jinnah became GG of Pakistan; India requested Mountbatten to continue as GG.

## Plan Balkan
* **Origin** ⇒ Formulated by Mountbatten (March–May 1947) as alternative to Cabinet Mission.
* **Provisions** ⇒ Power to be transferred to separate provinces (or potential confederation); Bengal & Punjab option to partition; Princely states rendered independent.
* **Fate** ⇒ Quickly abandoned after **violent reaction from Nehru**.

---

## Problems of Early Withdrawal
* **Anomalies & Punjab Massacre** ⇒ Attributable to breakneck haste of transfer:
  * **No transition structures** to tackle partition issues.
  * **Jinnah’s Ambition** ⇒ Wanted Pakistan GG position, spoiling Mountbatten's plan for a common GG linkage.
  * **Radcliffe Award Delay** ⇒ Prepared by **August 12, 1947**, but Mountbatten made it public after **August 15** to escape British responsibility for disturbances.

---

## Integration Of States
* **Socio-Political Upsurge (1946–47)** ⇒ State People’s Movement demanded political rights & CA representation.
* **Nehru Stance (AISPC Udaipur 1945 / Gwalior 1947)** ⇒ Non-joining states treated as "hostile".
* **Sardar Patel Role** ⇒ Took charge of **States Department** in **July 1947**; used skillful combinations of bait & mass pressure.

### Phase I (Until August 15, 1947)
* **Accession** ⇒ All states signed **Instrument of Accession** except **Kashmir, Hyderabad, and Junagarh**.
* **Princes' Compliance** ⇒ Surrendered only what they never fully possessed (Defense, External Affairs, Communications under British paramountcy); no change in internal structure.

### Phase II (Post-August 15, 1947)
* **Consolidation** ⇒ Skilful integration into neighboring provinces or unions (Kathiawar Union, Vindhya and Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh).
* **Constitutional Changes** ⇒ Effected in states retaining old boundaries (Hyderabad, Mysore, Travancore-Cochin) within a year.
* **Bait Strategy** ⇒ Generous **privy purse** + appointing princes as Governors/Rajpramukhs in free India.

## Inevitability Of Partition

### Why Congress Accepted Partition
* **Mass Integration Failure** ⇒ Long-term failure of Congress to integrate Muslim masses into the national movement (dichotomy of anti-imperialist success-failure).
* **Looming Anarchy** ⇒ Immediate transfer of power seen as only way to stop "direct action" and communal violence.
* **Collapse of Interim Government** ⇒ Complete breakdown of dual-party governance made partition seem unavoidable.
* **Prevention of Balkanisation** ⇒ Avoided independent princely states (secured unity of the rest).
* **Concession History** ⇒ Culmination of a step-by-step concession process:
  * *Cripps Mission (1942)* ⇒ Accepted province autonomy.
  * *Gandhi-Jinnah Talks (1944)* ⇒ Accepted self-determination of Muslim-majority areas.
  * *Cabinet Mission (1946)* ⇒ Separate CA possible; accepted compulsory grouping (Dec 1946).
  * *March 1947 CWC Resolution* ⇒ Demanded partition of Punjab and Bengal if country was divided.
* **Illusion & Wishful Thinking (Nehru’s Statements)**:
  * "Once the British left, Hindu-Muslim differences would be patched up..."
  * "Partition is only temporary."
  * "Partition would be peaceful - once Pakistan was conceded, what was there to fight for?"
* **Changed Nature of Communalism** ⇒ Shift from 1920s/30s liberal communalism to 1940s assertive, militaristic "Muslim Nation" effort (underestimated by Congress).

## Gandhi’s Helplessness
* **Communalised Masses** ⇒ Gandhi felt helpless as the people themselves had become communalized.
* **No Democratic Option** ⇒ Cannot launch a non-violent movement against communalism using a communalized population.
* **Final Request** ⇒ Urged Congressmen not to accept partition in their hearts.
