# Examination Structure

The Civil Services Examination (CSE) is conducted in three stages:

1. Preliminary Examination (Prelims)
2. Main Examination (Mains)
3. Personality Test / Interview

> Prelims is a screening examination. Marks obtained in the Preliminary Examination are not counted for final merit.
> Final ranking is based on marks obtained in the Mains Written Examination and the Personality Test.

---

# Preliminary Examination (Prelims)

The Preliminary Examination consists of two compulsory papers:

| Paper | Subject | Marks | Nature |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Paper I | General Studies Paper I | 200 | Merit / Screening |
| Paper II | General Studies Paper II (CSAT) | 200 | Qualifying |

- Both papers are objective-type (multiple-choice questions).
- Duration of each paper: 2 hours.
- General Studies Paper II (CSAT) is qualifying in nature.
- Candidates must secure at least **33% marks** in CSAT.
- Only marks obtained in **General Studies Paper I** are considered for determining the Prelims cutoff.

# General Studies Paper I

**Marks:** 200  
**Duration:** 2 Hours  
**Type:** Objective (MCQs)

## Official Syllabus

1. **Current events of national and international importance.**

2. **History of India and Indian National Movement.**

3. **Indian and World Geography**
   - Physical Geography
   - Social Geography
   - Economic Geography

4. **Indian Polity and Governance**
   - Constitution
   - Political System
   - Panchayati Raj
   - Public Policy
   - Rights Issues

5. **Economic and Social Development**
   - Sustainable Development
   - Poverty
   - Inclusion
   - Demographics
   - Social Sector Initiatives

6. **General issues on Environmental Ecology, Biodiversity and Climate Change**
   - General issues that do not require subject specialisation

7. **General Science**

# General Studies Paper II (CSAT)

**Marks:** 200  
**Duration:** 2 Hours  
**Type:** Objective (MCQs)  
**Nature:** Qualifying  
**Minimum Qualifying Marks:** 33%

## Official Syllabus

1. **Comprehension**

2. **Interpersonal Skills including Communication Skills**

3. **Logical Reasoning and Analytical Ability**

4. **Decision-Making and Problem-Solving**

5. **General Mental Ability**

6. **Basic Numeracy**
   - Numbers and their relations
   - Orders of magnitude
   - Level: Class X

   - Charts
   - Graphs
   - Tables
   - Data Sufficiency
   - Level: Class X

# Main Examination (Mains)

The Main Examination consists of:

- 9 written papers
- 2 qualifying papers
- 7 papers counted for merit

Candidates qualifying in the written examination are called for the **Personality Test / Interview**.

# Personality Test

**Marks:** 275

The Personality Test is intended to assess the candidate's personal suitability for a career in public service.

The interview is not intended to be merely a test of:

- Specialised Knowledge
- General Knowledge

These aspects have already been tested through the written examination.

## Qualities Assessed

The Personality Test broadly assesses:

- Mental Alertness
- Critical Powers of Assimilation
- Clear and Logical Exposition
- Balance of Judgement
- Variety and Depth of Interest
- Ability for Social Cohesion
- Leadership
- Intellectual and Moral Integrity

## Areas of Interest

The candidate is expected to demonstrate awareness and interest in:

- Events happening around them
- Events within India
- Events outside India
- Modern Currents of Thought
- New Discoveries

The test also assesses qualities relevant to a:

- Well-educated
- Responsible
- Capable Public Servant
