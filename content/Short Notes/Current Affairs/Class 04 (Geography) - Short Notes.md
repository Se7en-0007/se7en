## Geography
### Earth
#### Outer Core
* **Outer Core = Voluminous liquid layer** $\approx$ **2,800 km** beneath Earth’s surface.
* **Composition** ⇒ Filled with molten **iron and nickel** in constant convective motion.
* **Generator Effect** ⇒ Constant motion → creates **Earth’s magnetic field** → shields planet from **solar radiation**.
* **Reversal Event (2010)** ⇒ Liquid iron below the equatorial Pacific Ocean **changed direction** → shifted from slow westward crawl to rapid eastward surge.
  * **Historical Drift** ⇒ Westward flow explains why Earth’s magnetic field historically **drifted west**.
  * **Causal Link** ⇒ Reversal linked to seismic and geodetic shifts in Earth’s **solid inner core**.

## Climate
### Sky Color
* **Hotter Days Phenonemon** ⇒ Sky appears **less blue (pale grey haze)** due to changes in light scattering.
* **Rayleigh Scattering** ⇒ Air molecules scatter light of shorter wavelengths (**blue**) → occurs when scattering object is **smaller** than light's wavelength.
* **Mie Scattering** ⇒ Hotter days → warmer air holds more moisture (higher humidity / fine water droplets) + convective currents lift **dust, aerosols, and particulate matter**.
  * **Effect** ⇒ Large particles scatter **all wavelengths equally** → Mie scattering → creates **pale grey haze**.

## Monsoon

* **New System** ⇒ IMD launched **block-level monsoon onset forecast system** covering **15 States and 1 UT**.
* **Operational Mechanics**:
  * Blends outputs of **two forecasting models** + uses **AI-based analysis**.
  * Draws on IMD's nearly **100 years of meteorological data** + global weather models.

* **UP-Specific Model** ⇒ 10-day monsoon forecast model providing forecasts at **1 km resolution**.
* **Significance** ⇒ Forecast errors → direct crop losses; new model aims to make forecasts **agriculturally actionable**.

### Pre-Monsoon Issues

### Key Climate Terms in News
#### 1. Western Disturbance
* **Concept** ⇒ Eastward-moving, rain-bearing weather systems originating in the **Mediterranean–West Asian region**.
* **Classification** ⇒ **Extratropical cyclones**, formed by interaction between polar and tropical air masses.
* **Movement Corridor** ⇒ Along the **subtropical westerly jet stream** over Himalayas and Tibet (primarily **December–March**).
* **Impact**:
  * Positive → Winter rainfall + snow in Himalayas (recharges glaciers/rivers) + vital for **Rabi crops** (wheat, mustard).
  * Negative → Hailstorms, flash floods, unseasonal rainfall.

#### 2. El Niño: 2026 Outlook
* **ENSO** = Periodic fluctuation in Pacific Ocean sea surface temperatures (SSTs) and atmospheric circulation.
* **Three Phases** ⇒ **El Niño** (warm phase), **La Niña** (cool phase), and **Neutral** (normal phase); occur irregularly every **2–7 years**.
* **El Niño Impact on India** ⇒ Weak/delayed monsoon → longer dry spells → reduced agricultural productivity + severe heatwaves.
* **2026 Outlook** ⇒ US CPC projected **61% probability** of El Niño during **May–July 2026**.

#### 3. Wet-Bulb Temperature
* **Definition** ⇒ Indicates the lowest temperature to which an object can cool down through **evaporative water loss (sweat)** under ambient conditions.
* **Significance** ⇒ High humidity → slow evaporation → reduced body cooling → severe heat stress.
* **Thresholds**:
  * **>35°C** → Potentially **fatal** (IPCC limit; human body can no longer lose metabolic heat).
  * **>31°C** → Dangerous during intense physical activity.
* **Vulnerable Groups** ⇒ Outdoor workers, elderly, children, patients with heart disease or diabetes.

#### 4. “Feels Like” Temperature
* **Concept** ⇒ Apparent temperature combining actual temperature with **humidity and wind speed** to estimate perceived heat/cold.
* **Heat Index** ⇒ Combines air temperature + relative humidity to show perceived hot weather.
* **Wind Chill Index** ⇒ Combines air temperature + wind speed; wind strips thin warm air boundary layer from skin → feels colder.

### El Niño
* **2026 Projections** ⇒ NOAA and IMD indicate likely El Niño and below-normal monsoon (**92% of Long Period Average [LPA]**).
* **Economic Transmission Channels**:
  * Heat stress → cuts productivity of informal/daily wage workers (construction, delivery, street vendors) → reduces working hours.
  * SWM supplies **70% of India's rainfall** → El Niño makes sowing riskier + raises irrigation costs + drives groundwater over-extraction.
  * Small & marginal farmers face severe income shocks → destabilises rural economy.
* **Inflationary Impacts** ⇒ Surging prices of vegetables, pulses, and essentials → drags down GDP growth.
* **Urban Heat Trap** ⇒ Concretisation + shrunken green cover → creates severe heat islands; poor face water scarcity and overcrowding.

### Rain - Soil Moisture - Nitrogen
* **Concentrated Rainfall Effects** (Studies in *Nature* & *Nature Geoscience*):
  * Heavy downpours exceed soil's **infiltration capacity** → causes **infiltration excess** (water pools on surface).
  * Long dry intervals between heavy rain bursts → pooled water **evaporates** instead of recharging deep soil layers.
  * **Outcome** ⇒ **27% of global population** will face abnormally dry soils due to concentrated rainfall alone (even if total annual rainfall is unchanged).
* **Soil Nitrogen Leaching**:
  * **<700 mm/year Rain** → Soil retains nitrogen (limited water limits nutrient mobility; plants/microbes compete).
  * **>700 mm/year Rain** → Concentrated heavy rainfall washes away and leaches crucial **nitrogen** → limits plant growth.
* **Eco-system Shift** ⇒ Southwest monsoon is becoming highly **episodic** (intense downpours split by long dry spells).

### 35% Rainfall Deficit
* **IMD Regional Statistics**:
  * **Northwest India** → **+5%** (above normal).
  * **East & Northeast** → **43% deficit**.
  * **Central India** → **61% deficit**.
  * **Southern Peninsula** → **14% deficit**.
* **Administrative Strategy** ⇒ Agriculture Ministry directed States to draw up crop-wise contingency plans (alternative crops, advice) + weekly El Niño reviews + shifting farming toward **cotton and pulses**.

### ‘Super’ El Niño
* **Potency Metric** ⇒ Measured by how far SSTs in the **Niño 3.4 reference patch** of equatorial Pacific climb above long-term averages.
  * **Scale** ⇒ **0.5–1.0°C** (weak) | **1.0–1.5°C** (moderate) | **1.5–2.0°C** (strong) | **>2.0°C** (very strong / 'Super' El Niño).
  * **2026 Forecast** ⇒ NOAA indicates **63% odds** of very strong event, potentially approaching record of **2.5°C**.
  * **Historical Super Events** → **1972–73, 1982–83, 1997–98, 2015–16**.
* **Feedback Loop Mechanism** ⇒ Trade winds weaken → eastern Pacific warms → slackens trade winds further → loop amplifies warm anomaly.
  * **Climate Change Factor** ⇒ Warmer baseline ocean (global warming) provides more background heat → intensifies recent events.
* **Monsoon Suppression** ⇒ El Niño starts in spring, peaks in winter, weakens next spring → suppresses monsoon in **middle & later part** of SWM (July–September). June onset is governed by local/regional factors.
  * **Historical Ratio** ⇒ Of ~24 El Niño years since 1950 → **15** below normal monsoon | **10** outright deficiency (<90% of LPA).
  * **Worst Droughts** → **1972, 1982, 2009, 2015**.
* **Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD)** ⇒ 1997-98 El Niño brought 2% above normal rains due to a positive IOD (warm water pools in western Indian Ocean countered Pacific-induced drying).
  * **2026 Crisis** ⇒ Forecasts show the IOD won't be strong enough to counter El Niño.
* **Global Impacts**:
  * **1982–83** → Severe drought & bushfires in Australia + dry Indonesia.
  * **1997–98** → Indo forest fires & choking haze + killed 1/6th of world's coral reefs.
  * **2015–16** → Great Barrier Reef bleaching + extreme drought in East/Southern Africa.
  * **Tropical Cyclones** ⇒ Suppresses Atlantic hurricanes (via vertical wind shear) | Favors hurricanes/super-typhoons in central/eastern Pacific (typically recurving toward East Asia/Americas).

## Heatwave
### HAPs & Mandates
* **Chennai Third Master Plan** ⇒ First to integrate localized climate action mapping surface temperature, building density, and urban heat islands.
* **State Action Plans (SAPCC 2.0)** ⇒ Shifted from top-down to localized execution:
  * **Odisha** → Tracks funds through climate budgets.
  * **Kerala** → Advanced disaster management.
  * **Madhya Pradesh** → Robust climate knowledge platform.
  * **Tamil Nadu** → High multi-sectoral investment.
* **Institutional Tools**:
  * **CEEW** → Collaborates with cities to deploy scientific tools for Heat Action Plans (HAPs).
  * **Thane Task Force** → Uses a **"responsibility matrix"** for inter-departmental heat coordination.
* **Data Portals**:
  * **RDSS Portal** → Tracks power infra upgrades and transmission losses.
  * **PM Surya Ghar** → Monitors state-wise rooftop solar adoption.
  * **India Climate & Energy Dashboard** (NITI Aayog & Vasudha Foundation) → Integrates multi-ministry climate data.
* **Financial Devolution** ⇒ **16th Finance Commission** recommended making heatwaves a **nationally notified disaster** to release federal relief funds.

### Cooling Doctrine
* **HAP Limitations** ⇒ NDMA notes plans are often generic imitations relying only on temporary water kiosks, wait-shades, and advisories. They fail to alter underlying heat exposure of workers.
* **National Cooling Doctrine Objectives**:
  * Treat access to safe indoor temperatures as a **public-health entitlement**.
  * Mandate **minimum cooling standards** for indoor workplaces (factories, warehouses, kitchens, call centers, delivery hubs) backed by a strict inspection regime.
* **Technological Solutions** ⇒ Deploy passive cooling and reflective roofs + build **district cooling systems** for dense urban blocks + develop low-cost ACs optimized for humid, wet Indian heat.

### Steps To Address Delhi’s Heat
* **Urban Heat Island "Heat Re-trap" Causes**:
  * Loss of green cover, degraded wetlands, and Yamuna floodplains → reduces **evapotranspiration** cooling.
  * Concrete, asphalt, steel, glass absorb heat and release it slowly at night.
  * Vehicular activity → engine and exhaust thermal corridors.
  * High-density vertical constructions blocking wind.
* **AC Feedback Loop** ⇒ ACs cool indoor areas but release heat outdoors → raises ambient local temperature by **1–2°C** in dense blocks → triggers more AC demand.
  * **Projections** ⇒ National cooling demand is set to grow **8x by 2050**, stressing grids and risking blackouts.
* **Economic Losses** ⇒ Factory productivity falls **2–3%** per degree above optimal levels → India loses over **$100 billion annually** due to heat-induced productivity decline.
* **Key Solutions** ⇒ High-albedo cool roofs + urban forests & green-blue infrastructure + electric mobility + district cooling + subsidized cooling centers.

### Concrete fever
* **Rajasthan Extremes** ⇒ Sri Ganganagar touched **48°C**.
* **Climate Trends** (IMD & WMO):
  * Frequency of heatwave spells risen by **0.1 days per decade** since 1961.
  * Maximum duration of heatwaves increased by **0.55 days per decade**.
  * **2015–25** → Warmest 11-year stretch on record.
  * Urban heat islands run **2°C to 10°C hotter** than surrounding rural areas.
* **Required Adjustments** ⇒ Calibrate building codes + adjust labour laws + create dedicated national and state heat management budgets.

### Reasons of a Heatwave ?
* **Physical Drivers**:
  * Overhead sun heats ground + stable high-pressure system → sinking air suppresses cloud formation → direct solar heating.
  * Hot, dry **continental winds** blow from the interior.
  * Dry soil → solar heat goes directly into warming the air (sensible heat) rather than evaporating water.
* **Rossby Waves** ⇒ Large undulations in the polar jet stream → create atmospheric high-pressure **"heat domes"** that stagnate over a region.
* **Rajasthan Traditional Adaptation**:
  * **Thatched roofs** for insulation (+ water sprinkling to drop indoor temp).
  * Clay + cow dung walls to reduce heat conductivity.
  * Drip irrigation to sustain soil moisture.
  * Cultivating drought-resistant **Jowar (sorghum)** as livestock fodder.
  * Covering open irrigation channels of the **Indira Gandhi Canal** with concrete covers to prevent evaporation.

## Floods
### Uttarakhand Flood Map
* **Study Findings (*Current Science*)**:
  * Flood hazard assessments in UK underestimate risk by relying on **long-term average rainfall** rather than extreme downpours.
  * Over **90% of Uttarakhand** is classified under moderate, high, or severe hazard zones.
  * GIS hazard mapping combines: elevation, slope, drainage density, topographic wetness, land use, and rainfall.
* **Underestimation Risk** ⇒ Hazard maps using highest annual rainfall show expanding severe risk zones, whereas maps using long-term averages show risk zones shrinking (giving planners a false sense of safety).
* **Vulnerability Drivers**:
  * Rising frequency of extreme rainfall in Himalayas due to warming atmosphere.
  * Natural hazard catalysts → cloudbursts, glacial lake outbursts (GLOFs), and landslides (**Malpa landslide 1998, Kedarnath 2013, Chamoli flood 2021**).
  * During the **2013 Kedarnath disaster**, UK received **375%** of its benchmark monsoon rain.
  * Concretisation of soils reduces surface runoff absorption.

### Sustainable Mining: Oxymoron ?
* **Transition Paradox** ⇒ Low-carbon transition (wind turbines, solar, EV batteries) requires massive amounts of **lithium, cobalt, copper, and rare-earth elements** → mining disrupts landscapes, pollutes water, and destroys biodiversity.
* **"Weak Sustainability" (UN & IEA)** ⇒ Mining is acceptable if extracted resource values are converted into greater permanent assets (human capital, education, infrastructure) that outlast the mine + minerals must be **recycled indefinitely**.
* **Responsible Mining Standards** ⇒ Powering trucks with green hydrogen + solar/wind power for extraction equipment + securing **Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC)** of tribal communities + sharing profits directly.

## Earthquake
### ‘Controlled’ Earthquakes
* **BedrettoLab Experiment** (Switzerland):
  * Conducted in a **5.2 km ventilation tunnel** leading to the Furka railway tunnel in Southern Switzerland.
* **Induced Seismicity**:
  * Injected **750 cubic meters of water** to provoke a magnitude-1 earthquake.
  * Induced **~8,000 tiny earthquakes** (ranging from magnitude **-5 to -0.14** along targeted fault and perpendicular segments).
* **Significance** ⇒ Helps scientists master how to induce small quakes to prevent stress accumulation, mapping injection angles to avoid triggering destructive earthquakes.

### Venezuela Quakes
* **Doublet Event** ⇒ Two powerful earthquakes (**magnitude 7.2 and 7.5**) struck western Venezuela near Yumare in rapid succession.
* **Seismic Doublet** = Two earthquakes of similar major magnitudes occurring close in time and space, arising from distinct but closely related fault ruptures (unlike standard mainshock-aftershock patterns, both are major events).
  * **Mechanism** ⇒ First rupture transfers stress to adjacent fault segment → triggers second quake.
* **Seismic Drivers** ⇒ Location near **Caribbean–South American Plate boundary** + active strike-slip faults (**Boconó and San Sebastián faults**).

### Earthquake Basics
* **Earthquake** = Shaking caused by sudden release of energy when rocks slip along a fault.
* **Hypocentre (Focus)** → Point inside Earth where rupture begins.
* **Epicentre** → Point on surface directly above focus.
* **Prone Regions** ⇒ Pacific Ring of Fire | Alpide Belt | Caribbean Region | East African Rift System | San Andreas Fault.

## Ocean Currents
### AMOC System
* **AMOC** = Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation; massive conveyor system transporting warm, salty tropical water toward the North Atlantic.
* **The Cycle** ⇒ Salty water cools near Greenland → becomes denser and sinks into deep ocean → flows back southwards as cold, deep water.
* **Slowdown Drivers** ⇒ Climate change melts Arctic glaciers → adds massive volumes of **freshwater** to North Atlantic → fresher water is less dense and sinks slowly → AMOC could **weaken by 59% by 2100**.
* **Global Implications**:
  * Rapid sea-level rise in North America.
  * Shifts heat distribution between Atlantic and Pacific → makes El Niño more intense and unpredictable.
  * Shifts tropical rain belt southwards → weakens moisture-bearing winds from the Arabian Sea reaching India → **cripples Indian monsoon** → drives droughts and crop failures.

## Rivers, Lakes & Dams
### Hydel Projects in Upper Ganga
* **Central Policy** ⇒ Union government told Supreme Court it opposes any **new** hydel projects in upper reaches of Ganga in Uttarakhand (Alaknanda and Bhagirathi basins).
* **Grandfathered Exceptions** ⇒ **7 projects** already commissioned or substantially built can continue to protect public and private capital investments. None fall in the Bhagirathi Eco-Sensitive Zone.
* **Somanathan Committee (2024)** ⇒ Supreme Court panel narrowed 21 projects under review to **five** (*Bowala Nandprayag, Devsari, Bhyundar Ganga, Jhalakoti, Urgam-II*). Central government declined even these five due to:
  * "Bumper-to-bumper" dams cumulative ecological damage.
  * High seismic fragility of the upper Himalayas.
  * Recurrent disasters (2013 cloudburst, **2025 Dharali flash flood**).
* **Expert Appraisals**:
  * **Ravi Chopra Committee (2014)** → 23 of 24 hydel projects have severe ecological impacts on Alaknanda/Bhagirathi.
  * **Vinod Tare Panel** → Recommended projects should "not be taken up".
  * **B.P. Das Expert Body-II (2020)** → Permissive view.

### Glacial Lakes in Arunachal
* **Tawang Hazard Assessment** (Mago Chu Basin):
  * Satellite assessment of 5 glacial lakes found **four expanded** between 2016 and 2026.
  * **Sanhapo Lake** → Recorded most significant growth; highest priority for GLOF hazard modeling and early warnings.
  * Two NDMA "very high risk" lakes expanded by 1 hectare.
  * **Dharkha Tso** ("high risk") showed gradual growth.
* **Technological Monitoring** ⇒ Employs high-res satellite imagery from **ICEYE, PlanetScope, and LISS-IV** to bypass difficult terrain.
* **GLOF Risk** ⇒ Occurs when natural moraine dams fail, or when avalanches/landslides displace water generating massive waves (e.g., **2023 South Lhonak GLOF** in Sikkim which destroyed the **Chungthang hydro dam**).

### Afsluitdijk Dam
* **Concept** ⇒ **32-km dam & causeway** in the Netherlands.
* **Function** ⇒ Global benchmark in flood control and land reclamation; blocks the North Sea to protect land and enables massive freshwater storage.

### Nathpa Jhakri Hydro Power Project
* **Location** ⇒ Sutlej River (Nathpa in Kinnaur to Jhakri in Shimla, Himachal Pradesh).
* **Specs** ⇒ **Run-of-the-river** project; installed capacity **1,500 MW** (6 × 250 MW Francis turbine units).

### Kalpasar Project
* **Indo-Netherlands cooperation** ⇒ Technical engineering pact signed for the project.
* **Objective** ⇒ Water security for water-stressed **Saurashtra region** (Gujarat).
* **Features**:
  * Proposes a giant freshwater reservoir across **Gulf of Khambhat** (extending from Aladar to near Bhavnagar).
  * **Highway-cum-rail corridor** over the dam to cut Bhavnagar–Surat travel distance.
  * **2,470 MW of hybrid renewable energy** (1,000 MW solar, 1,500 MW wind) on reclaimed land.
* **Water Feeders** ⇒ Planned to receive water from **Sabarmati, Mahi, Dhadhar, Narmada**, and 7 smaller rivers (*Wadhwan Bhogavo, Limbdi Bhogavo, Sukhbhadar, Ghelo, Kalubhar*).
  * Includes a diversion canal from **Bhadbhut Barrage** on Narmada to divert surplus waters.
* **Benefits** ⇒ Restricts coastal salinity ingress, reclaims **1.76 lakh hectares** of land, and enables freshwater fisheries.

## Volcano
### Effects on Atmosphere
* **Chemical Hazards**:
  * Blasts **Sulphur dioxide ($SO_2$)** into stratosphere → forms sulfate aerosols → scatters incoming solar radiation → **cools Earth**.
  * Ash and dust cloud local sunlight (falls back in a few weeks) + releases $CO_2$ (human activity emits more, but volcanic $CO_2$ historically drove warming).
  * Emits compounds that cause acid rain and damage the ozone layer.
* **Hunga Tonga-Hunga Ha’apai Eruption (2022)** ⇒ Released massive quantities of **methane** into stratosphere → volcanic ash triggered chemical reactions converting methane into **formaldehyde**.

## Society
### Population
#### Demographic Transition
* **SRS Statistical Report (2024) Key Trends**:
  * **TFR dropped to 1.9** (now below the replacement level of 2.1).
  * Birth Rate: 21 (2014) → **18.3 (2024)** | Death Rate: 6.7 → **6.4**.
  * Median Age: **29.2 years** (vs. China’s 40.2 years).
  * Youth (15-29 years) $\approx$ **370–380 million** (27% of population). Over **65%** of India is <35 years.
* **Changing Fertility Patterns**:
  * Rural fertility lies near replacement level, but urban fertility crashed to **1.5**.
  * **Delhi TFR** is ultra-low at **1.2**; Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal stand at **1.3** (lower than US [1.6], Finland [1.4], and Japan [1.3]).
  * Northern States remain high: **Bihar (2.9), UP (2.6), MP (2.4), Rajasthan (2.3)**.
* **Greying Nation Strain**:
  * India has **150 million** people aged 60+; set to rise to **347 million** (1/5th of population) by 2050.
  * **NITI Aayog** → **70%** of elderly are dependent on others; **78%** have zero pension cover.
  * Standard old-age pension under **NSAP** is extremely low: **₹200/month** (60–79 years) and **₹500/month** (80+).
  * Narrow direct tax base (only **6%** are net taxpayers) → severely limits fiscal room for old-age social welfare.
  * Weakening joint-family structure under urbanization means female unpaid care is declining.
  * Geriatric health demand surging due to hypertension, diabetes, dementia, and palliative needs.
* **Federal Migration Aspect** ⇒ Rapidly aging Southern states will need to absorb young labor from Northern states → requires **portable welfare benefits** and inter-state entitlements.

#### Baby Bait
* **Policy Initiative** ⇒ Andhra Pradesh proposed cash and social incentives to encourage families to have **three or more children** as TFR fell to **1.5**.
* **Proposed Perks**:
  * **₹30,000** for 3rd child | **₹40,000** for 4th child | **₹1,000/month** cash support for 5 years.
  * Free education up to 18 years + mother work-from-home provisions.
  * Higher funding for *Thalliki Vandanam* (₹15,000/child for school attendance) + longer maternity leave and Anganwadi support.
* **Policy Challenges**:
  * Cash incentives rarely produce a sustained increase in TFR due to high cost of housing and private schooling.
  * France/Nordics succeeded because they built universal childcare, paid parental leave, flexible working, and high-quality public schools.
  * AP policy may encourage higher birth rates only in economically vulnerable, poor households seeking immediate cash, increasing poverty cycles.

### Tribes

* **Launch Date** ⇒ **October 2, 2024** (Named after Bhagwan Birsa Munda - "Dharti Aaba").

* **Launch** ⇒ PM launched in **September 2025** as part of Janjatiya Gaurav Varsh.
