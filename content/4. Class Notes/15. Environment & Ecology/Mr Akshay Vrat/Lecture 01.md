## Climate Change & Global Warming Fundamentals

- **Definitions**:
  - **Climate** = Long-term weather patterns (temperature, precipitation, humidity, wind) averaged over **≥ 30 years**.
  - **Climate Change** = Shift in long-term weather patterns.
- **Drivers of Climate Change**:
  - **Natural Drivers** → Volcanic ash reflection, Pangaea breakup / Tectonic continental drift.
  - **Contemporary Drivers (20th Century / Industrial Revolution ~1750 onwards)** → **Anthropogenic (man-made) activities** → **Global Warming**.
- **Global Temperature Data & Projections**:
  - Baseline Average Global Temp = **15°C** (Earth is naturally a cool planet).
  - Current Observed Rise = **+1.1°C to +1.2°C** (Current avg = **16.1°C – 16.2°C**).
  - Projected Temperature Trajectory:
    - **By 2030** → **+1.5°C**
    - **By 2050** → **+2.0°C**
    - **By 2100** → **+2.5°C to +3.0°C** (Business-as-usual scenario).
- **Significance of 2°C Shift**:
  - AC Analogy (24°C vs 26°C/27°C) → Causes severe heat stroke & dehydration in enclosed spaces.
  - Humans have artificial ACs; remaining **8.74 million species** have no technological adaptation.

## Dry Bulb vs. Wet Bulb Temperature

- **Definitions & Measurement**:
  - **Dry Bulb Temperature (DBT)** = Standard atmospheric air temperature measured by an exposed thermometer.
  - **Wet Bulb Temperature (WBT)** = Temperature measured by thermometer bulb wrapped in a water-soaked cotton wick.
    - *Mechanism*: Water evaporation absorbs ambient heat → Latent heat absorption → Cooling effect → **WBT < DBT** (under normal dry conditions).
- **Impact of High Humidity (Monsoon Scenario)**:
  - Hot + High Humidity → Air saturated with moisture → Evaporation slows/ceases → Zero cooling effect → **WBT approaches DBT (WBT ≈ DBT)**.
- **Biological & Physiological Impacts of High WBT**:
  - **Human Body**:
    - Perspiration fails to evaporate → Body core temperature rises → Continuous sweating → Severe dehydration → Heat stroke → **Organ failure**.
    - **Lethal Threshold**: **WBT ≥ 35°C** = Critical threshold for human & animal survival.
  - **Vector-Borne Diseases**:
    - Hot + Humid conditions ↑ Mosquito breeding speed → Outbreaks of Dengue/Malaria.
  - **Livestock & Temperature-Humidity Index (THI)**:
    - Optimal livestock temperature = **27°C**.
    - **THI Impact Rule**: Every **+1°C rise above 27°C** → Milk production **decreases by 5%** + increased infection vulnerability + higher maintenance costs.
  - **Agriculture**:
    - High WBT reduces transpiratory water loss in tropical plants → **Beneficial water management** for certain plants.
  - **Economic Cascades**:
    - Higher healthcare costs + increased AC electricity consumption (Coal burning → Higher emissions → Vicious climate feedback) + Food/Milk inflation.
  - *Exam Relevance*: UPSC Prelims 2024 Question.

## Mechanism of Global Warming – The Greenhouse Effect

- **Solar Radiation Spectrum**:
  - 7 Radiation Types: Radio, Micro, Infrared (IR), Visible Light, Ultraviolet (UV), X-rays, Gamma rays.
  - **90% Solar Radiation** = **Infrared (IR)** + **Visible Light** + **Ultraviolet (UV)**.
    - **UV Light** → Blocked by Stratospheric Ozone Layer.
    - **Visible Light** → Grants optical illumination.
    - **Infrared (IR)** → Carries **Thermal / Heat Signature** (e.g., Sun emits IR + heat; LED emits Visible light without IR/heat).
- **Greenhouse / Glasshouse Physics**:
  - Glass property = Transparent to High-Energy / Short-Wavelength IR + Visible Light.
  - Solar IR & Visible Light pass through glass → Strike internal soil/ground.
  - Surface collision → Energy absorption & reflection (Tennis ball analogy: bounced ball loses kinetic energy).
  - Bounced IR = **Low-Energy / Long-Wavelength IR**.
  - **Glass Trap Property**: Glass allows high-energy incoming IR, but **absorbs & blocks low-energy outgoing IR**.
  - Trapped IR → Heat buildup inside glasshouse → Enables cold-climate agriculture (e.g., **Buckwheat** in Greenland, Arctic Russia, Canada).
- **Polyhouses in India**:
  - Constructed using Polyethylene sheets instead of glass.
  - Benefits: Cheaper, temporary, removable, prevents frost damage for off-season vegetables.
- **Atmospheric Analogy**:
  - Atmospheric **Greenhouse Gases (GHGs)** mirror glass behavior → Trap outgoing low-energy terrestrial IR radiation → Global Warming (Topic continued in Lecture 02).
