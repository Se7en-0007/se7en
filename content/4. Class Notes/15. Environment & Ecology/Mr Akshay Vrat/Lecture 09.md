### Historical Context & Foundation of Environmental Governance

#### Evolution of UN Focus Areas
- **1945 → UN Foundation**: Primary objective = avoid $3^{rd}$ World War (80-year milestone in 2025).
- **1961–1962 → UNDP (United Nations Development Programme)**: Shift from security/politics to human development (poverty reduction, intellect, health, education, skills).
- **1972 → Environmental Recognition**: UN realization = human development impossible without environmental protection.

---

### Stockholm Conference (1972) & UNEP

#### UNCHE (United Nations Conference on Human Environment)
- **Official Name** = United Nations Conference on Human Environment (**UNCHE**).
- **Location** = Stockholm, Sweden (popularly termed **Stockholm Conference**).
- **Date of Signing** = **5th June 1972**.
- **Primary Outcome** = Establishment of **UNEP (United Nations Environment Programme)**.
- **World Environment Day** = Celebrated annually on **5th June** to commemorate UNEP's foundation.

#### UNEP Structure & Governance
- **Headquarters** = **Nairobi, Kenya** (*first UN HQ established outside developed nations / in Africa*).
- **Membership** = All 193 UN member states are de facto members of UNEP.
- **Official Publication** = **Global Environmental Outlook (GEO)**.

#### UNEA (United Nations Environment Assembly)
- **Location** = Nairobi, Kenya.
- **Creation** = Established in **June 2012** at Rio+20 (UNCSD).
- **Status** = Highest decision-making body of UN on global environmental issues.
- **Meeting Frequency** = Every **2 years** ($6^{th}$ session in 2024; $7^{th}$ session in 2026).
- **Key Recent Focus** = Negotiations on the **Global Plastic Treaty**.

### UNEP Climate & Sustainable Development Summits Timeline

1972 (Stockholm UNCHE) → 1992 (Rio UNCED) → 1997 (Rio+5 NYC) → 2002 (Rio+10 WSSD) → 2012 (Rio+20 UNCSD) → 2022 (Stockholm+50)

1. **1992 → UNCED (United Nations Conference on Environment and Development)**:
   - Location = **Rio de Janeiro, Brazil**.
   - Popular Names = **Rio Conference** / **Earth Summit**.
2. **1997 → Rio+5 Summit**:
   - Location = **New York, USA**.
3. **2002 → WSSD (World Summit on Sustainable Development) / Rio+10**:
   - Location = **Johannesburg, South Africa**.
   - Core Milestone = **Concept of SDGs first introduced**.
4. **2012 → UNCSD (United Nations Conference on Sustainable Development) / Rio+20**:
   - Location = **Rio de Janeiro, Brazil** (also called **Earth Summit 2012**).
   - **3 Major Outcomes**:
     - **Outcome A**: Creation of **UNEA** (United Nations Environment Assembly).
     - **Outcome B**: **PAGE (Partnership for Action on Green Economy)** & **Green GDP**:
       - *Traditional GDP* = Value of all goods & services produced in domestic territory.
       - *Green GDP* = Traditional GDP $-$ Cost of environmental damage/degradation.
       - *Goal* = **Decouple** economic growth from environmental degradation.
     - **Outcome C**: **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**:
       - **17 Goals, 169 Targets**.
       - *Implementation Period* = **1st January 2015 – 31st December 2030** (15 years).
       - *Legal Status* = Voluntary / non-legally binding pledges.
       - *Scope* = Integrated socio-economic & environmental goals.
       - **7 Environment-Specific SDGs**:
         - **SDG 6** = Clean Water & Sanitation
         - **SDG 7** = Affordable & Clean Energy
         - **SDG 11** = Sustainable Cities & Communities
         - **SDG 12** = Responsible Consumption & Production
         - **SDG 13** = Climate Action
         - **SDG 14** = Life Below Water
         - **SDG 15** = Life on Land
5. **2022 → Stockholm+50**:
   - Commemorated 50 years of 1972 Stockholm Conference (held during UNEA-5 at Nairobi).

### Outcomes of the 1992 Earth Summit (UNCED, Rio)

- **Duration** = 11 days covering all global environmental issues.
- **6 Primary Outcomes**:

#### 1. Rio Declaration on Environment and Development
- **27 Principles** guiding future sustainable development.
- **Legal Status** = Voluntarily implemented / non-binding.
- Termed **Third Generation Human Rights** (Environmental rights):
  - *1st Generation* = Political / Democratic rights.
  - *2nd Generation* = Socio-economic / Labor rights.
  - *3rd Generation* = Environmental rights (Right to clean environment, EIA, public participation in environmental clearance, internalization of environmental cost, indigenous community custodianship rights).

#### 2. Agenda 21
- **300-page comprehensive action plan** for UN, multilateral orgs, national governments, local bodies, and civil society.
- Target = Achieve sustainable development in the $21^{st}$ century.
- **Legal Status** = Voluntarily implemented / non-binding.

#### 3. Forest Principles
- Non-binding recommendations for sustainable management & conservation of global forest reserves.
- *Reason for non-binding nature of top 3* = Developing countries lack funds due to historical colonial wealth drain (e.g., India's 190-year British economic exploitation).

#### 4. GEF (Global Environment Facility)
- **Status** = Independent financial institution under the **World Bank**, created on the eve of 1992 Earth Summit.
- **Function** = Largest public funder of environmental initiatives globally.
- **6 Focal Areas** = Biodiversity, Climate Change, International Waters, Land Degradation, Ozone Layer, Persistent Organic Pollutants (**POPs** / Stockholm Convention).

#### 5. UNFCCC (United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change)
- **Status** = First and only international treaty dedicated to reducing GHG emissions and mitigating climate change.

#### 6. UNCBD (United Nations Convention on Biological Diversity)
- **Legal Status** = **Legally binding treaty** (requires national statutory legislation).
- **3 Core Objectives**:
  1. Conservation of global biodiversity.
  2. Sustainable use of its components.
  3. Fair and equitable access to benefits arising from utilization of biological resources & traditional knowledge (**ABS mechanism**).

*Note*: **UNCCD** (UN Convention to Combat Desertification) was tabled at 1992 Summit but adopted 1 year later (1994) due to time constraints.

### UNFCCC & COP Roadmap

#### Core Treaty Parameters
- **Introduced** = 1992 (Rio Earth Summit).
- **Adopted / Entered into Force** = 1994 (after parliamentary ratification by threshold countries).
- **Secretariat** = **Bonn, Germany**.
- **COP (Conference of Parties)** = Highest decision-making body of UNFCCC; convenes **annually**.

#### Chronological Timeline of UNFCCC COPs
- **COP 1 (1995)** = Berlin, Germany
- **COP 2 (1996)** = Geneva, Switzerland
- **COP 3 (1997)** = **Kyoto, Japan** (*Kyoto Protocol introduced*)
- **COP 7 (2000/2001)** = **Marrakesh, Morocco**
- **COP 8 (2001/2002)** = **New Delhi, India** (*only COP hosted by India*)
- **COP 13 (2007)** = **Bali, Indonesia**
- **COP 15 (2009)** = **Copenhagen, Denmark**
- **COP 16 (2010)** = **Cancun, Mexico**
- **COP 17 (2011)** = **Durban, South Africa**
- **COP 18 (2012)** = **Doha, Qatar**
- **COP 19 (2013)** = **Warsaw, Poland**
- **COP 20 (2014)** = **Lima, Peru**
- **COP 21 (2015)** = **Paris, France** (*Paris Agreement*)
- **COP 22 (2016)** = **Marrakesh, Morocco** (*only city to host COP twice*)

### Kyoto Protocol (COP 3, 1997)

#### Core Objective & Target
- **Introduced** = COP 3 (1997) in Kyoto, Japan.
- **Legal Nature** = **Legally binding** protocol under UNFCCC.
- **Core Objective** = Reduce global GHG emissions by **5% below 1990 baseline levels**.
- **Base Year** = **1990** (*latest complete data available in 1997*).
- **First Commitment Period** = **1st January 2008 – 31st December 2012** (5 years).

#### Central Ideology
- **CBDR-RC (Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capacities)**:
  - *Common Responsibility* = All nations share responsibility to combat climate change.
  - *Differentiated Burden* = Emission reduction targets allocated based on historical cumulative emissions and economic/technological capacity.

#### Classification of Nations under Kyoto Protocol

![[Kyoto Classification.png|900]]

1. **Annex I Countries (43 Nations)**:
   - Industrialized countries (EU member states + OECD nations + Economies in Transition like Russia).
   - Target = **Legally binding** emission reduction commitments.
2. **Annex II Countries (24 Nations)**:
   - Sub-set *within* Annex I (wealthy OECD developed nations).
   - Binding reduction targets + **Added Responsibility**: Mandatory financial and technical aid transfer to non-Annex I developing states (preventing developing nations from repeating historical carbon-heavy growth models).
3. **Non-Annex I Countries**:
   - Developing, underdeveloped, LDCs, and SIDS (Small Island Developing States).
   - Target = **Voluntary** emission reduction (non-binding).
   - *Examples* = India, China, Bangladesh, Bhutan.
   - *Case Study - Bhutan*: Net-negative carbon footprint; constitutional mandate maintaining $\ge 65\%$ forest cover; 100% organic agriculture; hydro-electricity powered.
1. *Future Additions (Doha Amendment 2012 preview)* = Annex B and Developing Country groups.

#### The Annex I Emission Reduction Dilemma
- **Example Case** = **Japan** (Annex I & Annex II state).
- **Permitted Emissions** = **Assigned Amount Units (AAUs)** (e.g., 90% allocation following 10% reduction mandate).
- **Compliance Dilemma**:
  - *Option 1: Reduce Production/GDP* → Rejected by all states.
  - *Option 2: High Capital Expenditure on Green Tech / CCS* → Unviable during economic stagnation (e.g., Japan's post-1990s 1–1.5% low growth rate & declining population).
- **Solution** = **Kyoto Flexible Mechanisms**:
  - Introduced to allow Annex I states to meet reduction commitments cost-effectively without hindering domestic economic growth (**Decoupling** growth from emissions).
