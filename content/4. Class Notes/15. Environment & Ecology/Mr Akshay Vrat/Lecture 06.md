## Recap: Ocean Acidification Mechanics

- **Definition**: Influx of weak carbonic acid ($H_2CO_3$) and $CO_2$ into ocean water (NOT oceans turning acidic instantly; pH drop = **8.5 → 8.2 / 8.1**; full acidification requires thousands of years of rain).
- **Chemical Cascade**:
  - Atmospheric $CO_2$ + $H_2O$ → **$H_2CO_3$ (Carbonic acid)**
  - $H_2CO_3$ dissociation → **$H^+$ (Hydronium $H_3O^+$)** + **$HCO_3^-$ (Bicarbonate ion)**
  - Free $H^+$ + **$CO_3^{2-}$ (Carbonate ion)** → **$HCO_3^-$**
  - **Result**: $CO_3^{2-}$ concentration ↓ | $HCO_3^-$ concentration ↑ | $H^+$ concentration ↑
- **Impact on Calcification**:
  - Calcifying organisms: **$Ca^{2+}$ + $CO_3^{2-} \rightarrow CaCO_3$ (Calcium Carbonate / Calcite)**
  - $CO_3^{2-}$ ↓ → Calcification rate ↓ & Calcite shell thickness ↓ → Shell deformation & dissolution vulnerability.
- **Saturation Horizon Dynamics**:
  - **Saturation Horizon** = Boundary separating oversaturated (upper) and undersaturated (lower) waters.
  - $CO_3^{2-}$ ↓ → **Saturation horizon rises upward** → Organisms exposed to undersaturated waters → Shell dissolution ↑.

## Ocean Deoxygenation

- **Mechanism**: **Marine Heatwaves / Global Warming** → Surface water temperature ↑ (up to **3°C**) → Fluid expansion → Dissolved oxygen (DO) released as gas bubbles (similar to boiling water).
- **Biological Role of DO**:
  - Essential for aquatic respiration & food combustion.
  - Fish: Absorb DO via **gills**.
  - Marine mammals (Whales, Dolphins): Absorb $O_2$ directly from atmosphere.
- **DO Concentration Thresholds & Map Indicators**:
  - **Red dots**: DO < **2 mg/L** (or **2 ppm**; <2 mg $O_2$ per 1 L ocean water).
  - **Light blue dots**: DO < **2 mg/L**.
  - **Blue dots**: DO = **0.7 mg/L**.
  - **Black dots**: DO = **0.07 mg/L** (Extreme hypoxia; cannot support major biodiversity).
  - **Geographic Pattern**: Maximum deoxygenation along the **Equator** (highest SST).
  - **Indian Waters**: Facing acute shortage of DO.
- **Socio-Economic & Ecological Impact**:
  - Marine Biodiversity ↓ → Fish mortality ↑
  - **Pisciculture / Fish Production** ↓ → Severe blow to **Blue Economy** & **Blue Revolution**.

## Deadly Trio Component: Coral Bleaching

### 1. Coral Fundamentals & Biology
- **Nature**: Calcifying **Animals** (Heterotrophs; cannot produce own food; immobile/sessile).
- **Structure**: Grow in colonies called **Polyps** (coral colonies).
- **Skeleton**: Absorb $Ca^{2+}$ + $CO_3^{2-}$ → Produce **$CaCO_3$** (naturally white).
- **Defense System**:
  - Possess stinging cells called **Nematocysts** (pneumatocysts) to sting predators/threats.
- **Taxonomy**:
  - Family/Phylum **Cnidaria (Cnidarians)**.
  - Related organisms: **Jellyfish**, **Sea Anemone**, **Sea Cucumber** (animal, not vegetable).

### 2. Mutualistic / Symbiotic Relationship (Corals + Zooxanthellae)
- **Zooxanthellae**: Autotrophic **algae** living inside coral tissues; contain photosynthetic pigments (chlorophyll + diverse color pigments).
- **Coral provides**: Shelter + Protection (via nematocysts) + Metabolic waste products.
- **Zooxanthellae provides**: Food (via photosynthesis) + Vibrant colors to corals.
- **Ecosystem Output**: Forms **Coral Reefs** = **"Rainforests of the Sea"** (support rich marine biodiversity).

### 3. Conditions Required for Coral Survival
1. **Water Transparency**: High clarity allowing sunlight penetration for algal photosynthesis.
2. **Moderately High Temperature**: **≥25°C** (cannot survive <25°C; sensitive to extreme heat).
3. **Optimal Salinity**: **35–42 g/L (ppk / parts per thousand)** (moderate salinity; intolerant to extreme fresh/salt influx).
4. **Agitated / Circulating Water**: Waves constantly replenish $Ca^{2+}$, $CO_3^{2-}$, and essential nutrients.

### 4. Global Warming Stressors on Corals
- Temperature ↑ → Heat stress on Zooxanthellae.
- Glacier / Ice cap melt → Fresh water influx → Salinity ↓ below 35 ppk.
- Sea Level Rise → Photic zone shifts upward relative to seabed → Benthic corals lose sunlight.
- Ocean Acidification → $CO_3^{2-}$ ↓ → Calcification rate ↓.

### 5. Coral Bleaching Process & Thresholds
- **Stress Phase**: Loss of ~**50%** photosynthetic pigment = **Stressed Corals**.
- **Bleaching Phase**: Loss of >**80%** photosynthetic pigment = **Bleached Corals** (bleaching to death; complete white calcium carbonate exposure).
- **Tipping Point**: Unrecoverable degradation state.
- **Case Studies & Statistics**:
  - **Western Australia (WA)**: Longest/most intense marine heatwave killed corals across **1500 km**.
  - **Great Barrier Reef (Australia)**: Largest reef globally; experiencing worst bleaching event since 2016; passed/passing tipping point.
  - **IPCC / Germanwatch Report (2023) Projections**:
    - **+1.5°C global warming** → **70–90%** tropical/subtropical reef loss.
    - **+2.0°C global warming** → **~100% (Near total) coral loss**.

### 6. Significance & Ecological Role
- **Foundational Species**: Most dominant organism shaping the ecosystem structure (analogous to Mahogany/Ebony in evergreen rainforests or Teak/Sal in deciduous forests).
- **Carbon Sink**: Traps marine carbon into solid $CaCO_3$ polyps.
- **Biodiversity Hotspot**: Coral reefs support **~60% of total ocean biodiversity**.

### 7. Geographical Distribution
- **Global Locations**:
  - **Great Barrier Reef**: Western/Eastern Australia.
  - **Red Sea Corals**: Saudi Arabia.
  - **Apo Reef**: Philippines (South China Sea dispute).
  - **Laccadive Sea**: Indian Ocean.
  - **Maldives**.
  - **Belize & Honduras**: Central America (Honduras contains richest species diversity).
  - **Coral Triangle**: Philippines (North) → Indonesia (West) → Micronesia (East; north of Australia). Holds **40% of global corals**.
- **Indian Locations**:
  - **Islands**: Lakshadweep Islands, Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
  - **Mainland**: Gulf of Mannar (Tamil Nadu), Gulf of Kutch (Gujarat), Netrani Island (Karnataka), Malvan Coast (Maharashtra).
  - **Status**: Most mainland corals (Gulf of Kutch, Gulf of Mannar) are dead.
- **Restoration & Protection Technologies in India**:
  1. **Biorock Technology**: Steel frames connected to low-voltage electric current (reverse electrolysis) → accelerates $CaCO_3$ deposition & coral growth.
  2. **Cemented Artificial Reefs**: Low-cost concrete blocks deployed on seabed (e.g., Chilika Lake) to provide calcium nutrients & attachment substrate.

## Biotic Interactions (Ecology Chapter)

- **Core Principle**: No organism lives in isolation; inter-species interaction is a prerequisite for survival.

![[Biotic Interaction.png|550]]

### Mutualism (Symbiotic Interaction) - (+, +)
- **Type**: **Obligatory** (Species dependent on each other; extinction of one leads to extinction of the other).
- **Examples**:
  1. **Corals + Zooxanthellae**: Coral (Shelter/Protection) ↔ Zooxanthellae (Food/Color).
  2. **Shark + Remora (Sucker Fish)**:
     - *Updated Research Status (April 2024, ResearchGate / Discovery)*: Reclassified from Commensalism to **Mutualism**.
     - Shark provides: Leftover food scraps + Transportation.
     - Remora provides: Eats food trapped in shark **gills** (prevents choking/death) + Protection against gill parasites.
  3. **Pollinating Insects (Honeybee, Butterfly, Bumblebee) + Flowering Plants**:
     - >**80%** of plants reproduce sexually via **Pollination** (Transfer of **Pollen** (male gamete) to **Stigma** (female organ) $\rightarrow$ Egg fusion $\rightarrow$ Seed/Fruit).
     - Plant provides: **Nectar** (food).
     - Insect provides: Cross-pollination service.
  4. **Rhizobium + Leguminous Plants (Pea, Pulses)**:
     - *Rhizobium* = Soil bacteria in roots (*Rhizo* = roots).
     - *Rhizobium* provides: Bio-available **Nitrogen**.
     - Leguminous plant provides: Shelter & carbohydrates.
  5. **Mycorrhiza + Higher Plants**:
     - *Mycorrhiza* = Soil fungus living in roots of tree-capable plants (*Higher plants*).
     - *Mycorrhiza* provides: **Phosphorus**.
     - Plant provides: Shelter & food.

#### *Plant Nutrition & Fertilizer Essentials (Side-Note)*:
- **Nitrogen (N)**: Central constituent of **amino acids**, **proteins** (keratin in hair/nails, ossein in bones, hemoglobin in blood, enzymes like amylase/lysozyme, hormones), and **DNA** (Sugar + Phosphate + Nitrogenous bases).
- **Phosphorus (P)**: Essential for **seed germination**, DNA backbone, and cellular energy storage in **ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate)** synthesized in mitochondria.
- **Potassium (K)**: Controls opening/closing of **stomata** (leaf pores) $\rightarrow$ confers **drought resilience**.
- **Indian Fertilizers**: **Urea** (Nitrogen) | **DAP (Diammonium Phosphate)** (N + P) | **Potash** (Potassium).

### Protocooperation - (+, +)
- **Type**: **Non-obligatory** (Both species benefit, but both can survive independently if separated).
- **Examples**:
  1. **Grazing Animals (Cow, Buffalo, Rhino, Horse) + Myna Bird**:
     - Myna bird sits on animal → Eats parasitic insects (ticks, blood-sucking lice) → Gets food.
     - Grazing animal → Gets protection from parasites.
  2. **Sea Anemone + Hermit Crab**:
     - Sea Anemone (Cnidarian with stinging nematocysts) sits on Hermit Crab shell.
     - Hermit Crab provides: Transportation + Leftover food scraps.
     - Sea Anemone provides: Predator protection via stinging nematocysts.
  3. **Egyptian Plover Bird + Crocodile**:
     - Plover bird flies inside crocodile's mouth → Eats meat/flesh residue between teeth → Gets food (Scavenger).
     - Crocodile → Gets dental cleaning (Crocodile has **21 cycles of teeth regrowth**, so non-obligatory).

### Commensalism - (+, 0)
- **Type**: One species benefited (**+**), the other unaffected (**0**).
- **Examples**:
  1. **Grazing Animals + Wetland / Field Birds (Egret, Heron, Crane)**:
     - Birds walk *behind* grazing animals. Animal hoofs disturb soil/wetland $\rightarrow$ soil becomes porous $\rightarrow$ worms/insects emerge $\rightarrow$ birds feed (**+**).
     - Grazing animals → Unaffected (**0**).
     - *Morphological Adaptations of Wetland Birds*: Thin legs (stand on muddy/watery surface) + Long neck (fetch deeper) + Long beak (probe 1-inch into soil).
  2. **Epiphytes + Host Trees**:
     - Epiphytes (*Epi* = top, *Phyte* = plant) grow on branches/canopy of trees for sunlight.
     - Tree provides: Mechanical support (**+**).
     - Tree receives: Unaffected (**0**).
  3. **Humpback Whale + Barnacles**:
     - Barnacles attach to whale skin → Gain transportation + filter food scraps (**+**).
     - Whale → Unaffected (**0**).
  4. **Apex / Top Predators (Tiger, Lion, Crocodile, Shark) + Scavengers (Hyena, Jackal, Fox, Crow, Vulture)**:
     - Apex predators hunt & kill prey → Scavengers live nearby, wait for predator to leave, eat leftovers (**+**).
     - Apex predator → Unaffected (**0**).
  5. **Cow Dung + Dung Beetle / Maggots**:
     - Insects derive food & shelter from cow dung (**+**).
     - Cow → Unaffected (**0**).
