### Recap: Types of Natural Carbon Sinks
- **Natural Carbon Sink** = Natural locations capturing & storing carbon indefinitely.
- **Green Carbon** = Carbon captured via **photosynthesis** → stored in vegetation & soil (**forests**, **grasslands**).
- **Blue Carbon** = Carbon captured in marine/coastal ecosystems (**mangroves**, **corals**, **kelp**, **seaweed**, **seagrass**).
  - **Kelp** = Underwater forest dominated by algae (*absent in India*).
  - **India's Blue Carbon** = Primarily **seaweed** plantations.
- **Teal Carbon** = Carbon captured by **wetlands** → stored long-term due to slow anaerobic decomposition.

### Carbon Deposits (NOT Sinks)

#### Black Carbon
- **Origin** = Incomplete combustion of **solid or liquid fossil fuels** (diesel vehicles, industrial emissions).
- **Physical Forms** = **Soot**, **smoke**, unburnt hydrocarbon deposits.
- **Optical Physics**:
  - Fundamental colors = **Red, Green, Blue (RGB)** (*Primary colors = Red, Blue, Yellow*).
  - **Black** = Condition where **all light (100% RGB)** is absorbed (*not a true optical color*).
  - Absorbs 100% solar radiation → zero reflection.
- **Environmental & Climate Impacts**:
  - Deposition on ice/snow → lowers **albedo** (reflectivity↓) → radiation absorption↑ → accelerated melting.
  - **Arctic vs. Antarctica**: Arctic ice melting faster due to heavy historical black carbon transport/deposition from industrialized Europe/Asia.
  - **Himalayan Glaciers**: High rate of black carbon deposition from vehicular emissions (diesel exhaust) → rapid glacial retreat.
  - **Atmospheric Effects**: Soot in clouds absorbs sunlight → cloud warming → disruption of cloud dynamics & rainfall patterns.
- **Case Study / Movement**:
  - **Sonam Wangchuk** (Ladakh activist, scientist, inventor) → **Climate Fast** against heavy expressways/highways & diesel vehicular tourism in fragile Ladakh ecology.
  - **Policy Demand**: Promotion of **Eco-tourism** over mass diesel-driven tourism.

#### Brown Carbon
- **Origin** = Incomplete combustion of **solid or liquid biomass** (wood, cow dung, crop residues).
- **Optical Physics**:
  - Completely absorbs **Blue light**; reflects **Red + Green light** ($Red + Green = Brown$).
  - Absorbs **~70%** of solar radiation.
- **Classification Anchor**: Both Black & Brown carbon are **carbon deposits** (pollutants/traps), NOT **carbon sinks**.

### Artificial Carbon Sinks (Carbon Capture & Sequestration / CCS)
- **Concept** = Man-made/engineered locations and technologies capturing excess atmospheric $CO_2$ and storing it indefinitely.
- **3 Primary Technical Categories**:
  1. **Oceanic Sequestration**
  2. **Terrestrial Sequestration**
  3. **Geological Sequestration**

---

### Oceanic Sequestration
- **Concept** = Capturing excess $CO_2$ artificially and injecting/storing it within oceanic waters (largest carbon reservoir).

#### Ocean Fertilization (Ocean $CO_2$ Fertilization)
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Iron ($Fe$ / Iron Oxide)** = Critical limiting micronutrient for marine producers (**phytoplankton**, **algae**, **seaweed**).
  - Iron dosing → phytoplankton/seaweed bloom → photosynthesis rate↑ → atmospheric $CO_2$ converted into marine biomass.
  - Harvested/dead biomass sinks to ocean bottom (**abyssal zone**) → low water circulation → carbon locked indefinitely.
- **Case Study: LOHA-EX (2009)**:
  - **Origin**: Joint proposal by **India** (2009) with **Germany**.
  - **Etymology**: "*Loha*" = Iron (Hindi/Sanskrit) + "*EX*" = Experiment (Iron Ocean Fertilization Experiment).
  - **Location**: Off the coast of **Chile** (South Pacific Ocean; low productivity marine patch).
  - **Objective**: Supplement iron oxide to induce seaweed/phytoplankton bloom → sink captured carbon biomass to benthic floor.
  - **International Controversy & Cancellation**:
    - Protests by international environmental NGOs & marine scientists.
    - **Concerns**: Disruption of natural marine food webs, zooplankton attraction, altered migratory routes, and altered global ocean biodiversity.
    - **Outcome**: **Germany withdrew in 2011** under severe criticism. India later conducted small-scale, controlled trials domestically.

### Terrestrial Sequestration
- **Concept** = Capturing excess carbon and storing it within terrestrial soil, biomass, or land ecosystems.

#### 1. $CO_2$ Fertilization & Desert Blooming
- Elevated atmospheric $CO_2$ → photosynthetic velocity↑ → plant biomass growth↑ in arid/semi-arid regions.

#### 2. Wetland Restoration
- **Soil Physics**:
  - Normal Topsoil: Pores filled with air → high oxygen channelization → rapid aerobic bacterial decomposition.
  - Wetland Soil: Pores filled with water (**Soil Hydration > Soil Aeration**) → anaerobic conditions → microbial decomposition halts/slows drastically.
- **Carbon Retention Value**: Organic matter remains locked indefinitely without releasing $CO_2$ or $CH_4$.
- **Historical Evidence**:
  - **Mummies**: Oldest human mummies discovered in **South American marshes** (anaerobic preservation), not Egypt.
  - **Mammoths**: Intact Woolly Mammoth remains preserved in **Siberian permafrost**.
- **Statistics & Policy Warning**:
  - **Global Wetland Outlook Report**: **>30%** of global wetlands degraded or disappeared since **1970** (due to land encroachment, urban expansion, and climate change).
  - Wetland destruction → oxygen re-entry → rapid bacterial action → massive release of stored $CO_2$ & $CH_4$.

#### 3. Zero Tillage Farming & Conservation Farming
- **Mechanized Tilling Impact**:
  - Tractor + Tiller flips topsoil with bottom soil → exposes buried organic matter to air → bacterial decay → release of trapped $CO_2$ & $CH_4$ + rapid soil moisture evaporation.
- **Conservation Farming (Climate-Smart Agriculture)**:
  - **Component 1: Zero Tillage**:
    - Direct seed sowing without tilling soil using **Happy Seeder** or **Turbo Seeder**.
    - Topsoil undisturbed → sub-surface organic carbon locked.
  - **Component 2: Mulching**:
    - Retaining crop leftovers/stubble on field surface (never removed/burned).
    - *Organic Mulch*: Crop residues decompose slowly → natural nutrient release + thermal insulation layer → soil moisture evaporation↓.
    - *Fungal Inoculants*: **Pusa Decomposer** (fungal consortium) accelerates stubble breakdown in **20–25 days**. Prevents stubble burning in Punjab, Haryana, UP.
    - *Plastic / Fiber Mulch*: Black plastic sheet isolates moisture + solar heating kills soil weeds/herbs.
  - **Component 3: Crop Rotation (3-Crop Cycle)**:
    - Alternating **Kharif**, **Rabi**, and **Zaid** crops.
    - Integration of **leguminous crops** (symbiotic *Rhizobium* bacteria in root nodules fix atmospheric $N_2$) → restores soil nitrogen naturally → chemical urea requirement↓.
- **Policy & Economic Impact**:
  - Government Scheme: **Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)**.
  - Initial Projection: **+20% farmer income** (via savings on tractor fuel & chemical fertilizers).
  - Field Case Study (Madhya Pradesh millet farmers): Farmer income increased by up to **150%**.

#### 4. Biochar Burial
- **Pyrolysis Physics**:
  - Thermal breakdown of solid biomass in the **absence/lack of oxygen ($O_2$)**.
  - Wood → Pyrolysis → **Charcoal** (burns with minimal smoke & low volatile organic compound / VOC eye irritants).
- **Biochar Definition**: Umbrella term for any solid biomass (wood, cow dung, agricultural stubble) processed via pyrolysis.
- **GOBARdhan Scheme (2019)**:
  - Ministry: **Ministry of Jal Shakti** (formerly Water Resources).
  - Full Form: **Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan**.
  - Objective: Rural waste-to-wealth / waste-to-energy conversion.
  - Dual Pathways:
    1. *Biogas*: High water dependency (impractical for water-scarce non-perennial Indian villages).
    2. *Biochar*: Non-water intensive → universally scalable across Indian villages.
- **3 Functions of Biochar**:
  1. Clean rural cooking fuel (smokeless replacement for raw wood/dung).
  2. Soil bio-fertilizer / humus substitute (rich in carbon, phosphorus, essential micro-nutrients).
  3. **Biochar Burial**: Burying excess biochar deep in soil → permanent terrestrial carbon sequestration.
- **Power Sector Integration**:
  - Scheme: **SAMARTH Initiative** (**Ministry of Power**).
  - Full Form: **Sustainable Agrarian Mission for use of Agro-residues in Thermal Power Plants**.
  - Objective: Co-firing agricultural crop residues & biochar in coal thermal power plants → reduces coal reliance & stubble burning.

### Geological Sequestration
- **Concept** = Capturing excess $CO_2$ and injecting/trapping it deep into the Earth's interior (deep crust/bedrock).

#### 1. Mineral Trapping
- **Mechanism**:
  - Injecting $CO_2$ into reactive basaltic/silicate rock formations.
  - Reaction: $CO_2$ + Metal Oxides (in **Basalt**, **Feldspar**, **Calcite**, **Dolomite**, **Ankerite**) → solid **Metal Carbonates** ($CaCO_3$, $MgCO_3$).
- **Features**: Permanent, highly stable chemical binding.
- **Real-World Examples**: **Project Orca** & **Mammoth Plant** (**CarbFix**, Iceland).

#### 2. Solubility Trapping
- **Mechanism**: Dissolving captured $CO_2$ into subsurface water/groundwater aquifers to form carbonic acid ($H_2CO_3$).
- **Feasibility & Country Analysis**:
  - **India**: **Unfeasible / Disastrous**. India is the world's largest consumer/extractor of groundwater; acidifying subsurface aquifers ruins drinking water supply.
  - **Glacial / Surface Water Countries**: Highly feasible for **Russia** (Lake Baikal holds world's largest liquid surface freshwater), **Canada**, **USA (Alaska)**, where reliance on groundwater for drinking is minimal.

#### 3. Hydrodynamic Trapping
- **Mechanism**: Injecting captured $CO_2$ into deep underground non-potable geological structures:
  1. **Deep Saline Formations / Aquifers**: Deep saltwater strata unfit for human consumption (e.g., Gujarat saline aquifers).
  2. **Depleted Oil & Gas Reservoirs**: Vacant subterranean hydrocarbon pockets.
  3. **Unminable Coal Seams / Coal Beds**: Deep coal deposits inaccessible for mining; $CO_2$ adsorbs onto coal matrix.
- **Suitability for India**: Highly viable due to extensive deep saline aquifers & unminable coal beds without affecting drinking water.

### Solar Geoengineering & Climate Engineering Summary
- **Ocean Fertilization**: Iron seeding → phytoplankton bloom → abyssal biomass sinking.
- **Marine Cloud Brightening**: Spraying sea-salt/sulfate aerosols into low marine clouds → cloud albedo↑.
- **Cirrus Cloud Thinning**: Thinning high-altitude **Cirrus clouds** (tropopause water-crystal clouds trapping outgoing longwave IR) → heat escapes to space.
- **Stratospheric Aerosol Injection (SAI)**: Injecting sulfate or silicate aerosols into stratosphere → reflects solar radiation.
- **BECCS / Biochar Burial**: Biomass pyrolysis → soil carbon burial.
- **Crop Albedo Modification**: Breeding crop varieties with higher solar reflectivity.
- **Desert Albedo Management**: Countering land heat absorption via afforestation along desert fringes.

### Concluding Insights
- **Natural Sinks Limitations**: Natural carbon sinks operate too slowly to counteract historic atmospheric $CO_2$ build-up independently.
- **Human-Assisted Technology**: Artificial geoengineering & human-assisted restoration (**Biorock / Cemented Artificial Reefs** for Gulf of Mannar corals) are required alongside natural sinks.
