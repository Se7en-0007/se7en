## Greenhouse Effect Physics & Radiative Forcing

- **Solar Radiation Flow**: Sun's radiation enters Earth's atmosphere → hits surface → Earth absorbs energy & re-emits as **longwave infrared light**.
- **Longwave Infrared** = **Low energy infrared light** (long wavelength = low frequency / low energy).
- **Reflection Mechanisms**:
  - Outer atmosphere reflects portion back to space.
  - Upper atmosphere clouds reflect light back to space.
- **IR Escape vs. Trapping**:
  - Portion passes directly through atmosphere to space.
  - Majority captured & retained by **Greenhouse Gases (GHGs)**.
- **Global Warming Sequence**: High atmospheric GHG concentration → Traps IR light → **Atmospheric temp ↑** → **Surface temp ↑** → **Hydrosphere/Ocean temp ↑** → **Global Warming**.
- **Radiative Forcing Concept**:
  - **Natural GHG Effect**: Naturally occurring gases (**CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, H₂O**) trap minimal heat → Maintains Earth's ambient/optimal temperature for life.
    - *Without GHGs*: Earth = "Ice ball" (extreme cold).
    - *Energy Balance*: Escaping heat > Reflected/trapped heat.
  - **Human-Induced / Enhanced GHG Effect**: Anthropogenic GHG emissions ↑ → Escaping heat ↓ + Redirected back heat ↑ → Planet overheating.

## Classification of Greenhouse Gases: Natural vs. Anthropogenic

| Parameter | Naturally Occurring GHGs | Anthropogenic / Man-Made GHGs |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Examples** | **CO₂**, **CH₄**, **N₂O**, **O₃**, **H₂O vapor**, **SO₂**, **NH₃** | **HFC**, **PFC**, **SF₆**, **NF₃**, **CFC**, **HCFC** |
| **Origin** | Natural biogeochemical processes (even without humans) | Industrial / Synthetic chemical synthesis |
| **Nature's Waste Principle** | Nature produces **zero waste** (waste of one organism = resource/nutrient for another) | Man-made synthetic gases = True waste (no natural organism/process absorbs them) |
| **Absorption Mechanism** | Nature possesses natural absorption sinks (e.g., **CO₂** absorbed via **photosynthesis**) | Zero natural absorption mechanism in nature (1 molecule released persists indefinitely) |
| **Management Solution** | **Reduce emissions** (nature balances rest) | **Complete elimination** of production/release |

> **Key Distinction**: **N₂ (Nitrogen gas)** makes up 78% of atmosphere and is **NOT** a GHG. **N₂O (Nitrous oxide)** is a potent GHG.

## Scientific Rule of Thumb for GHGs: Triatomic & Above

- **Common Structural Pattern**: **CO₂**, **N₂O**, **O₃**, **SO₂**, **H₂O** are all **triatomic** (composed of 3 atoms).
  - CO₂ = 1C + 2O = 3 atoms
  - N₂O = 2N + 1O = 3 atoms
  - O₃ = 3O = 3 atoms
  - SO₂ = 1S + 2O = 3 atoms
  - H₂O = 2H + 1O = 3 atoms
- **Universal Rule of Thumb**: **All gases on Earth composed of 3 or more atoms (triatomic and above) possess the molecular structure required to trap infrared light and act as greenhouse gases.**
- **Application to Other GHGs**:
  - **NF₃ (Nitrogen trifluoride)** → 4 atoms → GHG.
  - **SF₆ (Sulfur hexafluoride)** → 7 atoms → GHG.
  - **HFC, PFC, CFC, HCFC** → 3+ atoms → GHGs.

## GHGs Not Treated as Primary Concerns (Short Shelf Life)

### 1. Water Vapor (H₂O)

- **Status**: Highly potent GHG, but **NOT** considered a dangerous GHG of major concern.
- **Reason**: Extremely short shelf life / atmospheric residence time (a few hours).
- **Cycle**: Water evaporates → Vapor rises → Altitude ↑ → Air cools → Condensation into liquid droplets → Precipitates as rain.

### 2. Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)

- **Status**: GHG, but **NOT** a major threat due to short shelf life.
- **Chemical Mechanism**:
  - SO₂ + O → (Sunlight) SO₃ (Sulfur trioxide: water-soluble acidic gas).
  - SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄ (Sulfuric acid).
  - Dissolves in atmospheric moisture → Precipitates back to Earth as **acid rain**.
- **Chemical Nomenclature Rule**: Suffix **"-ate"** (sulfate, nitrate, phosphate, citrate) indicates presence of saturated oxygen.

### 3. Ammonia (NH₃)

- **Status**: GHG, but **NOT** a major concern due to short shelf life.
- **Mechanism**: Soil bacteria undergo **nitrification** → Breaks down NH₃ → **Nitrites (NO₂⁻)** & **Nitrates (NO₃⁻)** → Adds nutrition to soil.

## GHGs Banned Under Montreal Protocol (Ozone Depleting Substances)

- **CFCs (Chlorofluorocarbons)** & **HCFCs (Hydrochlorofluorocarbons)**:
  - Both are GHGs, but **NOT** active GHG targets today because they were banned earlier for **Ozone Depleting Properties**.
- **Ozone Layer & Antarctica History**:
  - **Antarctica Status**: Global common governed by **Antarctica Treaty of 1958** (military/weapons banned; scientific research & international collaboration permitted).
  - **1983 Discovery**: European & American scientists in Antarctica discovered the **Ozone Hole** in the stratosphere.
  - **Significance of Ozone (O₃)**: Shields Earth from lethal **Ultraviolet-C (UV-C)** radiation.
  - **Depletion Impact**: Extinction-level event (destroys all life: humans, plants, microorganisms, bacteria).
  - **1983 Cause**: Anthropogenic **Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS)** (not volcanic eruptions).
- **International Treaties**:
  - **Vienna Convention (1985)**: Signed 1985, enforced 1987 → Objective: Protect the Ozone Layer.
  - **Montreal Protocol (1987)**: Protocol under Vienna Convention; adopted/enforced **Jan 1, 1989**.
  - **Provision**: Global ban on production, use, and distribution of all **Ozone Depleting Substances (ODS)** including CFCs and HCFCs.

## HFCs, Industrial Codes & Refrigerant Alternatives

### Transition History

- **Refrigerants Use**: CFCs & HCFCs used as heat-exchange refrigerants in Air Conditioners (ACs) and refrigerators.
- **India Ban**: India phased out CFC/HCFC production in **2000**.
- **Replacement**: **HFCs (Hydrofluorocarbons)** replaced CFCs/HCFCs.
  - *Advantage*: HFC is **NOT** an ozone-depleting substance.
  - *Disadvantage*: HFC is an **extremely potent GHG** with high GWP.

### HFC Industrial Codes & Consumer Guidance

- **HFC Complexity**: Family of **19 different gases** (hydro-fluoro-carbon configurations).
- **Industrial Refrigerant Codes to Avoid** (All HFCs):
  - **R134A** → HFC code.
  - **R410A** → DFM (**Difluoromethane**).
  - **R440A** → HFC code.
- **Better Environmental Alternatives** (Shorter shelf life, lower climate impact):
  - **R32** → Ammonia / HFC-32 (better choice).
  - **R290A** → Isopropane.
  - **R600A** → Cyclopentane.

## Sources of Carbon Dioxide (CO₂)

### Biomass Physics & Decomposition Science

- **Biomass Definition** = Total dry weight of an organism = Total weight - Water weight.
  - Example: Human body ≈ 70% water by weight (100 kg person - 70 kg water = 30 kg biomass).
- **Elemental Composition**: Carbon (C), Hydrogen (H), Oxygen (O), Nitrogen (N), Sulfur (S).
- **Decomposers**: **Bacteria**, **Fungi**, **Slime Molds** (*Viruses are NOT decomposers*).
- **Aerobic Decomposition** (Above ground, O₂ present, sunlight present):
  - Fast process.
  - C + O₂ → CO₂
  - H + O → H₂O
  - N → Nitrite → Nitrate
  - S → SO₂

### Natural Sources of CO₂

- Aerobic degradation of biomass.
- Natural respiration of organisms (both **animals AND plants** undergo respiration; inhale O₂, exhale CO₂).
- Forest fires & agricultural stubble burning (**Punjab** reports highest stubble burning cases in North India).
- Volcanic eruptions.

### Anthropogenic / Developmental Sources of CO₂

- **Core Principle**: All developmental activities depend on carbon-based fuels (coal, petrol, diesel, kerosene, natural gas).
- **Cement Manufacturing**:
  - Limestone (CaCO₃, calcium carbonate) heated to 1,500°C - 3,000°C using coal/coke.
  - Chemical Reaction: CaCO₃ → {Heat} CaO (Quick lime) + CO₂ ↑
  - Coal combustion emits additional massive CO₂.
- **Power Sector & Steel Industry**:
  - Nearly **70% of electricity generation in India** comes from coal.
  - Iron manufacturing: Iron ore (Hematite) processed in coal-fired blast furnaces at 3,000°C.
- **Electric Vehicles (EVs) Con**:
  - Zero tailpipe emissions, BUT manufacturing & battery charging rely on 70% coal grid.
  - Lithium-ion battery mining releases trapped CO₂ & CH₄ from topsoil/rocks during extraction.

### Concept of Decoupling (21st Century Development Strategy)

- **Traditional View (20th Century)**: Development & CO₂ emissions were coupled (↑ Development → ↑ CO₂ emissions).
- **Decoupling Definition** = Unlinking economic growth/development from CO₂ emissions (↑ Development → ↓ CO₂ emissions).
- **Decoupling Industry Trends**:
  - **Green Steel**: Steel produced using electric arc furnaces powered by solar/renewable energy instead of coal-fired blast furnaces.
  - **Green Cement**: Cement produced using electric furnaces + Carbon Capture & Storage (CCS) + compensatory afforestation.

## Sources of Methane (CH₄)

### Anaerobic Decomposition Physics

- **Anaerobic Decomposition** (Buried underground, absence of O₂ and sunlight):
  - Slow process (accelerated by high temperature & humidity).
  - C → CH₄ (Methane)
  - H + O → H₂O
  - N → NH₃ (Ammonia)
  - S → H₂S (Hydrogen sulfide)

### Natural Sources of Methane

- **Wetlands**: Lands where **soil hydration > soil aeration** (water saturates all soil pores → displaces air/oxygen → anaerobic decomposition → CH₄ release).
- Volcanic eruptions.

### Anthropogenic Sources of Methane

- **Mining Activities**: Drilling/cutting into soil/coal/rocks releases trapped methane.
- **Fossil Fuels**: Natural gas extraction & pipe leakages.
- **Legacy Waste Sites (Landfills)**:
  - Municipalities empowered by **73rd & 74th Constitutional Amendments** for waste management.
  - Lack of infrastructure → Waste dumped outside cities year after year → Builds waste mountains (e.g., Delhi's 3 major legacy waste sites bordering UP & Haryana).
  - Surface waste → Aerobic (CO₂); Deep inner waste → Anaerobic (CH₄).

### UN FAO 2023 Report Statistics On Anthropogenic Methane

- **41% Total from Agriculture & Dairy**:
  - **Dairy Sector (Livestock)** = 33%
  - **Paddy / Rice Cultivation** = 8%
- **Legacy Waste Sites** = 20%

### Dairy or Livestock Methane Emission Mechanism

- **Con**: India = World's largest milk producer, highest livestock population.
- **Bovines / Cattle** = **Ruminant Cattle**.
- **Stomach Structure**: **4-chambered stomach** (unlike single-chambered human stomach). First chamber = **Rumen**.
- **Digestion Process**:
  - Cow swallows food whole without biting → Enters **Rumen**.
  - Rumen contains **Methanogenic Bacteria** (*Methanospirillum*, *Methanobacterium*, *Methanococcus*).
  - Bacteria break down food → Produces methane + partially digested food called **cud**.
  - **Belching / Cud Chewing**: Cow pushes cud back to mouth to chew → Releases large volumes of CH₄.
  - *Biological Necessity*: Without methane release, cow suffers severe abdominal bloating/swelling (life-threatening).

### Paddy or Rice Cultivation Methane Mechanism

- **Traditional Method**: **Transplantation of Rice (TPR)**.
  - Rice saplings planted in fields continuously flooded with standing water.
  - Flooded field acts as artificial wetland → Anaerobic decomposition of soil biomass → CH₄ release.

## Methane Control & Mitigation Strategies

### 1. Legacy Waste Sites

- **Solution**: **Land Reclamation** (reclaiming landfill sites & technological waste processing).

### 2. Paddy Cultivation: Direct Seeding of Rice (DSR) vs. TPR

- **TPR (Transplantation of Rice)** → Requires standing water → High CH₄ emissions + high water consumption.
- **DSR (Direct Seeding of Rice)** → Uses tractor-operated steel DSR seeder machines:
  - Seeds planted directly in soil; machine creates small grooves/channels.
  - Water supplied **only inside grooves** (no field flooding / no stagnant standing water).
- **DSR Impact**:
  - Water requirement reduced by **up to 80%**.
  - Methane emissions reduced by **up to 80%**.
  - Rice yield & quality remain completely unaffected.
