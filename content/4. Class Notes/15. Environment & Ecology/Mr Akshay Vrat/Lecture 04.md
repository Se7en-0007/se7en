## Recap & Core Carbon Concepts

### Carbon Footprint & Life Cycle Assessment
- **Carbon Footprint = Total GHG emitted** by person / product / factory / company / industry / country → deduced in **tons of CO₂ equivalent (CO₂e)** ($GHG \times GWP$).
- **Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)** = Method to assess total carbon emissions produced by a product across its entire lifespan.
- **Fugitive Emissions** = Unnoticed or unmeasured carbon emissions excluded during LCA.
  - *Examples*: Supply chain transport emissions, truck delivering urea/pesticides to shopkeeper, personal vehicle used to travel to purchase urea.

### Carbon Intensity
- **Carbon Intensity = Goods & services produced ÷ 1 ton CO₂ released**.
- **Carbon Footprint vs Carbon Intensity**:
  - **Carbon Footprint** → Calculates total volume of GHG emissions released.
  - **Carbon Intensity** → Calculates economic output (goods/services) generated per 1 ton of CO₂ emitted.
- **Comparison Example**:
  - *Manufacturer A*: Produces 1 ton plastic resin per 1 ton CO₂ emitted → **Higher carbon intensity**.
  - *Manufacturer B*: Produces 2 tons plastic resin per 1 ton CO₂ emitted → **Lower carbon intensity**.

## Tipping Point

### Definition & Mechanism
- **Tipping Point = Critical threshold** reached when regional climate shifts permanently from **one stable state → another stable state**.
- Characterized as **"Point of no return"** → Irreversible environmental shift.

### Case Study: Amazon Rainforest
- **Ideal Characteristics**: Hot & humid equatorial vegetation → daily rainfall throughout year → **4 p.m. showers** (cyclical hydrological flow: daytime evaporation/vaporization → evening condensation → 4 p.m. precipitation).
  - Dense canopy (ground invisible) + holds highest volume of river water on Earth + **"Lungs of Planet Earth"** (high O₂ release & CO₂ absorption; alongside **Congo Basin**).
- **Current Reality**: River water levels dipping (murky small streams) → severe droughts & rampant fires.
  - **Drought years**: 2023, 2024, 2025 (record-breaking consecutive droughts).
  - **Forest fire years**: 2018, 2019 (US Air Force deployed), 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024.
- **Deforestation Rankings (Past 10 Years - Utility Bidders Report)**:
  1. **Brazil** → Highest deforestation (clearing land for soya bean fields & livestock feeding pastures for milk/meat).
  2. **Indonesia** → 2nd highest (clearing land for palm oil plantation/cultivation).
  3. **India** → 3rd highest (clearing land for population settlement / non-forest activity / concretization).
- **Transformation Outcome**: Amazon shifting from **Rainforest → Savanna-like / Tropical Grassland / Deciduous Forest**.
- **Tipping Point Timeline**: Projected between **2030 and 2050** (*The Guardian* / Google AI report: **by 2050**).

### Global Tipping Points & Temperature Thresholds (PMF Map)
- **1.5°C – 2.0°C Increase (Red Zone)**:
  - Boreal permafrost melting (Siberia/Canada; Boreal = largest forest area on Earth).
  - Greenland ice sheet loss.
  - Barents Sea ice loss.
  - Low-latitude coral reef collapse (Indonesia = 40% of global corals).
  - West Antarctic ice sheet collapse.
  - *Current Status*: Earth average temperature at **+1.2°C**; **2024 recorded 80 days breaching +1.5°C**.
- **2.0°C – 3.7°C Increase (Orange Zone)**: Amazon rainforest tipping point, Eastern Antarctic subglacial basins.
- **3.7°C – 6.0°C Increase (Yellow Zone)**: Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (**AMOC**) distortion.
- **> 6.0°C Increase**: Complete collapse of almost all ecosystems, East Antarctic ice sheet lost, Arctic winter sea ice collapse.
- **Great Barrier Reef (Australia)**: World's largest coral reef system → Already crossed tipping point once (irreversible coral loss).

## Climate Forcing & Radiative Forcing

### Climate Forcing
- **Climate Forcing** = All natural or artificial factors that increase or decrease climate change impacts.
- **Positive Climate Forcing Factors** (accelerate climate change): ↑ GHG emissions, ↑ Deforestation, ↑ Fossil fuel consumption, ↑ Coal power without carbon capture, ↑ **Gray cement / Gray steel** (Gray = releases carbon; Green = carbon capture / low footprint).
- **Negative Climate Forcing Factors** (mitigate climate change): Renewable energy, afforestation, public transit over personal vehicles, ecotourism policies.

### Radiative Forcing Physics
- **Core Cause of Climate Change** = Trapping of **Infrared (IR) Radiation**.
- **Earth Radiation Budget**:
  - **Earth Albedo = 0.35** (35% reflected, 65% absorbed). Perfect black body = 0, perfect white body = 1.
- **Radiative Forcing Definition** = Difference between incoming solar radiation received and outgoing radiation reflected/irradiated back into space.
- **High Radiative Forcing** = More radiation reflected back to space → Narrow gap between received & reflected → Cooling / reduces climate change.
- **Low Radiative Forcing** = Less radiation reflected, more absorbed → Warming.
- **Direct Inducers**: Clouds, **Aerosols** (sulfates, nitrates) suspended in air → reflect sunlight directly.

### Stratospheric Aerosol Forcing / Injection (SAI)
- **Solar Geoengineering / Climate Engineering** = Technological manipulation of solar radiation and Earth's thermal budget.
- **Inspiration**: **Haiti Volcanic Eruption** (earthquake triggered dormant volcano):
  - Dust, ash, fly ash, black carbon released → blocked sunlight → surface temp dropped by **2–3°C** → air cooled/denser → high pressure vacuum pulled surrounding air → massive rainfall.
- **Atmospheric Layers Context**:
  - Troposphere (0–12 km): All weather events & cumulonimbus clouds occur here.
  - Stratosphere, Mesosphere, Thermosphere (includes Ionosphere), Exosphere.
- **SAI Mechanism**: Inject colloidal **sulfate particles** (or nitrates) into **Stratosphere** (bypassing Troposphere to avoid weather disruption) → acts as aerosols → increases Earth's radiative forcing (reflects sunlight) → cools planet.
- **Real-world Testing**:
  - **Texas, USA**: Sulfate particle sprinkling during farmer sowing season to manage heatwaves.
  - **Technology**: Boeing + University of Chicago developed prototype aircraft capable of Stratospheric flight & aerosol sprinkling.
- **Drawbacks & AI Model Warnings**:
  - Sky color shift: Blue sky turns **white-gray**.
  - Climate disruption: Severe monsoon flooding in India (e.g., Uttarakhand / Himachal Pradesh); severe droughts in China.
  - High economic & logistical cost of maintaining planetary aircraft fleet.

## Social Cost of Carbon (SCC)
- **Social Cost of Carbon (SCC)** = Economic cost (loss in GDP) resulting from emitting one additional ton of carbon dioxide (CO₂).

---

## Carbon Handprint
- **Carbon Handprint** = Amount of GHG emissions avoided or reduced compared to alternative options through better decisions (personal), policies (political), or initiatives (national).
- **Analogy**: Footprint = kicking carbon out into atmosphere; Handprint = catching/stopping carbon.
- **Example: Delhi Metro**:
  - Color-coded network (Blue, Red, Green, Magenta, Pink, Gray, Orange/Airport line).
  - End-to-end trip saves **~700 g CO₂ per individual** compared to personal vehicle travel.

## Ecological Footprint & Water Footprint

### Ecological Footprint
- **Definition** = Total demand on Earth's land resources required to support the lifestyle of a person, product, or service.
- **Calculation Methodology**: Individual lifestyle score multiplied by total global population (**~8 billion**).
  - Score = 1.5 → requires **1.5x Earth's total land resources** (unsustainable).
  - Score = 0.5 → requires **0.5x Earth's land resources** (sustainable).
  - **Average American Ecological Footprint** = **2.5** (requires 2.5 Earths).
  - **Global Average Ecological Footprint (WWF 2007)** = **1.5 – 1.75**.

### Overshoot Day
- **Definition** = Calendar date when humanity's resource consumption for the year exceeds Earth's capacity to regenerate those resources in that year.
- **Trend**: Earth Overshoot Day 2024 = **August 1, 2024**; 2025 = **July 24, 2025** (shifts ~1 week earlier each year toward January).
- **Originating Body**: **Club of Rome** (Global NGO network / think tank focusing on planetary limits & resource crises).

### Water Footprint
- **Definition** = Total volume of water resources required to produce all goods and services consumed by a person, product, factory, or company.

## Impacts of Global Warming

### Primary Physical Chain
- **1. Increase in Average Global Temperature**: Current +1.2°C (2024 had 80 days > 1.5°C). Projections: +1.5°C by 2030, +2.0°C by 2050, +2.5°C – 3.0°C by 2100.
- **2. Melting of Ice Caps & Glaciers**:
  - **Thwaites Glacier (Antarctica)** = Largest glacier on Earth, nicknamed **"Doomsday Glacier"**. Breaking into icebergs; collapse inevitable above +3°C.
- **3. Rise in Sea Water Level**: IPCC projects **1 m to 7 m sea level rise** under current melting trends.
- **4. Submergence of Coastal Areas & Islands**:
  - **SIDS (Small Island Developing States)**: Low-lying island nations (1–5 ft elevation, e.g., Male City, Maldives @ 5 ft). Highly vulnerable.
  - **Case Study 1: Tuvalu (Pacific Ocean)**:
    - Shrinkage visible in satellite imagery (1973–2015).
    - Evacuation: In 2015–2017, 7,000 out of 11,000 citizens (~70%) evacuated & resettled in Fiji. Current population ~3,000.
    - **UN Climate Change Refugees**: Officially recognized at **2017 UNFCCC COP (Bonn, under Fiji chairmanship)** as world's first **Climate Change Refugees**.
    - Adaptation: Purchasing land on Australian coast; creating a **Virtual Reality (VR) film** of the nation to preserve cultural memory.
  - **Other SIDS**:
    - *Pacific*: Nauru, Samoa, Palau, Fiji, Vanuatu, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Marshall Islands.
    - *Indian Ocean*: Maldives, Chagos Islands, Mauritius, Seychelles.
    - *Atlantic*: Belize, Bahamas, Trinidad & Tobago.
  - **Case Study 2: Carteret Island (Papua New Guinea)**: Submerged in 2010; population moved to Bougainville Island (internal relocation within PNG → NOT classified as international climate refugees).
  - **Case Study 3: Guna Yala Island (Panama, Atlantic coast)**: Submerged in 2024.

### Socio-Economic & Governance Chain
- **5. Mass Migration (Humans & Animals)** →
- **6. Increased Population Pressure on Available Land** + **Man-Animal Conflict** →
- **7. Resource Crisis** (Land, Food, Water) →
- **8. Food Inflation** →
- **9. Instability of Governance / Political Systems**.

### Geopolitical Case Study: Arab Spring (2009–2010)
- **Causal Sequence**:
  - 2007: India (world's top wheat producer) banned wheat exports to stabilize domestic prices →
  - Global wheat supply shortfall (Brazil could not fill gap) →
  - International wheat prices surged →
  - Severe food inflation in desert-dependent import nations (Tunisia) by 2009 →
  - Peaceful public protests in Dec 2009 →
  - Tunisian government deployed Army to suppress hawker/protesters → Hawker death triggered violent escalation →
  - Shift from food price protest → Pro-democracy revolution to eliminate monarchy/dictatorship (**Arab Spring**: Tunisia → Libya, Algeria, Egypt, Syria).

### Domestic Case Study: Agrarian Reservation Protests in India
- **Affected Communities**: Maratha (MH), Patidar (GJ), Jat (HR), Gujjar/Meena (RJ), Andhra, UP (landowning agrarian groups).
- **Root Cause**: Climate change → Decreased agricultural productivity & crop yields → Falling farm income + rising living inflation → Farmers seeking state job reservations for socioeconomic security.
- **Government Interventions**: Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana, National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA), Rashtriya Krishi Vikas Yojana, 80 crore people provided free food grain ration.
