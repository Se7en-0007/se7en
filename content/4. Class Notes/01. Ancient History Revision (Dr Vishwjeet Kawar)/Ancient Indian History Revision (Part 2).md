## 6th Century BC (India): Socio-Economic Transformations

### Economic Transformation & Technological Drivers
- **Iron Technology Expansion**: Use of **iron plowshare** restricted to **Ganga Valley** & **Cauvery Valley** (*NOT applied pan-India*).
- **Agricultural Impact**: Clearing dense Gangetic forests + iron plowshare → **Surplus food grain production**.
- **Urbanization Milestone**: Surplus food → Emergence of trade & towns (**Second Urbanization** in Gangetic Valley) → Most urban centers functioned as capitals of **Mahajanapadas**.
- **Urban Sanitary Feature**: **Ring Wells** = Rows of earthenware pots stacked vertically in individual urban houses for drainage/waste.
- **Trade Structure & Guilds**:
  - **Shrenis / Guilds** = Autonomous associations of artisans, craftsmen and merchants.
  - **Functions**: Provided technical training, procured raw materials, distributed finished goods, organized trade routes, acted as **banks** (deposits & loans). Autonomous rules (*king did not interfere*).
  - **Pottery Specialization**: **Northern Black Polished Ware (NBPW)** = Fine, glossy black, metallic-looking luxury ceramic used as tableware by wealthy classes.
- **Coinage System (First Metal Money in India)**:
  - **Type**: **Punch-Marked Coins** (punched symbols, non-inscribed).
  - **Metals**: Mainly **Silver** & **Copper** (*NO Gold coins*).
  - **Authority**: Issued primarily by kings/rulers, but also by **rich merchants / traders**.
  - **Absence**: **NO name** or **image of king**; **NO written inscriptions**.
- **Rural Social Stratification (Agrarian Hierarchy)**:
  - **North India**:
    1. **Gramabhojaka / Grahapati / Gahapati** = Largest landowner, village headman (*hereditary post, collected taxes, maintained law & order*).
    2. **Kamagaras** = Landless agricultural laborers.
    3. **Dasas** = Slaves.
  - **South India (Tamil Region)**:
    1. **Vellalar** = Large landowners.
    2. **Uzhavar** = Small plowers / farmers.
    3. **Kadaisiyar** = Landless agricultural laborers.
    4. **Adimai** = Slaves.

### Social Stratification & Legal Texts
- **Increased Rigidity & Patriarchy**: Brahmanical reaction to social mobility → Compilation of law books (**Dharmasutras** & **Dharmashastras**) → Subordination of women & lower Varnas.
- **Varna vs. Jati**:
  - **Varna System**: Fixed at **4 categories** (*Brahmana, Kshatriya, Vaishya, Shudra*), birth-based, rigid.
  - **Jati System**: Emerged in 6th C. BC; non-restricted numbers; assigned to occupational groups non-fitting into 4 Varnas; birth-based & rigid.
- **Eight Forms of Marriage (Manusmriti / Law Books)**:
  - *Accepted / Approved (First 4)*: **Brahma**, **Daiva**, **Arsha**, **Prajapatya** (*Mnemonic: Brahmadev Always Protects*).
  - *Condemned / Disapproved (Next 4)*: **Asura** (purchase), **Gandharva** (love), **Rakshasa** (capture/force), **Paishacha** (seduction/fraud).
- **Property & Inheritance Laws**:
  - **Stridhana**: Gifts given to bride at marriage (*movable property only*). Passed directly from mother to daughter (*husband/father had no claim*).
  - **Katyayana Smriti** (Gupta Period): Expanded scope of Stridhana gifts.
  - **Medieval Inheritance Law Schools**:
    - **Mitakshara**: Authored by **Vijnaneshwar** (11th/12th C. court scholar of Western Chalukya king **Vikramaditya VI**). Followed in **entire India except Bengal & Assam**. Sons acquire birthright to ancestral property.
    - **Dayabhaga**: Authored by **Jimutavahana** (11th/12th C. scholar). Followed in **Bengal & Assam**. Sons acquire right only after father's death.

## 16 Mahajanapadas & Magadhan Hegemony

### 16 Mahajanapadas (600 BC – 300 BC)
- **Primary Sources**: Mentioned in Buddhist (**Anguttara Nikaya**) and Jaina (**Bhagavati Sutra**) texts.
- **Mid-6th C. BC Dominant 4**: **Vatsa**, **Avanti**, **Kosala**, **Magadha**.

### Polity Classification: Monarchies vs. Oligarchies (Gana / Sangha)
- **Monarchies**: Power concentrated in a single king. Hereditary. Strong Brahmanical influence. Concentrated in **Gangetic Valley**.
- **Oligarchies / Gana-Sanghas (Early Republics)**:
  - Power vested in a council of heads of families / clan elders. Decisions made via consensus or majority vote.
  - **NO Brahmanical influence**; rejected Vedic rituals. Concentrated in **Himalayan foothills & NW India**.
  - Non-democratic elements: Slaves (**Dasas**) and landless laborers (**Kamakaras**) excluded from assembly.
  - *Key Confederacies*:
    - **Vajji Confederacy**: Group of **8 clans** (*Key 3*: **Lichchhavi** (Vaishali), **Videha**, **Jnhatrika / Naya / Naga** (Mahavira's clan)).
    - **Malla Confederacy**: Group of **9 clans** (*Capitals*: Kushinagar & Pawa).
    - **Shakya Clan**: Single clan republic (*Capital*: Kapilavastu; Buddha's clan).
  - *Longevity*: Republics survived from 6th C. BC until destroyed by **Samudragupta** (Gupta Period, 4th C. CE).

## Ancient Classical Literature, Dramatists & Learning Centers

### Major Sanskrit Dramatists & Plays
1. **King Harsha / Harshavardhana** (7th C. CE, Pushyabhuti Dynasty):
   - Plays: **Nagananda**, **Priyadarsika**, **Ratnavali** (*Mnemonic: NPR*).
   - *Priyadarsika* & *Ratnavali* are based on the life of **King Udayana** of Vatsa.
2. **Bhasa** (2nd C. CE):
   - Plays based on King Udayana: **Swapnavasavadattam**, **Pratijnayaugandharayana**.
   - Other plays: **Balacharita**, **Charudatta** (*incomplete play*).
3. **Shudraka** (5th C. CE, Gupta Period):
   - Play: **Mrcchakatika** (*"The Little Clay Cart"*). Completed Bhasa's incomplete play *Charudatta* (love story of merchant Charudatta & courtesan Vasantasena).
4. **Vishakhadatta** (5th–6th C. CE, Gupta Period):
   - **Devi Chandraguptam**: Story of Gupta king **Chandragupta II** defeating Shaka ruler and marrying Dhruvadevi.
   - **Mudrarakshasa**: Story of how **Chandragupta Maurya** and **Chanakya** overthrew **Dhana Nanda** (last Nanda king) and won over his minister **Rakshasa**.

### Takshashila (Taxila) Learning Center & Grammarians
- **Location**: Rawalpindi district, Punjab, Pakistan. Discovered in 19th C. by **Alexander Cunningham**. UNESCO World Heritage Site (1980).
- **Historical Trajectory**: 6th C. BC trade center on **Uttarapatha** → Vedic center → Buddhist learning center under **Kanishka**. Destroyed by **Huna invasion** (5th C. CE).
- **Notable Teachers at Takshashila**:
  - **Panini** (6th–4th C. BC): Sanskrit Grammarian. Authored **Ashtadhyayi** (*foundation of Sanskrit grammar*).
  - **Jivaka** (6th C. BC): Physician in the court of **Bimbisara** & **Ajatashatru**. Treated Lord Buddha and King Pradyota.
  - **Kautilya / Chanakya** (4th C. BC): Professor of political science/economics. Authored **Arthashastra**.
  - **Charaka** (1st–2nd C. CE): Court physician of Kushan king **Kanishka**. Authored **Charaka Samhita**.
- **Chronological Sequence of Sanskrit Grammarians**:
  - **Panini** (6th C. BC) → **Ashtadhyayi**
  - **Katyayana** (4th C. BC) → **Vartika** (*commentary on Ashtadhyayi*)
  - **Patanjali** (2nd C. BC, Shunga Period) → **Mahabhasha** (*commentary on Ashtadhyayi*) & **Yogasutra**.

## Buddhism

### Gautama Buddha: Life Events & Chronology
- **Founder**: **Siddhartha** (Gautama Buddha). Born in **Lumbini** (Nepal) near Kapilavastu.
- **Family**: Father **Shuddhodana** (head of **Shakya** Gana → Buddha called *Shakyamuni*); Mother **Mayadevi** (Koliya princess, died after birth); Foster mother **Mahaprajapati Gautami** (*first woman admitted to Sangha*).
- **Four Sights**: Old man, sick man, corpse, ascetic.
- **Mahabhinishkramana (Great Departure)**: Left home at age **29** with horse **Kanthaka**. Symbolized by **Horse**.
- **Nirvana / Nibbana (Enlightenment)**: Attained at age **35** under **Bodhi / Peepal tree** at **Bodh Gaya** on the 49th day of meditation. Symbolized by **Bodhi Tree / Peepal**.
- **Dhammacakkappavattana (First Sermon)**: Delivered at **Sarnath** (Isipatana) to 5 former companions. Symbolized by **Wheel / Chakra**.
- **Mahaparinirvana (Death / Passing Away)**: Died at age **80** in **483 BC** at **Kushinagar** (capital of Malla Mahajanapada). Symbolized by **Stupa**.
- **Funeral & Relics**: Body cremated → Relics divided into **8 parts** among 8 kings → **8 Sharirika Stupas** built. Ashoka later opened 7 stupas and distributed relics into 84,000 stupas across India.

### Core Philosophy & Doctrines of Buddhism
- **Rejection & Criticism**: Rejected authority of **Vedas**, condemned **animal sacrifices** & **Varna system** (*declared Varna a human creation*).
- **Non-Recognition**: Did **NOT** recognize God or immutable Soul (**Anatta / Anatman** = non-self; **Anicca / Anitya** = impermanence).
- **Four Noble Truths (Cattari Ariyasaccani)**:
  1. *Dukkha*: Life is full of suffering.
  2. *Dukkha Samudaya*: Cause of suffering is Desire (*Tanha / Trishna*).
  3. *Dukkha Nirodha*: Suffering can be ended by eliminating desire.
  4. *Dukkha Nirodha Gamini Pratipat*: Path to end suffering is the **Eightfold Path**.
- **Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Magga / Middle Path / Majjhima Patipada)**: Right View, Resolve, Speech, Action, Livelihood, Effort, Mindfulness, Samadhi.
- **Pratityasamutpada (Law of Dependent Origination)**: Cause-and-effect principle; everything arises in dependence upon cause.
- **Karma & Rebirth**: Believed in Karma; rebirth is driven by Karma (*rebirth of consciousness, not an eternal soul*).

### Monastic Order (Sangha) & Terminology
- **Sangha**: Order of monks (**Bhikkhus**) & nuns (**Bhikkhunis**).
- **Entry Rules**: Open to all Varnas. Required permission from parents (children), masters (slaves), creditors (debtors), husbands (women).
- **Theras & Theris**: Respected senior monks (**Theras**) and senior nuns (**Theris**).
- **Upasakas & Upasikas**: Male (**Upasakas**) and female (**Upasikas**) lay followers living householder lives.
- **Architectural Structures**:
  - **Vihara**: Residential monastery for monks.
  - **Chaitya**: Prayer hall / shrine containing a stupa.
  - **Stupa**: Relic mound.

### Symbols of Buddha's Life
- **Birth**: Lotus & Bull.
- **Great Departure (Mahabhinishkramana)**: Horse.
- **Enlightenment (Nirvana)**: Bodhi Tree / Peepal.
- **Meditation**: Empty Seat.
- **First Sermon (Dhammacakkappavattana)**: Wheel / Chakra.
- **Parinirvana**: Stupa.

### Four Buddhist Councils

| Council | Year & Venue | Patron King | President | Outcome / Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Council** | **483 BC**, Rajgir (Saptaparni Cave) | **Ajatashatru** (Haryanka) | **Mahakassapa** | Compilation of **Sutta Pitaka** (Ananda) & **Vinaya Pitaka** (Upali). |
| **2nd Council** | **383 BC**, Vaishali | **Kalashoka** (Shishunaga) | **Sabakami** | Disagreement over Vinaya rules → Split into **Sthaviravadins** (orthodox) & **Mahasanghikas** (liberal). |
| **3rd Council** | **250 BC**, Pataliputra | **Ashoka** (Maurya) | **Moggaliputta Tissa** | Compilation of 3rd Pitaka: **Abhidhamma Pitaka**. Sent missionaries abroad. |
| **4th Council** | **1st C. CE**, Kashmir (Kundalavana) | **Kanishka** (Kushan) | **Vasumitra** (Vice: Ashvaghosha) | Formal split into **Hinayana** & **Mahayana**. Composition of **Vibhasha Shastra** (*commentary on Tripitaka*). |

### Buddhist Canonical & Non-Canonical Literature

#### Pali Canonical Literature (Tripitaka / Three Baskets)
1. **Sutta Pitaka** (*Buddha's Teachings & Sermons*):
   - Divided into **5 Nikayas**: Digha, Majjhima, Samyutta, Anguttara, Khuddaka.
   - *Majjhima Nikaya*: Contains dialogue between King Avantiputta & disciple Kaccana criticizing Varna.
   - *Khuddaka Nikaya* (15 traditional books; 18 in Burmese tradition including *Milindapanho*, *Nettipakarana*, *Petakopadesa*):
     - **Theragatha**: Hymns of senior monks.
     - **Therigatha**: Hymns of senior nuns (*composed by women*).
     - **Jatakas**: 550+ stories of Buddha's previous births.
     - **Dhammapada**: Moral teachings of Buddha.
     - **Buddhavamsa**: Accounts of Gautama Buddha & 24 preceding Buddhas (*Dipankara = past Buddha, Gautama = present, Maitreya = future Buddha*).
2. **Vinaya Pitaka** (*Monastic Rules & Discipline*):
   - Divided into **Sutta Vibhanga** (227 rules for monks, 311 for nuns), **Khandhaka**, **Parivara**.
3. **Abhidhamma Pitaka** (*Philosophical & Psychological Interpretation*):
   - Contains 7 books including **Kathavatthu** (authored by Moggaliputta Tissa).

#### Non-Canonical Pali Texts
- **Milindapanho** (*"Questions of Milinda"*): Dialogue between Indo-Greek king **Menander I (Milinda)** and Buddhist monk **Nagasena**.
- **Nidanakatha**: First connected life story of Lord Buddha.
- **Dipavamsa & Mahavamsa**: Sri Lankan Pali chronicles.
- **Visuddhimagga** (*"Path of Purification"*): Authored by **Buddhaghosa** in Sri Lanka.

#### Buddhist Schools & Sub-schools
- **Sthaviravada (Theravada) Branch**:
  - Sub-schools: **Sammitiya**, **Sarvastivada** (*realism; all things exist past, present, future*), **Vibhajyavada**, **Vatsiputriya**.
  - Evolution: Formed base for **Hinayana Buddhism**.
- **Mahasanghika Branch**:
  - Sub-schools: **Lokottaravada** (*Buddha is supra-mundane/divine*), **Ekavyavaharika**, **Gokulika**.
  - Evolution: Formed base for **Mahayana Buddhism**.
- **Hinayana vs. Mahayana Comparison**:

| Feature | Hinayana (Theravada) | Mahayana |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meaning** | Lesser Vehicle / Abandoned Way. | Great Vehicle. |
| **Buddha's Nature** | Human teacher / Wise sage. | Divine figure / God (**Deification of Buddha**). |
| **Worship Form** | Symbol worship (*No idols*). | **Idol / Image Worship** of Buddha. |
| **Ideal Goal** | **Arhat** (*individual personal salvation via isolation*). | **Bodhisattva** (*universal salvation; compassionate beings who delay Nirvana to help others*). |
| **Language** | **Pali**. | **Sanskrit** / Hybrid Sanskrit. |
| **Patron Kings** | **Ashoka**. | **Kanishka**. |
| **Sub-schools** | **Vaibhashika** & **Sautrantika**. | **Madhyamaka** & **Yogachara**. |

- **Sub-schools of Mahayana**:
  - **Madhyamaka School**: Founded by **Nagarjuna** (2nd C. CE). Propounded **Shunyata** (*Theory of Emptiness / Dependent Origination*). Key texts: **Mulamadhyamakakarika**, **Prajnaparamita Karika**. Followers: **Buddhapalita**, **Chandrakirti**, **Aryadeva**.
  - **Yogachara / Vijnanavada School**: Founded by **Maitreyanatha** (3rd–4th C. CE). Mind-only idealism. Promoted by brothers **Asanga** & **Vasubandhu** and **Dharmakirti**.

#### Buddhist Sanskrit Literature
- **Ashvaghosha** (Court scholar of Kanishka): Authored **Buddhacharita** (biography of Buddha), **Saundarananda**, **Shariputraprakarana**, **Vajrasuchi**, **Mahalankara Sutralankara**.
- **Vasumitra**: Authored **Vibhasha Shastra** (*encyclopedia of Buddhist philosophy*).
- **Avadana Literature** (*Karma cause-and-effect stories*):
  - **Mahavastu** (Hybrid Sanskrit biography).
  - **Divyavadana** (contains **Ashokavadana**).
  - **Avadanashataka** (100 stories).
- **Lalitavistara**: Sanskrit biography of Buddha.
- **Piprahwa Relics**: Discovered in 1898 at Piprahwa (Siddharthnagar dt., UP) by **William Claxton Peppe**. Brahmi inscription confirms relics were deposited by Shakya clan members.

## Jainism

### Tirthankaras & Mahavira's Life
- **Concept of Tirthankara**: Lit. *"Ford-maker"* (one who creates a ford across the ocean of worldly suffering). 24 Tirthankaras.
- **1st Tirthankara**: **Rishabhadeva / Adinatha** (*symbol: Bull*).
- **23rd Tirthankara**: **Parshvanatha** (*symbol: Serpent*). Prince of Varanasi. Propounded **4 Vows** (*Satya, Ahimsa, Asteya, Aparigraha*). Allowed white garments.
- **24th Tirthankara**: **Vardhamana Mahavira** (*symbol: Lion*).
  - Born in **540 BC** at **Kundagrama** near Vaishali.
  - Clan: **Jnhatrika / Naya** clan (*Mahavira called Nayaputta / Nagaputa*).
  - Father **Siddhartha**; Mother **Trishala** (Lichchhavi princess, sister of Chetaka).
  - Left home at age **30**. Attained **Kevala Jnana** (omniscience / supreme enlightenment) at age **42** under a **Sal tree** on the banks of **Rijupalika River** near Jrimbhikagrama. Became **Jina** (*conqueror*).
  - First Sermon delivered at **Pawa / Pawapuri** to 11 chief disciples (**Gandharas**).
  - Died (**Nirvana / Moksha**) at age **72** in **468 BC** at **Pawapuri** (near Rajgir).

### Core Philosophy & Doctrines of Jainism
- **Rejection & Acceptance**: Rejected authority of **Vedas** & ritualism. Recognized God's existence, but **placed God BELOW Jina**. Did **NOT** explicitly condemn Varna system (*attributed Varna to sins in previous birth*).
- **Triratna (Three Jewels - Mnemonic: DGCA)**:
  1. **Samyak Darshana** (Right Faith).
  2. **Samyak Jnana** (Right Knowledge).
  3. **Samyak Charitra** (Right Conduct).
- **Five Great Vows (Pancha Mahavratas)**:
  1. **Ahimsa** (Non-violence - *strictest in Jainism; all matter/nature animated with Jiva/soul*).
  2. **Satya** (Truth).
  3. **Asteya** (Non-stealing).
  4. **Aparigraha** (Non-possession).
  5. **Brahmacharya** (Celibacy - *added solely by Mahavira*).
- **Epistemology**:
  - **Anekantavada**: Theory of manifold aspects of reality / non-absolutism.
  - **Syadvada**: Theory of conditioned / relative predication (*"May be"*).

### Jaina Schism: Digambaras vs. Shvetambaras
- **Cause**: 12-year famine in Magadha during **Chandragupta Maurya's** reign (~300 BC).
- **Digambara Group**: Monks led by **Bhadrabahu** migrated to South India (**Karnataka / Shravanabelagola**). Maintained strict nudity as taught by Mahavira. Believed women cannot attain Liberation without being reborn as men.
- **Shvetambara Group**: Monks led by **Sthulabhadra** remained in Magadha. Adopted white unstitched garments as allowed by Parshvanatha. Believed women can attain Liberation (19th Tirthankara **Malli / Mallinatha** was female according to Shvetambaras).

### Key Jaina Literature & Scholars
- **Pravachanasara**: Authored by **Acharya Kundakunda** (*essence of Jaina scriptures*).
- **Kalpasutra**: Authored by **Bhadrabahu** in Sanskrit (*biographies of Tirthankaras*).
- **Brihatkatha / Brihatkathakosha**: Authored by **Harishena** (Gupta court poet).
- **Mahapurana**: Composed by **Jinasena** (**Adipurana** - life of Rishabhadeva) and completed by pupil **Gunabhadra** (**Uttarapurana**). Contains accounts of 63 revered persons (*Trishashti-shalakapurusha*).
- **Suri Scholars & Works**:
  - **Haribhadrasuri**: Authored **Shaddarshanasamuccaya** (*summary of 6 orthodox philosophies*).
  - **Hemachandrasuri** (Court scholar of Solanki king **Kumarapala**): Authored **Trishashtishalakapurushacharita** (appendix: **Parishishtaparvan**) and **Kumarapalacharita**.
  - **Harivijayasuri**: Invited to Akbar's Ibadat Khana; conferred title **Jagatguru** by Akbar.
  - **Somadevasuri**: Authored **Nitavakyamrita** & **Yashastilaka**.
  - **Nayachandrasuri** (15th C.): Authored **Hammira Mahakavya** (biography of 13th C. Chauhan king Hammira Deva).

## Mauryan Empire (321 BC – 185 BC)

### Dynastic Sequence & Empire Building
- **Magadha Dynastic Order**: **Haryanka** → **Shishunaga** → **Nanda** → **Maurya** (*Mnemonic: HASHNA MANAH*).
- **Nanda Prelude**: Founded by **Mahapadma Nanda** (**First Empire Builder in India**, *Ekarat*, *Sarvakshatrantaka*). Last king **Dhana Nanda** (*overthrown 321 BC during Alexander's 326 BC invasion aftermath*).
- **Mauryan Founders**:
  - **Chandragupta Maurya** (321–297 BC): Overthrew Dhana Nanda with **Chanakya / Kautilya**. Defeated Seleucus I Nicator (305 BC) → gained Gedrosia, Arachosia, Aria, Paropamisadae → married Seleucus's daughter → received ambassador **Megasthenes** (*Indica*). Embraced Jainism under **Bhadrabahu** → performed *Sallekhana / Santhara* at **Shravanabelagola**.
  - **Bindusara** (297–272 BC): Called *Amitraghada* (Slayer of foes). Patronized **Ajivika sect**.
  - **Ashoka** (272–231 BC; crowned 268 BC): **First Pan-India Empire** covering entire subcontinent except Deep South (Cholas, Cheras, Pandyas) & Assam (Brahmaputra Valley).

### Highly Centralized Mauryan Administration

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Se7en-0007/images/refs/heads/main/Mauryan%20Adm.png)

- **Urban Administration (Pataliputra)**: Managed by a **Municipal Board of 30 members**, divided into **6 committees of 5 members each**:
  1. Industrial Arts Supervision.
  2. Care of Foreigners.
  3. Registration of Births and Deaths.
  4. Trade and Commerce.
  5. Supervision of Manufactured Goods.
  6. Collection of 10% Sales Tax.
- **Army & Navy**: Managed by a 30-member board (6 subcommittees of 5 members each: Infantry, Cavalry, Elephants, Chariots, Navy, Transport/Logistics).
- **Judicial System**:
  - Supreme Court at Pataliputra headed by **Dharmadhikari**.
  - Subordinate courts in provinces headed by **Amatyas**.
  - Courts classified into: **Dharmasthiya** (Civil Courts) & **Kantakasodhana** (Criminal Courts).

### Ashoka's Dhamma & Edicts

#### Kalinga War & Transformation (260 BC)
- Fought in **260 BC** (8th year of coronation) against Kalinga (coastal Odisha).
- Horrified by bloodshed → Abandoned **Bherighosha** (military conquest) → Adopted **Dhammaghosha** (cultural conquest via Dhamma). Recorded in **Major Rock Edict 13**.
- Converted to Buddhism gradually under Buddhist monk **Upagupta**.
- Convened **3rd Buddhist Council** at Pataliputra (250 BC).

#### Concept of Ashoka's Dhamma
- **Nature**: NOT a new religion or Buddhist dogma; a universal **moral code of conduct** / way of life.
- **Core Principles**: Religious tolerance, non-violence (*Ahimsa*), respect to parents/elders, humane treatment of slaves/servants, generosity to Brahmanas & Shramanas.
- **Implementation Mechanism**: Appointed **Dhamma Mahamattas** (5th regnal year); ordered **Pradeshika, Rajuka & Yukta** to go on tours (*Anusamyana*) every 5 years to spread Dhamma.

#### Decipherment, Scripts & Languages
- **Deciphered By**: **James Prinsep** in **1838** (deciphered Brahmi & Kharosthi scripts).
- **Identification with Ashoka**: Connected by **George Turner** via Sri Lankan texts (*Dipavamsa/Mahavamsa* using title *Devanampiya Piyadasi*). Confirmed by **Minor Rock Edict 1** containing both title + name **Ashoka** at 4 places:
  1. **Gujarra** (Datia district, MP).
  2. **Maski** (Raichur district, Karnataka).
  3. **Nittur** (Bellary district, Karnataka).
  4. **Udegolam** (Bellary district, Karnataka).
- **Script & Language Distribution**:
  - **Mainland India**: **Brahmi script** & **Prakrit language**.
  - **NW Pakistan** (*Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra*): **Kharosthi script** & **Prakrit language**.
  - **Afghanistan** (*Kandahar*): **Aramaic & Greek scripts & languages** (bilingual).

#### Classification of Edicts
1. **14 Major Rock Edicts** (14 distinct messages located at **10 sites**):
   - *7 Sites in India*: Girnar (GJ), Sopara (MH), Sanati (KA), Kalsi (UK), Yerragudi (AP), Dhauli (OD), Jaugada (OD).
   - *3 Sites Abroad*: Kandahar (AFG - *MRE 12 & 13 only*), Shahbazgarhi (PAK), Mansehra (PAK).
   - *Special Odisha Note*: At Dhauli & Jaugada, Major Rock Edicts 11, 12, 13 were omitted and replaced by **Separate Rock Edicts 1 & 2** (*motto: "All men are my children"*).
   - *Key Major Rock Edict Contents*:
     - **MRE 1**: Ban on animal sacrifice & festive gatherings.
     - **MRE 3**: Mentions Pradeshikas, Rajukas, Yuktas touring every 5 years.
     - **MRE 5**: Appointment of Dhamma Mahamattas.
     - **MRE 7 & 12**: **Religious tolerance** for all sects (*12 = Tolerance*).
     - **MRE 9**: Condemns useless rituals and ceremonies (*9 = No to rituals*).
     - **MRE 13**: **Kalinga War**, conversion to Dhamma, list of 5 contemporary Greek kings (*Antiochus II, Ptolemy II, Antigonus, Magas, Alexander*).
2. **Girnar / Junagadh Multilayered Rock**: Contains inscriptions of 3 different rulers across ages on the SAME rock:
   1. **Ashoka** (14 Major Rock Edicts).
   2. **Rudradaman I** (150 CE, Western Kshatrap - *1st long inscription in pure Sanskrit, details repair of Sudarshana Lake originally built by Pushyagupta under Chandragupta Maurya*).
   3. **Skandagupta** (5th C. CE, Imperial Gupta - *details 2nd repair of Sudarshana Lake*).
3. **Allahabad / Prayagraj Multilayered Pillar**: Contains inscriptions of 3 rulers across ages on the SAME pillar:
   1. **Ashoka** (Major Pillar Edicts & Schism Edict).
   2. **Samudragupta** (4th C. CE, Prayaga Prashasti by Harishena).
   3. **Jahangir** (17th C. CE, Mughal).
4. **7 Major Pillar Edicts** (Located at 7 sites): Topra (shifted to Delhi by Firoz Shah Tughlaq), Meerut (shifted to Delhi), Kausambi (shifted to Allahabad), Rampurva (Bihar), Lauriya-Araraj (Bihar), Lauriya-Nandangarh (Bihar), Kandahar (AFG - *only MPE 7*).
5. **Minor Pillar Edicts**:
   - **Rummindei / Lumbini Pillar**: Commemorates Ashoka's visit in 20th regnal year to Buddha's birthplace. Reduced tax (*Bali*) of Lumbini village to **1/8th**.
   - **Nigali Sagar Pillar**: Commemorates expansion of Konakamana Buddha stupa.
   - **Schism Edicts** (Sanchi, Sarnath, Kausambi): Orders punishment for causing schism in the Sangha.
