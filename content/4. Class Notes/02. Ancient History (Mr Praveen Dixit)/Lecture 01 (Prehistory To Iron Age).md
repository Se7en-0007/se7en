## Geological vs. Historical Time Divisions

### Geological Time Scale
- **Earth's Age** = **5 Billion Years**.
- **Eras Sequence**:
  - **Paleozoic Era** (~500 Million BP) → **Mesozoic Era** (Rise of Dinosaurs) → **Cenozoic Era** (Age of Mammals).
  - **Cenozoic** (from *Mama or Mummy*) = Mammals → Live body birth + Breastfeeding.
  - **Mass Extinctions** between eras → 99% species or dinosaurs extinct → Climate, rock formations & vegetation reset (Vegetation → Coal).
- **Cenozoic Periods** = Primary → Secondary → Tertiary → **Quaternary Period** (Recent).
- **Ice Age Oscillations**:
  - Cold Phase → Ice sheets expand towards Equator.
  - Warm Phase → Ice sheets recede towards Poles.
  - *Climate Debate*: Global warming negators view current warming as natural oscillation; Pro-global warming cite artificial CO₂/human acceleration.
- **Quaternary Epochs**:
  - **Pleistocene Epoch** = Last Ice Age (~2.5 Lakh BP to **10,000 BC / 12,000 BP**).
  - **Holocene Epoch** (**10,000 BC – Present**) = *Recent Age* → Earth warming → Enabled human expansion & dominance.
  - **Anthropocene Epoch** (Proposed) = 19th or 20th Century onward → Unnatural climate change driven by human action (**Anthropocene** = Human-induced).

### Historical Time Scale
- **Basis** = **Tool Technology** in Earth's Crust (Lower Stratum = Older Tools; Upper Stratum = Newer Tools).
- **19th-Century Two-Age Division**:
  - **Paleolithic Age** (Old Stone) = Big, crude, unpolished, non-symmetric tools → **Hunter-Gatherers** (Food Consumers).
  - **Neolithic Age** (New Stone) = Small, precision, symmetric tools → **Food Producers** (Agriculture + Livestock domestication).
- **1872 Revision** (Discoveries in France & N. Africa):
  - **Mesolithic Age** (Middle Stone) inserted → 2,000–3,000 year transition phase → **Microliths** + Domestication learning.
  - Sub-phases: **Epipaleolithic** (Early Mesolithic; Paleolithic continuation) → **Protoneolithic** (Late Mesolithic; Undeveloped Neolithic).

## Evolutionary Psychology & Human Behavioral Traits
- **Nocturnal Guarding Instinct** = Teenagers stay awake late → Genetic memory of Paleolithic camps (Elders hunted early morning; teenagers guarded camp at night).
- **Male vs. Female Sensory Adaptations**:
  - **Male (Hunter)** = Superior **peripheral vision** & motion tracking (Road driving focus).
  - **Female (Gatherer)** = Superior **color perception** & detail concentration (Selecting edible vs. poisonous fruits).
- **Triune Brain Evolution**:
  - **Reptilian Brain** = Motor trajectory, spatial reflexes, reaction speed (e.g., catching a ball).
  - **Monkey Brain** = Social hierarchy, dominance, group positioning.
  - **Frontal Cortex (Human Brain)** = **Homo sapiens** self-consciousness, pattern tracking, future planning, decision-making.
- **Natural Selection & Instincts**:
  - **Adrenaline** = Immediate pain suppression post-injury → Enabled escape from predators.
  - **Inherent Fears** (Height, Dark, Unknown) & **Sexual Urge** = Survival mechanisms preserved by natural selection.
  - **Irrationality & Communal Myth-making** = Shared imaginary fear/beliefs → Enabled large-scale group cooperation → Gave **Homo sapiens** survival edge over Neanderthals.

## Complete Breakdown of Stone & Metal Ages

### Lower Paleolithic Age
- **Timeline** = **20–25 Lakh BP** (World/Africa) | **18 Lakh BP – 1.5 Lakh BP** (India).
- **Hominid Evolution**:
  - **Homo habilis** (~20–25 Lakh BP, Africa) = First toolmaker hominid.
  - **Homo erectus** = Straight spine, bipedal walking, **90° opposable thumb** (Tool-holding capacity), multi-boned feet (Tree-climbing evolutionary remnant).
- **Indian Hominid Fossil**:
  - **Hathnora** (Narmada Valley, MP, 1982) by **Arun Sonakia**.
  - **Narmada Man / Narmada Human** = Cranial capacity ~**1,400 cc** (Female skull cap; dated ~**6 Lakh BP**).
- **Lifestyle & Habitat**:
  - **Band Size** = 20–30 individuals | High mortality, zero care for injured/elderly (No healed bones found).
  - **Living Preference** = Shallow river streams/springs (e.g., Rishikesh-style), rocky terrain (e.g., Karnataka).
  - **Avoided Environments** = Dense forests, alluvial plains (No agriculture), mountain peaks (Cold; no J&K Paleolithic sites).
- **Tool Traditions**:
  - **Oldowan / Oldwan Industry** = Core tools formed by **flaking** (Striking stone → Core = Main body; Flake = Broken chip).
  - **Sonian Tools** (India) = Oldowan-style chopper tools discovered in **Soan / Sohan Valley** (**Potwar Plateau**, Punjab/Pakistan/Haryana).
  - **Acheulian Industry** (France) = Triangular, **pear-shaped hand axes**, bifacial edges.
    - Represents **99%** of Indian Lower Paleolithic sites (**12 Lakh BP – 2 Lakh BP**).
  - **Madras Industry** = Dense Acheulian tool cluster near Chennai (**Attirampakkam**, **Gudiyam**).

---

### Middle Paleolithic Age
- **Timeline** = **1.5 Lakh BP – 35,000 BP**.
- **Dominant Hominid** = **Homo neanderthalensis (Neanderthals)** (Eurasia, Europe, SE Asia, China; missing in India).
  - *Traits*: Broad shoulders, stocky build, first evidence of **burial practices**, **religious/magical traditions** and bone-healing (Care for injured). Cross-breeding left Neanderthal genes in modern humans.
- **Hominid Emergence** = **Homo sapiens** appeared in Middle Paleolithic (*Thinking Human*).
- **Tool Tradition**:
  - **Mousterian Industry** (France) = Uses **Levallois Technique** (Side-striking to create tortoise-shell/flat-core tools).
  - **Nevasian Industry** (India) = Discovered by **Dr. H.D. Sankalia** at **Nevasa** (Maharashtra; also found at Bori).

---

### Upper Paleolithic Age
- **Timeline** = **35,000 BP – 10,000 BC**.
- **Hominid Status** = **Homo sapiens** became sole surviving hominid (Other hominids extinct; bottlenecked to ~700 individuals before population explosion).
- **Tool Technology**:
  - **Flake / Blade Tool Technology** = Parallel-sided blades; one edge intentionally blunted for hand-pressure.
  - **Substantial Bone Tools** = Scoops, chisels, scrapers, skinning tools (e.g., **Kurnool Caves** andhra Pradesh).
- **Socio-Cultural Innovations**:
  - **Fire Control / Domestication** = Brought inside caves/habitations.
  - **Personal Adornment & Toys** = Jewelry, beads, **ostrich eggshell artifacts** (e.g., Patne), stone/bone animal & human figurines.
  - **Grave Goods** = Pots & ornaments buried with dead → Indicates belief in **Afterlife**, luck consciousness and fear of dead person's bad luck.
  - **Earliest Religious Site in India** = **Bagor** (**Bhilwara**, Rajasthan) → Triangular stone on raised platform (~**11,000 BP**), locally called *Kali Mata Mandir*.
  - **Social Hierarchy** = Indicated by jewelry & surplus items.
- **Upper Paleolithic Rock Paintings**:
  - *Style* = Linear representation, stick-like human figures.
  - *Themes* = **Huge animal figures** (Reflects high threat/fear of wild animals vs small animals in Mesolithic).
  - *Colors* = Black, White, **Green** (Dancing/activities), **Red** (Hunting scenes; made from hematite/geru).
  - *Key Sites*:
    1. **Bhimbetka Caves** (**Raisen** district, Vindhya Range, MP).
    2. **Lakudiyar Paintings** (**Almora** district, Uttarakhand).

---

### Mesolithic Age (Middle Stone Age)
- **Timeline** = **10,000 BC – 8,000 BC** (Holocene transition).
- **Environmental Impact** = End of Ice Age → Earth warming → Environmental expansion.
- **Tool Innovation** = **Microliths** (1–4 cm, small, precision, symmetric stone tools).
  - **Compound Tools** = Microlith arrowheads & spearheads bound onto wooden/bone shafts → Drastically enhanced hunting speed & lethality.
- **Four Structural Sub-Phases**:
  1. **Epipaleolithic** = No microliths, no pottery | Specialized hunting-gathering targeting specific plants/animals (e.g., non-flying birds/chickens, mangos).
  2. **Early Mesolithic I** = Microliths appear | No pottery.
  3. **Early Mesolithic II** = Microliths + **Pottery appears** (Indicates non-regular/accidental food surplus storage).
  4. **Protoneolithic** = Abundant microliths + Pottery + Domestication of plants & animals completed.
- **Key Sites** = **Adamgarh** (MP), **Bagor** (Rajasthan).
- **Economic Shift** = Hunting-gathering receded to secondary activity but persisted substantially.

---

### Neolithic, Chalcolithic & Iron Ages (Overview)

#### Neolithic Age (New Stone Age)
- **Timeline** = **8th Millennium BC – 4th Millennium BC**.
- **Economy** = Agriculture + Livestock domestication → **Food Production**.
- **Settlement** = Emergence of permanent **Villages**.
  - *First Village in South Asia* = **Mehrgarh** (Balochistan).
- **Tooling** = Polished & ground stone tools.
- **Metals** = First invention of metal (rare/isolated pieces).

#### Chalcolithic Age (Stone-Copper Age)
- **Timeline** = **4th Millennium BC – Late 2nd/Early 1st Millennium BC**.
- **Core Feature** = **Substantial metal tools** (Copper + Stone; minimum 10% ratio).
- **Character** = Rural, agrarian, pottery-using cultures.

#### Iron Age
- **Timeline** = **Late 2nd / Early 1st Millennium BC – Present**.
- **Core Feature** = **Iron** becomes the **prime metal** of human civilization.
- **Impact** = Foundation of modern structural, agricultural & transportation infrastructure.
