## Philosophical Systems

- **Astika** = Accepts authority of **Vedas** (Six Orthodox Systems / **Shat-Darshana**).
- **Nastika** = Rejects authority of **Vedas** (**Jainism**, **Buddhism**, **Ajivika**, **Charvaka**).

---

## Six Orthodox Systems (Astika / Shat-Darshana)

### Pairing 1: Nyaya & Vaisheshika (Non-Dualist Systems)
- **Non-Dualist** = Do not differentiate or debate **Matter vs. Soul** (*Purusha* & *Prakriti*). Must always be paired together.

#### 1. Nyaya (Philosophy of Logic)
- **Author**: **Akshapada Gautama Rishi**
- **Core Text**: ***Nyaya Sutra***
- **Meaning**: **Nyaya = Logic**
- **Cause of Suffering**: **Ignorance** = Knowledge derived from non-logical / invalid sources.
- **Solution / Path**: Acquisition of **Right Knowledge** via **4 Valid Pramanas** (out of 6 total methods):
 1. **Pratyaksha** (Direct Perception)
 2. **Anumana** (Inference)
 3. **Upamana** (Comparison / Analogy)
 4. **Shabda** (Verbal Testimony)

#### 2. Vaisheshika (Philosophy of Atomism)
- **Author**: **Rishi Kanada** (**Kan**)
- **Core Text**: ***Vaisheshika Sutra***
- **Meaning**: **Vaisheshika = Atomism** / Empirical Analysis
- **Cause of Suffering**: **Ignorance** (Same as Nyaya).
- **Solution / Path**: **Practical / Empirical Knowledge** at atomic level (**Anu** & **Paramanu**).
 - **Empirical Methodology**: Break complex systems down into constituent components (*Galilean empirical approach*).
 - **Anu / Paramanu = Indestructible elemental components**.

---

### Pairing 2: Samkhya & Yoga (Dualist Systems)
- **Dualist** = Differentiates between **Purusha** (Consciousness) and **Prakriti** (Matter). Must always be paired together.

#### 1. Samkhya (Theoretical Dualism)
- **Author**: **Kapila Muni**
- **Core Text**: ***Samkhya Sutra***
- **Nature**: **Theoretical Philosophy**
- **Ontology**: World = **Purusha** (Consciousness) + **Prakriti** (Matter).
 - **Purusha**: Omnipresent, beyond *Gunas*, unmanifested light; requires *Prakriti* for expression.
 - **Prakriti**: 24 forms of matter, composed of **3 Gunas**:
 1. **Sattva** = Goodness, purity, balance.
 2. **Rajas** = Passion, action, potential (e.g., love, anger).
 3. **Tamas** = Darkness, inertia, lethargy.
- **Cause of Suffering**: **Imbalance between Purusha and Prakriti**.
- **Solution / Path**: **Right Knowledge** = Discriminative understanding that *Purusha* and *Prakriti* are distinct.

#### 2. Yoga (Practical Dualism)
- **Author**: **Rishi Patanjali**
- **Core Text**: ***Yoga Sutra***
- **Meaning**: **Yoga = Union / Addition** (Uniting Body & Soul / *Purusha* & *Prakriti*).
- **Nature**: **Practical Application of Samkhya**
- **Cause of Suffering**: **Imbalance between Purusha and Prakriti**.
- **Solution / Path**: **Physical & Mental Exercises** (Asana, Pranayama, Meditation) → Re-establishes balance of body and soul.

---

### Pairing 3: Mimamsa & Vedanta (Vedic Exegesis)

#### 1. Purva Mimamsa (Ritual Exegesis)
- **Author**: **Rishi Jaimini**
- **Core Text**: ***Mimamsa Sutra***
- **Focus**: **Ritual Orthopraxy** (*Karmakanda*)
- **Cause of Suffering**: **Displeasure of Deities** (e.g., wrath of Goddess Lakshmi).
- **Solution / Path**: **Karmakanda** = Precise performance of Vedic rituals, sacrifices and rites.

#### 2. Uttara Mimamsa / Vedanta (Metaphysical Exegesis)
- **Core Foundations**: ***Prasthana Trayi*** (Three Pillars):
 1. **Upadesha Prasthana** = ***Upanishads*** (Philosophical teachings)
 2. **Yukti Prasthana** = ***Brahma Sutra*** (Authored by **Rishi Badarayana**)
 3. **Sadhana Prasthana** = ***Bhagavad Gita*** (Practical spiritual discipline)
- **Cause of Suffering**: No external cause; **inherent nature of Atman (Soul) in worldly existence is to suffer**.
- **Solution / Path**: **Moksha** = Complete merger of **Atman** (Individual Soul) with **Brahma** (Universal Consciousness/Ultimate Reality).
- **4 Forms of Philosophical Debate (*Katha*)**:
 1. **Vada** = Constructive, truth-seeking debate.
 2. **Vivada** = Less constructive disagreement.
 3. **Vitanda** = Caviling / Refutation without establishing a counter-thesis.
 4. **Jalpa** = Wrangling / Argument for personal victory ending in conflict.
- **6 Sub-schools of Vedanta**:
 1. **Advaita** (Non-duality – Adi Shankara)
 2. **Dvaita** (Duality – Madhvacharya)
 3. **Vishishtadvaita** (Qualified non-duality – Ramanujacharya)
 4. **Dvaitadvaita** (Dualistic non-duality – Nimbarkacharya)
 5. **Shuddhadvaita** (Pure non-duality – Vallabhacharya)
 6. **Achintya Bheda-Abheda** (Incomprehensible difference & non-difference – **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu**):
 - **Core Principle**: Relationship between *Atman* and *Brahma* is beyond human intellect (*Achintya*).
 - **Path**: Absolute **Bhakti** (Devotion) → Experience oneness through divine love; *Bhakti* is superior to *Moksha*.

## Heterodox Systems (Nastika)

### 1. Charvaka / Lokayata (Absolute Hedonism)
- **Epithets**: **Lokayata** (Philosophy of the masses), **Brihaspatya** (Attributed in Vedas to Brihaspati).
- **Core Doctrine**: **Absolute Hedonism** + **Pure Materialism**.
- **Ontology**: Only **4 Elements** exist (**Fire, Earth, Water, Air**; *Char* = 4 → Charvaka).
 - Rejects **Space** (*Akasha*), **Soul** (*Atman*), **God** and **Afterlife**.
- **Critique of Religion**: **Tautology** = Religion assumes unproven concepts (Heaven, Hell, Karma) and presents assumptions as absolute truth.
- **Famous Maxim**: *"Yavat jivet sukham jivet, rinam kritva ghritam pibet"* (As long as you live, live happily; take loans and drink ghee).
- **Social & Moral Framework**:
 - Rejects accumulation of **Karma** (Good vs. Bad deeds).
 - Human behavior governed by **Survival, Social Laws, & Retribution** (fear of legal/social punishment), not fear of God or divine karma.

### 2. Akriyavada (Philosophy of Non-Action)
- **Expounder**: **Ajita Kesakambalin** (Name derived from wearing a blanket/quilt of human hair – *Kesa Kambala*, cold in winter, hot in summer).
- **Core Principle**: **Actions do not produce or accumulate Karma**.
- **Doctrine**: Good and bad deeds have no intrinsic moral transfer; moral labels are arbitrary mental constructs.

### 3. Uchhedavada (Theory of Annihilation)
- **Expounder**: **Ajita Kesakambalin** / Materialist thinkers.
- **Core Principle**: **Absolute Annihilation at Death**.
- **Doctrine**: Human existence begins at birth and ends completely at death. No rebirth, no afterlife, no post-mortem consequences.

### 4. Shashvatavada (Eternalism)
- **Expounder**: **Pakudha Kaccayana** (**Pakudha Katyayana**).
- **Core Principle**: **Immutability of Primary Elements**.
- **Doctrine**: Human body = Temporary grouping of eternal, uncreated elements. At death, elements separate and merge back into the cosmos; life is merely a temporary material journey between two points.

### 5. Ajnanavada (Absolute Skepticism & Agnosticism)
- **Expounder**: **Sanjaya Belatthiputta** (**Sanjaya Bhelaputta**).
- **Core Principle**: *"I do not know what I do not know, therefore I suspend my judgment."*
- **Doctrine**: **Absolute Skepticism** + **Agnosticism**. Metaphysical truths are unknowable; impossible to arrive at definitive judgments regarding transcendental questions.

## The Persian / Iranian Invasion (6th Century BC)

### Chronology & Military Campaigns
- **Cyrus (530 BC)**:
 - Infiltrated NW Indian territory around Indus River.
 - **Historical Significance**: **First recorded foreign invasion of Indian territory**.
- **Darius I (517 BC)**:
 - Advanced conquest up to **Jhelum River**.
 - Dispatched Greek navigator **Scylax** (**Salak**) to survey the course of the Indus River.
 - **Scylax’s Account**: Wrote ***Periplus of Scylax*** → **Earliest recorded usage of the term "India"**.
 - **Etymological Evolution**: *Sindhu* (Sanskrit) → *Hindu / Hind* (Persian S → H) → *Ind* (Greek H silent) → **India** (Greek suffix *-ia*).
 - Other cognates: Chinese = **Shenju**; Hebrew = **Hodos**.
- **Xerxes (490 BC)**:
 - Enlisted Indian infantry and cavalry contingents in European military campaigns during the invasion of Greek city-states (as depicted in Battle of Thermopylae era).
 - **Historical Significance**: **First recorded deployment of Indian soldiers on European soil**.
- **Herodotus (470 BC)**:
 - Authored first detailed geographical and ethnographic description of India in ***Histories***.
- **Ctesias (405–395 BC)**:
 - Greek court physician in Persia; authored ***Indica***.
- **Alexander the Great (328 BC)**:
 - Overthrew Achaemenid Empire under **Darius III**, ending Persian rule.

### Administrative Impact & Satrapies
- **Persian Satrapies**: Achaemenid Empire divided into 20 administrative provinces (*Satrapies*).
- **Indian Satrapies (3 Provinces)**: **Gandhara**, **Hind (Sindh)** and **Sattagydia**.
- **Revenue Contribution**: According to Herodotus, Indian satrapies yielded **32% of total imperial tax revenues** (360 Euboic talents of gold dust), while the remaining 19 satrapies contributed 68%.

## Legacies of The Persian Invasion

1. **Religious Influence**: Introduction of **Zoroastrianism** (Parsi faith) into NW India.
2. **Geopolitical Contact**: First direct geopolitical and cultural contact between **India and Greece / Europe**.
3. **Trade Expansion**: Opening of overland and maritime trade routes → **Initiation of Indian commodity exports to Europe**.
4. **Astrological Knowledge**: Transmission of **Babylonian astrology** into Indian astronomy.
5. **Linguistic & Script Transference**:
 - Introduction of **Aramaic language and script**.
 - **Evolution of Kharosthi Script**: Formed in NW India via fusion of **Brahmi** and **Aramaic** scripts (written Right-to-Left).
1. **Academic & Commercial Centers**: Rise of **Taxila** (**Takshashila**) as an international hub for commerce, culture and academic synthesis (Persian, Greek, Central Asian, Chinese, Indian scholars).
2. **Royal Court Ceremonies**:
 - **King's Hair-Washing Ceremony** (Ceremonial pouring of sacred water).
 - **Female Bodyguards** (Recorded by Megasthenes in Maurya court).
 - **Maintenance of Sacred Fire** in royal audience chambers.
8. **Architectural & Sculptural Influences**:
 - **Pillared Palaces**: Achaemenid pillared halls → Inspired Maurya pillared hall at **Kumrahar** (Patna).
 - **Monolithic Pillars**: Persian pillars of Darius → Inspired **Ashokan monolithic pillars**.
 - **Lustrous Polish**: Achaemenid stone polishing tech → **Maurya high-gloss stone polish**.
 - **Rock-Cut Architecture**: Arch of Lycian tombs in Persia → Inspired **Chaitya arch** design (e.g., **Lomas Rishi Cave** in Barabar Hills).
 - **Inscriptional Terminology**: Persian word ***Dipi*** (*Dipi-kāra*) → Transformed into Ashokan ***Lipi*** (Script/Inscription).
