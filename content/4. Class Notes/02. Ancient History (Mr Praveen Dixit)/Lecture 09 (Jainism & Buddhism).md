## Core Foundations of Jainism

* **Jainism = Naastik Sect** $\rightarrow$ Rejects authority of Vedas & Vedic rituals.
* **Cosmology & Universe**:
  * **No Creator, Manager, or Destroyer** $\rightarrow$ Universe is uncreated & eternal.
  * **Universal Law** $\rightarrow$ Governs universe; unimpeachable, unchangeable.
  * **No Divine Interference** $\rightarrow$ Gods, Prophets, Tirthankaras cannot alter or interfere with Universal Law.
  * **Analogy**: Ignorant child putting hand in fire burns regardless $\rightarrow$ Ignorance of law does not prevent karmic consequences.
* **Time Cycle (Kaal)**:
  * **Snake-like Motion** (vs. Cyclic in Hinduism, Linear/Cyclic in Buddhism).
  * **Utsarpini** = Rising time phase $\rightarrow$ Progress, improvement, increasing lifespan/virtue.
  * **Avsarpini** = Declining time phase $\rightarrow$ Decay, worsening conditions (Current era = Avsarpini).
* **24 Tirthankaras**:
  * Appear in **every time cycle** (24 in Utsarpini, 24 in Avsarpini).
  * **Function**: Awaken humanity to pre-existing Universal Laws $\rightarrow$ Neither create laws nor alter them.
* **Position of Gods**:
  * Gods exist $\rightarrow$ Positioned **below Tirthankaras**.
  * Worship of gods/Tirthankaras done by **Upasakas** (lay followers) $\rightarrow$ Cannot grant *Kevala Jnana* or change Universal Laws.

## Chronology of Tirthankaras

* **1st Tirthankara: Rishabhdev (Adinath)**
  * **Son 1: Bahubali / Gomateshwara** $\rightarrow$ Colossal statue at Shravanabelagola.
  * **Son 2: Bharat** $\rightarrow$ Jain tradition attributes name *Bharat* (India) to him.
  * **Daughter: Brahmi** $\rightarrow$ Taught writing by Rishabhdev $\rightarrow$ Origin of **Brahmi script**.
* **3rd to 9th Tirthankaras**:
  * Associated with urban settings $\rightarrow$ Modern scholars propose living during **Indus Valley Civilization**.
* **23rd Tirthankara: Parshvanatha**
  * Lived $pprox$ **250 years before Mahavira**.
  * Followers wore white garments $\rightarrow$ Historical existence archaeologically/historically indicated.
  * Propounded **4 Vows (Anuvratas)**: Satya, Ahimsa, Asteya, Aparigraha.
* **24th Tirthankara: Vardhamana Mahavira**
  * **Birth**: **599 BC** at Kundagrama (near Vaishali).
  * **Father**: **Siddhartha** $\rightarrow$ Chief of **Jnatrika clan** (part of **Vajji Mahajanapada**).
  * **Mother**: **Trishala** $\rightarrow$ Lichchhavi princess (sister of Chetaka).
  * **Marital Status**:
    * **Digambara view**: Unmarried / Celibate throughout.
    * **Svetambara view**: Married to **Yashoda** $\rightarrow$ Daughter Anojja / Priyadarshana.
  * **Renunciation**: Left household at age **30** (or 32 in oral variations).
  * **Enlightenment (Kevala Jnana)**: Attained at age **42** under Sal tree on banks of Rijupalika River at Jrimbhikagrama.
  * **First Sermon**: **Vipula Peak (Vipulachal)** in **Rajgriha** (Nalanda dist, Bihar).
  * **First Disciple**: **Indrabhuti Gautama**.
  * **Organization of Sangha**:
    * **Male Sangha Head / Chief Lay Patron**: **Shrenika** (**King Bimbisara** of Haryanka Dynasty).
    * **Female Sangha Head**: **Chandana**.
  * **Death / Death-Liberation**: Age **72** at **Pawapuri** (near Patna) in **527 BC**.
  * **Calendar Era**: **Veer Nirvan Samvat** (commenced 527 BC).
  * **Epithets & Titles**:
    * **Mahavira** = Great Kshatriya.
    * **Vardhamana** = Ever-growing (derived from *Vriddhi*).
    * **Nigantha Nataputta** (in Buddhist Texts) = Free from knots/bonds (*Nigranti* = unattached to worldly ties).

## Ontological Architecture: World, Jiva & Ajiva

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Se7en-0007/images/refs/heads/main/Samsara.png)

* **CRITICAL ONTO-FOUNDATION (2024 Prelims)**:
  * **Both Jiva and Ajiva contain Soul (Atman)**.
  * **Comparative Soul Matrix**:
    * **Jainism**: Soul exists in **both Living & Non-Living**.
    * **Buddhism**: Soul exists in **Neither** (*Anatta* / No-soul).
    * **Hinduism**: Soul exists **Only in Living**.
* **Distinction between Jiva & Ajiva**:
  * **Jiva**: Possesses **Consciousness (Chetana)** $\rightarrow$ Aware of existence.
  * **Ajiva**: Lacks **Consciousness** $\rightarrow$ Unconscious substance/energy.
* **5 Categories of Ajiva (Non-Living Substances)**:
  1. **Pudgala** = Matter $\rightarrow$ Tangible, touchable, physical substance.
  2. **Akasha** = Space / Empty consciousness context.
  3. **Kala** = Time $\rightarrow$ Governs temporal progression (*Utsarpini* & *Avsarpini*).
  4. **Dharma** = Motion / Principle of Movement $\rightarrow$ Growth, progress, duty (Lack of daily knowledge acquisition = *Adharma*).
  5. **Adharma** = Rest / Principle of Stoppage $\rightarrow$ Absence of motion.

## Path to Liberation: Triratna & 5 Anuvratas

### Samvara & Nirjara
* **Samvara** = Stopping inflow of new karmic dust.
* **Nirjara** = Shedding/burning off existing accumulated karma.
* **Result** $\rightarrow$ **Kevala Jnana** $\rightarrow$ **Nirvana / Moksha**.

### Triratna (Three Jewels)
1. **Samyak Darshana** = Right Faith / Belief (Balanced faith; *Svataha Pramana* / self-evident conviction).
2. **Samyak Jnana** = Right Knowledge.
3. **Samyak Charitra** = Right Conduct.
* **Sequential Order**: Right Faith $\rightarrow$ Right Knowledge $\rightarrow$ Right Conduct.

### 5 Anuvratas (Vows)
1. **Satya** (Truth) $\rightarrow$ Not dogmatic absolute truth, but accepting & accommodating all facets of truth/viewpoints.
2. **Ahimsa** (Non-Violence) $\rightarrow$ Individual duty to protect one's soul from karmic pollution (violence harms actor's soul first via karmic dust).
3. **Asteya** (Non-Stealing) $\rightarrow$ Not taking what belongs to another; basis for anti-exploitation / anti-colonialism.
4. **Aparigraha** (Non-Possession) $\rightarrow$ Restricting consumption to actual need; taking beyond need violates law.
5. **Brahmacharya** (Chastity) $\rightarrow$ Added by **Mahavira** (1st to 4th propounded by **Parshvanatha**):
   * **Monks**: Absolute celibacy / total abstinence.
   * **Laypersons (Upasakas)**: Restrained sexual activity strictly for progeny.

## Core Epistemological Philosophies of Jainism

1. **Anekantavada (Theory of Many-Sidedness / Pluralism)**:
   * Reality/Truth has **multiple facets**.
   * Ordinary unliberated humans see only **one facet** of truth.
   * Only **Kevala Gyani** perceives all facets simultaneously.
   * Illustrated by **Parable of 7 Blind Men & Elephant**.
   * **Core Rule**: **Accept all extremes / perspectives**.
2. **Syadvada (Theory of 'May Be' / Conditional Predication)**:
   * Human language is limited $\rightarrow$ Any statement is a subjective perception, not absolute truth.
   * Truth is non-binary $\rightarrow$ Prefix statements with **Syat** (*Perhaps / May be*).
1. **Nayavada & Saptabhangi Naya**:
   * Analytical logic evaluating contextual viewpoints.
   * **Saptabhangi** = 7 modes of predication (*Asti-Nasti* / Is and Is-Not).

## Spiritual Hierarchy (Shramana Ladder)

1. **Upasaka**: Lay follower / Commoner $\rightarrow$ Observes modified vows, funds monastic order, seeks merit.
2. **Bhikkhu / Bhikkhuni**: Monk / Nun $\rightarrow$ Observes strict vows (*Pancha Mahavrata*), actively seeks Nirvana.
3. **Arihant / Arhat**: Living enlightened individual $\rightarrow$ Attained *Kevala Jnana*, destroyed binding karmas; awaits body death for Moksha.
4. **Siddha**: Fully liberated soul after death $\rightarrow$ Achieved Moksha, resides in *Siddhashila*, permanently exited rebirth cycle.
5. **Tirthankara**: Supreme spiritual preceptor (24 per age) $\rightarrow$ Establishes four-fold order (*Sangha*) and teaches liberation pathway.

## Life & Chronology of Gautama Buddha

* **Founder**: **Siddhartha Gautama** (Sakyamuni / Tathagata).
* **Birth**: **563 BC** at **Lumbini** (present-day Nepal).
* **Parents**: Father **Suddhodana** (King of Sakya clan at Kapilavastu); Mother **Maya Devi** (Mahamaya - died 7 days post-birth).
* **Prophecy**: Ascetic predicted child would become either greatest **Chakravartin King** or greatest **Ascetic/Philosopher**.
* **4 Sights (in 20s)**:
  1. Old Man
  2. Sick Man
  3. Dead Body
  4. Ascetic / Monk
  * Charioteer companion: **Channa**.
* **Great Renunciation (Mahabhinishkramana)**: Left home at age **29**.
* **Teachers**: Studied under **Alara Kalama** and Udraka Ramaputta.
* **Enlightenment (Nirvana)**: Age **35** at **Bodh Gaya** under Bodhi Tree (Pipal tree) on Niranjana River.
* **First Sermon**: **Sarnath** (Deer Park, Varanasi) to 5 ascetics $\rightarrow$ **Dhammacakkappavattana** ("Setting in Motion the Wheel of Dhamma").
* **Death / Mahaparinirvana**: Age **80** at **Kushinagar** (**483 BC**).

## Four Foundational Principles of Buddhism

1. **Anatta / Anatman**: **No Soul** $\rightarrow$ Rejects permanent soul in living or non-living entities; soul is a false illusion generated by body-consciousness interaction.
2. **Anicca / Anitya**: **Impermanence** $\rightarrow$ Everything is transient and continuously changing; slow rate of change creates illusion of permanence.
3. **Sunya / Sunyata**: **Emptiness** $\rightarrow$ Lack of intrinsic, independent self-existence (e.g., Chariot analogy: assembly of parts lacks independent "chariot-soul").
4. **Dukkha**: **Suffering** $\rightarrow$ Universal condition; unlike Vedantic view (suffering as soul's inherent material trap), Buddhism posits **Suffering has a specific Cause** and can be systematically eliminated.

---

## Four Noble Truths (Cattari Ariyasaccani)

1. **Dukkha**: Life is suffering.
2. **Dukkha Samudaya**: Suffering has a cause ($\rightarrow$ Desire / Clinging / Ignorance / *Pratityasamutpada*).
3. **Dukkha Nirodha**: Suffering can cease ($\rightarrow$ Attainment of Nirvana / *Upeksha Bhava*).
4. **Dukkha Nirodha Gamini Pratipada**: There is a path leading to cessation of suffering $\rightarrow$ **Middle Path (Majjhima Patipada) / Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Marga)**.

## The Noble Eightfold Path (Ashtangika Marga)

* **Sila (Morality / Ethical Conduct)**:
  1. **Right Speech** (*Samyak Vak*)
  2. **Right Action** (*Samyak Karmanta*)
  3. **Right Livelihood** (*Samyak Ajiva*)
* **Samadhi (Concentration / Mental Discipline)**:
  4. **Right Effort** (*Samyak Vyayama*)
  5. **Right Concentration** (*Samyak Samadhi*)
  6. **Right Mindfulness** (*Samyak Smriti* / *Panna* $\rightarrow$ Awareness of body, breath, sensations).
* **Panna (Wisdom)**:
  7. **Right Thought / Intention** (*Samyak Sankalpa*)
  8. **Right Understanding / View** (*Samyak Drishti*)

## Three Core Philosophical Pillars of Buddhism

1. **Nairatmyavada / Anatmavada (Doctrine of No-Self)**:
   * Soul is an illusion generated by temporary aggregation of 5 Skandhas.
   * Focus effort on physical body & mental habits, not an imaginary static soul.
2. **Kshanikavada (Doctrine of Momentariness)**:
   * Everything in existence perishes every moment $\rightarrow$ Continuous process of flux.
1. **Pratityasamutpada (Law of Dependent Origination)**:
   * "This arising, that arises; this ceasing, that ceases."
   * Universe is a complex causal web $\rightarrow$ Every phenomenon is dependent on causes and acts as cause for future effects.

## Monastic Order, Triratna & Key Icons

* **Entry of Women into Sangha**:
  * First female disciple: **Mahaprajapati Gautami** (Buddha's foster mother/aunt).
  * Interceded by **Ananda** (favorite disciple) $\rightarrow$ Buddha affirmed anyone practicing Eightfold Path is part of Sangha.
* **Triratna of Buddhism**:
  1. **Buddha** (The Enlightened One)
  2. **Dhamma** (The Teachings/Laws)
  3. **Sangha** (The Monastic Order)
* **God & Idol Worship**:
  * Original Theravada/Hinayana: Silent on God, no idol worship, self-reliance via Dhamma.
  * Later Mahayana: Introduced idol worship, celestial Buddhas and savior Bodhisattvas.
* **Prominent Bodhisattvas**:
  * **Avalokiteshvara / Padmapani**: Bodhisattva of Compassion.
  * **Maitreya**: Future Buddha.
  * **Vairochana**: Celestial Buddha of cosmic space.
  * **Amitabha**: Buddha of the Pure Land.
