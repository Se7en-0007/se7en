## Mauryan Espionage System (Sources & Comparison)
* **Centralization Indicator**: All 3 independent sources emphasize an active spy system → Confirms **Highly Centralized Monarchy**.
* **Arthashastra (Kautilya)**: Detailed internal spy system (*Sanstha* & *Sanchari*, *Gudhapurusha*, *Vishakanya*).
* **Ashokan Inscriptions**:
  * **Pativedaka** (*Patravedaka / Patrikavedaka*): Reporters reporting directly to the King at all times (*Patravedaka* = Journalist/Reporter; *Patravedana* = Report).
  * **Pulisini**: Spies / vigilance officers (*Polis* = Greek root for city).
* **Indica (Megasthenes)**:
  * **Episkopoi**: Head of the espionage / spy system.

## Mauryan Judicial System
* **Core Concepts**:
  * **Dharma = Law / Morality / Duty** → Basis of law (*Yato Dharmas Tato Jayah* = "Where there is Law, there is Victory" on Supreme Court emblem).
  * **Nyaya = Logic** → Philosophy of logical knowledge (*Nyaya School*); justice relies on empirical logic.
* **Two Types of Courts (Arthashastra)**:
  1. **Dharmasthiya (Civil Court)**:
     * *Dharma* (Law) + *Sthiya / Madhyastha* (Mediation) = **Lawful Mediation / Arbitration**.
  2. **Kantakasodhana / Katakasodhana (Criminal Court)**:
     * *Kataka / Kantaka* = Military force / penal action.
     * *Sodhana* = Purification / Research.
     * *Kantakasodhana* = **Purification by Force** (Corporal punishment / beatings to purify criminal intent; medieval equivalent = *Faujdari Adalat*).
* **Four Sources of Law (Hierarchy)**:
  1. **Rajashasana (Royal Decree)** → State laws / King's executive orders (**Highest in Hierarchy**).
  2. **Dharma (Law Books)** → *Dharmasutras* & *Dharmashastras* (e.g., *Manusmriti*).
  3. **Vyavahara (Practicality / Custom)** → Traditional practices, local customs, practical necessity.
  4. **Charitra (Individual Conduct / Circumstances)** → Contextual background & nature of individuals involved in dispute.
* **Judicial Hierarchy & Officers**:
  * **Danda Root** = Punishment / Judicial authority (*Dandadhikari*, *Dandanayaka*, *Dandapati*, *Dandadhyaksha*).
  * **Central Level**: **King** (Supreme Judicial Head) & **Mahadandanayaka** (Chief Justice).
  * **Provincial Level**: **Dandanayaka**.
  * **District Level**: **Rajjuka** (Ashokan Inscriptions → Judicial powers + land measurement via *Rassi*).
  * **Village Level**: **Gramika / Gramini / Gramapati / Gram Pradhan**:
    * Administers justice alongside **Gram Vriddha** (Village Elders).
    * Both parties unanimously select **5 Elders** = **Panch** (5 fingers / fist) → **Sarpanch**.
    * Medieval terms for Sarpanch: **Mukaddam** (hence *Mukadama* = lawsuit), **Choudhary**, **Khut** (*Khap Panchayat*).

## Municipal Administration (City Governance)
* **Megasthenes' Indica**:
  * City Head: **Astronomoi** (Head of Municipal Administration).
  * Managed by **6 Committees / Boards of 5 Members Each** (Total 30 members + 1 Astronomoi = 31):
    1. **Census Committee**: Registration of births & deaths → Indicator of state centralization.
    2. **Foreigners Committee**: Assistance, screening and vigilance over foreign visitors/spies.
    3. **Excise & Industry Committee**: Manufacturing oversight & production tax.
    4. **Commerce & Sales Committee**: Trade regulation, weights and sales tax.
    5. **Customs & Tolls Committee**: Toll tax collection on goods entering city gates.
    6. **Inspection of Goods Committee**: Quality check & anti-adulteration.
* **Kautilya's Arthashastra**: City Head = **Nagaraka**.
* **Ashokan Inscriptions**: City Head = **Nagara-Viyohalaka**.

## Military Administration
* **Megasthenes' Indica**:
  * Commander-in-Chief: **Senapati**.
  * Managed by **6 Committees / Boards of 5 Members Each** (Total 30 members):
    1. **Infantry** (Foot soldiers).
    2. **Cavalry** (Horse unit).
    3. **Elephant Corps** (War elephants).
    4. **Chariots**.
    5. **Navy**.
    6. **Transport, Supply & Armory**.
* **Kautilya's Arthashastra**:
  * Commander-in-Chief: **Senani / Senapati**.
  * Four Traditional Military Arms = **Chaturanga** (Indian Army = **Chaturangini Sena**):
    1. **Padadhyaksha**: Head of Infantry (*Foot soldiers / Padata*).
    2. **Ashvadhyaksha**: Head of Cavalry (*Horses / Ashva*).
    3. **Hastyadhyaksha**: Head of Elephant Corps (*Elephants / Hasti*).
    4. **Rathadhyaksha**: Head of Chariots (*Chariots / Ratha*).
  * **Evolution of Chess**: *Chaturanga* (Sanskrit strategy game) → *Shatranj* (Arab) → *Chess*.

## Revenue System & Land Classification
* **Socio-Political Feature**: **No Landlordism / Feudalism (*Samantavada*) in Mauryan Age** → High Centralization.
  * *Samanta* Etymology: Originally meant **Neighboring King** (*Sam* = equal, *Anta* = border) in Arthashastra; later evolved into *Feudal Lord / Subordinate*.
* **Central Revenue Officers (Arthashastra)**:
  * **Samaharta / Samahatri**: Chief Collector of Revenue (Assessment & Collection).
  * **Sannidhata / Sannidhatri**: Chief Treasurer & Custodian of State Granaries/Warehouses.
    * *Why separate from Treasury Head (Koshadhyaksha)?* Most tax collected in **Kind** (grain) → Required network of local granaries.
* **Rural Administrator (Megasthenes)**: **Agronomoi** (Head of Rural Regions & Agricultural Tax).
* **Two Primary Types of Land**:
  1. **Private Land (Kshetra / Khetra / Khet)**:
     * Owned by individual farmers (**Krishaka / Kisak / Kisan**; female farmers = **Krishi Bala**).
     * Tenancy: **Upavasa** (Tenant tilling another's land).
     * Tax: **Bhaga** (Land Revenue Tax / Agricultural Share). Standard rate = **1/6th** (**Shashta**).
     * **Udakabhaga**: Water / Irrigation Tax (higher levy when state supplies irrigation).
  2. **Crown Land (Sita)**:
     * Meaning: Furrows / Trenches (*Sinchai*). Named after furrows where Sita was found by Janaka.
     * Head: **Sitadhyaksha** (Superintendent of Crown Lands).
     * Profits: 100% to State Treasury.
     * Tilled by:
       * **Karmakara / Purisa / Jana**: Hired wage laborers.
       * **Dasa**: Slaves / Prisoners of War.
       * **Visti / Vesti / Vethi / Vetti**: Forced labor tax paid via manual work by penniless/beggars.

## List of 10 Key Mauryan & Ancient Taxes
1. **Bhaga**: Standard agricultural land revenue tax (1/6th share = *Shashta*).
2. **Udakabhaga**: Irrigation / Water tax levied on state-irrigated land.
3. **Shulka**: Toll tax / Customs duty levied at city gates (*Chungi / Chungi Naka*).
4. **Pranaya**: Emergency tax levied during crises (wars, floods, droughts; literally "Tax of Love").
5. **Bali**: Famine emergency tax in Arthashastra. *(Box anchor: In Rig Vedic age, Bali = voluntary tribute; in Later Vedic age, Bali = mandatory tax).*
6. **Senabhaktam / Senabhatta**: Tax levied on villages along army march routes to pay for troop passage and prevent horses from grazing crops (origin of *Bhatta* / allowance; parallel to European *Quartering Act*).
7. **Hiranya**: Tax paid strictly in **Cash** (gold/silver) rather than kind (favored by state to reduce corruption, storage and transport loss).
8. **Pindikara**: Lump-sum tax assessed on an entire village (*Pinda* = cluster of houses) for state security, roads and infrastructure usage.
9. **Visti**: Tax paid via manual forced labor by landless/penniless individuals.
10. **Kara**: General periodic tax or specific property/hand-labor levy.

## Macro Import-Export Economy (Later Vedic to Mid-18th Century)
* **Structural Continuity**: Indian trade pattern remained stable from Later Vedic to mid-18th c. due to lack of state intervention in production technology.
* **Imports**:
  1. **Luxury Goods**: Glassware, Linen, Precious stones (**Carnelian**, **Lapis Lazuli**), Wine, Velvet (**Makhmal**), Embroidered cloth (**Zari**), Porcelain (**Chini Mitti**).
  2. **War Horses**: Heavy demand from Arabia & Central Asia via Arabian Sea routes (origin of **Horse Latitudes** & **Trade Winds**).
  3. **Gold & Silver**: Came into India as **Trade Surplus** (bullion/coins).
     * Roman Emperor **Vespasian** (1st c. AD) banned private trade with India due to gold drain.
     * Roman author **Pliny** complained of wealth drain to India for black pepper & luxury goods.
* **Exports**:
  1. **Textiles**: Cotton cloth, Woolen (**Pashmina / Cashmere**), Silk (**Varanasi/Kashi**, **Kanchipuram**, **Paithani**).
  2. **Spices & Food Grains**: Black pepper (**Yavanapriya**), ginger, food grains.
  3. **Jewelry & Perfumery**: Pearls, Ivory, Shells, Diamonds (**Golconda / Koh-i-Noor**, **Panna Emeralds**), **Gandha / Itra** (sold by **Gandhika** → root of *Gandhi*; **Mushk / Musk**).
  4. **Iron & Steel**: **Wootz Steel** / **Damascus Steel** / **Syrian Steel**.
* **Colonial Shift (18th-19th Century)**:
  * **Mercantile Phase (Post-1765 Treaty of Allahabad)**: EIC used Bengal revenue (*Diwani*) to purchase Indian goods; stopped gold/silver imports.
  * **Capitalist Phase (Charter Act 1813)**: British imposed **One-Way Free Trade** (*Calico Acts 1720/1721* in UK), destroyed Indian handicraft industry and converted India into an exporter of **Raw Cotton** and importer of British **Manufactured Textiles** (*De-industrialization*).

## Mauryan Statecraft Theories (Kautilya's Arthashastra)

### 1. Saptanga Theory (Organic Theory of State)
* State defined as an organic body with **7 Essential Organs (Limbs)**:
  1. **Swami (The King)** = **Head** → Supreme ruler and executive decision-maker.
  2. **Amatya & Mantri (Bureaucracy & Ministers)** = **Eyes** → Vision, administration and execution of state policy.
  3. **Janapada (Territory & Population)** = **Feet** → Physical base upon which the state stands and moves.
  4. **Durga (Forts / Fortified Capital)** = **Hands** → Defensive strength and military power projection.
  5. **Kosha (Treasury)** = **Face** → Financial vitality; attracts talent, allies and stability.
  6. **Bala / Danda (Army / Military Power)** = **Brain** → Source of strategic options, deterrence and force projection.
  7. **Mitra (Allies)** = **Soul / Atman** → External vital breath; an ally-less state is functionally a dead body.

### 2. Rajamandala Theory (Circle of States / Geopolitics)
* Geopolitical framework of 12 concentric circles of states.
* **Core Geopolitical Law**: **Your Neighbor's Neighbor is your Natural Ally**.
* **Classification**:
  * **Sahaja Mitra (Natural Allies)**: Odd-numbered states (1, 3, 5...) due to shared hostility toward common intermediate neighbors.
  * **Ari (Enemies) / Kritrima (Artificial Allies)**: Even-numbered states (2, 4, 6...) sharing immediate borders.
  * **Realpolitik Principle**: Geopolitical position and mutual security interests supersede religion, ideology, or personal morality (e.g., India-Japan, US-Pakistan, India-Afghanistan).
