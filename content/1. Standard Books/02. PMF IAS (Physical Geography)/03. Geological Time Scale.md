### The Evolution of The Earth’s Surface

*   **Geological Time Scale (GTS)** = Chronological division of Earth's history based on major physical, chemical, and biological changes.
*   **Time Unit Scale**: **SuperEon** ⇒ **Eon** ⇒ **Era** ⇒ **Period** ⇒ **Epoch** (expressed in **mya** = million years ago).
*   **Precambrian Supereon** (4,540 mya to 541 mya): Includes **Hadean**, **Archean**, and **Proterozoic** Eons.

---

#### Hadean Eon (4,540 mya To 4,000 mya)

*   **State of Earth**: Hot, molten, no reliable fossil record. High volcanism, radioactive heating, frequent impacts.
*   **Moon Formation**: Giant collision with planet-sized **Theia (~4.5 Bya)** ⇒ Ejected debris forms Moon.
*   **Late Heavy Bombardment (LHB) (~4.0 Bya)**: Asteroids heavily collide with inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars).
*   **Atmosphere & Hydrosphere**: 
    *   Volcanic outgassing ⇒ **Primordial atmosphere** (nearly zero $O_2$).
    *   Cooling crust + volcanism ⇒ Heavy $CO_2$ atmosphere + hydrogen + water vapour.
    *   **Liquid Water Oceans**: Existed at surface temp **230°C** because ambient atmospheric pressure was **>27 atm** (prevented boiling).
    *   Continuous cooling ⇒ $CO_2$ dissolved in oceans ⇒ Atmospheric $CO_2$ stripped.

#### Is It Possible To Boil Water (Change The State of Water From Liquid To Gas) at Room Temperature ?
*   **Yes** ⇒ **Decrease ambient pressure** (reduces resistance for water molecules to escape/evaporate).
*   **Relation**: Ambient Pressure $\downarrow$ ⇒ Boiling Point $\downarrow$.
*   **Opposite scenario**: Ambient Pressure $\uparrow$ ⇒ Liquid water stable above 100°C.

---

### Archean Eon (4,000 mya To 2,500 mya)

*   **Atmosphere & Ocean**: Still $O_2$-free; pressure **10 to 100 atm**; highly acidic oceans (due to dissolved $CO_2$).
*   **Tectonics**: Crust cooled sufficiently to initiate **continent formation**. Oldest exposed rocks on Earth date to this eon. Plate tectonics neared modern style by late Archean.
*   **Life (~3,500 mya)**: Origin of life ⇒ **cyanobacteria** emerge.
    *   Characteristics: **Prokaryotes** (simple single-celled, lacking nuclei).

---

### Proterozoic Eon (2,500 mya To 550 mya)

*   **Atmosphere & Life**: Bacteria produce $O_2$ (Oxygenation) ⇒ **Eukaryotes** emerge (possess nuclei; e.g., soft-bodied multicellular corals).
*   **Tectonics**: Cycle of supercontinents & modern mountain building (**orogeny**). Most modern continental crust formed.
    *   **Dominant Supercontinent**: **Rodinia (~1,000–750 Ma)**.
*   **Climate Tipping Point**: Early and late phases experienced **Snowball Earth** (extensive global glaciation, below-zero temperatures, dropping sea levels).
    *   **Snowball Earth** = Hypothesis that Earth's surface became entirely or nearly frozen at least once, prior to 650 Mya.

---

### Phanerozoic Eon (550 mya To Present)

*   **Characteristics**: First animal fossils; life macroscopic after **Cambrian Explosion (~541 Mya)**.
*   **Tectonics**: **Pangaea** forms ⇒ Splits into **Laurasia** (North) & **Gondwana** (South).
*   **Structure**: 3 Eras $\rightarrow$ 12 Periods.
    *   **Palaeozoic Era**: Age of arthropods, amphibians, fish, first land colonization.
    *   **Mesozoic Era**: Age of reptiles/dinosaurs; evolution of birds and mammals.
    *   **Cenozoic Era**: Age of mammals.

---

#### Paleozoic Era (550 mya To 250 mya)

##### Cambrian Period (550 mya To 485 mya)
*   **Cambrian Explosion** ⇒ Unprecedented surge in creature diversification; almost all marine phyla appeared. Algae evolved; **arthropods** dominated marine fauna.

##### Ordovician Period (485 mya To 440 mya)
*   **Fauna**: Primitive fish, corals, trilobites, snails, shellfish.
*   **Land Colonization**: First **arthropods** crept ashore (earliest terrestrial life).
*   **Gondwana Movement**: Moved from equator to South Pole ⇒ Glaciation ⇒ Sea levels dropped drastically ⇒ Coastal ecosystems devastated.
*   **Glaciation** ⇒ **Snowball Earth** ⇒ **Ordovician-Silurian extinction (First Mass Extinction)**.

##### Ordovician–Silurian Extinction (First Mass Extinction)
*   **Severity**: 2nd deadliest mass extinction in Earth's history (primarily affected marine communities).
*   **Causal Chain**: Volcanic activity erupts $\rightarrow$ Silicate rocks deposited $\rightarrow$ Weathering of silicates draws down atmospheric $CO_2$ $\rightarrow$ **Global cooling & glaciation** $\rightarrow$ Sea level drops $\rightarrow$ Mass Extinction.

##### Silurian Period (440 mya To 415 mya)
*   **Paleogeography**: Four distinct landmasses:
    1.  **Gondwana** (India, Africa, South America, Australia, Antarctica)
    2.  **Laurentia** (North America + parts of Europe)
    3.  **Baltica** (Rest of Europe)
    4.  **Siberia** (Northern Asia)

##### Devonian Period (415 mya To 360 mya)
*   **"Age of the Fishes"**: Massive diversification of fish (apex marine predators).
*   **Terrestrial Flora & Fauna**: Land plants diversified; first **trees and seeds** evolved. First **amphibians** evolved.

##### Late Devonian Extinction (Second Mass Extinction)
*   **Impact**: Wiped out 70% of all species.
*   **Causes**: Sea level fluctuations + **ocean anoxia** (triggered by global cooling or marine volcanism).

##### Carboniferous Period (360 mya To 300 mya)
*   **Swamps & Coal**: Dense tropical swamps dominated; massive vegetation burial under high carbon preservation ⇒ Major **modern coal deposits**.
*   **Land Adaptation**: Evolution of the **amniotic egg** ⇒ Allowed amphibians to move away from water and colonize deep inland.
*   **Climate**: Continuous cooling pattern ⇒ Glaciation of southern Gondwana (centered around South Pole).

##### Permian Period (300 mya To 250 mya)
*   **Geography**: All landmasses merged to form **Pangaea** supercontinent, surrounded by **Panthalassa** ocean.
*   **Climate**: Arid and extreme seasonal fluctuations (interior far from ocean temperature buffers).
*   **Flora & Fauna**: Conifers evolved and dominated land; reptiles flourished in dry settings (e.g., **Scutosaurus**, **gorgonopsids**).
*   **End**: Permian-Triassic extinction ("The Great Dying").

##### Permian–Triassic Extinction (Third Mass Extinction)
*   **Severity**: Earth's most catastrophic extinction event.
    *   **Marine species wiped**: 96%.
    *   **Terrestrial vertebrates wiped**: 70%.
    *   **Insects**: Only known mass extinction of insect classes.
*   **Causes**: Large meteor impacts + massive volcanism (**Siberian Traps**) + runaway greenhouse effect (microbial **methanogens** releasing ocean floor methane).

---

#### Mesozoic Era (250 mya To 66 mya)

##### Triassic Period (250 mya To 200 mya)
*   **Character**: Transitional period between the Permian extinction and the humid Jurassic.

###### Early Triassic (250 mya To 247 mya)
*   **Climate**: Extremely arid; deserts dominated Pangaea's interior.

###### Middle Triassic (247 mya To 237 mya)
*   **Tectonics**: **Pangaea** began to break apart; birth of the **Tethys Sea**.

###### Late Triassic (237 mya To 200 mya)
*   **Life**: Evolution of first **true dinosaurs** and **pterosaurs**.

##### Triassic–Jurassic Extinction Event (Fourth Mass Extinction)
*   **Consequences**: Large amphibians completely wiped out.
*   **Triggers**: Climate change + sea-level fluctuations + ocean acidification. Massive volcanic outgassing of $CO_2$/$SO_2$ (runaway global warming) or aerosol blockages (severe volcanic cooling).

##### Jurassic Period (200 mya To 145 mya)

###### Early Jurassic (200 mya To 175 mya)
*   First **true crocodiles** evolved (displaced remaining large amphibians).
*   First **true mammals** evolved (extremely small, shrew-sized).

###### Middle Jurassic (175 mya To 163 mya)
*   **Epoch of Reptiles**: Dinosaurs and reptiles reached their evolutionary zenith.

###### Late Jurassic (163 mya To 145 mya)
*   **Tectonics**: Pangaea fully separated into **Laurasia** and **Gondwana** during the Jurassic-Cretaceous transition.

##### Cretaceous Period (145 To 66 mya)

###### Early Cretaceous (145 mya To 100 mya)
*   First **true birds** evolved (sparked intense ecological competition with pterosaurs).

###### Late Cretaceous (100 mya To 65 mya)
*   **Climate & Life**: Global cooling restricted tropical ecosystems to the equator. Evolution of **marsupials** and first **flowering plants**.

##### Cretaceous–Paleogene Extinction Event (Fifth Extinction – 66 mya)
*   **Double Cataclysm**: 
    1.  **Deccan Traps** volcanism poisoned the atmosphere.
    2.  Massive meteor impact carved the **Chicxulub Crater** (Yucatan Peninsula, Mexico).
*   **Consequences**: Wiped out **all animals >10 kg** (including all non-avian dinosaurs).
*   **Aftermath**: **Adaptive radiation** ⇒ Survivors rapidly diverged. Mammals (horses, whales, bats, primates), birds, and fish experienced major diversification in the subsequent Paleogene.

---

#### Cenozoic Era (65 mya To present)

*   **Age of Mammals**: Mammals diversified and became the dominant land animals.

##### Paleogene Period (66 mya To 23 mya)

###### Paleocene (66 mya To 56 mya)
*   **Geography**: Continents drifted toward modern shapes but remained isolated. Afro-Eurasia separated from the Americas by the **Tethys Sea**; North and South America separated by the **Strait of Panama** (no Isthmus yet).
*   **Climate & Life**: Massive warming trend (tropical rainforests reached poles). Marine systems dominated by sharks. Mammals remained small; giant land reptiles (enormous crocodiles and snakes) acted as apex predators.

###### Eocene (56 mya To 33 mya)
*   Mammalian gigantism begins (e.g., ancestral whales grew to mammoth sizes).

###### Oligocene (33 mya To 23 mya)
*   **Grasslands**: Global expansion of grass led to herbivore diversification. First elephants, cats, dogs, and modern marsupials.
*   **Gigantism**: **Paraceratherium** (largest terrestrial mammal in Earth's history) evolved.

##### Neogene Period (23.03 mya To 2.58 mya)

###### Miocene (23.03 mya To 5.33 mya)
*   **Tethys Sea closure**: Arabian Peninsula formed, isolating Tethys remnants (forming **Black, Red, Mediterranean, and Caspian Seas**). Regional aridity increased.

###### Pliocene (5.33 mya To 2.58 mya)
*   **Climate**: Aridification expanded savannas globally. Initiation of the Indian monsoons, East Asian deserts, and early Sahara.
*   **Geography**: **Isthmus of Panama** closed, linking North and South America ⇒ Induced a major faunal interchange. Modern continental layout stabilized.
*   **Evolution**: **Australopithecus** emerged in Africa (beginning the human lineage).

##### Quaternary Period (2.58 mya To present)

###### Pleistocene (2.58 mya To 11,700 years ago)
*   **Ice Ages**: Mid-Eocene cooling culminated in repeating glacial cycles.
*   **Aridification**: Desertification in Africa birthed the **Sahara, Namib, and Kalahari deserts**.
*   **Life**: Megafauna (mammoths, dire wolves) flourished alongside **Homo sapiens**.
*   **Quaternary Extinction**: End-Pleistocene warming/hunting wiped out global megafauna and sibling hominid branches (e.g., **Neanderthals**).

###### Which of The Following Phenomena Might Have Influenced The Evolution of Organisms ?
1.  **Continental Drift**
2.  **Glacial Cycles**
*   **Answer**: **Both 1 and 2** (Both tectonics and climate oscillations drove evolutionary selection and isolation).

###### Holocene (11,700 Years Ago To Present)
*   **Recorded History**: Covers all human agricultural civilization and written history.
*   **Sixth Extinction**: Anthropogenic mass extinction starting ~10,000 years ago (extinction rates accelerated dramatically post-Industrial Revolution).
