## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Basis:** **52nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1985** added **Tenth Schedule** (*Anti-Defection Law*)
* **Amended Articles:** Modified 4 Articles - **Articles 101, 102, 190 and 191** (*vacation of seats & disqualification in Parliament & State Legislatures*)
* **subsequent Modification:** **91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003** omitted split exception provision

---

## PROVISIONS OF THE ACT

### 1. Disqualification
* **Members of Political Parties:** Disqualified if:
  1. Voluntarily gives up membership of political party
  2. Votes or abstains from voting contrary to party direction (*whip*) without prior permission, unless condoned by party within **15 days**
* **Independent Members:** Disqualified if joining **ANY** political party after election
* **Nominated Members:** Disqualified if joining any political party **AFTER expiry of 6 months** from taking seat (*can join within 6 months without disqualification*)

### 2. Exceptions
* **1. Merger:** Disqualification does **NOT** apply in case of party merger:
  * Merger valid ONLY when **>=2/3 (66.6%) members** of legislature party agree to merger
* **2. Presiding Officer:** Disqualification does **NOT** apply if elected Presiding Officer (Speaker/Chairman) voluntarily gives up party membership or rejoins after demitting office (*preserves dignity & impartiality*)
* *Deleted Provision:* **1/3 Split Exception** deleted by **91st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2003** (*defectors no longer protected under splits*)

### 3. Deciding Authority
* **Authority:** Question of disqualification decided by **Presiding Officer** (Speaker / Chairman) of concerned House
* **Time-Limit:** **NO time-limit** prescribed in Tenth Schedule for decision
* **Judicial Review (*Kihoto Hollohan v. Zachillhu, 1992*):**
  * Supreme Court declared original finality clause **unconstitutional**
  * Presiding Officer functions as a **tribunal** while adjudicating defection
  * Decision subject to **Judicial Review** by High Courts & Supreme Court on grounds of *mala fides*, perversity, etc.
  * SC upheld validity of vesting adjudicatory power in Presiding Officer

### 4. Rule-Making Power
* **Power:** Presiding Officer empowered to make rules to give effect to Tenth Schedule
* **Parliamentary Laying:** Rules placed before House for **30 days** (*House may approve, modify, or disapprove*)
* **Breach:** Wilful contravention treated as **breach of privilege** of the House
* **Complaint Requirement:** Proceedings initiated **ONLY on complaint** from a member of the House (*NOT automatic or suo motu*); accused given opportunity for explanation / reference to Committee of Privileges

## EVALUATION OF THE ACT
* **Prime Minister Rajiv Gandhi's View:** Called it *"first step towards cleaning-up public life"*

### Advantages
1. **Political Stability:** Checks unprincipled party-hopping by legislators
2. **Democratic Realignment:** Facilitates party mergers in legislature
3. **Reduces Corruption:** Curbs political corruption & non-developmental election expenditure
4. **Constitutional Recognition:** Grants first-time constitutional recognition to political parties

### Criticism
1. **Dissent vs. Defection:** Fails to differentiate honest dissent from defection; curbs freedom of conscience (*establishes party bossism*)
2. **Retail vs. Wholesale:** Irrational distinction between individual & group defection (*banned retail defection, legalised wholesale defection*)
3. **Outside Activities:** No provision for expulsion for activities outside legislature
4. **Independent vs. Nominated Member:** Anomaly in allowing nominated members 6 months while completely barring independent members
5. **Deciding Authority Concerns:** Presiding Officer may lack impartiality or legal acumen (*doubts expressed by former Speakers Rabi Ray & Shivraj Patil*)

## 91ST AMENDMENT ACT (2003)

### Reasons
1. **Strengthening Anti-Defection Law:** Address lacunae of bulk defections under split provisions
2. **Committee & Commission Recommendations:** Omission of split provision recommended by:
   * **Dinesh Goswami Committee on Electoral Reforms (1990)**
   * **Law Commission of India (170th Report, 1999)**
   * **NCRWC (Venkatachaliah Commission Report, 2002)**
3. **Debarring Defectors:** NCRWC recommended debarring defectors from holding public/ministerial posts
4. **Ceiling on Council of Ministers:** NCRWC recommended legal ceiling on oversized Councils of Ministers

### Provisions
1. **Central Council of Ministers Size:** Total Ministers (including PM) in Lok Sabha shall **NOT exceed 15%** of total strength of Lok Sabha
2. **Disqualification from Union Ministry:** Disqualified defector disqualified from being appointed Minister
3. **State Council of Ministers Size:**
   * Total Ministers (including CM) shall **NOT exceed 15%** of total strength of Legislative Assembly
   * Minimum number of Ministers (including CM) in a state shall **NOT be less than 12**
4. **Disqualification from State Ministry:** Disqualified defector disqualified from being appointed Minister in state
5. **Remunerative Political Post Bar:** Disqualified defector barred from holding any remunerative political post during remaining term or until fresh elections
   * *Remunerative Political Post:* Office under Central/State Govt paid out of public revenue, OR wholly/partially government-owned body with paid salary/remuneration (*excluding compensatory allowance*)
6. **Deletion of Split Provision:** Deleted 1/3 split exemption from Tenth Schedule
