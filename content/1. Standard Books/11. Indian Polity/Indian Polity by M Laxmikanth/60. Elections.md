## ELECTORAL SYSTEM
* **Constitutional Part & Articles:** **Part XV** (**Articles 324 to 329**)
* **8 Key Constitutional Provisions:**
  1. **Independent Election Commission (Article 324):** Vests power of superintendence, direction and conduct of elections to **Parliament, State Legislatures, Office of President and Office of Vice-President** (*Local bodies handled separately by **State Election Commission***)
  2. **Single Electoral Roll:** **One general electoral roll** for every territorial constituency (*abolished communal representation & separate electorates*)
  3. **Non-Discrimination & Equality:** No person ineligible for inclusion in electoral roll (or claim special roll) on grounds ONLY of **religion, race, caste, or sex** (**Article 325**)
  4. **Universal Adult Franchise (Article 326):** Elections to Lok Sabha and State Assemblies based on adult suffrage
     * **Voting Age:** Reduced from **21 to 18 years** by **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988** (*w.e.f. March 28, 1989*)
     * **Disqualification Grounds:** Non-residence, unsound mind, crime, or corrupt/illegal practice
  5. **Parliamentary Power (Article 327):** Parliament empowered to make provisions for electoral rolls, delimitation of constituencies and all election matters for Parliament & State Legislatures
  6. **State Legislature Power (Article 328):** State Legislatures can make election laws ONLY on matters not covered by Parliament (*supplementary power; cannot override Central law*)
  7. **Bar to Judicial Interference in Delimitation (Article 329):** Validity of delimitation or seat allotment laws **cannot be questioned in any court** (*Delimitation Commission orders are final*)
  8. **Election Petitions:** No election questioned except by an election petition presented to designated authority
     * **Triable Authority:** Tried by **High Courts alone** (*since 1966*); appellate jurisdiction lies with **Supreme Court**
     * **Article 323B & Election Tribunals:** Empowered creation of election tribunals → SC in ***L. Chandra Kumar v. Union of India (1997)*** declared Clause 3(d) unconstitutional (*appeals from tribunals lie to High Court*)

## ELECTION MACHINERY

### Key Officials & Hierarchy
1. **Election Commission of India (ECI) (Article 324):** **3-member body** (**1 Chief Election Commissioner + 2 Election Commissioners**) appointed by **President**; holds apex superintendence, direction and control
2. **Chief Electoral Officer (CEO):** Supervises election work at **State/UT level**; designated by ECI in consultation with State/UT Government
3. **District Election Officer (DEO):** Supervises election work at **District level**; State Government officer designated by ECI in consultation with State Govt
4. **Returning Officer (RO):** Responsible for conducting elections in a **Parliamentary or Assembly Constituency**; designated by ECI in consultation with State/UT Government
5. **Electoral Registration Officer (ERO):** Responsible for preparation and revision of **electoral rolls** for a constituency; appointed by ECI in consultation with State/UT Govt
6. **Presiding Officer:** Conducts poll at a **Polling Station** with assistance of Polling Officers; appointed by **DEO** (*in UTs, appointed by **RO***)

### Election Observers (3 Types Nominated by ECI)
1. **General Observers:** Monitor all stages of electoral process to ensure free and fair elections
2. **Expenditure Observers:** Monitor election expenditure of contesting candidates (assisted by Assistant Expenditure Observers)
3. **Police Observers:** **IPS officers** deployed at State/District levels based on constituency sensitivity; monitor force deployment, law & order and civil-police coordination

## ELECTION PROCESS

### 1. Time & Schedule of Elections
* **Frequency:** Every **5 years** for Lok Sabha and State Assemblies (*unless dissolved earlier*)
* **Dissolution Power:** President dissolves Lok Sabha before 5 years if Government loses confidence and no alternative available
* **Constitutional Gap Limit:** Max **6 months** between last session of dissolved House and recalling of new House
* **Notification Sequence:** ECI press conference announcement → **Model Code of Conduct (MCC)** comes into force immediately → Official Notification issued → **1 week** for candidate nomination filing → Scrutiny by RO (*summary hearing*) → **2 days** for candidate withdrawal

### 2. Oath or Affirmation
* Candidate must make & subscribe oath/affirmation before ECI-authorised officer (**RO or Assistant RO**)
* **Timing:** Immediately after presenting nomination papers, **not later than day prior to scrutiny**

### 3. Election Campaign and Model Code
* **Campaign Duration:** Lasts at least **2 weeks** from drawing list of nominated candidates; ends **48 hours before polling closes**
* **Manifestos & Rallies:** Parties issue manifestos detailing programmes, leadership strengths and opponent failures
* **Model Code of Conduct (MCC):**
  * Evolution: Evolved by consensus in **1968**; effectively put to use by ECI in **1991**
  * *Purpose:* Maintain healthy campaign, prevent conflicts, ensure level playing field and prevent ruling party from misusing official position

### 4. Polling Days & Voting Procedure
* **Staggered Polling:** Held on multiple days across constituencies for security deployment and law & order maintenance
* **Polling Station Norms:** Located in public institutions (schools/halls); within **2 km** of every voter; max **1,200 voters** per polling station; open for **at least 8 hours**
* **Voting Steps:** Secret ballot → Elector checked on electoral roll (*revised annually w.r.t. Jan 1 qualifying date*) → ID verified → Indelible ink applied on left forefinger → Voter slip issued → Ballot button activated on EVM Control Unit by Presiding Officer

### 5. Ballot Papers, EVMs and Voting Technology
* **Ballot Papers:** Printed with candidate names & symbols (*recognised parties get reserved symbols*)
* **EVM Adoption Timeline:**
  * **1998:** ECI introduced Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) on trial basis
  * **2003:** All State Assembly elections & by-elections held using EVMs
  * **2004:** **First nationwide Lok Sabha election** conducted entirely using EVMs (*>1 million EVMs used*)
* **4 Core Advantages of EVMs over Ballot Boxes:**
  1. Eliminates invalid and doubtful votes
  2. Speeds up vote counting process
  3. Eco-friendly (*saves paper and trees*)
  4. Reduces printing cost (*requires only 1 ballot sheet per Polling Station*)

### 6. Media Coverage & Opinion or Exit Poll Restrictions
* **Media Access:** ECI issues special passes for polling stations & counting halls
* **Opinion Polls Restriction:** Results **cannot be published** between **2 days prior to polling start** and close of polling
* **Exit Polls Restriction:** Results can ONLY be published **after 30 minutes of poll closing on final day of poll**

### 7. Counting of Votes & Declaration of Results
* Votes counted under supervision of **RO and ECI Observers**
* **Electoral System:** **First-Past-The-Post (FPTP) system** in single-member constituencies (*candidate securing maximum votes declared winner*)
* **Completion:** RO declares winner → ECI compiles list & issues Notification for due constitution of House → **President (LS)** or **Governor (Assembly)** convenes session

### 8. Election Petitions
* Filed by any elector or candidate for electoral malpractice
* **Jurisdiction:** Tried by **State High Court** (*appeal lies to Supreme Court*); can lead to restaging of election if upheld

## Articles Related to Elections 

| Article No. | Subject-Matter |
| :--- | :--- |
| **324** | Superintendence, direction and control of elections to be vested in an **Election Commission** |
| **325** | No person to be ineligible for inclusion in, or to claim to be included in a special, electoral roll on grounds of **religion, race, caste or sex** |
| **326** | Elections to the House of the People and to the Legislative Assemblies of states to be on the basis of **adult suffrage** |
| **327** | Power of **Parliament** to make provision with respect to elections to Legislatures |
| **328** | Power of **Legislature of a state** to make provision with respect to elections to such Legislature |
| **329** | **Bar to interference by courts** in electoral matters |
| **329A** | Special provision as to elections to Parliament in the case of Prime Minister and Speaker (*Repealed*) |
