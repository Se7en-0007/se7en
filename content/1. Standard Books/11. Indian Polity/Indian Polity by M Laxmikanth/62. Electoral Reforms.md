## ELECTORAL REFORMS BEFORE 1996

* **Lowering of Voting Age:** **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988** reduced voting age from **21 to 18 years** for Lok Sabha and Assembly elections (*w.e.f. 1989*) → Enable unrepresented youth to participate in political process
* **Deputation to Election Commission (1988/1989):** Officers/staff engaged in preparation, revision and correction of electoral rolls deemed to be on deputation to ECI → Subject to control, superintendence and discipline of ECI
* **Increase in Number of Proposers (1988/1989):** Nomination papers for Rajya Sabha and State Legislative Council elections required **10% of constituency electors OR 10 electors** (whichever is less) as proposers → Check non-serious/frivolous candidates
* **Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) (1988/1989):** Enacted provision facilitating EVM usage
  * *1998:* First experimental use in selected constituencies (Rajasthan, MP, Delhi Assemblies)
  * *1999:* First state-wide general election use in **Goa Assembly**
* **Booth Capturing (1988/1989):** Mandated adjournment of poll or countermanding of election in case of booth capturing
  * *Includes:* Seizure of polling station/ballot papers/machines, taking possession to allow only own supporters, threatening electors and seizure of counting places
* **Elector’s Photo Identity Card (EPIC) (1993):** ECI decision to issue photo identity cards nationwide → Check bogus voting and impersonation (*electoral roll forms basis for EPICs*)

## ELECTORAL REFORMS OF 1996
* **Background:** Based on **Dinesh Goswami Committee Report (1990)** appointed by National Front Govt (V.P. Singh); implemented via **Representation of the People (Amendment) Act, 1996**

* **Listing of Names of Candidates:** Contesting candidates classified into 3 distinct categories on ballot papers/lists (arranged alphabetically within each category):
  1. Candidates of recognised political parties
  2. Candidates of registered-unrecognised political parties
  3. Other (independent) candidates
* **Disqualification for Insulting the National Honour Act:** Conviction under **Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971** disqualifies candidate for **6 years** from contesting Parliament & State Legislature elections
  * *Offences:* Insulting National Flag, insulting Constitution of India, or preventing singing of National Anthem
* **Prohibition on the Sale of Liquor:** No liquor/intoxicants sold, given, or distributed in polling area within **48 hours** ending with hour fixed for conclusion of poll
* **Number of Proposers:**
  * **Unrecognised/Independent Candidates:** Subscribed by **10 registered electors** as proposers
  * **Recognised Party Candidates:** Subscribed by **1 proposer**
  * *Objective:* Discourage non-serious candidates
* **Death of a Candidate:** Election no longer countermanded on death of candidate before polling
  * *Recognised Party Candidate:* Concerned party given option to propose alternative candidate within **7 days**
* **Time Limit for By-Elections:** By-elections must be held within **6 months** of vacancy occurrence
  * *2 Exceptions:* Remaining term is **<1 year**, OR ECI certifies in consultation with Central Govt that holding elections is difficult
* **Holiday to Employees on the Polling Day:** Registered voters (including daily wagers) in trade/business/industry/establishments entitled to paid holiday (*Exception: Absence causes danger or substantial loss*)
* **Contestants Restricted to Two Constituencies:** Candidate restricted to contest from max **2 Parliamentary or Assembly constituencies** in general/by-elections (*similar restriction for Rajya Sabha & State Councils*)
* **Prohibition of Arms:** Entering neighbourhood of polling station with arms is a **cognizable offence** (*Exempts RO, Presiding Officer, police officers, or authorised order-maintenance personnel*)
* **Effective Campaigning Period Reduced:** Minimum gap between last date for withdrawal of candidature and polling date reduced from **20 to 14 days**

## ELECTORAL REFORMS AFTER 1996

* **Presidential and Vice Presidential Elections (1997):**
  * Proposers & Seconders increased: **10 to 50** for President; **5 to 20** for Vice President
  * Security deposit increased from **₹2,500 to ₹15,000** for both offices
* **Requisitioning of Staff for Election Duty (1998):** Employees of local authorities, nationalised banks, universities, LIC, PSUs and government-aided institutions made eligible for deployment on election duty
* **Voting through Postal Ballot (1999):** ECI in consultation with Govt can notify specific classes of persons eligible to vote by postal ballot
* **Facility to Opt to Vote Through Proxy (2003):** Provided to **service voters** of Armed Forces and forces under Army Act → Appoint proxy in prescribed format and notify RO
* **Declaration of Criminal Antecedents, Assets, etc., by Candidates (2003):** ECI order directing every candidate filing nomination paper to furnish affidavit disclosing:
  1. Past criminal convictions, acquittals, discharges, fines, or imprisonments
  2. Pending criminal cases (accused of offence punishable with ≥2 years imprisonment where charges framed / cognizance taken ≥6 months prior)
  3. Movable/immovable assets & bank balances of self, spouse and dependents
  4. Liabilities & financial dues to public financial institutions or government
  5. Educational qualifications
  * *False affidavit:* Constitutes an electoral offence
* **Changes in Rajya Sabha Elections (2003):**
  1. **Domicile Requirement Removed:** Candidate no longer required to be elector in the concerned state; sufficient to be registered elector in **any Parliamentary constituency in India**
  2. **Open Ballot System:** Replaced secret ballot with open ballot system to curb cross-voting and money power (*voter shows marked ballot paper to nominated agent of political party*)
* **Exemption of Travelling Expenditure (2003):** Travel expenditure incurred by star campaigners / party leaders exempted from candidate’s election expense ceiling
* **Free Supply of Electoral Rolls, etc. (2003):** Government supplies free copies of electoral rolls and prescribed materials to recognised party candidates for Lok Sabha & Assembly elections
* **Parties Entitled to Accept Contribution (2003):** Parties can accept contributions from individuals/companies (*excluding Govt companies*); contributions **>₹20,000** reported to ECI for tax relief; 100% tax exemption for donor companies
* **Allocation of Time on Electronic Media (2003):** ECI allocates equitable airtime on cable TV networks & electronic media during elections based on past poll performance of recognised parties
* **Introduction of Braille Signage Features in EVMs:**
  * *2004:* Tested in Asifnagar Assembly by-election (Andhra Pradesh)
  * *2008:* Implemented across all NCT of Delhi Assembly constituencies
  * *2009:* Rollout across 15th Lok Sabha General Elections & simultaneous Assembly elections

## ELECTORAL REFORMS SINCE 2010

* **Restrictions Imposed on Exit Polls (2009/2010):** Conducting & publishing exit poll results prohibited during notified period for Lok Sabha and Assembly elections (*prohibits dissemination via print/electronic media*)
* **Time-Limit for Submitting a Case for Disqualification (2009):** **3-month time-limit** for specified authority to submit corrupt practice cases to President for determining disqualification
* **All Officials Included in Corrupt Practice (2009):** All officials appointed/deputed by ECI (*govt or non-govt service*) brought within scope of corrupt practice of assisting candidates
* **Increase in Security Deposit (2009):**
  * **Lok Sabha:** Increased to **₹25,000** (General) and **₹12,500** (SC/ST) (*from ₹10,000 and ₹5,000*)
  * **State Assembly:** Increased to **₹10,000** (General) and **₹5,000** (SC/ST) (*from ₹5,000 and ₹2,500*)
* **Appellate Authority within the District (2009):** Appeal against Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) order lies with **District Magistrate / ADM / Executive Magistrate / Collector** (*instead of CEO*); second appeal lies with **Chief Electoral Officer (CEO)**
* **Voting Rights to Citizens of India Living Abroad (2010):** Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) retaining Indian citizenship & not registered elsewhere entitled to register in electoral roll at Indian passport address
* **Online Enrolment in the Electoral Roll (2013):** Enabled online filing of applications for electoral enrolment (*Registration of Electors (Amendment) Rules, 2013*)
* **Introduction of NOTA Option (2013):** Supreme Court directive to ECI to provide **None of the Above (NOTA)** option on EVMs/ballots to ensure secret right not to vote (*introduced in 2013 State Assemblies & 2014 Lok Sabha elections*)
  * *Rule:* Candidate getting highest votes wins even if NOTA receives more votes
* **Introduction of VVPAT (2013):** Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail prints paper slip visible for **7 seconds** (*shows serial no, name, symbol*) before dropping into sealed box
  * *History:* SC permitted in 2013 (*Indispensable for free & fair elections*); first tested in **Noksen Assembly by-election (Nagaland) in 2013**
* **Persons in Jail or Police Custody Can Contest Elections (2013):** SC in *Jan Chaukidar case (2013)* held jailed persons lose voting right & elector status → Parliament passed **RPA (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2013** negating SC order (*person does not cease to be elector due to voting prohibition in custody*)
* **Immediate Disqualification of Convicted MPs and MLAs (2013):** SC in ***Lily Thomas v. Union of India (2013)*** struck down **Section 8(4) of RPA 1951** as unconstitutional → Convicted MPs/MLAs face **immediate disqualification** without 3-month appeal window
* **Photos of Candidates on EVMs and Ballot Papers (2015):** ECI mandated candidate photographs alongside name & symbol for elections held post **May 1, 2015** to prevent confusion among namesakes (*non-submission is NOT ground for nomination rejection*)
* **Ceiling on Cash Donations Lowered (2017):** Anonymous cash donation limit to political parties reduced from **₹20,000 to ₹2,000** (*donations >₹2,000 require recorded donor details*)
* **Cap on Corporate Contributions Lifted (2017):** Removed **7.5% net profit cap** on corporate donations & lifted requirement to disclose party names in P&L account
* **Introduction of Electoral Bonds (2018):** Promissory note bearer banking instruments (*no buyer/payee name*) issued in denominations of **₹1,000, ₹10,000, ₹1,00,00,000, ₹10,00,000 and ₹1,00,00,000**
  * *Eligibility:* Only registered parties securing **≥1% votes** in last LS or Assembly election
  * *Encashment:* Exclusively via authorized bank accounts; buyer info kept confidential by bank
* **Foreign Funding Allowed (2018):** FCRA 2010 amended to redefine foreign companies → Political parties allowed to receive foreign corporate funding
* **Election Laws (Amendment) Act, 2021:**
  1. **Aadhaar Linking:** Enabled voluntary linking of electoral roll data with Aadhaar ecosystem to curb duplicate enrolments
  2. **4 Qualifying Dates:** Established **1st January, 1st April, 1st July and 1st October** as quarterly qualifying dates for voter registration (*earlier only Jan 1*)
  3. **Gender-Neutral Service Voting:** Replaced 'wife' with **'spouse'** to allow male/female spouses of service voters to vote by proxy/postal ballot
  4. **Expanded Premises Requisition:** Expanded power to requisition premises for counting, machine/VVPAT storage, poll material and security/polling staff accommodation
