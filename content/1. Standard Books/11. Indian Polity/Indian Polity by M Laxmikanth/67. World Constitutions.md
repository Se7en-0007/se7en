## AMERICAN CONSTITUTION
* **Origin & Adoption:** Formed **1787** following American Revolution (1775–1783) → Adopted at **Philadelphia Convention (1787)** → Came into force **1789**

### Salient Features
* **1. Written Constitution:** Oldest existing written constitution; ~12 pages; consists of **Preamble + 7 Articles + 27 Amendments**
* **2. Rigid Constitution:** Cannot be amended like ordinary laws → Special amendment procedure under Article V:
  * *Method 1:* Proposal by **2/3 votes of both Houses of Congress** → Ratification by legislatures of **3/4 (38 of 50) States** within 7 years
  * *Method 2:* Proposal by Constitutional Convention called by Congress on petition of **2/3 (34 of 50) State Legislatures** → Ratification by convention in **3/4 (38 of 50) States**
  * *Result:* Amended only **27 times** since 1789
* **3. Federal Constitution:** First & oldest modern federal state; **50 states + District of Columbia** (*originally 13 states*); enumerated limited powers to Centre, **residuary powers to States**; each state has own Constitution, legislature, governor & Supreme Court
* **4. Presidential Government:** Chief executive = President (Head of State + Head of Government)
  * Fixed 4-year tenure; removed only by impeachment
  * Governs via advisory ‘Kitchen Cabinet’ (non-elected departmental secretaries, responsible solely to President)
  * President & Secretaries not members of Congress, do not attend sessions, not responsible to Congress
  * President cannot dissolve House of Representatives
* **5. Separation of Powers:** Basis of constitutional system:
  * **Article I:** Legislative powers → **Congress**
  * **Article II:** Executive powers → **President**
  * **Article III:** Judicial powers → **Supreme Court & inferior courts**
* **6. Checks and Balances:** Prevents autocracy via mutual partial control:
  * President can veto bills (Pocket veto & Qualified veto)
  * Senate confirms higher appointments & international treaties
  * Congress determines judicial organisation & appellate jurisdiction
  * President appoints judges with Senate consent
  * Supreme Court can declare Congressional laws & Presidential orders *ultra vires*
* **7. Supremacy of Constitution and Judicial Review:** Hierarchy of laws → Written Constitution = supreme law; SC = custodian via judicial review (*declares unconstitutional laws ultra vires*)
* **8. Bill of Rights:** Added **1791** via first 10 amendments; guarantees life, liberty & property under **due process of law**
* **9. Bicameralism:** **Congress** = **Senate** (Upper House; 100 members, 2 per state, fixed 6-year term; most powerful second chamber in world) + **House of Representatives** (Lower House; 435 members, single-member constituencies, fixed 2-year term)

---

### American President
* **Mode of Election:** Indirect constitutionally via Electoral College, but effectively direct due to political parties/conventions
  * **Electoral College Composition:** **538 total votes** (**435** House + **100** Senate + **3** District of Columbia); candidates need **270 votes** to win
  * Electors are non-Congress members elected directly by state voters; college dissolves post-election
  * *Deadlock:* If no majority, **House of Representatives** elects President from top 3 candidates (*occurred 1800, 1824, 1876*)
* **Qualifications, Term and Removal:**
  * *Qualifications:* (1) Natural born US citizen (*naturalised ineligible*), (2) ≥35 years of age, (3) US resident for ≥14 years
  * *Term:* **4 years**; max **2 terms** / 10 years (**22nd Amendment Act, 1951**); term starts **January 20**
  * *Removal:* Impeachment for treason, bribery, or high crimes/misdemeanors → Initiated by House of Representatives (majority vote) → Tried by Senate as judicial tribunal chaired by **Chief Justice of Supreme Court** → Requires **2/3 Senate majority** (*5 attempts: Andrew Johnson 1868, Richard Nixon 1874/resigned, Bill Clinton 1998, Donald Trump 2019 & 2021; none removed*)
* **Powers and Functions:** Strongest democratic office (*"greatest office in the world" - Lord Bryce / Munro / Laski*)
  1. Enforces Constitution, federal laws, treaties, and court decisions
  2. Supreme Commander of US Armed Forces
  3. Appoints Supreme Court judges, ambassadors, department heads, diplomats, consuls
  4. Formulates foreign policy & conducts foreign affairs
  5. Grants pardons/reprieves for federal offences (*except impeachment*)
  6. Bill Veto options: (i) Assent, (ii) Rejection → Return to Congress → Overridden by **2/3 majority in both Houses** (*Qualified Veto*), (iii) Reserve bill → Law after 10 days without signature; if Congress adjourns within 10 days, bill dies (*Pocket Veto*)
  7. Sends legislative messages to Congress; calls special sessions
  8. Prepares national budget for Congress approval
  9. Issues executive orders (*delegated legislation*)
  10. Adjourns Congress in case of disagreement between Houses on adjournment date

## BRITISH CONSTITUTION
* **Origin & Nature:** Constitution of UK (Great Britain = England + Wales {1535} + Scotland {1707}; Northern Ireland added {1921}); oldest constitutional & democratic system ("Mother of Constitutions"); blend of monarchy, aristocracy & democracy

### Salient Features
* **1. Unwritten Constitution:** Evolved over history (*not enacted*); dynamic blend of formal law, precedent & tradition (*L.S. Amery*); 5 sources:
  1. **Conventions:** Unwritten political practices / custom maxims (e.g. Sovereign acts on Cabinet advice; appoints majority leader in Commons as PM; dissolves Commons on PM advice; assents to all bills; Cabinet collectively responsible to Commons)
  2. **Great Charters:** Historical landmarks defining Crown powers & civic liberties (Magna Carta 1215, Petition of Rights 1628, Bill of Rights 1689)
  3. **Statutes:** Parliamentary Acts regulating institutions (Habeas Corpus Act 1679, Statute of Westminster 1931, Ministers of the Crown Act 1937, People’s Representation Act 1948)
  4. **Common Law:** Judge-made law & immutable legal usage enforced by courts
  5. **Legal Commentaries:** Textbooks by experts (Dicey’s *Law of the Constitution*, Bagehot’s *English Constitution*, Blackstone’s *Commentaries*)
* **2. Flexible Constitution:** No special amendment procedure; amended by Parliament via ordinary law-making process; no distinction between constitutional & ordinary law
* **3. Unitary Constitution:** Supreme power vested in Central Government in London; local bodies created/abolished by Centre
* **4. Parliamentary Government:** Executive derived from & responsible to legislature
  * Nominal executive = King/Queen (Head of State); Real executive = Cabinet / PM (Head of Government)
  * Majority party forms government; Ministers individually & collectively responsible to House of Commons
  * Sovereign dissolves House of Commons on PM advice; Ministers are MPs
* **5. Sovereignty of Parliament:** Cardinal principle; Parliament can make, amend, or repeal any law (*"can do everything except make a woman a man and a man a woman" - De Lolme*); no distinction between constituent & legislative authority; **NO judicial review**
* **6. Rule of Law:** Enunciated by **A.V. Dicey (1885)** - 3 implications:
  1. Absence of arbitrary power (punishment only for breach of law)
  2. Equality before the law (all citizens subject to ordinary courts)
  3. Primacy of individual rights (Constitution results from judicial decisions defining rights, not vice versa)
* **7. Constitutional Monarchy:** Limited hereditary monarchy; Crown = institution (immortal), King = person (mortal) → *"The King is dead; long live the King"*; Monarch reigns but does not rule
* **8. Bicameralism:** **House of Lords** (Upper House; 677 appointed/hereditary members, aristocratic element, oldest second chamber) + **House of Commons** (Lower House; 659 elected members, popular & dominant body)

---

### British Cabinet
* **Composition:** Real executive headed by Prime Minister + ~20 senior ministers (Chancellor of the Exchequer, Lord Privy Seal, Lord President of Council, Lord Chancellor, Secretaries of State for Home, Foreign, Defence, etc.; *AG, SG, Lord Advocate, Paymaster-General excluded*)
* **Privy Council:** Originated 15th century as advisory body to monarch; 330 members (includes past & present cabinet ministers); presided by Lord President of the Council; Cabinet functions as its inner committee
* **Prime Ministerial Government:** PM evolved from *primus inter pares* (first among equals) to dominant figure (*Crossman, Mackintosh*)
* **Shadow Cabinet:** Unique institution formed by official Opposition to balance ruling cabinet & prepare for office; members shadow corresponding ministers; Leader of Opposition = "alternative PM" (holds ministerial status & salary)

## FRENCH CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** French Revolution (1789–1799) → Adopted 16 Constitutions since revolution (~every 12 years); **Fifth Republic Constitution** enacted **1958** under General de Gaulle for strong/stable government

### Salient Features
* **1. Written Constitution:** Preamble + 92 Articles in 15 Chapters; motto = *"Liberty, Equality, Fraternity"*; France = *"indivisible, secular, democratic and social republic"*

#### French Constitution (As Amended)
* **Preamble:** Article 1
* **Chapter I:** Sovereignty (Arts 2 to 4)
* **Chapter II:** The President of the Republic (Arts 5 to 19)
* **Chapter III:** The Government (Arts 20 to 23)
* **Chapter IV:** Parliament (Arts 24 to 33)
* **Chapter V:** Relations between Parliament and Government (Arts 34 to 51-2)
* **Chapter VI:** Treaties and International Agreements (Arts 52 to 55)
* **Chapter VII:** The Constitutional Council (Arts 56 to 63)
* **Chapter VIII:** The Judicial Authority (Arts 64 to 66-1)
* **Chapter IX:** The High Court (Arts 67 to 68)
* **Chapter X:** The Criminal Liability of the Government (Arts 68-1 to 68-3)
* **Chapter XI:** The Economic, Social and Environmental Council (Arts 69 to 71)
* **Chapter XI-A:** The Defender of Rights (Art 71-1)
* **Chapter XII:** Territorial Communities (Arts 72 to 75-1)
* **Chapter XIII:** Transitional Provisions Pertaining to New Caledonia (Arts 76 to 77)
* **Chapter XIV:** On the French-Speaking World and Association Agreements (Arts 87 to 88) (*Arts 78 to 86 repealed*)
* **Chapter XV:** On the European Union (Arts 88-1 to 88-7)
* **Chapter XVI:** On Amendments to the Constitution (Art 89)

---

* **2. Rigid Constitution:** Special amendment procedure: 60% majority in both Houses of Parliament, OR national referendum called by President; republican form of government **cannot be amended**
* **3. Unitary Constitution:** All powers in Central Government at Paris; local units created/abolished for administrative convenience
* **4. Quasi-Presidential and Quasi-Parliamentary:** Hybrid system: powerful directly elected President + nominated Council of Ministers headed by PM responsible to Parliament (*Ministers CANNOT be MPs*)

#### Amended Provisions Relating to French President
1. **Mode of Election:** Indirect → **Direct election** (*1962 Referendum*)
2. **Tenure of Office:** 7 years → **5 years** (*2000 Amendment*)
3. **Number of Terms Allowed:** No limit → **Max 2 consecutive terms** (*2008 Amendment*)

---

* **5. Bicameralism:** **National Assembly** (Lower House; 577 directly elected members, 5-year term, dominant) + **Senate** (Upper House; 348 indirectly elected members, 6-year term)
* **6. Rationalised Parliament:** Restricted law-making powers specified in Constitution; non-enumerated subjects legislated via executive decree / delegated legislation
* **7. Constitutional Council:** 9 members appointed for 9 years; advisory watchdog ensuring decrees/laws conform to Constitution (*opinions non-binding*)
* **8. Recognition of Political Parties:** First French Republican Constitution to formally recognise political parties (*must respect national sovereignty & democracy*)

---

### French President
* **Mode of Election:** Direct election by universal suffrage via 2-ballot system (absolute majority in 1st ballot, or runoff between top 2 candidates in 2nd ballot)
* **Tenure and Removal:** 5-year term (max 2 consecutive terms); no formal age/qualification criteria; interim vacancy handled by **President of Senate**; removed via impeachment for high treason (absolute majority in both Houses → trial by High Court of Justice)
* **Powers and Functions:** Real head of state & nation’s pivot:
  1. Appoints PM and accepts resignation
  2. Appoints & dismisses Ministers on PM advice
  3. Presides over Council of Ministers meetings
  4. Appoints civil & military officers; Commander-in-Chief
  5. Negotiates & ratifies treaties
  6. Appoints President & 3 members of Constitutional Council
  7. Promulgates laws within 15 days (can request parliamentary reconsideration)
  8. Submits government bills to referendum
  9. Presides over Higher Council of Judiciary & appoints 9 members
  10. Exercises special emergency powers (after consulting PM, Assembly Presidents & Constitutional Council)
  11. Dissolves National Assembly (after consulting PM & Assembly Presidents; max once per 12 months, prohibited during emergency)

## JAPANESE CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** Meiji Restoration (1868) → Meiji Constitution (1889–1947; autocratic) → Post-WWII Allied Occupation under Gen. Douglas MacArthur → New democratic Constitution adopted **1946**, operative **1947** (*MacArthur / Showa Constitution; "Showa" = Radiant Peace*) under Emperor Hirohito & PM Shidehara

### Salient Features
* **1. Written Constitution:** Preamble + 103 Articles in 11 Chapters; popular sovereignty; blend of US & British systems

#### Japanese Constitution
* **Chapter I:** The Emperor (Arts 1 to 8)
* **Chapter II:** Renunciation of War (Art 9)
* **Chapter III:** Rights and Duties of the People (Arts 10 to 40)
* **Chapter IV:** The Diet (Arts 41 to 64)
* **Chapter V:** The Cabinet (Arts 65 to 75)
* **Chapter VI:** Judiciary (Arts 76 to 82)
* **Chapter VII:** Finance (Arts 83 to 91)
* **Chapter VIII:** Local Self-government (Arts 92 to 95)
* **Chapter IX:** Amendments (Art 96)
* **Chapter X:** Supreme Law (Arts 97 to 99)
* **Chapter XI:** Supplementary Provisions (Arts 100 to 103)

---

* **2. Rigid Constitution:** Amendment initiated by Diet (2/3 majority of both Houses) → Approved by popular majority in special referendum / election → Promulgated by Emperor; **NEVER amended till date** (remains as in 1947)
* **3. Unitary Constitution:** Central Government in Tokyo holds supreme authority over provinces
* **4. Parliamentary Government:** British pattern with 4 differences:
  * PM chosen by Diet resolution, appointed by Emperor
  * PM appoints & removes Ministers directly
  * Only majority (*not all*) of Ministers must be Diet members
  * Cabinet collectively responsible to Diet; Emperor dissolves House of Representatives on PM advice
* **5. Constitutional Monarchy:** Emperor = symbol of state & unity (*sovereignty resides with people*); hereditary; performs purely ceremonial functions on Cabinet advice/approval
* **6. Supremacy of Constitution and Judicial Review:** Supreme Court holds explicit constitutional power of judicial review over laws/orders
* **7. Fundamental Rights:** Elaborate Bill of Rights (31 Articles, Arts 10–40) - "eternal and inviolate" civil, political, economic, educational & cultural rights
* **8. Renunciation of War (Article 9):** Unique provision forever renouncing war & threat/use of force; prohibits land/sea/air forces & belligerency right (*maintains 'Self-Defence Forces' for national security*)
* **9. Bicameralism:** **Diet** = **House of Councillors** (Upper House; 252 members, 6-year term; 152 local + 100 national) + **House of Representatives** (Lower House; 512 members, 4-year term, dominant in finance); Diet = highest state organ & sole law-maker

## SOVIET CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** Formed **1917** post-Bolshevik Revolution led by **V.I. Lenin**; 4 Constitutions (1918, 1924, 1936 Stalin, 1977 Brezhnev); collapsed 1989–1991; first socialist state based on Marxism-Leninism

### Salient Features (1977 Brezhnev Constitution)
* **1. Written Constitution:** Drafted under Brezhnev; 9 Parts, 20 Chapters, 174 Articles
* **2. Rigid Constitution:** Amended by **2/3 majority in each House** of Supreme Soviet
* **3. Socialist Constitution:** State ownership of production means (state property + collective farm/cooperative property)
* **4. Federal Constitution:** "Federation of Federations" comprising **15 Union Republics** (*with right to secede*) + 20 Autonomous Republics, 8 Autonomous Regions & 10 Autonomous Areas; residuary powers to units
* **5. Parliamentary Government:** Executive = Council of Ministers headed by Premier, elected by & responsible to Supreme Soviet
* **6. Bicameralism:** **Supreme Soviet** = **Soviet of the Union** (Lower House; 750 members) + **Soviet of the Nationalities** (Upper House; 750 members); 5-year term, equal powers
* **7. Presidium:** 39-member standing committee of Supreme Soviet (Chairman = Head of State); collective/plural executive ("Collegial Executive") combining executive, legislative, diplomatic & judicial functions (*20th Century Innovation*)
* **8. One-Party Dictatorship:** Monopoly of power by **Communist Party of the Soviet Union (CPSU)** as leading/guiding force
* **9. Democratic Centralism:** Electiveness of state bodies + strict subordination of lower bodies to higher ones
* **10. Fundamental Rights:** Socialist rights (Right to work, rest/leisure, health, housing, education, free speech, etc.) subordinate to state/society interests
* **11. Fundamental Duties:** Inseparable from rights (observe Constitution/laws, labour discipline, protect socialist property, defend motherland, military service, protect nature/monuments, promote world peace)

## RUSSIAN CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** Dissolution of USSR (Dec 1991) → Russia (75% area, 50% population of USSR) adopted new Constitution **Dec 20, 1993**
* **Key Features:**
  1. Sovereign multi-ethnic federal republic
  2. **Federation Units:** 21 Republics, 6 Krai (Territories), 49 Oblast (Regions), 10 Autonomous Okrug, 2 Federal Cities (Moscow & St. Petersburg), 1 Autonomous Region (Birobijan)
  3. Liberal-democratic order with guaranteed fundamental rights & multi-party system
  4. Separation of powers among independent executive, legislature & judiciary
  5. **Bicameral Federal Assembly:** **Federation Council** (Upper House; 178 members, 2 per unit) + **State Duma** (Lower House; 450 directly elected members, 4-year term)
  6. **President:** Head of State & Commander-in-Chief, directly elected for 4 years; appoints PM (Chairman of Government) & ministers; can dismiss ministers
  7. **Impeachment:** Federal Assembly removes President for high treason/grave crime (2/3 majority in both Houses)
  8. **Constitutional Court:** 19 members deciding constitutionality of decrees, orders & laws

## CHINESE CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** Communist Revolution (1949) led by Mao Zedong → 4 Constitutions (1954, 1975, 1978, 1982); 1982 Constitution promulgated **December 4, 1982**

### Salient Features
* **1. Written Constitution:** Preamble + 138 Articles in 4 Chapters

#### Chinese Constitution
* **Chapter I:** General Principles (Arts 1 to 32)
* **Chapter II:** Fundamental Rights and Obligations of Citizens (Arts 33 to 56)
* **Chapter III:** State Institutions (Arts 57 to 140):
  * Sec 1: National People’s Congress
  * Sec 2: President of PRC
  * Sec 3: State Council
  * Sec 4: Central Military Commission
  * Sec 5: Local People’s Congresses & Governments
  * Sec 6: Autonomous Organs of Ethnic Areas
  * Sec 7: Commissions of Supervision
  * Sec 8: People’s Courts & Procuratorates
* **Chapter IV:** National Flag, National Anthem, National Emblem and Capital (Arts 141 to 143)

---

* **2. Rigid Constitution:** Amended by **National People’s Congress (NPC)** by **2/3 majority**; proposed by Standing Committee or **≥1/5 NPC deputies**
* **3. Socialist Constitution:** Socialist state under people's democratic dictatorship led by working class; CPC leadership = defining feature
* **4. Unitary Constitution:** Unitary multinational state; all powers in Central Government in Beijing
* **5. Parliamentary Government:**
  * **State Council** (Central People’s Government) = highest executive/administrative organ headed by Premier, responsible to NPC
  * Premier chosen by NPC on President nomination; President appoints/removes Premier
  * **President of China:** Ceremonial head of state; directly elected by NPC for 5 years (min age 45)
* **6. Unicameralism:** **National People’s Congress (NPC)** = unicameral supreme state power organ (~3,000 deputies, 5-year term); permanent organ = **Standing Committee** (~200 members)
* **7. Communist Party Leadership:** Multi-party cooperation under explicit CPC leadership (guided by Marxism-Leninism, Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, Three Represents, Scientific Outlook on Development, Xi Jinping Thought)
* **8. Democratic Centralism:** State organs elected democratically, responsible to & overseen by People's Congresses
* **9. Fundamental Rights:** Civil, political, economic, social & cultural rights (equality, voting, speech/press, religion, work, education, etc.)
* **10. Fundamental Duties:** Safeguard national unity, abide by Constitution/laws, keep state secrets, protect public property, defend motherland, military service, tax payment, work, education, family planning

## SWISS CONSTITUTION
* **Origin:** 3 Constitutions (1848 federal state, 1874, 1999); 1999 Constitution adopted Dec 18, 1998, approved April 18, 1999, operative **January 1, 2000**

### Salient Features
* **1. Written Constitution:** Comprehensive document; Preamble + 196 Articles in 6 Titles

#### Swiss Constitution
* **Title I:** General Provisions (Arts 1 to 6)
* **Title II:** Fundamental Rights, Citizenship and Social Goals (Arts 7 to 41)
  * Ch 1: Fundamental Rights | Ch 2: Citizenship & Political Rights | Ch 3: Social Goals
* **Title III:** Confederation, Cantons and Communes (Arts 42 to 135)
  * Ch 1: Confederation-Canton Relations | Ch 2: Powers | Ch 3: Financial System
* **Title IV:** The People and the Cantons (Arts 136 to 142)
  * Ch 1: General Provisions | Ch 2: Initiative and Referendum
* **Title V:** Federal Authorities (Arts 143 to 191c)
  * Ch 1: General Provisions | Ch 2: Federal Assembly | Ch 3: Federal Council | Ch 4: Federal Supreme Court
* **Title VI:** Revision of the Federal Constitution and Transitional Provisions (Arts 192 to 197)

---

* **2. Rigid Constitution:** 2 types of revision (Total & Partial):
  * *Total Revision:* Proposed by people (100,000 voters) or Federal Assembly chambers; requires popular + cantonal referendum
  * *Partial Revision:* Requested by 100,000 voters (unformulative/general proposal or formulative/specific draft) or Federal Assembly; must respect cohesion of subject matter & form
  * Enters into force upon approval by **majority of People AND Cantons**
* **3. Federal Constitution:** Federal republic of **26 cantons** (20 full cantons + 6 half cantons); residuary powers to cantons; each canton has own constitution/governance
  * *Full vs. Half Cantons:* Half cantons have **1 representative** in Council of States (*vs 2 for full*) and **half vote** on constitutional revisions (*vs 1 for full*)
* **4. Council Model of Government:** Unique plural/collegial executive = **Federal Council (Bundesrat)**
  * **7 co-equal members** elected by Federal Assembly for 4-year term (reflecting regional/linguistic diversity)
  * President & Vice-President elected for 1-year non-renewable term from members (*President = primus inter pares, ceremonial head*)
  * Non-partisan, non-removable by no-confidence or impeachment; participate in Assembly without voting rights
* **5. Bicameralism:** **Federal Assembly** = **Council of States** (Upper House; 46 cantonal reps, 2 per full / 1 per half) + **National Council** (Lower House; 200 popular reps elected via proportional representation, 4-year term); both Houses have **strictly equal powers** (*C.F. Strong*)
* **6. Direct Democracy:** Direct citizen participation via:
  * **Mandatory Referendum:** Constitutional amendments, security organisation accessions, non-constitutional emergency acts → voted by People & Cantons; popular initiatives for revision → voted by People
  * **Optional Referendum:** Requested by **50,000 voters OR 8 cantons** on federal acts, emergency acts (>1 yr validity), treaties
  * **Popular Initiative:** **100,000 voters** can propose total/partial constitutional revision
* **7. Triple Citizenship:** Citizenship of **Commune (Municipality)** → **Canton** → **Federation** (*Communal citizenship = foundation*)
* **8. Fundamental Rights:** Comprehensive list (human dignity, equality, life/liberty, privacy, property, media, assembly, legal recourse, etc.)
* **9. Social Goals of the Government:** State endeavour goals (social security, healthcare, family protection, fair work, housing, education, old-age protection; *non-enforceable direct rights*)
