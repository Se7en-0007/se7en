## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Location:** **Part XXI** entitled *‘Temporary, Transitional and Special Provisions’* (**Articles 371 to 371-J**)
* **Coverage:** Special provisions for **12 states** (**Maharashtra, Gujarat, Nagaland, Assam, Manipur, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Sikkim, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Goa and Karnataka**)
* **4 Core Objectives:**
  1. Meet aspirations of people of backward regions
  2. Protect cultural and economic interests of tribal people
  3. Deal with disturbed law and order conditions
  4. Protect interests of local people
* **Origin:** Not in original Constitution → Incorporated via subsequent **Constitutional Amendments** during reorganisation or statehood conferment

---

## PROVISIONS FOR MAHARASHTRA AND GUJARAT
* **Article 371:** President authorises Governors of Maharashtra and Gujarat to have special responsibility for:
  1. **Development Boards:** Establishment of separate development boards for:
     * **Maharashtra:** Vidarbha, Marathwada and rest of Maharashtra
     * **Gujarat:** Saurashtra, Kutch and rest of Gujarat
  2. **Annual Report:** Working report of boards placed every year before State Legislative Assembly
  3. **Development Funds:** Equitable allocation of funds for developmental expenditure
  4. **Education & Employment:** Equitable facilities for technical education, vocational training and employment in state services

## PROVISIONS FOR NAGALAND
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **13th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1962** → **Article 371-A**
* **4 Exemption Areas (Parliamentary Acts non-applicable unless State Assembly decides):**
  1. Religious or social practices of the Nagas
  2. Naga customary law and procedure
  3. Administration of civil and criminal justice involving decisions according to Naga customary law
  4. Ownership and transfer of land and its resources
* **Governor’s Special Responsibilities:**
  1. **Law & Order:** Special responsibility so long as internal disturbances by hostile Nagas continue (*acts in individual judgment after consulting Council of Ministers; ceases when President directs*)
  2. **Earmarked Funds:** Ensures Central development grants are included in demand for grant relating to specific purpose
* **Tuensang District Special Setup:**
  * **Regional Council:** **35-member regional council** established for Tuensang district (*Ex-officio Chairman = Deputy Commissioner; Vice-Chairman elected*)
  * **10-Year Administration Provisions:**
    * Administered directly by Governor
    * Equitable money distribution between Tuensang & rest of Nagaland in Governor’s discretion
    * Nagaland Assembly Acts apply **ONLY on Governor’s direction** (on Regional Council recommendation)
    * Governor can make Regulations repealing/amending Parliamentary Acts
    * Dedicated **Minister for Tuensang Affairs** appointed from Tuensang assembly members
    * Governor's final decision in discretion
    * Tuensang members in Nagaland Assembly **elected by Regional Council** (*not directly by people*)

## PROVISIONS FOR ASSAM AND MANIPUR

### Assam
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **22nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1969** → **Article 371-B**
* **Assembly Committee:** President empowered to create a committee of Assam Legislative Assembly consisting of members elected from **Tribal Areas** (Sixth Schedule: North Cachar Hills, Karbi Anglong, Bodoland Territorial Areas)

### Manipur
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **27th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1971** → **Article 371-C**
* **4 Special Provisions:**
  1. Creation of a committee of Manipur Legislative Assembly consisting of members elected from **Hill Areas**
  2. Governor's special responsibility to secure proper functioning of Hill Areas Committee
  3. Governor submits annual report to President regarding Hill Areas administration
  4. Central Government can issue directions to State Govt regarding Hill Areas administration

---

## PROVISIONS FOR ANDHRA PRADESH OR TELANGANA
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **32nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1973** → **Articles 371-D and 371-E**
  * *Telangana Extension:* Article 371-D extended to **Telangana** by **Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014**

### Article 371-D (Equitable Opportunities & Administrative Tribunal)
1. **Equitable Opportunities:** President empowers State Govt to ensure equitable opportunities/facilities in public employment and education for different parts of state
2. **Local Cadres & Reservations:** President may direct creation of local cadres for civil posts and local area reservations for educational admissions
3. **Administrative Tribunal (APAT):**
   * President established Administrative Tribunal to deal with civil post appointments, allotments and promotions
   * Functions **outside purview of State High Court** (*jurisdiction subject ONLY to Supreme Court*)
   * President can abolish Tribunal when no longer necessary

### Article 371-E (Central University)
* Empowers Parliament to establish a **Central University** in Andhra Pradesh

## PROVISIONS FOR SIKKIM
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **36th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1975** → **Article 371-F** (*full-fledged statehood*)
* **5 Special Provisions:**
  1. **Assembly Strength:** Sikkim Legislative Assembly to consist of **not less than 30 members**
  2. **Lok Sabha Representation:** **1 seat** allotted in Lok Sabha (entire state forms 1 Parliamentary constituency)
  3. **Sectional Representation:** Parliament empowered to reserve Assembly seats & delimit constituencies to protect rights/interests of different sections of Sikkim population
  4. **Governor’s Special Responsibility:** Governor has special responsibility for peace & equitable social/economic advancement (*acts in discretion subject to President’s directions*)
  5. **Extension of Laws:** President empowered to extend any state law of Indian Union to Sikkim with modifications/restrictions

---

## PROVISIONS FOR MIZORAM
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **53rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1986** → **Article 371-G**
* **4 Exemption Areas (Parliamentary Acts non-applicable unless State Assembly decides):**
  1. Religious or social practices of the Mizos
  2. Mizo customary law and procedure
  3. Administration of civil and criminal justice involving decisions according to Mizo customary law
  4. Ownership and transfer of land
* **Assembly Strength:** Legislative Assembly to consist of **not less than 40 members**

---

## PROVISIONS FOR ARUNACHAL PRADESH AND GOA

### Arunachal Pradesh
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **55th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1986** → **Article 371-H**
* **Special Provisions:**
  1. Governor has **special responsibility for law and order** (*acts in individual judgment after consulting Council of Ministers; decision final; ceases when President directs*)
  2. Legislative Assembly to consist of **not less than 30 members**

### Goa
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **56th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1987** → **Article 371-I**
* **Assembly Strength:** Legislative Assembly to consist of **not less than 30 members**

---

## PROVISIONS FOR KARNATAKA
* **Enacting Amendment:** Inserted by **98th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2012** → **Article 371-J**
* **Background:** 2010 Assembly & Council resolutions sought special provisions to accelerate development of backward northern region & reduce inter-regional disparities
* **Governor’s Special Responsibilities for Hyderabad-Karnataka Region (6 Districts: Gulbarga, Bidar, Raichur, Koppal, Yadgir, Bellary):**
  1. Establishment of a separate **Development Board** for Hyderabad-Karnataka region
  2. Annual report on working of Board placed before State Legislative Assembly
  3. Equitable allocation of funds for regional developmental expenditure
  4. **Seat Reservation:** Reservation of seats in educational and vocational training institutions for local region students
  5. **Post Reservation:** Reservation in state government posts (local cadres) for local region candidates

* **Article 371E:** Establishment of Central University in Andhra Pradesh
* **Article 371F:** Special provisions with respect to the state of Sikkim
* **Article 371G:** Special provision with respect to the state of Mizoram
* **Article 371H:** Special provision with respect to the state of Arunachal Pradesh

* **Article 371J:** Special provisions with respect to the state of Karnataka
