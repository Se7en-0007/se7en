## INTRODUCTION
* **Definition:** Act or process of fixing limits or boundaries of territorial constituencies in a country/province having a legislative body
* **Executing Body:** Assigned to a high-powered body known as **Delimitation Commission** or **Boundary Commission**

---

## RATIONALE OF DELIMITATION
1. **Foundation of Democratic Representation:** Secures fair elections; periodic drawing of electoral boundaries is legally mandated in single-member constituency representative systems
2. **Reflecting Ground Realities:** Stepping stone for territorial elected representation; must be conducted periodically to reflect changing demographics
3. **One Man, One Vote Parity:** Ensures parity in vote value among electors (*SC in **R.C. Poudyal v. Union of India, 1993**: vote value varies if population across constituencies differs*)

---

## STATUTORY STATUS AND POWERS
* **Nature:** **Statutory body** (*NOT a constitutional body*); established by Central Government under Parliamentary law
* **Function:** Demarcate boundaries of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies
* **Powers & Legal Force:**
  * Orders have the **force of law** and **cannot be challenged in any court**
  * Orders come into force on date specified by **President of India**
  * Orders laid before Lok Sabha and State Assemblies → **NO modifications permitted**

## CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS
* **Governing Articles:** **Articles 81, 82, 170, 330 and 332**
* **Constitutional Amendments:** Amended by **84th Constitutional Amendment Act (2001)** and **87th Constitutional Amendment Act (2003)**
* **Cumulative Effect of Amendments:**
  1. **Lok Sabha Seats Freeze:** Total Lok Sabha seats allocated to states based on **1971 census** frozen till post-**2026** census
  2. **Assembly Seats Freeze:** Total State Assembly seats based on **1971 census** frozen till post-**2026** census
  3. **SC/ST Reservation Base:** SC/ST reserved seats in Lok Sabha & Assemblies re-worked based on **2001 census** (*originally 1991 per 84th CAA; updated to 2001 by 87th CAA*)
  4. **Territorial Re-delimitation Base:** Redelimitation of territorial constituencies within states based on **2001 census**; extent frozen till post-**2026** census
  5. **Population Parity:** Population per constituency (2001 census) maintained roughly equal throughout state as far as practicable

## FOURTH DELIMITATION COMMISSION
* **Enacting Legislation:** **Delimitation Act, 2002**
* **Scope:** Readjust Parliamentary & Assembly constituencies in all states (*except J&K*) based on **2001 census**
* **3-Member Composition:**
  1. **Chairperson:** Serving or retired **Supreme Court Judge** (**Justice Kuldip Singh** appointed)
  2. **Ex-Officio Member:** **Chief Election Commissioner** (or nominated Election Commissioner)
  3. **Ex-Officio Member:** **State Election Commissioner** of concerned State/UT
* **Associate Members (10 per State):**
  * **5 MPs** from Lok Sabha + **5 MLAs** from State Assembly (nominated by respective Speakers)
  * *Constraint:* **NO right to vote** and **NO right to sign** Commission orders

---

## IMPLEMENTATION OF THE RECOMMENDATIONS
* **Submission & Implementation:** Submitted report in **2007**; President notified implementation in **2008**
* **Excluded States (5):**
  * **Deferred (4 NE States):** Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur and Nagaland (*due to security/peace threat*)
  * **Nullified (1 State):** Jharkhand (*final order nullified*)
* **2009 Elections Impact:** 2009 Lok Sabha elections held on new boundaries for **499 out of 543** constituencies

## DELIMITATION IN J&K
* **Historical Context:**
  * *Pre-2019 Status:* LS seats governed by Indian Constitution; Assembly seats governed by **J&K Constitution** & **J&K Representation of People Act, 1957**
  * *Past Delimitations:* **1957, 1966, 1975 and 1995** (*based on 1981 census*)
  * *Seat Freeze:* J&K Assembly passed law freezing seat delimitation till post-2026 census; total **111 seats** (including 4 in Ladakh and **24 reserved vacant for PoJK**)
* **Post-2019 Framework (J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019):**
  * Created UT of J&K with Legislative Assembly = **114 seats**
  * **24 seats** remain vacant for Pakistan occupied J&K (excluded in delimiting territorial constituencies)
  * **2 Women Members:** Provision for LG nomination if women inadequately represented
  * **Elected Seats Increase:** Total elected seats increased by **7** (from **83 to 90**)
  * **Census Base:** Based on **2011 census** (*100%+ population growth over 1981*)
  * **ST Reservation First Time:** SCs and STs reserved seats worked out under Arts 330 & 332 (*erstwhile J&K Constitution lacked ST reservation*)

---

## ORDER OF THE COMMISSION
* **May 2022 Final Order Provisions:**
  1. **Single Entity:** J&K treated as single entity; **5 Parliamentary constituencies**, each containing **18 Assembly constituencies**
  2. **Anantnag-Rajouri PC:** Created by combining Kashmir region (Anantnag) & Jammu region (Rajouri & Poonch)
  3. **Assembly Seat Breakdown (90 total):** **43 seats for Jammu region** + **47 seats for Kashmir region** (*6 new seats for Jammu, 1 new seat for Kashmir*)
  4. **ST Reserved Seats:** **9 Assembly seats** (6 in Jammu, 3 in Kashmir)
  5. **SC Reserved Seats:** **7 Assembly seats**
  6. **Constituency Renaming:** Renamed constituencies (e.g. *Shri Mata Vaishno Devi*)

---

## RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE COMMISSION
1. **Kashmiri Migrants:** Provision for at least **2 nominated members** (at least **1 female**) in J&K Assembly with powers par with nominated Puducherry Assembly members
2. **Displaced Persons from PoJ&K:** Central Govt to consider representation via nomination for displaced persons from PoJ&K
