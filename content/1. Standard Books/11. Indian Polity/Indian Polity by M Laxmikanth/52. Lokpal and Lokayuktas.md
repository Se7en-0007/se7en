## GLOBAL SCENARIO
* **Context:** Expansion of bureaucracy & administrative process → Abuse of power & discretion → Citizens' grievances
* **3 Major Institutional Devices for Redressal:**
  1. The Ombudsman System
  2. The Administrative Courts System
  3. The Procurator System

### Ombudsman System
* **Origin:** First created in **Sweden in 1809** (*‘Ombud’ = Swedish term for representative or spokesman*)
* **Definitions & Quotes:**
  * **Donald C. Rowat:** Officer appointed by legislature to handle complaints against administrative & judicial action; *"bulwark of democratic government against tyranny of officialdom"*
  * **Gerald E. Caiden:** *"Institutionalised public conscience"*
* **Swedish Ombudsman Scope (5 Matters):**
  1. Abuse of administrative discretion (misuse of power/authority)
  2. Maladministration (inefficiency in achieving targets)
  3. Administrative corruption (demanding bribery)
  4. Nepotism (favouring kith and kin)
  5. Discourtesy (misbehaviour/abusive language)
* **Powers & Limits of Swedish Ombudsman:**
  * Constitutional authority; supervises public officials (civil, judicial, military)
  * Can act on complaint or **suo motu**; can prosecute erring officials
  * **No power to reverse or quash decisions**; no direct control over administration/courts; cannot inflict punishment (*reports to higher authorities for corrective action*)
* **Global Spread:**
  * **Finland (1919)**, **Denmark (1955)**, **Norway (1962)**
  * **New Zealand (1962):** **First Commonwealth country** to adopt Ombudsman (*Parliamentary Commissioner for Investigation*)
  * **United Kingdom (1967):** Adopted as **Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration**
  * **India:** Known as **Lokpal / Lokayukta**

### Administrative Courts System
* **Origin:** **France**; specialized administrative courts for handling disputes between citizens and public administration

### Procurator System
* **Origin:** Socialist countries (**former USSR / Russia**, **China**)

## LOKPAL

### First ARC Recommendations (1966–1970)
* **Interim Report (1966):** First Administrative Reforms Commission (headed by **Morarji Desai**) recommended 2 special authorities: **Lokpal** and **Lokayukta**
* **Purview:**
  * **Lokpal:** Handles complaints against **Ministers** and **Secretaries** at Central & State levels
  * **Lokayukta:** Handles complaints against other specified higher officials
  * **Judiciary Excluded:** Purview excluded judiciary (*on New Zealand pattern; unlike Sweden where judiciary is included*)
* **Appointing Authority:** **President** after consultation with **Chief Justice of India**, **Speaker of Lok Sabha** and **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**
* **8 Recommended Features:**
  1. Independent and impartial
  2. Informal & private investigations/proceedings
  3. Non-political appointment
  4. Status comparable with highest judicial functionaries
  5. Deal with discretionary fields involving injustice, corruption, or favouritism
  6. No judicial interference in proceedings
  7. Maximum powers/latitude to obtain relevant information
  8. No pecuniary advantage/benefit from executive

## LOKPAL AND LOKAYUKTAS ACT (2013)

### Features
1. **Uniform Roadmap:** Establishes **Lokpal at Centre** and **Lokayukta at State level** within 365 days
2. **Composition:** **1 Chairperson + Max 8 Members**
   * **50% Judicial Members**
   * **≥50% Members from SCs, STs, OBCs, Minorities and Women**
1. **Selection Committee (5 Members):**
   * **Prime Minister** (Chairperson)
   * **Speaker of Lok Sabha**
   * **Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha**
   * **Chief Justice of India** (or nominated SC Judge)
   * **Eminent Jurist** (nominated by President based on recommendations of first 4 members)
1. **Search Committee:** Assists Selection Committee; **≥50% members** from SCs, STs, OBCs, Minorities and Women
2. **Purview / Jurisdiction:** Covers **Prime Minister** (with subject-matter exclusions & special complaint procedures), **Ministers**, **MPs** and **Groups A, B, C, D Central officers/employees**
3. **Role of CVC:**
   * Group A & B officers: CVC sends preliminary inquiry report back to Lokpal for decision
   * Group C & D employees: CVC proceeds under CVC Act, subject to Lokpal review/reporting
7. **Superintendence over CBI:** Lokpal has powers of superintendence & direction over investigating agencies (including CBI) for referred cases
8. **CBI Director Selection:** High-Powered Committee chaired by **Prime Minister** recommends CBI Director selection
9. **CBI Strengthening Provisions:**
   * Directorate of Prosecution under Director of Prosecution (controlled by Director of CBI)
   * Director of Prosecution appointed on recommendation of **CVC**
   * Panel of advocates maintained by CBI with Lokpal consent
   * Transfer of CBI officers investigating Lokpal cases requires **Lokpal approval**
   * Adequate funds provided to CBI for Lokpal cases
10. **Confiscation of Property:** Provisions for attachment/confiscation of corruptly acquired property even during pending prosecution
11. **Strict Timelines & Special Courts:**
    * **Preliminary Inquiry:** 3 months (extendable by 3 months)
    * **Investigation:** 6 months (extendable by 6 months at a time)
    * **Trial:** 1 year (extendable by 1 year); **Special Courts** to be set up
12. **Enhanced PC Act Penalties:**
    * Max punishment increased from 7 to **10 years**
    * Min punishment under Sec 7, 8, 9, 12 = **3 years**; attempt under Sec 15 = **2 years**
13. **Government-Financed Bodies:** Covers fully/partly financed institutions (*government-aided institutions EXCLUDED*)
14. **FCRA Foreign Donations:** Entities receiving foreign donations > **₹10 lakh per year** brought under Lokpal
15. **Prosecution Sanction:** Lokpal granted power to sanction prosecution (*replaces Government/competent authority*)
16. **Protection to Honest Servants:** Incorporates safeguards for upright public servants
17. **Lokayukta Mandate for States:** Mandatory enactment of Lokayukta Act by State Legislatures within **365 days**

### Drawbacks
1. **No Suo Motu Power:** Cannot initiate proceedings *suo motu* against public servants
2. **Emphasis on Form over Substance:** Over-emphasis on technical format of complaints
3. **Heavy Punishment for False Complaints:** Heavy penalty for false/frivolous complaints deters genuine whistleblowers
4. **No Anonymous Complaints:** Anonymous complaints strictly prohibited (cannot drop plain paper complaints)
5. **Legal Aid to Accused:** Provision of legal assistance to accused public servants
6. **Limitation Period:** Strict **7-year limitation period** for filing complaints
7. **Non-Transparent PM Complaint Procedure:** Opaque handling process for complaints against Prime Minister

## LOKAYUKTAS
* **Early State Initiatives:** Many states established Lokayuktas prior to 2013 Central Act
  * **First State to Establish:** **Maharashtra (1971)**
  * **First State to Pass Act:** **Odisha (1970)** (*passed Act in 1970, established later*)

### Key Features Across States
1. **Structural Variations:** Some states created **Lokayukta + Upalokayukta**; others created Lokayukta only
2. **Appointing Authority:** **Governor of the State**
3. **Qualifications:** Judicial qualifications prescribed in some states; no specific qualifications in others
4. **Tenure:** Mostly **5 years** or **65 years of age** (whichever is earlier)
5. **Jurisdiction Variations:**
   * **Chief Minister:** Included in some states; excluded in others
   * **Ministers & Higher Civil Servants:** Included in almost all states
   * **MLAs:** Included in some states
   * **Local Bodies, Corporations & PSUs:** Included in most states
6. **Initiation Power:** Most states allow initiation on complaint OR **suo motu**
7. **Grievances vs Allegations:**
   * Some states cover BOTH **grievances** (maladministration) and **allegations** (corruption)
   * Other states restrict scope ONLY to **allegations** (corruption)
8. **Annual Report:** Submitted to **Governor** → Placed before **State Legislature** with explanatory memorandum
9. **Nature of Recommendations:** **Advisory ONLY** (*not binding on State Government*)
