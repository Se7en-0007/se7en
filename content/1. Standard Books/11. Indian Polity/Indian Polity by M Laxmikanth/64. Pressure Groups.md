## MEANING AND TECHNIQUES
* **Origin:** Term originated in **USA**
* **Definition:** Organised group actively promoting/defending common interests by exerting pressure on government
* **Role:** Acts as liaison between government and its members
* **Nomenclature:** Also called **interest groups** or **vested groups**
* **Pressure Groups vs. Political Parties:**
  * Do **NOT** contest elections or capture political power (*unlike political parties*)
  * Confined to protecting/promoting specific interests by influencing government
* **Methods of Influence:**
  * **Legitimate/Legal:** Lobbying, correspondence, publicity, propagandising, petitioning, public debating, legislator contacts
  * **Illegitimate/Illegal:** Strikes, violent activities, corruption (*damages public interest & administrative integrity*)
* **3 Techniques (Odegard):**
  1. **Electioneering:** Placing favourably disposed persons in public office
  2. **Lobbying:** Persuading public officers to adopt/enforce beneficial policies
  3. **Propagandising:** Influencing public opinion to exert indirect influence on government

## PRESSURE GROUPS IN INDIA
* **Status:** Less developed compared to Western countries (**USA, Britain, France, Germany**)
* **11 Categories of Pressure Groups in India:**

### 1. Business Groups
* Sophisticated, powerful, and largest pressure group in India
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **FICCI:** Federation of Indian Chamber of Commerce and Industry
  2. **ASSOCHAM:** Associated Chamber of Commerce and Industry of India
  3. **FAIFDA:** Federation of All India Foodgrain Dealers Association
  4. **AIMO:** All-India Manufacturers Organisation

### 2. Trade Unions (Labour Groups)
* Voice demands of industrial workers; directly/indirectly affiliated with political parties
* **First Trade Union in India:** **All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)** founded in **1920** (*First President = **Lala Lajpat Rai***; central organisation until **1945** split on political lines)
* **Major Trade Unions & Affiliations:**
  1. **AITUC:** CPI
  2. **INTUC:** Congress
  3. **HMS (Hind Mazdoor Sabha):** Socialists
  4. **CITU:** CPM
  5. **BMS (Bharatiya Mazdoor Sangh):** BJP

### 3. Agrarian Groups
* Represent farmers and agricultural labour class
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **Bhartiya Kisan Union:** Wheat belt of North India
  2. **All India Kisan Sabha:** Oldest and largest agrarian group
  3. **Bhartiya Kisan Sangh:** Gujarat
  4. **Shetkari Sanghatana:** Maharashtra
  5. **All-India Kisan Sammelan**
  6. **United Kisan Sabha**

### 4. Professional Associations
* Raise demands for service conditions of doctors, lawyers, journalists, and teachers
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **IMA:** Indian Medical Association
  2. **BCI:** Bar Council of India
  3. **IFWJ:** Indian Federation of Working Journalists
  4. **AIFUCT:** All India Federation of University and College Teachers

### 5. Student Organisations
* Represent student community; affiliated with political parties
* **Key Unions & Affiliations:**
  1. **ABVP:** BJP
  2. **AISF:** CPI
  3. **NSUI:** Congress
  4. **SFI:** CPM

### 6. Religious Organisations
* Play important role in Indian politics
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **VHP:** Vishwa Hindu Parishad
  2. **Jamaat-e-Islami**
  3. **All-India Conference of Indian Christians**
  4. **Shiromani Akali Dal:** Concerned with preserving Sikh identity against assimilation (*viewed as religious pressure group*)

### 7. Caste Groups
* Major factor in state competitive politics (caste rivalries)
* **State-Wise Caste Rivalries:**
  * **Tamil Nadu & Maharashtra:** Brahmin vs Non-Brahmin
  * **Rajasthan:** Rajput vs Jat
  * **Andhra Pradesh:** Kamma vs Reddy
  * **Haryana:** Ahir vs Jat
  * **Gujarat:** Baniya Brahmin vs Patidar
  * **Bihar:** Kayastha vs Rajput
  * **Kerala:** Nair vs Ezhava
  * **Karnataka:** Lingayat vs Okkaliga
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **Nadar Caste Association:** Tamil Nadu
  2. **Marwari Association**
  3. **Harijan Sevak Sangh**
  4. **Kshatriya Maha Sabha**
  5. **Kayastha Sabha**

### 8. Tribal Organisations
* Active in MP, Chhattisgarh, Bihar, Jharkhand, WB, and North-East (Assam, Manipur, Nagaland)
* Demands range from reforms to secession/insurgency
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **NSCN:** National Socialist Council of Nagaland
  2. **TNV:** Tribal National Volunteers (Tripura)
  3. **PLA:** People’s Liberation Army (Manipur)
  4. **Tribal Sangh of Assam**
  5. **United Mizo Freedom Organisation**

### 9. Linguistic Groups
* Language = main basis for states reorganisation
* **Key Organisations:**
  1. **Tamil Sangam**
  2. **Anjuman Tarraki-i-Urdu**
  3. **Andhra Maha Sabha**
  4. **Hindi Sahitya Sammelan**
  5. **Nagari Pracharani Sabha**
  6. **Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha**

### 10. Ideology Based Groups
* Formed to pursue specific cause, principle, or programme
* **Key Groups:**
  1. **Environmental Protection Groups:** Narmada Bachao Andolan, Chipko Movement
  2. **Democratic Rights Organisations**
  3. **Civil Liberties Associations**
  4. **Gandhi Peace Foundation**
  5. **Woman Rights Organisations**

### 11. Anomic Groups
* **Definition (Almond & Powell):** Spontaneous breakthrough into political system via riots, demonstrations, assassinations
* **Cause:** Technocratic/anti-political frame of mind of govt/bureaucracy denying particularistic demands → Alienation of interest groups
* **Key Groups:**
  1. **Naxalite Groups**
  2. **JKLF:** Jammu and Kashmir Liberation Front
  3. **ULFA:** United Liberation Front of Assam
  4. **Dal Khalsa**
