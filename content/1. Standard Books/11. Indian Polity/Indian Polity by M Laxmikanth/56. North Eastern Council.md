## ESTABLISHMENT
* **Statutory Status:** Statutory (non-constitutional) advisory body
* **Enacting Act:** **North Eastern Council Act, 1971** (*passed 1971; came into force **August 1, 1972**; repealed North Eastern Council Act, 1970*)
* **Member States (8):** **Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura**
  * **Sikkim Addition:** Became **8th member state** in **2002** (*via **North Eastern Council (Amendment) Act, 2002***)
* **Secretariat (Headquarters):** Located at **Shillong, Meghalaya**
* **Core Objectives:**
  1. Secure balanced development of North Eastern Region
  2. Effect better inter-state coordination
  3. Maintain security and public order in the region
* **Regional Planning Body Mandate:** Designated as **Regional Planning Body** for North Eastern Region via **2002 Amendment Act**
* **Administrative Ministry:** Functions under administrative control of **Union Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (DONER)**

---

## COMPOSITION
* **Council Members:**
  1. **Governors** of the 8 member states
  2. **Chief Ministers** of the 8 member states (*if no Council of Ministers in a state, President nominates 1 representative*)
  3. **3 Members** nominated by **President of India**
  4. **Union Minister** nominated by President of India (*if deemed necessary*)
* **Leadership (Since July 2018):**
  * **Chairman (Ex-Officio):** **Union Home Minister** (*nominated by President*)
  * **Vice-Chairman (Ex-Officio):** **Minister (In-charge), Ministry of DONER** (*Cabinet Minister or Minister of State; nominated by President*)

---

## FUNCTIONS
1. **Regional Planning Body:** Formulate regional plans for North Eastern area giving priority to schemes/projects benefiting 2 or more states
   * *Sikkim Exception:* Formulate specific projects/schemes for Sikkim including implementation review
2. **Implementation Review:** Review implementation of regional plan projects/schemes & recommend inter-state coordination measures
3. **Multi-State Projects Management:** Recommend execution, management, benefit-sharing and expenditure-incurring mechanisms for projects benefiting 2 or more states
4. **Security & Public Order Review:** Periodically review measures taken by member states for maintenance of security and public order & recommend further necessary measures
5. **Delegated Powers:** Exercise powers delegated by Central Government

---

## REVITALISATION
* **North Eastern Council Revitalisation Committee (2003):**
  * **Setup:** Constituted in **2003** by Ministry of DONER following 2002 Amendment
  * **Objective:** Suggest measures to operationalise revised mandate as Regional Planning Body & revitalise NEC
  * **Composition:** **11-member committee** headed by **P.P. Shrivastav, IAS (Retd.)**
  * **Report:** Submitted to Government in **2004**
* **9 Core Recommendation Areas:**
  1. Composition of the NEC
  2. Strengthening/restructuring organisational set-up of NEC Secretariat
  3. Setting up of sectoral empowered committees
  4. Measures for regional planning for sustainable growth and development
  5. Financing regional plan from **Gross Budgetary Support** in place of Non-Lapsable Central Pool of Resources
  6. Measures for implementation of regional plan
  7. Monitoring and evaluation of development projects
  8. Role of NEC in border trade
  9. Operationalising NEC’s mandate relating to security and public order
