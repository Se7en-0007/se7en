## INTRODUCTION
* **97th Constitutional Amendment Act of 2011:** Granted constitutional status and protection to co-operative societies (*commencement date: **February 15, 2012***)
* **3 Major Constitutional Changes:**
  1. **Fundamental Right:** Right to form co-operative societies under **Article 19(1)(c)**
  2. **DPSP:** New Directive Principle on promotion of co-operative societies under **Article 43-B** (*State shall endeavour to promote voluntary formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control and professional management*)
  3. **New Part IX-B:** Added Part IX-B entitled *‘The Co-operative Societies’* (**Articles 243-ZH to 243-ZT**)

---

## CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS

### 1. Incorporation of Co-operative Societies (Article 243-ZI)
* State legislature provides for incorporation, regulation and winding-up based on principles of voluntary formation, democratic member-control, member-economic participation and autonomous functioning

### 2. Number and Term of Members of Board and Its Office Bearers (Article 243-ZJ)
* **Board Strength:** Specified by State Legislature; maximum **21 directors**
* **Mandatory Reservation:** **1 seat for SCs/STs** + **2 seats for Women** on the board
* **Tenure:** **5 years** from date of election for elected members and office bearers
* **Co-opted Members:** Max **2 members** with expertise in banking, management, finance, or related fields (in addition to 21 directors)
  * *Restrictions:* **NO voting rights** in elections; **ineligible** to be elected as office bearers
* **Functional Directors:** Members of board; **excluded** from counting max limit of 21 directors

### 3. Election of Members of Board (Article 243-ZK)
* Election conducted before expiry of board term
* Superintendence, direction and control of electoral rolls & elections vested in body provided by State Legislature

### 4. Supersession and Suspension of Board and Interim Management (Article 243-ZL)
* **Maximum Period:** Cannot exceed **6 months** (*Exception: Max **1 year for co-operative banks***)
* **5 Grounds for Supersession/Suspension:**
  1. Persistent default
  2. Negligence in performance of duties
  3. Act prejudicial to interests of society or members
  4. Stalemate in board constitution/functioning
  5. Election body failure to conduct timely elections
* **Government Shareholding Exception:** Board **cannot** be superseded/suspended where there is **NO government shareholding, loan, financial assistance, or guarantee**
* **Interim Administrator:** Appointed administrator must conduct elections within 6 months and hand over management

### 5. Audit of Accounts of Co-operative Societies (Article 243-ZM)
* Audited at least once each financial year by auditor/firm appointed by general body from panel approved by State Govt / authorised body
* Audit completed within **6 months** of close of financial year
* Audit report of apex co-operative society laid before **State Legislature**

### 6. Convening of General Body Meetings (Article 243-ZN)
* Annual general body meeting convened within **6 months** of close of financial year

### 7. Right of a Member to Get Information (Article 243-ZO)
* Access to books, info and accounts; management participation; co-operative education & training

### 8. Returns (Article 243-ZP)
* Filed within **6 months** of close of financial year to designated State authority; includes:
  1. Annual activity report
  2. Audited statement of accounts
  3. Surplus disposal plan approved by general body
  4. List of by-law amendments
  5. Declaration regarding dates of general body meeting and elections

### 9. Offences and Penalties (Article 243-ZQ)
* State legislature specifies offences & penalties for:
  1. Wilful false returns / information
  2. Wilful disobedience of summons/orders
  3. Employer failing to pay deducted employee amounts within **14 days**
  4. Officer failing to handover custody of records/cash
  5. Corrupt practices before, during, or after elections

### 10. Application to Multi-State Co-operative Societies (Article 243-ZR)
* References to State Legislature / Act / Government construed as **Parliament / Central Act / Central Government**

### 11. Application to Union Territories (Article 243-ZS)
* Applies to UTs; President may direct non-applicability to any UT or part thereof

### 12. Continuance of Existing Laws (Article 243-ZT)
* Inconsistent state laws continue until amended/repealed or expiry of **1 year** from 97th CAA commencement (*states required to amend laws before Feb 14, 2013*)

## REASONS FOR 97TH AMENDMENT
* **Background & Weaknesses:**
  * Postponement of elections indefinitely; nominated office bearers / administrators remaining in charge long-term
  * Reduced accountability to members & lack of professional management → Poor service and low productivity
  * Need to ensure autonomy, democratic functioning and professional management
* **Constitutional Entry:** Co-operative societies = **Entry 32, State List (Seventh Schedule)**
* **Objective:** Keep co-operatives free from unnecessary outside interference and secure autonomous, democratic, professional set-up

---

## CONSTITUTIONAL VALIDITY OF 97TH AMENDMENT
* ***Rajendra N. Shah v. Union of India (2013, Gujarat High Court):*** Declared Part IX-B ultra vires for want of state ratification under **Article 368**; held Art 19(1)(c) and Art 43-B valid
* ***Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah (2021, Supreme Court):*** Upheld Gujarat HC judgment EXCEPT that Part IX-B remains **operative insofar as it concerns Multi-State Co-operative Societies (MSCS)** in states and union territories
