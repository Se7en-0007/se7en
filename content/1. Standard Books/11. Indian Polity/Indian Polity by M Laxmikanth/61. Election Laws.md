## REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE ACT, 1950

* **Constitutional Background:**
  * **Article 81 & 170:** Lay down max seats & allocation principles for **Lok Sabha** & **State Legislative Assemblies** → Actual seat allocation left to law
  * **Article 171:** Lays down max/min seats & allocation methods for **State Legislative Councils** → Actual seat numbers left to law
* **Enactment & Core Objectives:** Enacted to provide for allocation of seats & registration of electors in Parliament and State Legislatures
* **Key Provisions:**
  1. **Seat Allocation:** Allocation of seats in House of the People, State Assemblies and Legislative Councils
  2. **Delimitation Powers:** Confers power on **President** to delimit constituencies after consulting **Election Commission**
  3. **Registration of Electors:** Registration rules, qualifications and disqualifications for voters in Parliamentary, Assembly and Council constituencies
  4. **Electoral Machinery:** Appointment of election officers (**Chief Electoral Officers, District Election Officers, Electoral Registration Officers**)
  5. **UT Representation in Rajya Sabha:** Manner of filling Council of States seats by Union Territory representatives
  6. **Local Authorities:** Designation of local authorities for State Legislative Council elections
  7. **Bar on Court Jurisdiction:** Bars jurisdiction of civil courts in electoral roll & delimitation matters

## REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE ACT, 1951

* **Background & Need:** RPA 1950 provided seat allocation & electoral rolls but omitted actual conduct of elections, member qualifications/disqualifications, corrupt practices and disputes → RPA 1951 enacted to fill these gaps
* **12 Major Subject Matters Covered:**
  1. **Qualifications & Disqualifications:** Criteria for membership of Parliament and State Legislatures
  2. **General Elections Notification:** Formal notification process for initiating elections
  3. **Administrative Machinery:** Machinery and staff for conducting elections
  4. **Registration of Political Parties:** Legal framework for registering political parties with ECI
  5. **Conduct of Elections:**
     * Nomination of candidates
     * Candidates and their election agents
     * General voting procedures
     * Polling process
     * Counting of votes
     * Multiple elections rules
     * Publication of election results and nominations
     * Declaration of assets and liabilities by candidates
     * Regulation of election expenses
  6. **Free Material Supply:** Supply of specific materials (e.g. electoral rolls) to candidates of recognised political parties
  7. **Election Disputes:**
     * Presentation of election petitions directly to **High Court**
     * Trial procedure of election petitions
     * Withdrawal and abatement of petitions
     * Appeals to **Supreme Court**
     * Costs and security for costs
  8. **Corrupt Practices & Electoral Offences:** Definition and penal provisions for election offences
  9. **ECI Inquiry Powers:** Powers of Election Commission regarding member disqualification inquiries
  10. **Bye-Elections:** Vacancy filling procedures and strict time limits
  11. **Miscellaneous Provisions:** General operational rules for elections
  12. **Bar on Court Jurisdiction:** Barring jurisdiction of civil courts over election matters

## DELIMITATION ACT, 2002

* **Constitutional Basis:**
  * **Articles 82 & 170:** Mandate readjustment and territorial constituency division of each state based on **2001 census**
  * **Articles 330 & 332:** Mandate re-fixing reserved seats for **SCs and STs** in Lok Sabha and Assemblies based on **2001 census**
* **Rationale:** Previous delimitation was based on **1971 census** → Uneven population growth & rural-to-urban migration created severe population size distortions across constituencies
* **Core Function:** Enacted to establish **Delimitation Commission** to re-demarcate constituencies using **2001 census** data (*earlier Delimitation Acts enacted in **1952, 1962 and 1972***)

## OTHER ACTS RELATING TO ELECTIONS

1. **Parliament (Prevention of Disqualification) Act, 1959:** Declares specific offices of profit under Government that do not disqualify holders from MP membership
2. **Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Orders (Amendment) Act, 1976:** Provides for inclusion/exclusion in SC/ST lists and readjustment of constituency representation
3. **Government of Union Territories Act, 1963:** Regulates governance and election framework in Union Territories
4. **Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi Act, 1991:** Regulates assembly elections and governance in NCT of Delhi
5. **Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952:** Regulates all matters connected with elections to offices of President and Vice-President of India

---

## RULES RELATING TO ELECTIONS

1. **Registration of Electors Rules, 1960:** Rules for preparation and annual publication of electoral rolls
2. **Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961:** Detailed procedure for conducting free and fair elections to Parliament and State Legislatures
3. **Prohibition of Simultaneous Membership Rules, 1950:** Prevents simultaneous membership in multiple Houses
4. **Members of Lok Sabha (Disqualification on Ground of Defection) Rules, 1985:** Procedural rules for anti-defection disqualification in Lok Sabha
5. **Members of Rajya Sabha (Disqualification on Ground of Defection) Rules, 1985:** Procedural rules for anti-defection disqualification in Rajya Sabha
6. **Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Rules, 1974:** Governs conduct of Presidential and Vice-Presidential elections (*repealed 1952 Rules*)
7. **Members of Lok Sabha (Declaration of Assets and Liabilities) Rules, 2004:** Mandatory financial asset/liability disclosure by Lok Sabha members
8. **Members of Rajya Sabha (Declaration of Assets and Liabilities) Rules, 2004:** Mandatory financial asset/liability disclosure by Rajya Sabha members

---

## ORDERS RELATING TO ELECTIONS

1. **Election Symbols (Reservation and Allotment) Order, 1968:** Governs specification, reservation, choice and allotment of election symbols and recognition of political parties
2. **Registration of Political Parties (Furnishing of Additional Particulars) Order, 1992:** Mandates submission of additional details by associations seeking ECI registration as political parties
