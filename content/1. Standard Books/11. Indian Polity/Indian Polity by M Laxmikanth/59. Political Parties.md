## MEANING AND TYPES
* **Definition:** Voluntary associations / organised groups of individuals sharing same political views, seeking political power through constitutional means to promote national interest
* **4 Types based on Ideology & Goals:**
  1. **Reactionary:** Cling to old socio-economic and political institutions
  2. **Conservative:** Believe in status quo
  3. **Liberal:** Aim at reforming existing institutions
  4. **Radical:** Aim at establishing new order by overthrowing existing institutions
* **Ideological Spectrum:**
  * **Left (Radical):** e.g. CPI, CPM
  * **Centre (Liberal):** e.g. INC (Congress)
  * **Right (Reactionary & Conservative):** e.g. BJP
* **3 Party Systems (Global):**
  1. **One-Party System:** Single ruling party, no opposition permitted (*e.g. former USSR, East European countries*)
  2. **Two-Party System:** 2 major parties (*e.g. USA - Democratic & Republican; Britain - Conservative & Labour*)
  3. **Multi-Party System:** Multiple political parties (*e.g. France, Switzerland, Italy, India*)

## PARTY SYSTEM IN INDIA

### Key Characteristic Features
1. **Multi-Party System:**
   * World's largest number of political parties
   * *Drivers:* Continental size, social diversity, universal adult franchise, political process
   * *Outcome:* Hung Parliaments/Assemblies and coalition governments
2. **One-Dominant Party System:**
   * Prolonged Congress dominance → **Rajni Kothari** coined **‘one party dominance system’** or **‘Congress system’**
   * *Decline since 1967:* Rise of regional parties and national parties (**Janata 1977, Janata Dal 1989, BJP 1991**) → Competitive multi-party system
3. **Lack of Clear Ideology:**
   * Except BJP, CPI and CPM, most parties lack clear-cut ideology
   * Resemblance in programmes (democracy, secularism, socialism, Gandhism)
   * *Focus:* Power capture; pragmatism & issue-based politics replace principles
4. **Personality Cult:**
   * Parties organised around eminent leaders (leader > party/ideology)
   * Manifestos overshadowed by leadership (*“political personalities rather than political parties”*)
5. **Based on Traditional Factors:**
   * Western parties = socio-economic/political programmes
   * Indian parties = religion, caste, language, culture, race → Promote communal/sectional interests over public interest
6. **Emergence of Regional Parties:**
   * Ruling parties in states; coalition governments at Centre elevated them to national significance
7. **Factions and Defections:**
   * Factionalism, splits, mergers, fragmentation, polarisation
   * Defection prevalence surged post-**4th General Elections (1967)** → Political instability & party disintegration
8. **Lack of Effective Opposition:**
   * Essential for parliamentary democracy to check autocracy & provide alternative government
   * Absence of strong, organized national opposition (except in flashes); lack of unity and conflicting positions

## RECOGNITION OF NATIONAL AND STATE PARTIES
* **Granting Authority:** **Election Commission of India (ECI)** registers parties & grants national/state recognition based on poll performance
* **Unrecognised Parties:** Declared as **registered-unrecognised parties**
* **Privileges of Recognition:** Allocation of reserved party symbols, free broadcast time on state-owned TV/Radio, access to electoral rolls

### Symbol Allocation
* **Reserved Symbols:** Exclusively reserved for candidates of recognised national or state parties
* **Free Symbols:** Selected by registered-unrecognised parties and independent candidates from ECI list

### Conditions for Recognition as a National Party
* A party is recognised as a **National Party** if it fulfills **ANY ONE** of the following 3 conditions:
  1. Secures **≥6% valid votes** polled in **4 or more states** in Lok Sabha or Assembly general election **AND** wins **≥4 Lok Sabha seats** from any state(s); **OR**
  2. Wins **≥2% of total Lok Sabha seats** (**11 seats out of 543**) elected from **≥3 states**; **OR**
  3. Recognised as a **State Party in ≥4 states**

### Conditions for Recognition as a State Party
* A party is recognised as a **State Party** in a state if it fulfills **ANY ONE** of the following 5 conditions:
  1. Secures **≥6% valid votes** in state Assembly general election **AND** wins **≥2 Assembly seats** in that state; **OR**
  2. Secures **≥6% valid votes** in Lok Sabha general election from that state **AND** wins **≥1 Lok Sabha seat** from that state; **OR**
  3. Wins **≥3% of total Assembly seats** **OR** **3 seats** in the Assembly (*whichever is higher*); **OR**
  4. Wins **≥1 Lok Sabha seat for every 25 seats** (or fraction thereof) allotted to the state; **OR**
  5. Secures **≥8% of total valid votes** polled in the state in Lok Sabha or Assembly general election

### Historical Statistics
* **1st Lok Sabha (1952):** **14 national parties** + **39 state parties**
* **17th Lok Sabha (2019):** **7 national parties** + **52 state parties**
