## ESTABLISHMENT
* **1978 (Executive Resolution):** Government of India (Ministry of Home Affairs resolution dated Jan 12, 1978) set up non-statutory **Minorities Commission** to safeguard interests of minorities
* **Statutory Status:** **National Commission for Minorities Act, 1992** (*enacted by Parliament; came into force **May 17, 1993***) granted statutory status & renamed body as **National Commission for Minorities (NCM)**
* **First Statutory Commission:** Constituted in **1993**
* **Administrative Ministry:** Autonomous body under administrative control of **Ministry of Minority Affairs** (*carved out of Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment in **2006***)
* **Definition & Notification of Minorities:**
  * Act **does NOT define** the term 'minority'
  * Empowers **Central Government** to notify minority communities
  * **1993 (5 Religious Communities Notified):** Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists and Zoroastrians (Parsis)
  * **2014 (6th Community Added):** **Jains** notified as 6th minority community (*Notification dated **January 27, 2014***)

## COMPOSITION
* **Multi-Member Body:** **1 Chairperson + 1 Vice-Chairperson + 5 Members** (*total 7 members; earlier 1 Chairperson + 6 Members prior to **NCM Amendment Act, 1995***)
* **Nomination:** Nominated by **Central Government** from persons of eminence, ability and integrity
* **Mandatory Representation:** At least **5 members including Chairperson** must be from amongst the minority communities
* **Tenure:** **3 years**
* **Resignation:** Addressed in writing to **Central Government**
* **Service Conditions:** Salaries, allowances & service conditions prescribed by Central Government (Ministry of Minority Affairs)
* **Removal Grounds (by Central Government):**
  1. Undischarged insolvent
  2. Convicted & sentenced to imprisonment for an offence involving moral turpitude
  3. Declared of unsound mind by competent court
  4. Refuses to act or becomes incapable of acting
  5. Absents from **3 consecutive meetings** without obtaining leave of absence
  6. Abused official position rendering continuance detrimental to interests of minorities or public interest

## FUNCTIONS
* **9-Point Mandate:**
  1. **Evaluate Progress:** Evaluate progress of development of minorities under Union and States
  2. **Monitor Safeguards:** Monitor working of constitutional and statutory safeguards for minorities
  3. **Recommendations:** Recommend effective implementation of safeguards for protection of minority interests
  4. **Inquire Complaints:** Look into specific complaints regarding deprivation of rights/safeguards and take up with appropriate authorities
  5. **Studies on Discrimination:** Cause studies on problems arising out of discrimination against minorities & recommend measures for removal
  6. **Socio-Economic Research:** Conduct studies, research and analysis on socio-economic and educational development of minorities
  7. **Suggest Measures:** Suggest appropriate measures in respect of any minority to Central or State Governments
  8. **Periodical/Special Reports:** Submit periodical/special reports to Central Govt on minority matters & difficulties confronted
  9. **Central Reference:** Look into any other matter referred by Central Government

---

## POWERS
* **Civil Court Powers:** Vested with powers of a civil court while evaluating/monitoring matters or inquiring into complaints, specifically regarding:
  1. Summoning and enforcing attendance of any person from any part of India & examining on oath
  2. Requiring discovery and production of any document
  3. Receiving evidence on affidavits
  4. Requisitioning public record from any court or office
  5. Issuing summons for examination of witnesses and documents
  6. Any other prescribed matter

---

## REPORT
* **Submission:** Submits annual/special reports to **Central Government**
* **Parliamentary Laying:** Central Govt lays report before **Parliament** along with **action taken memorandum** (explaining action taken and reasons for non-acceptance of recommendations)
* **State Assembly Laying:** State-related report copy forwarded to **State Government** → Laid before **State Legislature** with action-taken memorandum & reasons for non-acceptance
