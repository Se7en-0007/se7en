## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Classification (Article 1):** Territory of India comprises 3 categories:
  1. Territories of the states (28 states)
  2. Union territories (8 UTs)
  3. Territories that may be acquired by GOI at any time (Nil at present)
* **Nature of Relationship:**
  * **States:** Members of federal system → Share distribution of power with Centre
  * **Union Territories:** Centrally administered territories under direct control & administration of Central Government → Unitary relationship (*conspicuous departure from federalism*)

## CREATION OF UNION TERRITORIES
* **Historical Evolution:**
  * **1874:** Constituted as **‘scheduled districts’** during British Rule
  * **Later:** Renamed **‘chief commissioners provinces’**
  * **1950:** Categorised as **Part ‘C’ States and Part ‘D’ Territories**
  * **1956:** Reconstituted as **‘union territories’** by **7th Constitutional Amendment Act (1956)** and **States Reorganisation Act (1956)**
* **Elevated to Statehood:** Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, Tripura, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh and Goa (originally UTs)
* **Acquired Foreign Enclaves Formed as UTs:** Portuguese enclaves (Goa, Daman & Diu, Dadra & Nagar Haveli) and French enclave (Puducherry)

### List of Present 8 Union Territories (with Creation Year)
1. **Andaman and Nicobar Islands:** 1956
2. **Delhi:** 1956 (*redesignated National Capital Territory of Delhi in 1992*)
3. **Lakshadweep:** 1956 (*known as Laccadive, Minicoy & Amindivi Islands till 1973*)
4. **Puducherry:** 1962 (*known as Pondicherry till 2006*)
5. **Chandigarh:** 1966
6. **Jammu and Kashmir:** 2019
7. **Ladakh:** 2019
8. **Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu:** 2020 (*D&NH created 1961, D&D created 1962; merged 2020*)

### Reasons for Creation of Union Territories
1. **Political and Administrative Considerations:** Delhi and Chandigarh
2. **Cultural Distinctiveness:** Puducherry, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu
3. **Strategic Importance:** Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep
4. **Special Treatment & Care of Backward/Tribal People:** Mizoram, Manipur, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh (*later elevated to states*)

### Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation (2019)
* **Bifurcation:** Erstwhile state of Jammu & Kashmir bifurcated into 2 separate UTs under **Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019**:
  * **Union Territory of Ladakh:** Large area, sparse population, difficult terrain, long-pending demand → **Without Legislature**
  * **Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir:** Prevailing internal security situation & cross-border terrorism → **With Legislature**

### Merger of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu (2020)
* **Enacting Legislation:** **Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union Territories) Act, 2019** (*w.e.f. Jan 26, 2020*)
* **Reasons for Merger:**
  * Shared administrative setup, history, language, culture and common AIS officers
  * Elimination of administrative duplication, parallel secretariats and wasteful expenditure
  * Adherence to policy of **“Minimum Government, Maximum Governance”**

## ADMINISTRATION OF UNION TERRITORIES
* **Constitutional Location:** Enshrined in **Part VIII** of the Constitution (**Articles 239 to 241**)
* **Lack of Administrative Uniformity:** All UTs belong to one category, but administrative systems vary
* **Executive Head = Administrator:**
  * Administered by **President** acting through an **Administrator** appointed by him/her
  * Administrator = **Agent of the President** (*NOT a constitutional head of state like a Governor*)
  * **Designations:**
    * **Lieutenant Governor:** Delhi, Puducherry andaman & Nicobar Islands, Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh
    * **Administrator:** Chandigarh, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu and Lakshadweep
  * **Governor as Administrator:** President can appoint Governor of an adjoining state as Administrator of a UT (*Governor acts independently of State Council of Ministers*)

### UTs with Popular Legislative Institutions
* **3 UTs with Assembly & Council of Ministers:** **Puducherry (1963)**, **Delhi (1992)** and **Jammu and Kashmir (2019)**
  * *Assembly Strengths:* Puducherry = **30**, Delhi = **70**, Jammu and Kashmir = **83** (*excluding 24 PoK seats*)
  * *Remaining 5 UTs:* Lack popular political institutions; President/Parliament supreme

### Legislative Powers over Union Territories
* **Parliamentary Supremacy:** Parliament can make laws on **ANY subject of ALL 3 Lists (including State List)** for UTs
  * Power remains **unaffected** by establishment of local legislatures in Puducherry, Delhi and J&K
* **Local Assemblies Legislative Competence:**
  * **Puducherry Assembly:** State List and Concurrent List
  * **Delhi Assembly:** State List (*EXCEPT 3 subjects: **Public Order, Police and Land***) and Concurrent List
  * **J&K Assembly:** State List (*EXCEPT 2 subjects: **Public Order and Police***) and Concurrent List

### Presidential Regulations (Article 240)
* **Applicability:** President can make regulations for peace, progress & good government of **Andaman & Nicobar Islands, Lakshadweep, Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu and Ladakh**
* **Puducherry Exception:** President can make regulations **ONLY when Assembly is suspended or dissolved**
* **Force & Effect:** Regulation has same force/effect as an Act of Parliament; can repeal or amend any Parliamentary Act in relation to these UTs

### High Courts for Union Territories
* **Parliamentary Authority:** Parliament can establish a High Court for a UT or place it under jurisdiction of adjacent state High Court
* **Delhi:** Only UT with a **separate High Court** of its own (*established 1966*)
* **Common High Courts / Jurisdiction:**
  * **Bombay High Court:** Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu
  * **Calcutta High Court:** Andaman & Nicobar Islands
  * **Punjab and Haryana High Court:** Chandigarh
  * **Kerala High Court:** Lakshadweep
  * **Madras High Court:** Puducherry
  * **Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh High Court:** Common High Court for J&K and Ladakh

### Acquired Territories
* **Provisions:** Constitution contains no separate provisions for acquired territories; constitutional provisions for UTs apply equally to acquired territories

## SPECIAL PROVISIONS FOR DELHI
* **69th Constitutional Amendment Act (1991):** Provided special status to UT of Delhi (*w.e.f. Feb 1, 1992*):
  * Redesignated as **National Capital Territory (NCT) of Delhi**
  * Administrator designated as **Lieutenant Governor**
  * Created a Legislative Assembly and Council of Ministers

### Key Provisions for NCT of Delhi
1. **Legislative Assembly:**
   * Strength fixed at **70 members** (directly elected by people; elections conducted by Election Commission of India)
   * Can legislate on all matters of **State List and Concurrent List** (*EXCEPT 3 State List subjects: **Public Order, Police and Land***)
   * Parliamentary laws prevail over Assembly laws in case of conflict
2. **Council of Ministers:**
   * Strength fixed at **10% of total assembly strength = 7** (1 Chief Minister + 6 Ministers)
   * **Chief Minister Appointed by:** **President** (*NOT by Lt. Governor*)
   * **Ministers Appointed by:** **President** on advice of Chief Minister
   * Tenure: Hold office during **pleasure of the President**; collectively responsible to Assembly
3. **Role of Lt. Governor & Aid and Advice:**
   * Council of Ministers aids and advises Lt. Governor (*except in discretionary matters*)
   * **Difference of Opinion:** Lt. Governor refers matter to **President for decision** and acts accordingly
4. **Failure of Constitutional Machinery:**
   * President can suspend operation of constitutional provisions and impose **President's Rule** (resembles **Article 356**) on report of Lt. Governor or otherwise
5. **Ordinance-Making Power of Lt. Governor:**
   * Promulgates ordinances during assembly recess (same force as Assembly Act)
   * Must be approved by Assembly within **6 weeks from reassembly**
   * **Prerequisites:** Requires **prior permission of President** to promulgate or withdraw
   * Cannot promulgate when Assembly is dissolved or suspended

## ADVISORY COMMITTEES OF UNION TERRITORIES
* **Nodal Ministry:** **Ministry of Home Affairs** is nodal ministry for all UT matters (legislation, finance, budget, services, Lt. Governors/Administrators appointments) under *Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules 1961*
* **Advisory Committees for 5 UTs without Legislature:**
  * **Home Minister’s Advisory Committee (HMAC):** Chaired by **Union Home Minister**
  * **Administrator’s Advisory Committee (AAC):** Chaired by **Administrator** of concerned UT
  * **Composition:** MPs and elected local body members (District Panchayats & Municipal Councils) from respective UTs
  * **Function:** Discusses general social and economic development issues of UTs

## Comparing States and Union Territories

| Feature | States | Union Territories |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Relationship with Centre** | **Federal** | **Unitary** |
| **2. Distribution of Power** | Share distribution of power with Centre | Under direct control and administration of Centre |
| **3. Autonomy** | Enjoy autonomy | Have no autonomy |
| **4. Administrative Uniformity** | Uniformity in administrative set-up | **No uniformity** in administrative set-up |
| **5. Executive Head** | Executive head = **Governor** | Executive head = **Administrator / Lt. Governor / Chief Commissioner** |
| **6. Status of Head** | Governor is **constitutional head** of state | Administrator is an **agent of the President** |
| **7. Parliamentary Legislation** | Parliament cannot legislate on State List except extraordinary circumstances | Parliament can legislate on **any subject of all 3 lists** |

## Articles Related to Union Territories

* **Article 239:** Administration of Union territories
* **Article 239A:** Creation of local Legislatures or Council of Ministers or both for certain Union territories
* **Article 239AA:** Special provisions with respect to Delhi
* **Article 239AB:** Provision in case of failure of constitutional machinery
* **Article 239B:** Power of administrator to promulgate Ordinances during recess of Legislature
* **Article 240:** Power of President to make regulations for certain Union territories
* **Article 241:** High Courts for Union territories
* **Article 242:** Coorg (*Repealed*)
