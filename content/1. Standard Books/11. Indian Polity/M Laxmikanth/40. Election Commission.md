## INTRODUCTION
* **Status:** Permanent and independent body established directly by Constitution of India
* **Objective:** Ensure free and fair elections in the country
* **Constitutional Provision (Article 324):** Power of superintendence, direction and control of elections vested in Election Commission
* **Elections Covered:**
  1. Parliament
  2. State Legislatures
  3. Office of President of India
  4. Office of Vice-President of India
* **Jurisdiction Scope:** All-India body (common to Centre and States)
* **Local Bodies Exception:** **NOT** concerned with elections to Panchayats and Municipalities in states (*handled by separate **State Election Commission** under **73rd and 74th Amendments, 1992***)

---

## COMPOSITION
* **Article 324 Provisions:**
  1. Consists of **Chief Election Commissioner (CEC)** + such number of other **Election Commissioners (ECs)** as President may fix from time to time
  2. **Appointment:** Made by **President**
  3. **Chairman:** CEC acts as Chairman when other ECs are appointed
  4. **Regional Commissioners:** Appointed by President after consultation with Election Commission to assist Commission
  5. **Conditions of Service & Tenure:** Determined by **President** (subject to Parliamentary law)

### Evolution of Multi-Member Body
* **1950 – Oct 15, 1989:** Single-member body (CEC only)
* **Oct 16, 1989:** President appointed 2 more ECs → Became **3-member body** (*Trigger:* Lowering of voting age from **21 to 18 years** by **61st Constitutional Amendment Act, 1988**)
* **Jan 1990:** 2 posts of ECs abolished → Reverted to single-member body
* **Oct 1993:** President appointed 2 more ECs → Re-converted to **3-member body** (*functioning continuously as multi-member body since Oct 1993*)

### Status, Salary and Transaction of Business
* **Status & Salary:** CEC and 2 ECs have **equal powers** and receive equal salary, allowances and perquisites (= **Judge of Supreme Court**; revised to **₹2.50 lakh per month** in 2018)
* **Decision-Making:** In case of difference of opinion, matter decided by **majority vote**
* **Tenure:** **6 years** or until attaining **65 years of age** (whichever is earlier); resignation addressed to **President**

---

## INDEPENDENCE
* **Article 324 Constitutional Safeguards:**
  1. **Security of Tenure:** CEC provided with security of tenure; removed ONLY in same manner and on same grounds as a **Judge of Supreme Court** (*removed by President on resolution passed by both Houses of Parliament with **Special Majority** on ground of **proved misbehaviour or incapacity***; does NOT hold office during pleasure of President)
  2. **Protection of Service Conditions:** Service conditions of CEC cannot be varied to his/her disadvantage after appointment
  3. **Removal of Other Commissioners:** ECs or Regional Commissioners cannot be removed **except on recommendation of CEC**

### Constitutional Flaws or Shortcomings
1. **Qualifications Not Prescribed:** Constitution has not prescribed legal, educational, administrative, or judicial qualifications of members
2. **Term Not Specified:** Constitution has not specified term of members in Article 324
3. **Post-Retirement Ban Absent:** Constitution has not debarred retiring ECs from further government appointments

### Supreme Court Directions (*Anoop Baranwal Case, 2023*)
* **3-Member Selection Committee:** Appointment of CEC and ECs to be made on recommendation of committee comprising:
  1. **Prime Minister**
  2. **Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha** (or leader of largest opposition party in LS)
  3. **Chief Justice of India**
* **Removal Protection:** Recommended removal grounds for ECs be same as CEC (like SC Judge) subject to CEC recommendation
* **Service Conditions:** Conditions of service of ECs shall not be varied to their disadvantage post-appointment
* *Validity:* Directions remain effective until Parliament enacts a law in consonance with Article 324

## POWERS AND FUNCTIONS
* **3 Broad Categories:**
  1. **Administrative**
  2. **Advisory**
  3. **Quasi-Judicial**

### Detailed Powers and Functions
1. Determine territorial areas of electoral constituencies nationwide per **Delimitation Commission Acts** (*enacted in **1952, 1962, 1972 and 2002***)
2. Prepare and periodically revise **electoral rolls**; register all eligible voters
3. Notify election dates & schedules; scrutinise nomination papers
4. Grant recognition to political parties; allot election symbols
5. Act as a **court** for settling disputes relating to recognition of political parties and symbol allotment
6. Appoint inquiry officers for disputes relating to electoral arrangements
7. Determine **Model Code of Conduct (MCC)** for parties and candidates during elections
8. Prepare roster for radio & TV publicity of political party policies during elections
9. Advise **President** on matters relating to disqualification of Members of Parliament (MPs)
10. Advise **Governor** on matters relating to disqualification of Members of State Legislature (MLAs/MLCs)
11. Cancel polls in event of rigging, booth capturing, violence, or other irregularities
12. Request President or Governor for requisitioning staff necessary for conducting elections
13. Supervise election machinery nationwide to ensure free and fair elections
14. Advise President whether elections can be held in a state under President's Rule to extend emergency beyond 1 year
15. Register political parties; grant status of national or state parties based on poll performance

## ELECTION MACHINERY & STAFF STRUCTURE
* **Central Level Secretariat:**
  * Commission assisted by **Deputy Election Commissioners** (drawn from civil services, appointed on tenure system)
  * Assisted by Secretaries, Joint Secretaries, Deputy Secretaries and Under Secretaries
* **State Level:**
  * Headed by **Chief Electoral Officer (CEO)** (appointed by CEC in consultation with State Government)
* **District & Booth Level:**
  * **District Collector** acts as **District Returning Officer**
  * Appoints **Returning Officer (RO)** for every constituency in district
  * Appoints **Presiding Officer** for every polling booth in constituency

## VISION, MISSION AND PRINCIPLES

### Vision
* Strives to be an **Institution of Excellence** by enhancing active engagement and participation and deepening and strengthening electoral democracy in India and globally

### Mission
* Maintains independence, integrity and autonomy; ensures accessibility, inclusiveness and ethical participation of stakeholders; adopts highest standards of professionalism for delivering free, fair and transparent elections

### Guiding Principles (10 Governance Principles)
1. Uphold constitutional values (**equality, equity, impartiality, independence, rule of law**)
2. Conduct elections with highest credibility, freeness, fairness, transparency, integrity, accountability, autonomy, professionalism
3. Ensure inclusive, voter-centric, voter-friendly environment for all eligible citizens
4. Engage with political parties and all stakeholders
5. Promote electoral process awareness among voters, parties, candidates, functionaries and public
6. Develop human resources for effective and professional service delivery
7. Build quality infrastructure for smooth conduct of elections
8. Adopt technology across all areas of electoral process
9. Adopt innovative practices for achieving excellence
10. Reinforce democratic values, public confidence and trust in electoral system
