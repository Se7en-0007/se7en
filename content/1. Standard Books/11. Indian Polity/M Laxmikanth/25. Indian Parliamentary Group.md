## RATIONALE OF THE IPG
* **Need:** Inter-parliamentary relations = regular activity of national parliaments; received new thrust due to **increased global interdependence**
* **M.N. Kaul & S.L. Shakdher Rationale:** Parliamentarians must join hands to **safeguard democracy** and work in synergy to confront global challenges → Convert challenges into opportunities for peace & prosperity

---

## COMPOSITION OF THE IPG
* **Nature:** Autonomous body; formed in **1949** pursuant to Constituent Assembly (Legislative) motion adopted on **August 16, 1948**
* **Membership:**
  * Open to **all Members of Parliament** (sitting MPs)
  * Former MPs can become **Associate Members** (or **Life Members** on paying fixed life subscription)
  * *Associate Member Limitations:* Not entitled to representation at IPU & CPA meetings/conferences or travel concessions provided by CPA branches
* **Ex-Officio Office Bearers:**
  * **President:** **Speaker of the Lok Sabha**
  * **Vice-Presidents:** **Deputy Speaker of Lok Sabha** & **Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha**
  * **Secretary-General:** **Secretary-General of Lok Sabha**

---

## OBJECTIVES OF THE IPG
1. Promote **personal contact** among MPs of India
2. Study public importance questions likely to come before Parliament → Arrange **seminars, discussions, orientation courses** and publications
3. Arrange **lectures** on political, defence, economic, social and educational issues by MPs and eminent persons
4. Organise **foreign tours** for members to contact parliamentarians of other countries

---

## FUNCTIONS OF THE IPG
1. Serves as **link** between Parliament of India and other parliaments of the world
2. Functions as: (a) **National Group of the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU)**, (b) **Main Branch of Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA) in India**
3. Arranges **addresses to MPs** by visiting foreign Heads of State/Government and talks by eminent persons
4. Organises **seminars & symposia** on topical parliamentary subjects at national and international levels
5. Issues **letters of introduction** for members visiting abroad to IPU/CPA branch Secretaries (+ informs Indian Missions for courtesies/assistance)
6. **Delegation Eligibility:** Only members with **at least 6 months’ standing** in IPG can be included in Indian Parliamentary Delegations abroad
7. **IPG Newsletter:** Published **quarterly** to maintain uninterrupted information flow to all members (including associate members)
8. **Outstanding Parliamentarian Award:** Instituted in **1995** (given annually); nominated & finalised by **5-member committee** constituted by Lok Sabha Speaker

---

## PARLIAMENTARY FRIENDSHIP GROUPS
* **Purpose:** Constituted by IPG with other countries in Indian Parliament to **encourage bilateral relations**
* **Composition:** Sitting MPs from **both Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha**
* **Appointment & Control:**
  * **President:** Appointed by **Speaker of the Lok Sabha**
  * **Management & Control:** Vested exclusively in **IPG**
* **Meetings:** Held within **Parliament House complex** on sidelines of visiting foreign parliamentary delegations/groups
* **Aims & Objectives:**
  1. Maintain political, economic, social and cultural contact between the two countries
  2. Create favourable conditions for continuous development of inter-parliamentary contacts (talks, exchanges, cooperation)
  3. Assist in exchanging information and experiences on parliamentary activities
  4. Promote cooperation between delegations in international organisations and consultations on mutual interest
  5. Increase ties between member countries

---

## THE IPG AND IPU
* **Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU):** International organisation of sovereign state parliaments (**179 member parliaments**)
  * *Aim:* Work for peace, cooperation, firm establishment of representative institutions, exchange of experience and views on burning international questions
* **Advantages of IPG as National Group of IPU:**
  1. Develop contacts with parliamentarians of IPU member countries
  2. Opportunity to study & understand contemporary global changes/reforms
  3. Facilities to meet foreign parliamentarians during tours abroad
  4. Eligibility to visit foreign countries as members of Indian Parliamentary delegations to Inter-Parliamentary Conferences
  5. Holds office bearer positions in IPU bodies (Committees, Rapporteurs, Chairmen of Drafting Committees) → Effectively puts forward **India’s viewpoint**

---

## THE IPG AND CPA
* **Commonwealth Parliamentary Association (CPA):** Association of ~**17,000 Parliamentarians and staff** across **180 Parliaments/Legislatures in 53 Commonwealth countries**
  * *Aim & Mission:* Promote knowledge of constitutional, legislative, economic, social & cultural systems within parliamentary democratic framework; advance parliamentary democracy & deepen Commonwealth democratic commitment
* **Advantages of IPG as Main Branch of CPA in India:**
  1. **Conferences & Seminars:** Participation in plenary & regional conferences, seminars, visits and delegation exchanges
  2. **Publications:** Free receipt of ***‘The Parliamentarian’* (quarterly)** and ***‘First Reading’* (bimonthly newsletter)**
  3. **Information:** Access to Parliamentary Information and Reference Centre of CPA Secretariat
  4. **Introductions:** CPA branches assist in arranging introductions when visiting other jurisdictions
  5. **Parliamentary Facilities:** Courtesies, access to debates and local members in Commonwealth countries
  6. **Travel Facilities:** Study tours / ad hoc visits arranged by branches to compare political and procedural developments
