## INTRODUCTION
* **Location:** Enshrined in **Part VI** of the Constitution (**Articles 168 to 212**)
* **Position:** Pre-eminent and central position in the political system of a state

---

## ORGANISATION OF STATE LEGISLATURE
* **System Types:** Most states have **unicameral system**; only **6 states** have **bicameral system**
  * **6 Bicameral States:** **Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka**
  * *Abolished / Unenforced:* J&K Legislative Council abolished (**J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019**); Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Act 2010 not enforced; MP Legislative Council provision (**7th CAA 1956**) pending Presidential notification
* **Unicameral Composition:** Governor + Legislative Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)
* **Bicameral Composition:** Governor + Legislative Assembly (Lower House / First Chamber / Popular House) + Legislative Council (Vidhan Parishad; Upper House / Second Chamber / House of Elders)
* **Abolition or Creation of Legislative Council (Article 169):**
  * **Parliamentary Power:** Parliament can create or abolish a Legislative Council by law if State Assembly passes a resolution
  * **State Assembly Resolution:** Passed by **Special Majority** (majority of total membership + ≥2/3 members present and voting)
  * **Nature of Parliamentary Act:** Passed by **Simple Majority** (deemed **NOT** an amendment under Article 368)
* **Historical Timelines of Councils:**
  * **Andhra Pradesh:** Created 1957 → Abolished 1985 → Revived 2007 (**AP Legislative Council Act, 2005**)
  * **Tamil Nadu:** Abolished 1986
  * **Punjab & West Bengal:** Abolished 1969

## COMPOSITION OF TWO HOUSES

### Composition of Assembly (Vidhan Sabha)

#### 1. Strength
* **Direct Election:** Directly elected by people based on **Universal Adult Franchise**
* **Limits:** Maximum = **500**, Minimum = **60**
* **Exceptions to Minimum Strength:**
  * **Arunachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Goa:** Fixed minimum = **30**
  * **Mizoram:** **40**
  * **Nagaland:** **46**
  * *Indirect Election:* Some members in Sikkim and Nagaland elected indirectly

#### 2. Nominated Member
* **Governor Nomination:** Formerly 1 Anglo-Indian nominated by Governor if inadequately represented
* **Discontinuation:** **104th Amendment Act (2019)** discontinued nomination of Anglo-Indians in Assemblies (**w.e.f. Jan 25, 2020**)

#### 3. Territorial Constituencies
* **Uniformity:** Ratio between constituency population and allocated seats is uniform throughout the state

#### 4. Readjustment after each Census
* **Delimitation Commission Acts:** Enacted in **1952, 1962, 1972, and 2002**
* **42nd Amendment Act (1976):** Froze seat allocation and constituency division till 2000 at **1971 level**
* **84th Amendment Act (2001):** Extended seat freeze till **2026**; allowed rationalisation based on **1991 census**
* **87th Amendment Act (2003):** Delimitation of constituencies based on **2001 census** (without altering total state seat count)

#### 5. Reservation of Seats for SCs and STs
* **Basis:** Reserved based on **population ratios** in each state
* **104th Amendment Act (2019):** Extended SC/ST seat reservation in State Assemblies until **2030**

---

### Composition of Council (Vidhan Parishad)

#### 1. Strength
* **Limits:** Maximum = **1/3rd of total strength of Legislative Assembly**; Minimum = **40**
* **Parliamentary Authority:** Composition layout in Constitution is tentative; Parliament empowered to modify/replace

#### 2. Manner of Election (5 Breakdown Categories)
* **Indirect Election (5/6th) + Governor Nomination (1/6th):**
  1. **1/3rd** elected by **members of local bodies** (municipalities, district boards, etc.)
  2. **1/12th** elected by **graduates** of 3 years' standing residing in the state
  3. **1/12th** elected by **teachers** of 3 years' standing (secondary school or higher standard)
  4. **1/3rd** elected by **members of Legislative Assembly (MLAs)** from non-assembly members
  5. **1/6th** nominated by **Governor** from persons having special knowledge/practical experience in **literature, science, art, cooperative movement, and social service**
* **Voting System:** Proportional Representation by means of **Single Transferable Vote (PR-STV)**

## DURATION OF TWO HOUSES

### Duration of Assembly
* **Normal Term:** **5 years** from date of 1st meeting (automatic dissolution upon expiry)
* **Early Dissolution:** Governor authorised to dissolve assembly anytime
* **National Emergency Extension:** Extended by Parliamentary law for **1 year at a time** (cannot extend beyond **6 months** post-emergency)

### Duration of Council
* **Permanent Body:** Continuing chamber; **not subject to dissolution**
* **Retirement Cycle:** **1/3rd of members retire every 2nd year** (eligible for re-election/renomination indefinitely)
* **Term of Member:** **6 years**

## MEMBERSHIP OF STATE LEGISLATURE

### 1. Qualifications (Article 173)
1. Citizen of India
2. Oath or affirmation before person authorised by Election Commission
3. Age: Not less than **30 years for Legislative Council** and **25 years for Legislative Assembly**
4. Additional qualifications under **Representation of the People Act (1951)**:
   * Elector for an assembly constituency in concerned state
   * Resident of state to be qualified for Governor's nomination to Council
   * SC/ST member to contest reserved seats

### 2. Disqualifications (Constitutional & Statutory)
* **Constitutional Grounds:**
  1. Holds office of profit under Union or State Govt (except Minister or exempted office)
  2. Unsound mind (declared by court)
  3. Undischarged insolvent
  4. Non-citizen or voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship
  5. Disqualified under any parliamentary law
* **Representation of the People Act (1951) Grounds:**
  1. Convicted for electoral offences / corrupt practices
  2. Convicted for offence resulting in **imprisonment for ≥2 years** (*preventive detention is NOT a disqualification*)
  3. Failed to lodge election expense account in time
  4. Interest in government contracts, works or services
  5. Director/managing agent in corporation with ≥25% government share
  6. Dismissed from government service for corruption or disloyalty
  7. Convicted for promoting enmity, bribery, untouchability, dowry, or sati
* **Deciding Authority:** Governor’s decision is **final** (must obtain binding opinion of **Election Commission**)

### Disqualification on Ground of Defection (Tenth Schedule)
* **Deciding Authority:** **Chairman (Legislative Council)** / **Speaker (Legislative Assembly)**
* **Judicial Review:** Deciding authority's decision subject to **judicial review** (***Kihoto Hollohan case, 1992***)

### 3. Oath or Affirmation (Article 188)
* Administered by **Governor** or appointed person
* Penalty for sitting/voting without oath/disqualified = **₹500 per day**

### 4. Vacation of Seats
1. **Double Membership:** Cannot be member of both Houses simultaneously
2. **Disqualification:** Under Constitution, RPA 1951, or 10th Schedule
3. **Resignation:** Addressed in writing to Speaker (Assembly) or Chairman (Council)
4. **Absence:** Absent from all meetings for **60 consecutive days** without permission
5. **Other Cases:** Election declared void by court; expelled by House; elected President/VP; appointed Governor

## PRESIDING OFFICERS OF STATE LEGISLATURE

### Speaker of Assembly
* **Election:** Elected by Legislative Assembly from amongst its members
* **Vacation of Office:** Ceases member, resigns to **Deputy Speaker**, or removed by **Effective Majority** (14 days advance notice)
* **Powers & Functions:**
  1. Maintains order and decorum in House (final authority)
  2. Final interpreter of Constitution, Rules of Procedure, and Parliamentary Precedents within Assembly
  3. Adjourns assembly or suspends meeting in absence of **quorum**
  4. Casting vote in case of a tie
  5. Secret sitting on request of Leader of House
  6. Final authority to decide whether a bill is a **Money Bill**
  7. Decides 10th Schedule defection questions (*subject to judicial review*)
  8. Appoints chairmen of assembly committees; chairs **Business Advisory, Rules, and General Purpose Committees**

### Deputy Speaker of Assembly
* Elected by Assembly from amongst members; performs Speaker duties when office vacant or Speaker absent

### Chairman of Council
* Elected by Legislative Council from amongst its members
* Vacates if ceases member, resigns to **Deputy Chairman**, or removed by **Effective Majority** (14 days advance notice)
* Powers similar to Speaker **EXCEPT**: Cannot decide whether a bill is a Money Bill (exclusive power of Assembly Speaker)

### Deputy Chairman of Council
* Elected by Council from amongst members; performs Chairman duties when office vacant or Chairman absent

## SESSIONS OF STATE LEGISLATURE

### Summoning, Adjournment, Prorogation, and Dissolution
* **Summoning:** Governor summons each House; **max gap between sessions = 6 months** (must meet at least twice a year)
* **Adjournment / Adjournment Sine Die:** Suspends sitting; power lies with **Presiding Officer**
* **Prorogation:** Terminates sitting AND session; power lies with **Governor**
* **Dissolution:** Ends life of Legislative Assembly (**Governor**); Council is permanent (no dissolution)

### Position on Lapsing of Bills on Assembly Dissolution
* **Bills that LAPSE:**
  1. Bill pending in Assembly (originating or transmitted by Council)
  2. Bill passed by Assembly but pending in Council
* **Bills that DO NOT LAPSE:**
  1. Bill pending in Council but **not passed by Assembly**
  2. Bill passed by Assembly (or both Houses) but **pending assent of Governor or President**
  3. Bill passed by Assembly (or both Houses) but **returned by President for reconsideration**

### Quorum, Voting, and Language
* **Quorum:** **10 members** OR **1/10th of total membership** of House (whichever is greater)
* **Voting:** Simple majority of members present and voting; Presiding Officer exercises **casting vote** only
* **Language:** **Official language(s) of state, Hindi, or English** (Presiding officer can permit mother tongue)
* **Rights of Ministers and Advocate General:** Right to speak/participate in either House or Committee without voting right (unless member)

## LEGISLATIVE PROCEDURE IN STATE LEGISLATURE

### Ordinary Bills
* **Originating House:** Introduced in **either House** by minister or private member; passes 3 readings
* **Bill in Second House (Legislative Council):**
  * 4 Options: Pass without amendments, pass with amendments, reject, or keep pending
  * **Delay Limits:** Council can delay bill for **max 4 months total** (**3 months** in 1st instance + **1 month** in 2nd instance)
  * **Assembly Override:** If Assembly passes bill 2nd time, bill deemed passed in Assembly's form if Council rejects/delays >1 month → **Assembly's will prevails**
  * **NO Joint Sitting:** Constitution provides **NO joint sitting mechanism** for State Legislature (unlike Parliament)
  * **Council-Originated Bill:** If bill originating in Council is rejected by Assembly → **Bill ends and dies**
* **Assent of Governor (Article 200):** Assent, Withhold assent, Return for reconsideration (**Suspensive Veto**), or **Reserve for President**
* **Assent of President (Article 201):** Assent, Withhold assent, or Return for reconsideration (reconsidered within **6 months**; President **NOT bound** to give assent)

### Money Bills (Article 199)
* **Procedure:** Introduced **ONLY in Legislative Assembly**, **ONLY on Governor's recommendation**, **ONLY by a minister**
* **Council Power:** Cannot reject or amend; **14-day return limit** with optional recommendations
* **Assent:** Governor gives assent, withholds, or reserves for President (**cannot return** for reconsideration)

## BILLS RESERVED FOR PRESIDENT’S CONSIDERATION
* **Sarkaria Commission 3 Categories:**
  1. **Mandatory Reservation:** Bills derogating High Court powers endangering its constitutional position (**Article 200**); water/electricity tax (**Article 288**); Financial Emergency (**Article 360**)
  2. **Specific Purpose Reservation:** Immunity from Arts 14 & 19 (estate acquisition **Art 31A**, DPSPs **Art 31C**); Concurrent List repugnancy to Union law (**Art 254**); trade restrictions without prior sanction (**Art 304**)
  3. **General Reservation:** Other bills reserved under Governor's discretion (**Article 200**)

## LEGISLATIVE PROCEDURE COMPARED (PARLIAMENT vs STATE LEGISLATURE)

### Ordinary Bills Comparison
* **Parliament:** Deadlock resolved via **Joint Sitting**; Rajya Sabha equal in resolving deadlock
* **State Legislature:** No joint sitting; Legislative Assembly overrides Legislative Council via **2nd passage** (Council is dilatory chamber only)

### Money Bills Comparison
* Both: Introduced in Lower House only on recommendation of executive head (President/Governor) by minister; Upper House delay limit = **14 days**

## POSITION OF LEGISLATIVE COUNCIL

### Equal Status with Assembly
1. Introduction & passage of ordinary bills (*subject to Assembly override*)
2. Approval of ordinances issued by Governor
3. Selection of ministers (including Chief Minister)
4. Consideration of reports of State Finance Commission, SPSC, and CAG
5. Enlargement of SPSC jurisdiction

### Unequal Status with Assembly
1. Money Bill introduction, amendment, or rejection (Assembly exclusive)
2. Money Bill decision (Assembly Speaker final)
3. Ordinary Bill override power (Assembly prevails; Council delay max 4 months)
4. Budget voting on demands for grants (Assembly exclusive privilege)
5. No-confidence motion (CoM responsible only to Assembly)
6. Council-originated bill rejection by Assembly → Bill dies
7. President and Rajya Sabha elections (Council members **do NOT participate**)
8. Constitutional amendment ratification (Assembly will prevails)
9. Council's very existence depends on Assembly recommendation (can be abolished under **Art 169**)

### Utility of Council
* Checks hasty, defective, and ill-considered legislation
* Facilitates representation of eminent professionals and experts via Governor's 1/6th nomination

## PRIVILEGES OF STATE LEGISLATURE
* **Scope:** Extended to State Ministers and Advocate General (*does **NOT** extend to Governor*)

### Collective Privileges
1. Right to publish/prohibit reports, debates, and proceedings (*44th CAA 1978 restored press freedom for true reports except secret sittings*)
2. Exclude strangers and hold secret sittings
3. Make rules regulating procedure and conduct of business
4. Punish members and outsiders for breach of privilege or contempt
5. Right to receive immediate info of member's arrest, detention, conviction, and release
6. Institute inquiries and order witness attendance
7. Courts barred from inquiring into House proceedings
8. No arrest or legal process served within precincts without Presiding Officer permission

### Individual Privileges
1. Immunity from civil arrest during session and **40 days before & after** (*no immunity in criminal / preventive detention*)
2. Freedom of speech in legislature (no court liability for statements/votes, subject to Art 211 prohibition on discussing SC/HC judges' conduct)
3. Exemption from jury service during session

## Laws Made by Parliament (under Article 169) for Abolition or Creation of Legislative Councils
* **1. West Bengal Legislative Council (Abolition) Act, 1969:** Abolished West Bengal Council
* **2. Punjab Legislative Council (Abolition) Act, 1969:** Abolished Punjab Council
* **3. Andhra Pradesh Legislative Council (Abolition) Act, 1985:** Abolished AP Council
* **4. Tamil Nadu Legislative Council (Abolition) Act, 1986:** Abolished TN Council
* **5. Andhra Pradesh Legislative Council Act, 2005:** Created AP Council
* **6. Tamil Nadu Legislative Council Act, 2010:** Provided creation of TN Council (*not enforced*)
