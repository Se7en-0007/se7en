## MEANING AND SIGNIFICANCE
* **2 Kinds of People:** **Citizens** (full members, owe allegiance, enjoy all civil/political rights) vs **Aliens** (citizens of another state, do not enjoy all rights)
* **Categories of Aliens:**
  * **Friendly Aliens:** Subjects of countries with cordial relations
  * **Enemy Aliens:** Subjects of countries at war with India (enjoy lesser rights; **no protection against arrest and detention under Article 22**)
* **Rights & Privileges Exclusive to Citizens (Denied to Aliens):**
  1. **Article 15:** Right against discrimination (religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth)
  2. **Article 16:** Right to equality of opportunity in public employment
  3. **Article 19:** Right to 6 freedoms (speech & expression, assembly, association, movement, residence, profession)
  4. **Articles 29 & 30:** Cultural and educational rights
  5. **Voting Right:** Elections to Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies
  6. **Contesting Right:** Membership of Parliament and State Legislatures
  7. **Public Offices Eligibility:** President, Vice-President, SC/HC Judges, Governor, Attorney General, Advocate General
* **Duties of Citizens:** Pay taxes, respect national flag & anthem, defend country, etc.
* **Eligibility for President:**
  * **India:** Citizen by **birth as well as naturalised** citizen eligible
  * **USA:** Only citizen by **birth** eligible (naturalised ineligible)

## CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS
* **Part II (Articles 5 to 11):** Deals with citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution (**Jan 26, 1950**)
  * *Nature:* Neither permanent nor elaborate provisions; identifies persons who became citizens on Jan 26, 1950; empowers Parliament to enact citizenship laws
* **4 Categories of Citizens at Commencement (Jan 26, 1950):**
  1. **Domicile in India (Article 5):** Domiciled + fulfilled 1 of 3 conditions:
     * Born in India; OR
     * Either parent born in India; OR
     * Ordinarily resident in India for 5 years immediately before commencement
  2. **Migrated from Pakistan (Article 6):** Person or either parent or any grandparent born in undivided India + fulfilled 1 of 2 conditions:
     * *Migrated before July 19, 1948:* Ordinarily resident in India since migration date
     * *Migrated on/after July 19, 1948:* Registered by officer after being resident for 6 months preceding application
  3. **Migrated to Pakistan but Returned (Article 7):** Migrated to Pakistan after March 1, 1947, but returned under resettlement permit → Registered after being resident for 6 months preceding application
  4. **Persons of Indian Origin Residing Abroad (Article 8):** Born in undivided India (or parents/grandparents) + registered as citizen by diplomatic/consular representative of India in country of residence
* **Other Constitutional Provisions:**
  * **Article 9:** No citizenship if a person voluntarily acquires citizenship of a foreign state (**No Dual Citizenship**)
  * **Article 10:** Every citizen continues to be a citizen, subject to any law made by Parliament
  * **Article 11:** Parliament empowered to make provisions for acquisition, termination, and all other citizenship matters

## CITIZENSHIP ACT, 1955
* **Enactment:** Governs acquisition and loss of citizenship post-commencement (amended 1986, 1992, 2003, 2005, 2015, 2019)
* **Commonwealth Citizenship:** Originally provided in 1955 Act; **repealed by Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2003**

### Acquisition of Citizenship
* **5 Standard Ways:** Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalisation, Incorporation of Territory + Special Provisions (Assam Accord, CAA 2019)

#### 1. By Birth
* **Jan 26, 1950 – June 30, 1987:** Citizen by birth irrespective of parents' nationality
* **July 1, 1987 – Dec 3, 2004:** Citizen only if **either parent** is an Indian citizen at birth
* **Dec 3, 2004 onwards:** Citizen only if **both parents** are Indian citizens OR **one parent** is an Indian citizen and the other is **not an illegal migrant** at birth
* **Exceptions:** Children of foreign diplomats and enemy aliens **cannot** acquire Indian citizenship by birth

#### 2. By Descent
* **Jan 26, 1950 – Dec 10, 1992:** Born outside India → Citizen if **father** was an Indian citizen at birth
* **Dec 10, 1992 – Dec 3, 2004:** Born outside India → Citizen if **either parent** was an Indian citizen at birth
* **Dec 3, 2004 onwards:** Born outside India → **Not a citizen by descent** unless birth is registered at an Indian consulate within **1 year** (or with Central Gov permission) + parents' undertaking that child holds no foreign passport
* **Minor dual citizens:** Must renounce foreign citizenship within **6 months** of attaining full age (18 years) or lose Indian citizenship

#### 3. By Registration
* **Target:** Persons (not illegal migrants) applying to Central Government:
  1. Person of Indian Origin (PIO) ordinarily resident in India for **7 years** before application
  2. PIO ordinarily resident in any country outside undivided India
  3. Married to an Indian citizen + ordinarily resident in India for **7 years**
  4. Minor children of Indian citizens
  5. Person of full age/capacity whose parents are registered Indian citizens
  6. Person of full age/capacity (or either parent) who was earlier citizen of independent India + resident in India for **12 months**
  7. Person of full age/capacity registered as OCI cardholder for **5 years** + resident in India for **12 months**
* **PIO Definition:** Person (or parents/grandparents) born in undivided India or acquired territory post-Aug 15, 1947
* **Oath:** Mandatory oath of allegiance before registration

#### 4. By Naturalisation
* **Target:** Persons (not illegal migrants) possessing qualifications:
  1. Not citizen of a country where Indians are prevented from becoming naturalised citizens
  2. Undertakes to renounce foreign citizenship upon acceptance
  3. Resided in India / Government service for **12 months** preceding application
  4. During 14 years preceding the 12 months → Resided / Government service for aggregate **11 years**
     * *CAA 2019 Exception:* Reduced to **5 years** for Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian migrants from Afghanistan, Bangladesh, or Pakistan
  5. Good character
  6. Adequate knowledge of a language in **8th Schedule**
  7. Intention to reside in India or enter/continue Government/international organization/company service
* **Waiver:** Central Gov can waive conditions for persons rendering distinguished service to science, philosophy, art, literature, world peace, or human progress
* **Oath:** Mandatory oath of allegiance

#### 5. By Incorporation of Territory
* **Mechanism:** Foreign territory becomes part of India → Central Gov specifies citizens by notification
* **Example:** Puducherry integrated → **Citizenship (Pondicherry) Order (1962)** issued

#### 6. Special Provisions As To Citizenship of Persons Covered by The Assam Accord
* **1985 Amendment (Assam Accord):**
  1. **Came before Jan 1, 1966:** PIOs entering Assam from Bangladesh before Jan 1, 1966 & ordinarily resident → Deemed Indian citizens from Jan 1, 1966
  2. **Came Jan 1, 1966 – March 25, 1971:** PIOs entering Assam from Bangladesh between Jan 1, 1966 & March 25, 1971 + detected as foreigners → Must register → Deemed Indian citizens **after 10 years** from detection date (enjoy all rights except voting during 10-year period)

#### 7. Special Provisions As To Citizenship of Persons Migrated from Afghanistan, Bangladesh or Pakistan
* **CAA 2019 Provisions (In force Jan 10, 2020):**
  * **Eligible Communities:** Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian migrants from Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Pakistan
  * **Cut-off Date:** Entered India on or before **Dec 31, 2014**
  * **Legal Relief:** Exempted from penal consequences of *Passport Act 1920* & *Foreigners Act 1946*; pending illegal migration proceedings abate upon citizenship grant; granted citizenship from date of entry
  * **Exempted Regions:**
    1. Tribal areas of **Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Tripura** (Sixth Schedule)
    2. Areas under **Inner Line Permit (ILP)** notified under *Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation, 1873*

### Loss of Citizenship
* **3 Modes:** Renunciation, Termination, Deprivation

#### 1. By Renunciation
* **Voluntary Declaration:** Citizen of full age/capacity declares renunciation → Ceases to be citizen upon registration
  * *War exception:* Registration withheld if made during war
* **Minor Children:** Minor child of renouncing person loses citizenship → Can resume Indian citizenship within **1 year** of attaining 18 years

#### 2. By Termination
* **Voluntary Acquisition:** Indian citizen consciously acquires citizenship of another country → Indian citizenship **automatically terminates** (does not apply during war)

#### 3. By Deprivation
* **Compulsory Termination by Central Government if:**
  1. Obtained citizenship by fraud
  2. Disloyalty to Constitution of India
  3. Unlawful trade/communication with enemy during war
  4. Imprisoned in any country for 2 years within 5 years of registration/naturalisation
  5. Ordinarily resident outside India continuously for **7 years** (except students, government service, or annual consulate registration)

## SINGLE CITIZENSHIP
* **Concept:** Single uniform Indian citizenship for entire country (federal dual polity, but single citizenship) ← Derived from **Canadian model**
* **Contrast:** Dual citizenship in **USA & Australia** (national + state citizenship → creates state-level discrimination)
* **Goal:** Promote fraternity, unity, and an integrated Indian nation
* **4 Constitutional Exceptions to Non-Discrimination:**
  1. **Article 16(3):** Parliament can prescribe residence requirement for non-gazetted employment in a state/UT (*Public Employment Act 1957* expired in 1974; currently applicable only in **Andhra Pradesh & Telangana** under Art 371D)
  2. **Article 15(1):** Prohibits discrimination on grounds of religion, race, caste, sex, place of birth (Residence not mentioned → State can grant educational fee concessions to residents)
  3. **Article 19(5):** Restrictions on movement & residence to protect interests of **Scheduled Tribes** (protect culture, language, customs, land)
  4. **Erstwhile J&K (Article 35A / Art 370):** Empowered J&K assembly to define permanent residents & grant special employment/property rights → **Abolished in 2019** via *The Constitution (Application to J&K) Order, 2019*

## OVERSEAS CITIZENSHIP OF INDIA
* **L.M. Singhvi Committee (Sept 2000):** High Level Committee on Indian Diaspora → Submitted report Jan 2002 → Recommended dual citizenship for PIOs in specified countries
* **Legislative Steps:**
  * **CAA 2003:** Introduced OCI for PIOs of 16 countries; repealed Commonwealth citizenship
  * **CAA 2005:** Extended OCI to PIOs of all countries (except Pakistan & Bangladesh) allowing dual citizenship
  * **CAA 2015:** Merged PIO Card scheme (rescinded Jan 9, 2015) & OCI scheme into **"Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder"**

### I. Registration of Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder
* **Eligibility:** Foreign citizens who:
  1. Were citizens of India on/after Jan 26, 1950; OR
  2. Were eligible to become Indian citizens on Jan 26, 1950; OR
  3. Belonged to territory becoming part of India post-Aug 15, 1947; OR
  4. Are children/grandchildren/great-grandchildren of such citizens; OR
  5. Are minor children of such persons OR whose parent(s) are Indian citizens; OR
  6. Foreign spouse of Indian citizen / OCI cardholder (marriage registered & subsisted for at least **2 years**)
* **Ineligibility:** Anyone who (or whose parents/grandparents/great-grandchildren) was a citizen of **Pakistan, Bangladesh**, or specified countries

### II. Conferment of Rights on Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder
* **Rights Granted:** Specified by Central Gov (lifelong multiple-entry visa, parity with NRIs in economic/financial/educational fields)
* **Rights Denied (Not entitled to):**
  1. Equality of opportunity in public employment (Art 16)
  2. Election as President (Art 58) or Vice-President (Art 66)
  3. Appointment as SC Judge (Art 124) or HC Judge (Art 217)
  4. Registration as a voter
  5. Member of Parliament (LS/RS) or State Legislature (LA/LC)
  6. Appointment to public services/posts under Union/State (unless specified)

### III. Renunciation of Overseas Citizen of India Card
* **Declaration:** OCI cardholder renounces status → Registered by Central Gov → Ceases to be OCI
* **Spouse & Minors:** Foreign spouse and minor children automatically lose OCI status

### IV. Cancellation of Registration as Overseas Citizen of India Cardholder
* **Grounds for Cancellation:**
  1. Obtained card by fraud, false representation, concealment
  2. Disaffection towards Constitution
  3. Unlawful trade/communication with enemy during war
  4. Imprisoned for 2+ years within 5 years of registration
  5. Violation of Citizenship Act 1955 or specified laws (*Added by CAA 2019*)
  6. Necessary in interest of sovereignty, integrity, security, friendly relations, public interest
  7. Marriage dissolved OR solemnised marriage with another person during subsistence of marriage
  * *Natural Justice:* Mandatory reasonable opportunity of being heard before cancellation (*Added by CAA 2019*)

## Comparing NRI, PIO and OCI Cardholder

| Elements of Comparison | Non-Resident Indian (NRI) | Person of Indian Origin (PIO) | Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Cardholder |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Who?** | Indian citizen residing outside India holding Indian Passport | Foreign national (holding foreign passport) with Indian national ancestors | Person registered as OCI Cardholder under Citizenship Act, 1955 |
| **2. Status / Scheme** | Citizen of India | Scheme rescinded w.e.f. Jan 9, 2015 (merged into OCI) | OCI Cardholder |
| **3. Ineligible Nationals** | N/A | N/A | Citizens (or ancestors) of Pakistan, Bangladesh, or specified countries |
| **4. Visa Requirement** | No visa required | Required visa | OCI Card is a **multiple-entry lifelong visa** |
| **5. Police Registration** | Not required | Required if stay exceeds 180 days | **Exempted** from FRRO/FRO registration for any length of stay |
| **6. Key Benefits** | All benefits of Indian citizens | Restricted | Lifelong visa; parity with NRIs in economic, financial, educational fields, adoption, domestic airfares, entry fee to monuments/national parks, pursuing professions (doctors, advocates, architects, CAs), faculty in IITs/NITs/IIMs/AIIMS |
| **7. Restricted Activities** | All allowed | Restricted | Prior permission needed for: research, missionary/tabligh/mountaineering/journalistic activities, diplomatic mission internships/employment, visiting Protected/Restricted/Prohibited areas |
| **8. Property Rights** | All properties | Restricted | Parity with NRIs: Can buy/sell immovable property **EXCEPT agricultural land, farmhouse, or plantation property** |
| **9. Political Rights** | Voting rights available | No political rights | **No voting rights**, cannot hold constitutional/public offices or contest elections |
| **10. Acquiring Citizenship** | Already an Indian citizen | Resident in India for 7 years before application | Registered as OCI for 5 years + resident in India for **12 months** before application |

## Articles Related To Citizenship At A Glance

* **Article 5:** Citizenship at the commencement of the Constitution
* **Article 6:** Rights of citizenship of certain persons who have migrated to India from Pakistan
* **Article 7:** Rights of citizenship of certain migrants to Pakistan
* **Article 8:** Rights of citizenship of certain persons of Indian origin residing outside India
* **Article 9:** Persons voluntarily acquiring citizenship of a foreign State not to be citizens
* **Article 10:** Continuance of the rights of citizenship
* **Article 11:** Parliament to regulate the right of citizenship by law
