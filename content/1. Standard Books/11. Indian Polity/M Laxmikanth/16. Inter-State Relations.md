## INTRODUCTION
* **Federal Harmony:** Successful federal functioning requires cooperation between Centre–States & States *inter se*
* **Constitutional Provisions for Inter-State Comity:**
  1. Adjudication of inter-state water disputes (**Article 262**)
  2. Coordination through inter-state councils (**Article 263**)
  3. Mutual recognition of public acts, records and judicial proceedings (**Article 261**)
  4. Freedom of inter-state trade, commerce and intercourse (**Articles 301–307**)
* **Statutory Machinery:** Zonal Councils created by Parliamentary Act

---

## INTER-STATE WATER DISPUTES
* **Article 262 Provisions:**
  1. **Parliamentary Power:** Parliament may by law provide for adjudication of any dispute/complaint regarding use, distribution, or control of waters of any inter-state river/river valley
  2. **Bar on Court Jurisdiction:** Parliament may exclude **Supreme Court & all other courts** from exercising jurisdiction over such disputes
* **2 Parliamentary Laws Enacted under Article 262:**
  1. **River Boards Act (1956):** Establishment of river boards by Central Govt on request of concerned states to advise on regulation & development of inter-state rivers/river valleys
  2. **Inter-State River Water Disputes Act (1956):** Empowers Central Govt to set up an ad hoc tribunal for adjudication of water dispute between 2 or more states
     * Tribunal decision = **final and binding** on parties
     * Jurisdiction of Supreme Court and all other courts **barred** regarding disputes referred to tribunal

## INTER-STATE COUNCILS
* **Article 263:** President can establish Inter-State Council in public interest to effect coordination; defines nature of duties, organisation and procedure
* **3 Mandated Duties under Article 263:**
  1. Enquiring into & advising upon disputes arising between states
  2. Investigating & discussing subjects of common interest between States or Centre & States
  3. Making recommendations for better coordination of policy & action
* **Advisory Nature vs SC Jurisdiction:**
  * Enquiring function is complementary to SC jurisdiction under **Article 131**
  * **Article 263 Council:** Deals with legal & non-legal disputes; function is **purely advisory**
  * **Article 131 SC:** Decides legal controversies only; decision is **binding**
* **Other Councils Established under Article 263:**
  1. Central Council of Health and Family Welfare
  2. Central Council of Local Government (*originally Central Council of Local Self-Government, 1954*)
  3. 4 Regional Councils for Sales Tax (Northern, Eastern, Western, Southern Zones)

### Establishment of Inter-State Council
* **Sarkaria Commission Recommendation (1983–88):** Recommended permanent Inter-State Council under Art 263; suggested naming it **‘Inter-Governmental Council’**; charged with duties under clauses (b) and (c)
* **Setup (1990):** Established by **Janata Dal Govt (V.P. Singh)** via Inter-State Council Order (May 28, 1990)
* **Composition:**
  1. **Chairman:** Prime Minister
  2. Chief Ministers of all States
  3. Chief Ministers of UTs with Legislative Assemblies
  4. Administrators of UTs without Legislative Assemblies
  5. Governors of States under President’s Rule
  6. **6 Union Cabinet Ministers** (including Union Home Minister) nominated by PM
  * *Permanent Invitees:* 5 Ministers of Cabinet rank / Minister of State (Independent Charge) nominated by PM
* **Functions & Working:** Recommendatory body for inter-state, Centre-State & Centre-UT relations; meets at least **thrice a year**; held in camera; decisions by **consensus**
* **Standing Committee of the Council (Established 1996):**
  * *Purpose:* Continuous consultation & processing of matters for Council
  * *Composition:* Union Home Minister (**Chairman**) + 5 Union Cabinet Ministers + 9 Chief Ministers
* **Inter-State Council Secretariat (Established 1991):** Headed by Secretary to GoI; functions as secretariat of **Zonal Councils** since 2011

## PUBLIC ACTS, RECORDS AND JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS
* **Article 261 ("Full Faith and Credit" Clause):** Removes territorial jurisdiction limitations of individual states
* **3 Key Declarations:**
  1. **Full Faith & Credit:** Given throughout India to public acts (legislative & executive), public records (official registers/books) and judicial proceedings of Centre & every state
  2. **Proof & Effect:** Mode of proof & effect determined as provided by laws of **Parliament**
  3. **Execution of Civil Judgments:** Final judgments/orders of civil courts anywhere in India capable of execution anywhere within India without fresh suit
     * *Limitation:* Applies **ONLY to civil judgments** (does not require courts to enforce penal/criminal laws of another state)

## INTER-STATE TRADE AND COMMERCE
* **Location:** **Part XIII, Articles 301 to 307**
* **Article 301:** Trade, commerce & intercourse throughout territory of India shall be free (covers inter-state AND intra-state trade; breaks state border barriers)
* **Restrictions / Exceptions to Article 301 Freedom (Articles 302–305):**
  1. **Parliamentary Restrictions (Article 302):** Parliament can impose restrictions in public interest; **cannot** discriminate/prefer one state over another EXCEPT in case of **scarcity of goods** in any part of India (Article 303)
  2. **State Legislature Restrictions (Article 304(b)):** State can impose reasonable restrictions in public interest; bill requires **prior sanction of President**; state cannot discriminate between states
  3. **Non-Discriminatory Tax on Imported Goods (Article 304(a)):** State can impose tax on goods imported from other states/UTs if similar goods produced in that state are subject to same tax (prohibits discriminatory taxation)
  4. **State Monopolies / Nationalisation Laws (Article 305):** Freedom subject to laws providing for Central or State monopolies (to complete/partial exclusion of citizens)
* **Article 307 Authority:** Parliament empowered to appoint appropriate authority to carry out trade freedom provisions (no such authority appointed yet; *comparison: USA Inter-State Commerce Commission*)

## ZONAL COUNCILS
* **Nature:** **Statutory bodies** (NOT constitutional) → Established by Parliament via **States Reorganisation Act of 1956**
* **5 Zones Created:** Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern
* **Formative Factors:** Natural divisions, river systems/communications, cultural & linguistic affinity, economic development, security, law and order
* **Composition:**
  * **Common Chairman:** Union Home Minister
  * **Vice-Chairman:** Chief Ministers of states in the zone by rotation (**1-year term**)
  * **Members:** Chief Minister + 2 other ministers from each state in zone + Administrator of each UT in zone
  * **Advisors (Non-voting):** 1 nominee of **NITI Aayog** + Chief Secretary of each state in zone + Development Commissioner of each state in zone
* **Role & Objectives:** Deliberative & advisory bodies; promote cooperation/coordination; achieve emotional integration; arrest acute state-consciousness & regionalism; enable Centre-State cooperation in social/economic matters & major development projects

### North-Eastern Council
* **Creation:** Created by separate Parliamentary Act — **North-Eastern Council Act of 1971** (*came into existence Aug 8, 1972*)
* **Members (8 States):** Assam, Manipur, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Tripura and **Sikkim** (*added in 2002 as 8th member*)
* **Special Functions:** Formulates unified & coordinated regional plans; periodically reviews security & public order measures in the region

## Articles Related To Inter-State Relations At A Glance
* **Article 261:** Public acts, records and judicial proceedings
* **Article 262:** Adjudication of disputes relating to waters of inter-state rivers or river valleys
* **Article 263:** Provisions with respect to an inter-state council
* **Article 301:** Freedom of trade, commerce and intercourse
* **Article 302:** Power of Parliament to impose restrictions on trade, commerce and intercourse
* **Article 303:** Restrictions on the legislative powers of the Union and of the states with regard to trade and commerce
* **Article 304:** Restrictions on trade, commerce and intercourse among states
* **Article 305:** Saving of existing laws and laws providing for state monopolies
* **Article 306:** Power of certain states in Part B of the First Schedule to impose restrictions on trade and commerce *(Repealed)*
* **Article 307:** Appointment of authority for carrying out the purposes of Articles 301 to 304
