## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Status:** Constitutional body directly established under **Article 338-A** of the Constitution
* **Geographical & Cultural Distinction:** STs geographically and culturally distinct from SCs with different problems

---

## SEPARATE COMMISSION FOR STs
* **65th Constitutional Amendment Act (1990):** Set up combined **National Commission for SCs and STs** under **Article 338** (*came into force **March 12, 1992***)
* **Creation of Ministry of Tribal Affairs (1999):** Created to provide sharp focus to welfare and development of STs (*Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment coordinates SCs*)
* **89th Constitutional Amendment Act (2003):** Bifurcated combined body to safeguard ST interests effectively (*came into force **February 19, 2004***):
  * Amended **Article 338** (NCSC)
  * Inserted new **Article 338-A** (NCST)
* **Establishment & Composition:** Separate NCST came into existence in **2004**
  * **Strength:** **5 members** (**1 Chairperson + 1 Vice-Chairperson + 3 other Members**)
  * **Appointment:** **President** by warrant under his hand and seal
  * **Tenure & Rules:** **3 years** (*under **2004 Rules***); **max 2 terms** eligible; conditions of service determined by President

## FUNCTIONS
1. **Investigate & Monitor:** All matters relating to constitutional and legal safeguards for STs; evaluate working
2. **Inquire Complaints:** Specific complaints regarding deprivation of rights and safeguards of STs
3. **Socio-Economic Planning:** Participate and advise on planning process of socio-economic development of STs; evaluate progress under Union or State
4. **Report to President:** Present annual and other reports upon working of safeguards
5. **Recommendations:** Recommend measures for effective implementation of safeguards, protection, welfare and socio-economic development of STs
6. **Other Presidential Duties:** Discharge other functions relating to protection, welfare, development and advancement of STs specified by President

---

## OTHER FUNCTIONS
* **Presidential Specification (2005 Rules):** President specified 8 additional functions (*under **NCST Specification of Other Functions Rules, 2005***):
  1. **Minor Forest Produce:** Conferring ownership rights over minor forest produce to STs living in forest areas
  2. **Natural Resources Safeguards:** Safeguarding rights of tribal communities over mineral and water resources per law
  3. **Livelihood Strategies:** Working for development and more viable livelihood strategies for tribals
  4. **Relief & Rehabilitation (R&R):** Improving efficacy of R&R measures for tribal groups displaced by development projects
  5. **Land Alienation Prevention:** Preventing land alienation of tribals and effectively rehabilitating alienated tribals
  6. **Forest Protection & Afforestation:** Eliciting maximum cooperation/involvement of tribal communities in protecting forests and social afforestation
  7. **PESA Act Implementation:** Ensuring full implementation of **Provisions of Panchayats (Extension to Scheduled Areas) Act, 1996 (PESA)**
  8. **Eliminating Shifting Cultivation:** Reducing and ultimately eliminating practice of shifting cultivation leading to disempowerment and environmental degradation

---

## REPORT
* **Submission:** Submits annual/special reports to **President**
* **Parliamentary Laying:** President places reports before **Parliament** along with **explanatory memorandum** (explaining action taken and reasons for non-acceptance of recommendations)
* **State Assembly Laying:** President forwards state-related report to **State Governor** → Governor places before **State Legislature** with explanatory memorandum

---

## POWERS
* **Procedure:** Power to regulate its own procedure
* **Civil Court Powers:** Vested with powers of a civil court while investigating/inquiring, specifically regarding:
  1. Summoning and enforcing attendance of any person from any part of India & examining on oath
  2. Requiring discovery and production of any document
  3. Receiving evidence on affidavits
  4. Requisitioning public record from any court or office
  5. Issuing summons for examination of witnesses and documents
  6. Any other matter determined by President
* **Mandatory Government Consultation:** Central and State Governments **must consult** Commission on all major policy matters affecting STs
