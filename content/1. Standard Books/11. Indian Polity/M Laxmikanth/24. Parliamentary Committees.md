## MEANING & ESSENTIAL CONDITIONS
* **Need:** Parliament is too unwieldy; functions are complex, varied and voluminous; lacks adequate time and technical expertise → Assisted by Parliamentary Committees
* **Constitutional Status:** Mentioned in Constitution at various places without specific provisions on composition, tenure, or functions (governed by Rules of respective Houses)
* **4 Essential Conditions to qualify as a Parliamentary Committee:**
  1. Appointed or elected by the House OR nominated by the Speaker/Chairman
  2. Works under the direction of the Speaker/Chairman
  3. Presents its report to the House or to the Speaker/Chairman
  4. Provided with a secretariat by the Lok Sabha / Rajya Sabha
* **Consultative Committees Exception:** Consist of MPs, but **NOT** parliamentary committees (do not fulfill the 4 essential conditions)

---

## CLASSIFICATION OF PARLIAMENTARY COMMITTEES
* **Broad Categorisation:**
  1. **Standing Committees:** Permanent, continuous, constituted annually or periodically
  2. **Ad Hoc Committees:** Temporary, cease to exist on completion of assigned task

---

## STANDING COMMITTEES (6 CATEGORIES)

### 1. Financial Committees

#### (a) Public Accounts Committee (PAC)
* **Origin:** First set up in **1921** under **Government of India Act 1919**
* **Composition:** **22 members** (**15 Lok Sabha + 7 Rajya Sabha**)
* **Election:** Elected annually by Parliament from amongst members via **Proportional Representation by Single Transferable Vote (PR-STV)**
* **Membership Rules:** Minister **cannot** be elected; tenure = **1 year**
* **Chairman:** Appointed by Speaker from members; since **1967**, convention dictates Chairman is selected from **Opposition** (*prior to 1967, belonged to ruling party*)
* **Primary Function:** Examines annual audit reports of **Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG)** laid before Parliament by President:
  1. Audit report on Appropriation Accounts
  2. Audit report on Finance Accounts
  3. Audit report on Public Undertakings
* **Scope of Scrutiny:** Examines legal/formal aspects + economy, prudence, wisdom and propriety to expose waste, corruption and extravagance
* **CAG Assistance:** CAG acts as **‘friend, philosopher and guide’** to PAC
* **Limitations:** Post-mortem examination; cannot question policy in broader sense; no power to disallow expenditure; findings advisory only

#### (b) Estimates Committee
* **Origin:** Traced to Standing Financial Committee (1921); first post-independence committee set up in **1950** on recommendation of **John Mathai** (then Finance Minister)
* **Composition:** **30 members** - **ALL from Lok Sabha ONLY** (*Rajya Sabha has NO representation*); originally 25 members, raised to 30 in **1956**
* **Election:** Elected annually by Lok Sabha via **PR-STV**; minister **cannot** be elected; tenure = **1 year**
* **Chairman:** Appointed by Speaker from members; invariably selected from **ruling party**
* **Primary Function:** Examines budget estimates and suggests alternative policies/economies → Described as **‘Continuous Economy Committee’**
* **Limitations:** Examines estimates ONLY after voted by Parliament; cannot question policy laid down by Parliament; advisory only

#### (c) Committee on Public Undertakings (COPU)
* **Origin:** Created in **1964** on recommendation of **Krishna Menon Committee**
* **Composition:** **22 members** (**15 Lok Sabha + 7 Rajya Sabha**); originally 15 members (10 LS + 5 RS), raised to 22 in **1974**
* **Election:** Elected annually via **PR-STV**; minister **cannot** be elected; tenure = **1 year**
* **Chairman:** Appointed by Speaker **exclusively from Lok Sabha members** (*Rajya Sabha member cannot be appointed Chairman*)
* **Primary Function:** Examines reports, accounts and CAG audit reports of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs); checks efficient management per sound business principles
* **Limitations:** Cannot examine major government policy decisions or day-to-day administrative matters of PSUs

### 2. Department-Related Standing Committees (DRSCs)
* **Count:** **24 DRSCs** (**16 under Lok Sabha + 8 under Rajya Sabha**)
* **Evolution:** 3 created in **1989**; expanded to 17 in **1993** (*inaugurated March 31, 1993 by K.R. Narayanan*); restructured to **24 in July 2004**
* **Composition:** **31 members** per committee (**21 nominated by Speaker from Lok Sabha + 10 nominated by Chairman from Rajya Sabha**)
  * *Historical Note:* Strength was 45 (30 LS + 15 RS) till 13th Lok Sabha; reduced to 31 in 2004
* **Membership Rules:** Minister **cannot** be nominated; tenure = **1 year**
* **4 Core Functions:**
  1. Consider **Demands for Grants** of concerned ministries/departments before discussion/voting in Lok Sabha (*reports must not suggest cut motions*)
  2. Examine **bills** pertaining to concerned ministries/departments
  3. Consider **annual reports** of ministries/departments
  4. Consider **national basic long-term policy documents**
* **Working Procedure:** Post general discussion on budget, Houses adjourn for **3 to 4 weeks** while DRSCs examine Demands for Grants and submit reports
* **Limitations:** Cannot consider day-to-day administration matters or issues handled by other specialised committees
* **Merits:** Proceedings devoid of party bias; procedure flexible; makes parliamentary control over executive continuous and in-depth; creates a **‘corpus of knowledge’**

### 3. Committees to Inquire

#### (a) Committee on Petitions
* **Function:** Examines petitions on bills or matters of general public importance
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (b) Committee of Privileges
* **Function:** Semi-judicial nature; examines cases of breach of parliamentary privilege and recommends action
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (c) Ethics Committee
* **Origin:** Constituted in **Rajya Sabha in 1997**, **Lok Sabha in 2000**
* **Function:** Enforces code of conduct of MPs; maintains discipline and decorum
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

---

### 4. Committees to Scrutinise and Control

#### (a) Committee on Government Assurances
* **Origin:** Set up in **1953**
* **Function:** Scrutinises assurances, promises and undertakings given by ministers on floor of House
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (b) Committee on Subordinate Legislation
* **Origin:** Set up in **1953**
* **Function:** Examines whether delegated legislative powers (rules, regulations, sub-rules, bye-laws) are properly exercised by Executive
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **15 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (c) Committee on Papers Laid on the Table
* **Origin:** Constituted in **1975**
* **Function:** Examines papers laid by ministers to check compliance with Constitution, Acts, or Rules (*excludes statutory orders handled by Subordinate Legislation Committee*)
* **Composition:** **15 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (d) Committee on Welfare of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes
* **Composition:** **30 members** (**20 Lok Sabha + 10 Rajya Sabha**)
* **Function:** Considers reports of National Commissions for SCs and STs; examines implementation of constitutional and statutory safeguards

#### (e) Committee on Empowerment of Women
* **Origin:** Constituted in **1997**
* **Composition:** **30 members** (**20 Lok Sabha + 10 Rajya Sabha**)
* **Function:** Considers reports of National Commission for Women; examines measures for women's status, dignity and equality

#### (f) Joint Committee on Offices of Profit
* **Composition:** **15 members** (**10 Lok Sabha + 5 Rajya Sabha**)
* **Function:** Examines composition of committees/bodies appointed by Central, State and UT governments; recommends offices exempt from MP disqualification

---

### 5. Committees Relating to the Day-to-Day Business of the House

#### (a) Business Advisory Committee
* **Function:** Regulates timetable and allocates time for transaction of legislative and government business
* **Composition & Chairmanship:**
  * **Lok Sabha:** **15 members** (**Speaker = Ex-officio Chairman**)
  * **Rajya Sabha:** **11 members** (**Chairman RS = Ex-officio Chairman**)

#### (b) Committee on Private Members’ Bills and Resolutions
* **Function:** Classifies private members' bills and allocates time for discussion on bills and resolutions
* **Composition:** Special committee of **Lok Sabha only** (**15 members**; **Deputy Speaker = Chairman**); Rajya Sabha handles this work within its Business Advisory Committee

#### (c) Rules Committee
* **Function:** Considers matters of procedure and conduct of business in the House; recommends amendments to rules
* **Composition & Chairmanship:**
  * **Lok Sabha:** **15 members** (**Speaker = Ex-officio Chairman**)
  * **Rajya Sabha:** **16 members** (**Chairman RS = Ex-officio Chairman**)

#### (d) Committee on Absence of Members from the Sittings of the House
* **Function:** Considers leave of absence applications and examines cases of members absent for **60 consecutive days or more** without permission
* **Composition:** Special committee of **Lok Sabha only** (**15 members**); Rajya Sabha handles absence matters directly on the floor of the House

---

### 6. House-Keeping Committees (Service Committees)

#### (a) General Purposes Committee
* **Function:** Advises on house affairs not falling under any other committee
* **Composition:** Presiding Officer (**Ex-officio Chairman**) + Deputy Presiding Officer + Panel members + Chairpersons of all Standing Committees + Leaders of recognised parties/groups

#### (b) House Committee
* **Function:** Deals with residential accommodation and amenities (food, medical) for MPs
* **Composition:** **12 members in Lok Sabha**, **10 members in Rajya Sabha**

#### (c) Library Committee
* **Function:** Considers matters relating to Parliament Library and assists members
* **Composition:** **9 members** (**6 Lok Sabha + 3 Rajya Sabha**)

#### (d) Joint Committee on Salaries and Allowances of Members of Parliament
* **Origin:** Set up under **Salary, Allowances and Pension of Members of Parliament Act, 1954**
* **Composition:** **15 members** (**10 Lok Sabha + 5 Rajya Sabha**)
* **Function:** Frames rules regulating payment of salary, allowances and pension to MPs

## AD HOC COMMITTEES
* **Nature:** Temporary bodies set up for specific tasks; cease to exist once task completed and report submitted
* **2 Categories:**

### 1. Inquiry Committees
* Appointed by motion of House or Presiding Officer to inquire into specific matters/scams
* *Examples:* Committee on Draft Five-Year Plans, Bofors Contract Joint Committee, Stock Market Scam JPC, Telecom License Scam JPC, Committee on Conduct of Members

### 2. Advisory Committees
* Include **Select Committees** or **Joint Committees** appointed to consider and report on specific Bills
* Follow clause-by-clause scrutiny procedure; take evidence from experts/public bodies; members can append **minutes of dissent**

---

## CONSULTATIVE COMMITTEES
* **Nature:** Attached to various Central Ministries/Departments; **NOT** parliamentary committees
* **Constitution:** Constituted by **Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs** (formulates guidelines, arranges meetings)
* **Chairmanship:** **Minister / Minister of State in charge** of concerned ministry acts as Chairman
* **Function:** Provides informal discussion forum between ministers and MPs on government policies, programmes and implementation
* **Timing:** Meetings held during both session and inter-session periods

## Department-Related Standing Committees (DRSCs) and Jurisdiction
* **Total:** 24 DRSCs (8 under Rajya Sabha, 16 under Lok Sabha)
* **8 Committees under Rajya Sabha:** Commerce; Home Affairs; Education, Women, Children, Youth & Sports; Industry; Science & Technology, Environment, Forest & Climate Change; Transport, Tourism & Culture; Health & Family Welfare; Personnel, Public Grievances, Law & Justice
* **16 Committees under Lok Sabha:** Agriculture, Animal Husbandry & Food Processing; Information Technology; Defence; Energy; External Affairs; Finance; Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution; Labour, Textiles & Skill Development; Petroleum & Natural Gas; Railways; Housing & Urban Affairs; Water Resources; Chemicals & Fertilizers; Rural Development & Panchayati Raj; Coal, Mines & Steel; Social Justice & Empowerment
