## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Status:** Constitutional office provided under **Article 76** (Part V, Chapter 1 'The Executive')
* **Position:** Highest law officer in the country

---

## APPOINTMENT AND TERM
* **Appointing Authority:** Appointed by **President of India**
* **Qualifications:** Qualified to be appointed as a **Judge of the Supreme Court**:
  1. Citizen of India
  2. Judge of a High Court for **5 years**, OR
  3. Advocate of a High Court for **10 years**, OR
  4. Eminent jurist in the opinion of the President
* **Term of Office:** **Not fixed** by the Constitution
* **Removal Procedure & Grounds:** **Not contained** in the Constitution
* **Tenure Condition:** Holds office during the **pleasure of the President** (can be removed by President at any time)
* **Resignation:** Submits resignation in writing to the **President**
  * *Convention:* Resigns when government (Council of Ministers) resigns or is replaced (appointed on its advice)
* **Remuneration:** **Not fixed** by the Constitution; receives remuneration as **President may determine**

---

## DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS
* **Role:** Chief law officer of the Government of India
* **Core Duties:**
  1. Advice GOI upon legal matters referred by President
  2. Perform other legal duties assigned by President
  3. Discharge functions conferred by Constitution or any other law
* **Specific Duties Assigned by President:**
  1. Appear on behalf of GOI in all cases in **Supreme Court** in which GOI is concerned
  2. Represent GOI in any reference made by President to Supreme Court under **Article 143**
  3. Appear in any **High Court** in cases in which GOI is concerned (when required by GOI)

---

## RIGHTS AND LIMITATIONS

### Rights
1. **Right of Audience:** Right of audience in **all courts** in the territory of India
2. **Parliamentary Participation:** Right to speak & take part in proceedings of both Houses of Parliament, joint sittings and Parliamentary committees (if named a member) — **WITHOUT right to vote**
3. **Privileges & Immunities:** Enjoys all privileges and immunities available to a Member of Parliament (**Article 105**)

### Limitations
1. Should NOT advise or hold a brief **against GOI**
2. Should NOT advise or hold a brief in cases where called upon to advise or appear for GOI
3. Should NOT defend accused persons in criminal prosecutions **without GOI permission**
4. Should NOT accept appointment as a director in any company/corporation **without GOI permission**
5. Should NOT advise any ministry/department/statutory body/PSU unless reference received through **Ministry of Law and Justice, Department of Legal Affairs**

### Legal Practice Status
* **NOT** a full-time counsel for the Government
* **NOT** categorized as a government servant
* **NOT debarred** from private legal practice

---

## SOLICITOR GENERAL OF INDIA
* **Other Law Officers:** **Solicitor General of India** and **Additional Solicitor General of India**
* **Role:** Assist Attorney General in official responsibilities
* **Constitutional Status:** **Non-Constitutional** (*Article 76 mentions ONLY Attorney General; Solicitor General & Additional Solicitor General NOT mentioned in Constitution*)
* **Cabinet Status:** AG is **NOT** a member of Central Cabinet (*separate **Law Minister** in Cabinet looks after legal matters at government level*)

---

## Articles Related to Attorney-General of India

* **Article 76:** Attorney-General of India
* **Article 88:** Rights of Attorney-General as respects the Houses of Parliament and its Committee
* **Article 105:** Powers, privileges and immunities of Attorney-General
