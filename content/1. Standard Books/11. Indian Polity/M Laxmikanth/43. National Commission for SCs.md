## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Status:** Constitutional body directly established under **Article 338** of the Constitution
* **Statutory Bodies Comparison:** NCW (1992), NCM (1993), NHRC (1993), NCPCR (2007) = statutory bodies (created by Acts of Parliament)

---

## EVOLUTION
* **Original Article 338:** Provided for **Special Officer for SCs and STs** (designated as **Commissioner for SCs and STs**)
* **1978 (Resolution):** Government set up non-statutory multi-member Commission for SCs & STs (*Commissioner office continued*)
* **1987 (Resolution):** Modified functions; renamed as **National Commission for SCs and STs** (national-level advisory body)
* **65th Constitutional Amendment Act (1990):** Replaced single Special Officer with high-level multi-member **National Commission for SCs and STs** (*came into force **March 12, 1992***)
* **89th Constitutional Amendment Act (2003):** Bifurcated combined body into 2 separate constitutional bodies (*came into force **February 19, 2004***):
  1. **National Commission for Scheduled Castes (NCSC)** under **Article 338**
  2. **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes (NCST)** under **Article 338-A**
* **Establishment & Composition:** Separate NCSC came into existence in **2004**
  * **Strength:** **5 members** (**1 Chairperson + 1 Vice-Chairperson + 3 other Members**)
  * **Appointment:** **President** by warrant under his hand and seal
  * **Tenure & Rules:** **3 years** (*under 2004 Rules*); **max 2 terms** eligible; conditions of service determined by President

---

## FUNCTIONS
1. **Investigate & Monitor:** All matters relating to constitutional and legal safeguards for SCs; evaluate working
2. **Inquire Complaints:** Specific complaints regarding deprivation of rights and safeguards of SCs
3. **Socio-Economic Planning:** Participate and advise on planning process of socio-economic development of SCs; evaluate progress under Union or State
4. **Report to President:** Present annual and other reports upon working of constitutional/legal safeguards
5. **Recommendations:** Recommend measures for effective implementation of safeguards, protection, welfare and development of SCs
6. **Other Presidential Duties:** Discharge other functions relating to protection, welfare and development of SCs specified by President

---

## REPORT
* **Submission:** Submits annual/special reports to **President**
* **Parliamentary Laying:** President places reports before **Parliament** along with **explanatory memorandum** (explaining action taken and reasons for non-acceptance of recommendations)
* **State Assembly Laying:** President forwards state-related report to **State Governor** → Governor places before **State Legislature** with explanatory memorandum

---

## POWERS
* **Procedure:** Power to regulate its own procedure
* **Civil Court Powers:** Vested with powers of a civil court while investigating/inquiring, specifically regarding:
  1. Summoning and enforcing attendance of any person from any part of India & examining on oath
  2. Requiring discovery and production of any document
  3. Receiving evidence on affidavits
  4. Requisitioning public record from any court or office
  5. Issuing summons for examination of witnesses and documents
  6. Any other matter determined by President
* **Mandatory Government Consultation:** Central and State Governments **must consult** Commission on all major policy matters affecting SCs
* **Additional Jurisdictional Duties:**
  * **Anglo-Indian Community:** Discharges similar functions regarding Anglo-Indian Community (**Article 338(10)**)
  * **OBCs Exclusion:** Handled OBC functions till 2018 → Relieved by **102nd Constitutional Amendment Act (2018)** (*transferred to National Commission for Backward Classes under Article 338B*)
