## ESTABLISHMENT
* **Statutory Status:** Statutory (non-constitutional) body set up in **2007** under **Commissions for Protection of Child Rights (CPCR) Act, 2005** (*enacted by Parliament; came into force **February 5, 2007***)
* **Core Mandate:** Protect, promote and defend child rights in the country; ensure effective implementation of child-related laws & programmes
* **Definition of Child Rights:** Includes rights adopted in **UN Convention on the Rights of the Child** (**November 20, 1989**; ratified by India **December 11, 1992**) → Child defined as human being **below 18 years of age**
* **Nodal Ministry:** **Ministry of Women and Child Development**, Government of India (*created as separate ministry in 2006; earlier a department under Ministry of HRD since 1985*)
* **2 Primary Objectives:**
  1. Take cognizance and redress matters pertaining to child rights violations
  2. Ensure laws, policies, programmes and administrative mechanisms conform to child rights perspective in Constitution and UN Convention

---

## COMPOSITION
* **Multi-Member Body:** **1 Chairperson + 6 Members** (*at least **2 members must be women***)
* **Chairperson Qualifications:** Person of eminence who has done outstanding work for promoting welfare of children
* **6 Members Qualifications:** Persons of eminence, ability, integrity, standing and experience in:
  1. Education
  2. Child healthcare, welfare, or child development
  3. Juvenile justice or care of neglected/marginalised children or children with disabilities
  4. Elimination of child labour or children in distress
  5. Child psychology or sociology
  6. Laws relating to children
* **Appointing Authority:** **Central Government**
  * **Selection Committee for Chairperson:** Appointed on recommendation of **3-member Selection Committee** chaired by **Minister-in-charge of Ministry of Women and Child Development** (*earlier HRD Minister prior to 2006 Amendment*)
* **Tenure & Age Limit:**
  * **Tenure:** **3 years**; eligible for **maximum 2 terms**
  * **Upper Age Limit:** **65 years** for Chairperson; **60 years** for Members
* **Resignation:** Addressed in writing to **Central Government**
* **Removal Grounds (by Central Government):**
  1. Proved misbehaviour or incapacity
  2. Adjudged as insolvent
  3. Engages in paid employment outside duties during term
  4. Refuses to act or becomes incapable of acting
  5. Declared of unsound mind by competent court
  6. Abused office rendering continuance detrimental to public interest
  7. Convicted & sentenced to imprisonment involving moral turpitude
  8. Absents from **3 consecutive meetings** without obtaining leave of absence
* **Service Conditions:** Prescribed by Central Govt (*cannot be varied to disadvantage post-appointment*)

---

## FUNCTIONS
1. **Examine & Review Safeguards:** Review legal safeguards for child rights and recommend effective implementation
2. **Annual & Special Reports:** Present reports upon working of safeguards to Central Govt
3. **Inquire Violations:** Inquire into child rights violations and recommend initiation of proceedings
4. **Examine Inhibiting Factors:** Study factors inhibiting child rights (terrorism, communal riots, natural disasters, domestic violence, HIV/AIDS, trafficking, exploitation, prostitution) and recommend remedies
5. **Special Care Children:** Look into matters relating to children in distress, marginalised, juveniles, conflict with law, without family, children of prisoners
6. **Treaty & Policy Review:** Study international treaties/instruments and review existing policies/programmes
7. **Research & Literacy:** Undertake/promote research and spread child rights literacy/awareness
8. **Inspect Custodial Institutions:** Inspect juvenile homes, care homes and custodial places (Govt or social organisations) and recommend remedial action
9. **Inquire Complaints & Suo Moto Notice:** Look into complaints and take *suo moto* notice of:
   * Deprivation and violation of child rights
   * Non-implementation of child protection laws
   * Non-compliance of welfare policy decisions/instructions
10. **Prohibition / Bar:** **Prohibited** from inquiring into any matter pending before a **State Commission** or any other Statutory Commission

---

## POWERS
* **Civil Court Powers:** Vested with powers of a civil court while inquiring into any matter, specifically regarding:
  1. Summoning and enforcing attendance of any person & examining on oath
  2. Requiring discovery and production of any document
  3. Receiving evidence on affidavits
  4. Requisitioning public records from any court or office
  5. Issuing summons for examination of witnesses or documents

---

## WORKING
* **Post-Inquiry Action Steps:**
  1. Recommend prosecution or suitable action against concerned person to Govt/authority
  2. Approach Supreme Court or concerned High Court for directions, orders, or writs
  3. Recommend grant of immediate interim relief to victim
* **Reporting Procedure:**
  * Submits annual or special reports to **Central Government** and concerned **State Government**
  * Reports laid before respective legislatures with **explanatory memorandum** of action taken and reasons for non-acceptance within **1 year**

---

## FUNCTIONS UNDER OTHER ACTS
* Assigned additional monitoring/inquiring functions under **3 major Child Welfare Acts**:

### 1. Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education (RTE) Act, 2009
* Examine and review safeguards for rights under RTE Act
* Inquire into complaints relating to child's right to free & compulsory education
* Exercises same civil court powers as under CPCR Act 2005

### 2. Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012
* Monitor implementation of POCSO provisions
* Inquire into offences with CPCR Act 2005 powers
* Include POCSO activities in annual report

### 3. Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015
* Monitor implementation of JJ Act provisions
* Inquire into offences with CPCR Act 2005 powers
* Include JJ Act activities in annual report

## STATE COMMISSION FOR PROTECTION OF CHILD RIGHTS (SCPCR)
* **Statutory Basis:** Constituted by **State Governments** via Official Gazette notification under **CPCR Act, 2005**
* **Composition:** **1 Chairperson + 6 Members** (*at least **2 members must be women***)
* **Appointing Authority:** **State Government** on recommendation of **3-member Selection Committee** chaired by **Minister-in-charge of Department dealing with children**
* **Qualifications & Term:** Same fields of expertise as National Commission; term = **3 years** (**max 2 terms**); upper age limit = **65 years** (Chairperson) / **60 years** (Members)
* **Removal & Resignation:** Handled by **State Government** on identical grounds as Central Govt for NCPCR
* **Functions & Powers:** Same functions and civil court powers at state level
* **Reports:** Submits annual/special reports to **State Government** → Laid before **State Legislature** with action-taken memorandum within **1 year**

---

## CHILDREN’S COURTS
* **Provision:** Provided under **CPCR Act, 2005** for speedy trial of offences against children or child rights violations
* **Establishment Authority:** Specified/established by **State Government** with concurrence of **Chief Justice of State High Court**
* **Court Rank:** Existing **Court of Session** in each district specified as Children's Court
* **Prosecutor:** State Govt specifies a **Public Prosecutor** or appoints an advocate with **at least 7 years of practice** as **Special Public Prosecutor**
