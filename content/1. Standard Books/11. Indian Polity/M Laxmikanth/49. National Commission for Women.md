## ESTABLISHMENT
* **Origins & Recommendations:**
  * **1974:** Committee on Status of Women in India recommended apex body for surveillance & grievance redressal
  * **1988:** National Perspective Plan for Women recommended constitution of apex women's body
* **Statutory Status:** Autonomous **statutory body** (*not a constitutional body*)
* **Enabling Act:** **National Commission for Women Act, 1990** (*enacted by Parliament; came into force **31st January 1992***)
* **Nodal Ministry:** **Ministry of Women and Child Development** (*established as separate ministry in 2006; earlier a department under Ministry of HRD since 1985*)
* **4 Specific Objectives:**
  1. Review constitutional and legal safeguards for women
  2. Recommend remedial legislative measures
  3. Facilitate redressal of grievances
  4. Advise government on all policy matters affecting women

## COMPOSITION
* **Multi-Member Body:** **1 Chairperson + 5 Members + 1 Member-Secretary**
* **Chairperson:** Person committed to the cause of women (nominated by Central Govt)
* **5 Members:** Persons of ability, integrity & standing with experience in law/legislation, trade unionism, industry/employment management, women’s voluntary organisations, administration, economic development, health, education, or social welfare
  * **Mandatory Representation:** At least **1 member from SCs** and **1 member from STs**
* **Member-Secretary:** Expert in management/sociological movement OR officer of Civil Service of Union / All-India Service / civil post under Union
* **Nomination & Service Conditions:** All nominated by **Central Government** (Ministry of WCD); salaries/allowances prescribed by Central Govt
* **Tenure:** **3 years**; resignation addressed to **Central Government**
* **Removal Grounds (by Central Government):**
  1. Undischarged insolvent
  2. Convicted & sentenced to imprisonment for offence involving moral turpitude
  3. Declared unsound mind by competent court
  4. Refuses to act or becomes incapable of acting
  5. Absent from **3 consecutive meetings** without leave of absence
  6. Abused official position rendering continuance detrimental to public interest

## FUNCTIONS
* **14-Point Mandate:**
  1. **Investigate & Examine:** All matters relating to constitutional/legal safeguards for women
  2. **Annual/Special Reports:** Present reports on working of safeguards to Central Govt
  3. **Recommendations:** Suggest effective implementation measures for improving women's conditions
  4. **Review Laws:** Review constitutional/legal provisions & recommend amendments for inadequacies
  5. **Take Up Violations:** Take up cases of rights violations with appropriate authorities
  6. **Complaints & Suo Moto Notice:** Look into complaints and take *suo moto* notice regarding:
     * Deprivation of women’s rights
     * Non-implementation of protective/equality laws
     * Non-compliance of policies/instructions for women's welfare & relief
  7. **Special Studies & Investigations:** Call for studies into discrimination/atrocities & recommend strategy removal
  8. **Promotional & Educational Research:** Suggest ways for due representation; identify factors impeding advancement (housing, basic services, occupational hazards)
  9. **Socio-Economic Planning:** Participate and advise on socio-economic development planning process
  10. **Evaluate Development Progress:** Evaluate progress of women's development under Union and States
  11. **Inspection of Custodial Institutions:** Inspect jails, remand homes, women's institutions/places of custody
  12. **Litigation Funding:** Fund litigation involving issues affecting a large body of women
  13. **Periodical Reports:** Submit periodical reports on women's toil & difficulties to government
  14. **Central Reference:** Look into any other matter referred by Central Government
* **Reporting Procedure:**
  * Submits annual/special reports to **Central Government**
  * Central Govt lays report before **Parliament** with **explanatory memorandum** (action taken + reasons for non-acceptance)
  * State-related report copies sent to **State Government** → Laid before **State Legislature** with explanatory memorandum

## POWERS
* **Committees & Co-opted Members:**
  * Can appoint committees for special issues; co-opt outside experts (can participate in proceedings **without voting right**)
* **Procedural Autonomy:** Regulates its own procedure and that of its committees
* **Civil Court Powers:** Vested with powers of a civil court while investigating/inquiring, specifically regarding:
  1. Summoning and enforcing attendance of any person from any part of India & examining on oath
  2. Requiring discovery and production of any document
  3. Receiving evidence on affidavits
  4. Requisitioning public record from any court or office
  5. Issuing summons for examination of witnesses and documents
  6. Any other prescribed matter
* **Mandatory Government Consultation:** Central Government **must consult** Commission on all major policy matters affecting women

## WORKING
* Processes verbal/written complaints and takes *suo moto* notice of cases
* **23 Registered Head Categories:**
  1. Rape/attempt to rape
  2. Acid attack
  3. Sexual assault
  4. Sexual harassment
  5. Stalking/Voyeurism
  6. Trafficking/prostitution of women
  7. Outraging modesty of women/Molestation
  8. Cyber crimes against women
  9. Police apathy against women
  10. Harassment of married women/dowry harassment
  11. Dowry death
  12. Bigamy/Polygamy
  13. Protection of women against domestic violence
  14. Women’s right of custody of children/Divorce
  15. Right to exercise choice in marriage/Honour Crimes
  16. Right to live with dignity
  17. Sexual harassment of women at workplace (*SC benchmark: **Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan 1997***)
  18. Denial of maternity benefits to women
  19. Gender discrimination (equal education & work rights)
  20. Indecent representation of women
  21. Sex Selective Abortions / Female Foeticide / Amniocentesis
  22. Traditional practices derogatory to women (Sati Pratha, Devdasi Pratha, Witch Hunting)
  23. Free legal aid for women
* **Action Mechanism:**
  * Sends police apathy cases to police authorities & monitors investigation
  * Resolves family disputes/strikes compromises through counseling
  * Disaggregates data for state authorities
  * Urges organisations to expedite workplace sexual harassment disposal
  * Constitutes **Inquiry Committees** for serious crimes to provide immediate relief/justice

## PARIVARIK MAHILA LOK ADALAT
* **Concept:** Innovative concept evolved by NCW to supplement **District Legal Services Authority (DLSA)** for speedy disposal of matrimonial/family pending disputes
* **Model:** Functions on the model of the **Lok Adalat**
* **Funding:** NCW provides financial assistance to NGOs, State Women Commissions, or State Legal Services Authorities
* **4 Primary Objectives:**
  1. Provide speedy and cost-free dispensation of justice to women
  2. Generate public awareness regarding conciliatory mode of dispute settlement
  3. Encourage public to settle disputes outside formal court set-up
  4. Empower public (especially women) to participate in justice delivery mechanism
