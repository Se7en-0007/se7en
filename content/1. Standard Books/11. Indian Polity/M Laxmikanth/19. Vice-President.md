## INTRODUCTION
* **Rank:** Occupies **2nd highest office** in India (accorded rank next to President in official warrant of precedence)
* **Model:** Borrowed from and modelled on lines of **American Vice-President**
* **Primary Function:** Maintenance of political continuity of Indian State

---

## ELECTION
* **System:** Indirect election via an **Electoral College** consisting of members of both Houses of Parliament (Lok Sabha + Rajya Sabha)
* **11th Constitutional Amendment Act (1961):** Changed original election mode (joint sitting of Parliament) to Electoral College system
* **2 Key Differences from Presidential Electoral College:**
  1. Consists of **both elected & nominated members** of Parliament (Presidential = elected members only)
  2. Does **NOT include members of State Legislative Assemblies** (Presidential = includes elected MLAs)
  * *Dr. B.R. Ambedkar’s Reason:* President is head of State (Union + State administration); Vice-President's normal function is presiding over Council of States (Rajya Sabha), assuming presidential duties only rarely/temporarily → State legislatures' participation unnecessary
* **Manner of Voting:** Proportional representation by means of **Single Transferable Vote** via **Secret Ballot**
* **Disputes (Article 71):**
  * All doubts & disputes inquired into & decided exclusively by **Supreme Court** (decision = **final**)
  * Cannot be challenged on ground of any vacancy in Electoral College
  * Acts done prior to void declaration by Supreme Court remain **valid and in force**

## QUALIFICATIONS, OATH AND CONDITIONS

### Qualifications
1. Citizen of India
2. Completed **35 years of age**
3. Qualified for election as a member of **Rajya Sabha** (vs President = Lok Sabha)
4. Must **not hold any office of profit** under Union, State, or local/public authority
   * *Exemptions (Not deemed office of profit):* Sitting President, Vice-President, Governor of any state, Union or State Minister
* **Nomination Requirements:**
  * Subscribed by at least **20 electors as proposers** and **20 electors as seconders** (*Presidential & Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952, amended 1997*)
  * Security deposit = **₹15,000** in Reserve Bank of India (forfeited if fails to secure 1/6th of total valid votes)

### Oath or Affirmation (Article 69)
* **Swears/Affirms to:**
  1. Bear true faith and allegiance to the Constitution of India
  2. Faithfully discharge the duties of his/her office
* **Administered by:** **President** (or person appointed in that behalf by President)

### Conditions of Office
1. **No Legislative Membership:** Cannot be a member of either House of Parliament or State Legislature (seat deemed vacated upon entering Vice-Presidential office)
2. **No Office of Profit:** Prohibited from holding any other office of profit

## TERM AND VACANCY

### Term of Office (Article 67)
* **Tenure:** **5 years** from date of entering office
* **Resignation:** Addressed in writing to the **President**
* **Removal Procedure:**
  * **No formal impeachment required**
  * Resolution introduced **ONLY in Rajya Sabha** (requires **14 days' advance notice**)
  * Passed in Rajya Sabha by **Effective Majority** (majority of all the then members) AND agreed to by Lok Sabha by **Simple Majority**
  * **Grounds for Removal:** **No ground mentioned** in the Constitution
* **Continuance:** Holds office beyond 5 years until successor assumes charge
* **Re-election:** Eligible for re-election for **any number of terms** (*Dr. S. Radhakrishnan & Mohd. Hamid Ansari served 2 terms*)

### Vacancy in Office (Article 68)
* **5 Causes of Vacancy:**
  1. Expiry of 5-year tenure
  2. Resignation
  3. Removal
  4. Death (*Krishna Kant = 1st Vice-President to die in office*)
  5. Disqualification / election declared void by Supreme Court
* **Filling Tenure Expiry Vacancy:** Election held before term expires; outgoing Vice-President continues till successor assumes charge
* **Filling Unexpected Vacancy (Resignation, Removal, Death, Otherwise):**
  * Election held **as soon as possible** after vacancy occurs
  * Newly elected Vice-President serves full **5-year term**

## POWERS AND FUNCTIONS
* **Two-Fold Functions:**
  1. **Ex-Officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha:** Powers & functions similar to Speaker of Lok Sabha (*resembles US Vice-President / Chairman of US Senate*)
  2. **Acts as President / Discharges Functions:**
     * **Casual Vacancy (Resignation, Impeachment, Death, Otherwise):** Acts as President for **maximum 6 months** (new President must be elected within 6 months)
       * *Historical Examples:* Dr. Zakir Husain died (1969) → **V.V. Giri** acted as President; Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed died (1977) → **B.D. Jatti** acted as President
     * **Temporary Absence / Illness:** Discharges functions until President resumes office
       * *Historical Examples:* **Dr. S. Radhakrishnan** discharged functions in June 1960 (Dr. Rajendra Prasad USSR tour) & July 1961 (Dr. Rajendra Prasad illness)
* **Restrictions & Privileges While Acting as President:**
  * **Does NOT perform duties of RS Chairman** (duties performed by Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha)
  * Entitled to **salary, allowances & privileges of President** (not RS Chairman)
* **Emoluments & Pension:**
  * Constitution has **not fixed any salary** for Vice-President in that capacity → Draws salary as ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha
  * **Salary:** **₹4 lakh per month** (*Finance Act 2018, w.e.f. Jan 1, 2016 amending Salaries & Allowances of Officers of Parliament Act, 1953*)
  * **Pension:** **50% of monthly salary** (*Vice-President's Pension (Amendment) Act, 2008 amending 1997 Act*) + daily allowance, free furnished residence, medical, travel facilities

## INDIAN AND AMERICAN VICE-PRESIDENTS COMPARED
* **Succession Difference:**
  * **American Vice-President:** Succeeds to presidency when vacant & remains President for **unexpired term** of predecessor
  * **Indian Vice-President:** Does **NOT** assume office for unexpired term; merely serves as **acting President** until new President assumes charge (max 6 months)
* **Scholarly Epithet:** Constitution assigned no significant function to Vice-President in that capacity → Called **‘His Superfluous Highness’** (office created solely for political continuity)

## Articles Related to Vice-President

* **Article 63:** The Vice-President of India
* **Article 64:** The Vice-President to be ex-officio Chairman of the Council of States
* **Article 65:** The Vice-President to act as President or to discharge his functions during casual vacancies in the office, or during the absence, of President
* **Article 66:** Election of Vice-President
* **Article 67:** Term of office of Vice-President
* **Article 68:** Time of holding election to fill vacancy in the office of Vice-President and the term of office of person elected to fill casual vacancy
* **Article 69:** Oath or affirmation by the Vice-President
* **Article 70:** Discharge of President’s functions in other contingencies
* **Article 71:** Matters relating to, or connected with, the election of Vice-President
