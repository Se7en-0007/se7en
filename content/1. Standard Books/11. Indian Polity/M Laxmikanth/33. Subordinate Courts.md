## INTRODUCTION
* **Position:** Functions below and under the High Court at district and lower levels
* **Constitutional Location:** Enshrined in **Part VI** of the Constitution (**Articles 233 to 237**)
* **Objective:** Regulates organisation of subordinate courts and ensures independence from executive

---

## CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS

### 1. Appointment of District Judges (Article 233)
* **Appointing Authority:** **Governor of the State** in consultation with the **High Court**
* **3 Qualifications for Appointment:**
  1. Not already in service of Central or State Government
  2. Advocate or pleader for **at least 7 years**
  3. Recommended by High Court for appointment

### 2. Validation of Appointments (Article 233A)
* **Added By:** **20th Constitutional Amendment Act (1966)**
* **Effect:** Retrospectively validated appointments of and judgments delivered by, certain district judges

### 3. Appointment of Other Judges (Article 234)
* **Appointing Authority:** **Governor of the State**
* **Mandatory Consultation:** **State Public Service Commission (SPSC)** + **High Court**
* *Practical Execution:* SPSC conducts competitive examination for state judicial service recruitment

### 4. Control over Subordinate Courts (Article 235)
* **Vested In:** **High Court**
* **Scope of Control:** Postings, promotions, leave of persons in state judicial service holding posts inferior to district judge

### 5. Interpretation (Article 236)
* **District Judge Includes:** City civil court judge, additional district judge, joint district judge, assistant district judge, chief judge of small cause court, chief presidency magistrate, additional chief presidency magistrate, sessions judge, additional sessions judge, assistant sessions judge
* **Judicial Service =** Service consisting exclusively of persons intended to fill post of district judge and other civil judicial posts inferior to district judge

### 6. Application to Certain Magistrates (Article 237)
* **Governor's Power:** May direct that above provisions apply to any class/classes of magistrates in the state

## STRUCTURE AND JURISDICTION
* **Framework:** Organisational structure, jurisdiction and nomenclature laid down by states → Differs slightly state-to-state
* **3-Tier Structure Below High Court:**


**High Court**
    ↓
**District and Sessions Judge's Court**
- **(Civil Side)** → **Subordinate Judge's Court** → **Munsiff's Court**
- **(Criminal Side)** → **Chief Judicial Magistrate's Court** → **Judicial Magistrate's Court**

### 1. District and Sessions Judge's Court
* **Status:** Highest judicial authority in the district
* **Dual Jurisdiction:** Original and appellate jurisdiction in civil and criminal matters
* **Dual Nomenclature:**
  * **District Judge:** Handles civil cases
  * **Sessions Judge:** Handles criminal cases
* **Powers:** Exercises judicial, administrative and supervisory powers over all district subordinate courts
* **Appeals:** Lies directly to **High Court**
* **Sentencing Power:** Can impose any sentence including life imprisonment and **capital punishment (death sentence)**
  * *Mandatory Condition:* Death sentence **MUST be confirmed by High Court** before execution (irrespective of whether appeal is filed)

### 2. Middle Level: Subordinate Judge's Court vs Chief Judicial Magistrate's Court
* **Civil Side — Subordinate Judge's Court:**
  * Also known as *Civil Judge (Senior Division) / Class I*
  * Exercises **unlimited pecuniary jurisdiction** over civil suits
  * May be conferred powers of Assistant Sessions Judge (combining civil & criminal powers)
* **Criminal Side — Chief Judicial Magistrate's Court:**
  * Decides criminal cases punishable with imprisonment **up to 7 years**

### 3. Lowest Level: Munsiff's Court vs Judicial Magistrate's Court
* **Civil Side — Munsiff's Court:**
  * Also known as *Civil Judge (Junior Division) / Class II*
  * Possesses **limited pecuniary jurisdiction** over small civil suits
* **Criminal Side — Judicial Magistrate's Court:**
  * Tries criminal cases punishable with imprisonment **up to 3 years**

## Articles Related to Subordinate Courts

* **Article 233:** Appointment of district judges
* **Article 233A:** Validation of appointments of and judgements, etc., delivered by certain district judges
* **Article 234:** Recruitment of persons other than district judges to the judicial service
* **Article 235:** Control over subordinate courts
* **Article 236:** Interpretation
* **Article 237:** Application of the provisions of this Chapter to certain class or classes of Magistrates
