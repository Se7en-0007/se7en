## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Provision:** **Article 244** in **Part X** of the Constitution envisages a special system of administration for 'scheduled areas' and 'tribal areas'
* **Fifth Schedule:** Deals with administration and control of scheduled areas and scheduled tribes in any state **EXCEPT** the 4 northeastern states (**Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram**)
* **Sixth Schedule:** Deals with administration of tribal areas in the 4 northeastern states (**Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram**)

---

## ADMINISTRATION OF SCHEDULED AREAS (FIFTH SCHEDULE)
* **Objective:** Preserve tribal autonomy, culture, economic empowerment; ensure social, economic and political justice; protect tribals from social injustice and exploitation → Described as **“a constitution within a constitution”**
* **Present Coverage:** 10 states have scheduled areas (**Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha and Rajasthan**)

### 1. Declaration of Scheduled Areas
* **Authority:** **President** is empowered to declare, alter, increase/decrease area, or rescind/redesignate scheduled areas in consultation with the **Governor** of the concerned state
* **4 Established Criteria (Not explicitly spelled out in Constitution):**
  1. Preponderance of tribal population
  2. Compactness and reasonable size of the area
  3. Viable administrative entity (District, Block, or Taluk)
  4. Economic backwardness compared to neighbouring areas

### 2. Executive Power of State and Centre
* **State Power:** Executive power of state extends to scheduled areas therein; Governor has special responsibility
* **Governor’s Report:** Governor submits annual report (or whenever required) to **President** regarding administration
* **Centre Power:** Executive power of Centre extends to giving directions to states regarding administration of scheduled areas

### 3. Tribes Advisory Council (TAC)
* **Mandatory Setup:** Established in each state having scheduled areas to advise on welfare and advancement of Scheduled Tribes
* **Composition:** **20 members** (**3/4ths = representatives of STs** in State Legislative Assembly)
* **Non-Scheduled Area Setup:** Can also be established in a state having STs but no scheduled areas if **President** so directs

### 4. Law Applicable to Scheduled Areas
* **Governor’s Power:** Governor empowered to direct that any particular Act of Parliament or State Legislature does NOT apply or applies with specified modifications/exceptions
* **Regulations:** Governor can make regulations for peace and good government after consulting **Tribes Advisory Council**:
  * Prohibit/restrict transfer of land by or among ST members
  * Regulate allotment of land to ST members
  * Regulate business of money-lending in relation to STs
  * Repeal or amend any Act of Parliament or State Legislature applicable to a scheduled area
* **Presidential Assent:** All regulations made by Governor **require the assent of the President**

### 5. Commissions on Scheduled Areas
* **Requirement:** President required to appoint a commission to report on administration of scheduled areas and welfare of STs in states (at any time, compulsorily after 10 years of Constitution)
* **Commissions Appointed:**
  1. **First Commission (1960):** Headed by **U.N. Dhebar** (submitted report 1961)
  2. **Second Commission (2002):** Headed by **Dilip Singh Bhuria** (submitted report 2004)

## ADMINISTRATION OF TRIBAL AREAS (SIXTH SCHEDULE)
* **Rationale:** Preserves democratic traditions and cultural diversity through **tribal self-rule** (communities ruled themselves until British subjugation in 19th century)
* **Scope:** Covers 10 tribal areas in **4 northeastern states** (**Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram**)

### Key Features of Administration
1. **Autonomous Districts:** Tribal areas constituted as **autonomous districts** (*do NOT fall outside executive authority of concerned state*)
2. **Reorganisation Power:** Governor empowered to organize/reorganize autonomous districts (increase/decrease area, change name, define boundaries)
3. **Autonomous Regions:** If different tribes inhabit an autonomous district, Governor can divide district into several **autonomous regions**
4. **District and Regional Councils:**
   * **District Council Composition:** **30 members** (**4 nominated by Governor + 26 elected** on adult franchise)
   * **Tenure:** Elected members hold office for **5 years**; nominated members hold office during **pleasure of the Governor**
   * **Regional Councils:** Separate regional council for each autonomous region
5. **Legislative Powers:**
   * District and regional councils can make laws on land, forests, canal water, shifting cultivation, village administration, property inheritance, marriage/divorce, social customs
   * **Assent:** All council laws **require the assent of the Governor**
6. **Judicial Powers:**
   * Councils can constitute village councils or courts for trial of suits/cases between tribes and hear appeals
   * High Court jurisdiction over suits/cases is specified by Governor
7. **Administrative & Financial Powers:**
   * Construct/manage primary schools, dispensaries, markets, ferries, fisheries, roads
   * Regulate money lending and trading by non-tribals (*requires Governor’s assent*)
   * Assess and collect land revenue and impose specified taxes
8. **Applicability of Acts of Parliament and State Legislature:**
   * Central or State laws do NOT apply or apply with specified modifications/exceptions
   * **Directing Authority:**
     * **Assam:** **Governor** directs for BOTH Acts of Parliament and State Legislature
     * **Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram:** **President** directs for Acts of Parliament; **Governor** directs for Acts of State Legislature
9. **Commission on Autonomous Districts:** Governor can appoint a commission to examine/report on administration of autonomous districts/regions; Governor can dissolve a council on commission's recommendation

## Articles Related to Scheduled and Tribal Areas

* **Article 244:** Administration of Scheduled Areas and Tribal Areas
* **Article 244A:** Formation of an autonomous state comprising certain tribal areas in Assam and creation of local legislature or Council of Ministers or both therefor
* **Article 339:** Control of the Union over the administration of Scheduled Areas and the welfare of Scheduled Tribes
