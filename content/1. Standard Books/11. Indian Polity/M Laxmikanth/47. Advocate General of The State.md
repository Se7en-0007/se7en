## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Status:** Constitutional office provided under **Article 165** (Part VI 'The States', Chapter 2 'The Executive')
* **Position:** Highest law officer in the state (*corresponds to Attorney General of India at Union level*)

---

## APPOINTMENT AND TERM
* **Appointing Authority:** Appointed by **Governor** of the state
* **Qualifications:** Qualified to be appointed as a **Judge of a High Court**:
  1. Citizen of India
  2. Held **judicial office** in territory of India for **10 years**, OR
  3. Advocate of a High Court (or HCs in succession) for **10 years**
  * *Key Distinction:* **No provision for "eminent jurist"** for High Court judge appointment (*unlike Supreme Court / Attorney General*)
* **Term of Office:** **Not fixed** by the Constitution
* **Removal Procedure & Grounds:** **Not contained** in the Constitution
* **Tenure Condition:** Holds office during the **pleasure of the Governor** (can be removed by Governor at any time)
* **Resignation:** Submits resignation in writing to the **Governor**
  * *Convention:* Resigns when government (Council of Ministers) resigns or is replaced (appointed on its advice)
* **Remuneration:** **Not fixed** by the Constitution; receives remuneration as **Governor may determine**

---

## DUTIES AND RIGHTS

### Duties
* **Role:** Chief law officer of the government in the state
* **Core Functions:**
  1. Advice State Government upon legal matters referred by Governor
  2. Perform other duties of legal character assigned by Governor
  3. Discharge functions conferred by Constitution or any other law

### Rights
1. **Legislative Participation:** Right to speak & take part in proceedings of **both Houses of State Legislature** (or any committee of State Legislature of which named a member) — **WITHOUT right to vote** (**Article 177**)
2. **Privileges & Immunities:** Enjoys all privileges and immunities available to a **Member of State Legislature** (**Article 194**)

---

## Articles Related to Advocate-General of The State

* **Article 165:** Advocate-General of the State
* **Article 177:** Rights of Advocate-General as respects the Houses of State Legislature and its Committee
* **Article 194:** Powers, privileges and immunities of Advocate-General
