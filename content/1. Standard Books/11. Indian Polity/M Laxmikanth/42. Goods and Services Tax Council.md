## ESTABLISHMENT
* **Constitutional Basis:** Inserted by **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016** → **Article 279-A**
* **Constitutional Status:** First constitutional federal body vested with powers to take all major GST decisions
* **Presidential Order:** Empowered **President** to constitute GST Council (*Presidential order issued **September 15, 2016***)
* **Secretariat & Location:** Located at **New Delhi**
* **Ex-Officio Secretary:** **Union Revenue Secretary** (Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance)

---

## VISION AND MISSION
* **Core Philosophy:** Guided by need for harmonised GST structure and development of harmonised national market
* **Vision:** Establish highest standards of **co-operative federalism** in Council's functioning
* **Mission:** Evolving a user-friendly and **information technology-driven** GST structure through wider consultation

---

## COMPOSITION
* **Joint Forum:** Represents Centre and States
  1. **Chairperson:** **Union Finance Minister**
  2. **Member (Centre):** **Union Minister of State** in-charge of Revenue or Finance
  3. **Members (States):** **Minister in-charge of Finance or Taxation** (or any other Minister nominated by each State Government)
* **Vice-Chairperson:** Chosen by State members from amongst themselves (*term decided by State members*)
* **Permanent Invitee (Non-Voting):** **Chairperson of Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)**

---

## WORKING
* **Quorum:** **1/2 (one-half)** of total members of Council
* **Decision Majority:** **≥3/4th (75%) weighted majority** of members present and voting
* **Weightage of Votes:**
  * **Central Government:** **1/3rd weightage** of total votes cast
  * **State Governments (Combined):** **2/3rds weightage** of total votes cast
* **Validity Protection:** Proceedings **NOT** invalid due to:
  1. Any vacancy or defect in Council constitution
  2. Defect in member appointment
  3. Any procedural irregularity not affecting case merits

---

## FUNCTIONS
* **Recommends to Centre & States on:**
  1. Taxes, cesses, surcharges of Centre, States & local bodies merged into GST
  2. Goods and services subjected to or exempted from GST
  3. **Model GST Laws**, principles of levy, **apportionment of IGST** (inter-state trade/commerce) and place of supply principles
  4. **Threshold limit** of turnover below which goods and services are exempted
  5. GST rates including **floor rates with bands**
  6. **Special rates** during natural calamities/disasters to raise additional resources
  7. **Special provisions for 11 States:** Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Jammu and Kashmir, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura, Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand
  8. Any other matter relating to GST as decided by Council

---

## OTHER FUNCTIONS
1. **Petroleum Products Taxation:** Recommends date on which GST may be levied on **5 petroleum items**:
   * Petroleum crude
   * High speed diesel
   * Motor spirit (petrol)
   * Natural gas
   * Aviation turbine fuel (ATF)
2. **Dispute Adjudication Mechanism:** Establishes mechanism to adjudicate disputes arising from recommendations or implementation between:
   * Centre vs 1 or more States
   * Centre + State(s) vs State(s)
   * State(s) vs State(s)
3. **State Revenue Loss Compensation:** Recommends compensation to States for revenue loss for **5 years** → Parliament enacted **Goods and Services Tax (Compensation to States) Act, 2017**
