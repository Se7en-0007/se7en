## MEANING & CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISION
* **Constitutional Status:** Quasi-judicial body established directly under **Article 280** of the Constitution of India
* **Constitution & Frequency:** Constituted by **President of India** every **5th year** (or at such earlier time as deemed necessary)
* **Total Commissions Constituted:** **15 Finance Commissions** constituted till date

---

## COMPOSITION
* **Total Membership:** **5 members** (**1 Chairman + 4 other members**) appointed by **President**
* **Tenure & Reappointment:** Hold office for period specified by President in order; **eligible for reappointment**
* **Parliamentary Authority:** Constitution authorises Parliament to determine qualifications and selection manner → Parliament enacted **Finance Commission Act, 1951**

### Statutory Qualifications (Finance Commission Act, 1951)
* **Chairman:** Person having experience in **public affairs**
* **4 Members:** Selected from amongst the following:
  1. Judge of High Court (or qualified to be appointed as one)
  2. Person with specialised knowledge of **finance and accounts** of government
  3. Person with wide experience in **financial matters and administration**
  4. Person with special knowledge of **economics**

---

## FUNCTIONS
* **Core Mandate:** Recommends to **President of India** on 4 key matters:
  1. **Tax Devolution:** Distribution of net proceeds of shared taxes between Centre and States + allocation among States of their respective shares
  2. **Grants-in-Aid:** Principles governing grants-in-aid to States out of **Consolidated Fund of India**
  3. **Local Body Resources Augmentation:** Measures needed to augment Consolidated Fund of a State to supplement resources of **Panchayats and Municipalities** based on State Finance Commission recommendations (*added by **73rd and 74th Amendment Acts, 1992***)
  4. **Presidential Reference:** Any other matter referred by President in interests of sound finance
* **Historical Jute Export Grant:** Suggested temporary 10-year grants-in-aid to **Assam, Bihar, Odisha and West Bengal** in lieu of jute export duty net proceeds (*ceased in 1960*)
* **Report Submission Procedure:** Submits report to **President** → President lays it before both Houses of Parliament with **explanatory memorandum** on action taken

---

## ADVISORY ROLE
* **Nature of Recommendations:** **Advisory ONLY** (*not binding on Union Government; creates no legal right for states*)
* **Dr. P.V. Rajamannar (4th FC Chairman) View:** Recommendations of quasi-judicial constitutional body should **NOT be turned down** by Centre unless compelling reasons exist
* **Role in Fiscal Federalism:** Envisaged as **‘balancing wheel of fiscal federalism’**
* **Impact of Planning Bodies:**
  * Role previously undermined by **Planning Commission** (non-constitutional, non-statutory body) due to overlapping functions in federal fiscal transfers
  * **2015 Transition:** Planning Commission replaced by **NITI Aayog** (National Institution for Transforming India)
