## INTRODUCTION
* **Concept:** System of urban local government in India = governance of an urban area by the people through elected representatives; jurisdiction limited to demarcated urban area
* **Types of ULBs:** **8 types** of urban local bodies (Municipal Corporation, Municipality, Notified Area Committee, Town Area Committee, Cantonment Board, Township, Port Trust, Special Purpose Agency)
* **Constitutionalisation:** Constitutionalised through **74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992**
* **Constitutional Entry:** **Seventh Schedule, State List (Entry 5)** = ‘Local Government’
* **Central Level Administrative Ministries:**
  1. **Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs** (general urban local bodies)
  2. **Ministry of Defence** (Cantonment Boards)
  3. **Ministry of Home Affairs** (Union Territories)

## EVOLUTION OF URBAN BODIES

### Historical Perspective
* **1687–1688:** **First Municipal Corporation** in India set up at **Madras** (Charter issued Dec 30, 1687; came into existence Sept 29, 1688)
* **1726:** Municipal Corporations set up in **Bombay and Calcutta**
* **1870:** **Lord Mayo’s Resolution** on financial decentralisation visualised development of local self-government
* **1882:** **Lord Ripon’s Resolution** = **‘Magna Carta’** of local self-government; Lord Ripon = **‘Father of local self-government in India’**
* **1907:** **Royal Commission on Decentralisation** appointed (Chairman: **C.E.H. Hobhouse**; report submitted 1909)
* **1919:** **Government of India Act 1919** introduced dyarchy → Local self-government became a **transferred subject** under a responsible Indian minister
* **1924:** **Cantonments Act** passed by Central legislature
* **1935:** **Government of India Act 1935** introduced provincial autonomy → Local self-government declared a **provincial subject**

### Committees and Commissions

  2. **1953–54:** Taxation Enquiry Commission (**John Matthai**)
  3. **1963–65:** Committee on the Training of Municipal Employees (**Nur-Ud-din Ahmed**)
  4. **1963–66:** Rural-Urban Relationship Committee (**A.P. Jain**)
  5. **1963:** Committee of Ministers on Augmentation of Financial Resources of Urban Local Bodies (**Rafiq Zakaria**)

  9. **1985–88:** National Commission on Urbanisation (**C.M. Correa**)

### Constitutionalisation
* **Rajiv Gandhi Government (August 1989):** Introduced **65th Constitutional Amendment Bill** (Nagarpalika Bill) in Lok Sabha → Passed in LS, defeated in Rajya Sabha (Oct 1989) → Lapsed
* **V.P. Singh Government (September 1990):** Introduced revised Nagarpalika Bill in Lok Sabha → Lapsed due to dissolution of LS
* **Narasimha Rao Government (September 1991):** Introduced modified Municipalities Bill in Lok Sabha → Passed by both Houses (Dec 1992), ratified by states, assented by President (April 1993) → **74th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992** came into force on **June 1, 1993**

## 74TH AMENDMENT ACT OF 1992
* **Constitutional Addition:** Added **Part IX-A** (*‘The Municipalities’*, **Articles 243-P to 243-ZG**) and **Twelfth Schedule** (**18 functional items**, **Article 243-W**)
* **Nature:** Brings municipalities under justiciable part of Constitution → Constitutional obligation on states to adopt system

### Salient Features

#### 1. Three Types of Municipalities
1. **Nagar Panchayat:** For a **transitional area** (rural to urban area)
2. **Municipal Council:** For a **smaller urban area**
3. **Municipal Corporation:** For a **larger urban area**
* *Industrial Township Exception:* If municipal services provided by an industrial establishment, Governor may specify area as **Industrial Township** → Municipality **not** constituted
* *Factors for Governor's Area Determination:* Population size, population density, revenue generated for local administration, % non-agricultural employment, economic importance

#### 2. Composition
* **Direct Election:** All members directly elected by people from territorial constituencies called **wards**
* **Chairperson Election:** Manner of election provided by State Legislature
* **Representation Provisions (State Legislature discretion):**
  1. Persons with **special knowledge or experience** in municipal administration (without right to vote in municipal meetings)
  2. MPs (Lok Sabha) and MLAs representing constituencies comprising wholly/partly municipal area
  3. MPs (Rajya Sabha) and MLCs registered as electors within municipal area
  4. Chairpersons of committees (other than wards committees)

#### 3. Wards Committees
* **Mandatory Provision:** Constituted for one or more wards within municipality having population of **3 lakh or more**
* **Composition & Area:** State Legislature provides composition, territorial area, and manner of filling seats

#### 4. Other Committees
* State Legislature allowed to constitute other committees; chairpersons may be made members of municipality

#### 5. Reservation of Seats
* **SC/ST Reservation:** Population-proportional reservation of seats in every municipality
* **Women Reservation:** Not less than **1/3rd of total seats** reserved for women (including SC/ST women)
* **Chairperson Offices:** State Legislature determines reservation of offices of chairpersons for SCs, STs, and women
* **Backward Classes:** State Legislature empowered to reserve seats/offices for backward classes (OBCs)
* **Expiration:** SC/ST reservation tied to **Article 334** period (currently **80 years**, till 2030)

#### 6. Duration of Municipalities
* **Term:** **5 years** from date of 1st meeting
* **Early Dissolution:** Can be dissolved before 5 years
* **Fresh Elections:** Completed before expiry of 5-year duration; or within **6 months** from date of dissolution
* **Remainder Period Rule:**
  * Reconstituted municipality continues **ONLY for remaining period** of 5 years (does NOT enjoy fresh 5 years)
  * If remaining period is **less than 6 months**, no election needed for remainder
* **Dissolution Safeguards:** Mandatory reasonable opportunity of being heard before dissolution; no amendment causally dissolves municipality before 5 years

#### 7. Disqualifications
* Disqualified under state legislature election laws, or laws made by state legislature
* **Age Exception:** Minimum age for municipal membership = **21 years** (not 25 years)
* **Deciding Authority:** Questions referred to authority determined by State Legislature

#### 8. State Election Commission
* Superintendence, direction, and control of electoral rolls & conduct of municipal elections vested in **State Election Commission**
* State Legislature provides for all election-related matters

#### 9. Powers and Functions
* State Legislature endows powers & authority to enable functioning as self-government institutions
* **Devolution:** Preparation of plans & implementation of schemes for economic development and social justice, including **18 matters of Twelfth Schedule**

#### 10. Finances
* State Legislature may: (a) authorise levying/collecting/appropriating taxes, duties, tolls, fees; (b) assign state-collected taxes; (c) provide grants-in-aid from Consolidated Fund of State; (d) constitute municipal funds

#### 11. Finance Commission
* **State Finance Commission** (constituted under Article 243-I) reviews financial position every **5 years** and recommends to Governor:
  1. Distribution/allocation of net tax proceeds between State and municipalities
  2. Determination of assigned taxes, duties, tolls, fees
  3. Grants-in-aid from Consolidated Fund of State
  4. Measures needed to improve financial position of municipalities
* **Central Finance Commission Role:** Recommends measures to augment Consolidated Fund of State to supplement municipal resources based on State Finance Commission recommendations

#### 12. Audit of Accounts
* State Legislature makes provisions for maintenance and auditing of municipal accounts

#### 13. Application to Union Territories
* Applies to UTs; President may specify exceptions and modifications
* *Example (2001):* President directed that **Articles 243ZD (DPC)** and **243ZE (MPC)** shall **NOT apply to NCT of Delhi**

#### 14. Exempted Areas
* **Exempted Regions:** Scheduled Areas (10 states) and Tribal Areas (10 autonomous districts in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram) + **Darjeeling Gorkha Hill Council** (West Bengal)
* **Parliament Power:** Parliament can extend Part IX-A provisions to Scheduled/Tribal Areas via law

#### 15. District Planning Committee (DPC - Article 243ZD)
* **Purpose:** Consolidates plans prepared by panchayats & municipalities in district; prepares draft development plan for district as a whole
* **Composition Ratio:** **4/5ths of members** elected by elected members of district panchayat and municipalities from amongst themselves
* **Representation:** Proportional to ratio between **rural and urban population** in district
* **Draft Plan Considerations:** Spatial planning, water & physical/natural resource sharing, integrated infrastructure development, environmental conservation, available financial/other resources
* **Forwarding:** Chairperson forwards draft development plan to **State Government**

#### 16. Metropolitan Planning Committee (MPC - Article 243ZE)
* **Metropolitan Area:** Area having population of **10 lakh (1 million) or more**, in 1 or more districts, consisting of 2 or more municipalities/panchayats
* **Purpose:** Prepares draft development plan for metropolitan area as a whole
* **Composition Ratio:** **2/3rds of members** elected by elected members of municipalities and chairpersons of panchayats in metropolitan area from amongst themselves
* **Representation:** Proportional to ratio between population of municipalities and panchayats in that area
* **Considerations:** Plans prepared by municipalities & panchayats, coordinated spatial planning, water/resource sharing, GOI/State Govt priorities & investment estimates
* **Forwarding:** Chairperson forwards development plan to **State Government**

#### 17. Continuance of Existing Laws and Municipalities
* **1-Year Transition Window:** State laws brought in conformity within **1 year** from June 1, 1993 (max till May 31, 1994)
* Existing municipalities continue till expiry of term unless dissolved earlier by state legislature

#### 18. Bar to Interference by Courts in Electoral Matters
* Courts barred from questioning constituency delimitation or seat allotment laws
* Election questioned **ONLY via election petition** presented to authority provided by State Legislature

## TWELFTH SCHEDULE (18 FUNCTIONAL ITEMS - ARTICLE 243-W)
1. Urban planning including town planning
2. Regulation of land use and construction of buildings
3. Planning for economic and social development
4. Roads and bridges
5. Water supply for domestic, industrial, and commercial purposes
6. Public health, sanitation, conservancy, and solid waste management
7. Fire services
8. Urban forestry, protection of environment, and promotion of ecological aspects
9. Safeguarding interests of weaker sections (including handicapped & mentally retarded)
10. Slum improvement and upgradation
11. Urban poverty alleviation
12. Provision of urban amenities & facilities (parks, gardens, playgrounds)
13. Promotion of cultural, educational, and aesthetic aspects
14. Burials, burial grounds, cremations, cremation grounds, and electric crematoriums
15. Cattle ponds, prevention of cruelty to animals
16. Vital statistics including registration of births and deaths
17. Public amenities including street lighting, parking lots, bus stops, and public conveniences
18. Regulation of slaughter houses and tanneries

## TYPES OF URBAN GOVERNMENTS (8 ULBs)

### 1. Municipal Corporation
* **Area:** Big cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bangalore, etc.)
* **Creation:** State Legislature Act in States; Parliamentary Act in UTs
* **3 Authorities:**
  1. **Council:** Deliberative & legislative wing; directly elected Councillors + nominated members; headed by **Mayor** (assisted by Deputy Mayor); Mayor elected annually in most states, **ornamental figure / formal head**, presides over meetings
  2. **Standing Committees:** Facilitate council working; deal with public works, health, finance, taxation; take field-level decisions
  3. **Municipal Commissioner:** Chief executive authority; implements decisions of council & committees; appointed by State Govt (**IAS officer**)

### 2. Municipality
* **Area:** Towns and smaller cities (also known as Municipal Council, Committee, Board, Borough, City Municipality)
* **Creation:** State Legislature Act in States; Parliamentary Act in UTs
* **3 Authorities:**
  1. **Council:** Deliberative & legislative wing; directly elected members; headed by **President / Chairman** (assisted by Vice-President); **pivot of administration**, presides meetings AND enjoys **executive powers** (*unlike Mayor*)
  2. **Standing Committees:** Facilitate council working (public works, taxation, health, finance)
  3. **Chief Executive Officer / Chief Municipal Officer:** Day-to-day general administration; appointed by State Govt

### 3. Notified Area Committee
* **Area:** Fast developing industrial town OR town not fulfilling municipality criteria but deemed important by State Govt
* **Creation:** Created by notification in government gazette (functions under State Municipal Act provisions notified in gazette)
* **Nature:** **Neither an elected body nor a statutory body**; **entirely nominated** body (all members + chairman nominated by State Govt); powers equal to municipality

### 4. Town Area Committee
* **Area:** Small town; semi-municipal authority
* **Functions:** Limited civic functions (drainage, roads, street lighting, conservancy)
* **Creation:** Separate Act of State Legislature
* **Composition:** Wholly elected, wholly nominated, or partly elected and partly nominated

### 5. Cantonment Board
* **Area:** Municipal administration for civilian population in cantonment area (delimited military station area)
* **Creation & Control:** Created & administered by **Central Government** under **Cantonments Act, 2006** (repealed 1924 Act) under **Ministry of Defence**
* **Classification (Table 40.2):** 62 cantonment boards in India grouped into 4 categories based on civil population:
  * **Category I:** Population > 50,000
  * **Category II:** Population 10,000 to 50,000
  * **Category III:** Population 2,500 to 10,000
  * **Category IV:** Population < 2,500
* **Composition:** Partly elected (5-year term) + partly nominated / ex-officio members
  * **President of Board:** **Military Officer Commanding the Station** (Ex-officio President)
  * **Vice-President:** Elected by elected members from amongst themselves (5-year term)
  * **Category I Members (15 total):** Station Commander, Executive Engineer, Health Officer, 1st Class Magistrate (nominated by DM), 3 Military Officers (nominated by Station Commander), 8 Elected Members, Chief Executive Officer
* **Executive Officer:** Appointed by **President of India**; belongs to Central cadre; implements board decisions

### 6. Township
* **Area:** Established by large public enterprises near plant housing colonies for staff/workers
* **Administration:** Enterprise appoints **Town Administrator** assisted by engineers/staff
* **Nature:** **No elected members**; extension of bureaucratic structure of enterprise

### 7. Port Trust
* **Area:** Major port areas (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, etc.)
* **Objectives:** Manage/protect ports + provide civic amenities
* **Creation:** Act of Parliament
* **Composition:** Elected and nominated members; official Chairman

### 8. Special Purpose Agency
* **Nature:** Function-based / single-purpose / uni-purpose / functional local bodies (not area-based)
* **Examples:** Town Improvement Trusts, Urban Development Authorities, Water Supply & Sewerage Boards, Housing Boards, Pollution Control Boards, Electricity Supply Boards, City Transport Boards
* **Creation:** Statutory bodies by State Legislature Act OR departments by executive resolution
* **Status:** Autonomous bodies; **NOT subordinate** to local municipal bodies

## MUNICIPAL PERSONNEL
* **3 Personnel Systems:**
  1. **Separate Personnel System:** Each local body appoints, administers & controls own personnel; **non-transferable**; upholds local autonomy & undivided loyalty; widely prevalent
  2. **Unified Personnel System:** State Government creates state-wide cadres for all ULBs; **transferable between local bodies** in state (AP, TN, UP, Rajasthan, MP)
  3. **Integrated Personnel System:** State Govt and local body personnel form part of same service; **transferable between local bodies AND state government departments** (Odisha, Bihar, Karnataka, Punjab, Haryana)

### Municipal Training Institutions
1. **All-India Institute of Local Self-Government (Mumbai, 1927):** Private registered society
2. **Centre for Urban and Environmental Studies (New Delhi, 1967):** Set up post **Nur-ud-din Ahmed Committee (1963–65)**
3. **Regional Centres for Urban and Environmental Studies (Kolkata, Lucknow, Hyderabad, Mumbai, 1968):** Set up post Nur-ud-din Ahmed Committee
4. **National Institute of Urban Affairs (New Delhi, 1976)**
5. **Human Settlement Management Institute (New Delhi, 1985)**

## MUNICIPAL REVENUE
* **5 Sources of Income:**
  1. **Tax Revenue:** Property tax (**most important**), entertainment tax, advertisement tax, professional tax, water tax, animal tax, lighting tax, pilgrim tax, market tax, bridge tolls, cesses (library, education, beggary cesses)
  2. **Non-Tax Revenue:** Rent on municipal properties, fees & fines, royalty, profits & dividends, interest, **user charges** (water, sanitation, sewerage charges)
  3. **Grants:** Central and State Government grants for development, infrastructure, urban reforms
  4. **Devolution:** Transfer of funds from State Govt based on **State Finance Commission** recommendations
  5. **Loans:** Raised from State Govt and financial institutions for capital expenditure (requires State Govt approval)

## CENTRAL COUNCIL OF LOCAL GOVERNMENT
* **Setup:** Established **1954** under **Article 263** by Presidential Order
* **Nomenclature:** Originally *Central Council of Local Self-Government*; term ‘self-’ dropped in 1980s
* **Scope Shift:** Dealt with rural + urban local govts till 1958; **post-1958 deals ONLY with urban local government**
* **Composition & Chairmanship:** Advisory body; **Union Minister for Housing and Urban Affairs (Chairman)** + State Ministers for Local Self-Government
* **6 Key Functions:** Policy recommendations, legislative proposals, Centre-State cooperation, common action programmes, Central financial assistance recommendation, reviewing Central assistance work
