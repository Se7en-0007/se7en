## INTRODUCTION
* **Constitutional Status:** Original Constitution contained **no provisions** for tribunals
* **42nd Constitutional Amendment Act (1976):** Added **Part XIV-A** titled **‘Tribunals’** consisting of **2 Articles**:
  * **Article 323A:** Administrative Tribunals
  * **Article 323B:** Tribunals for other matters

---

## ADMINISTRATIVE TRIBUNALS (ARTICLE 323A)
* **Scope & Purpose:** Empowers **Parliament alone** to establish administrative tribunals for adjudication of service disputes of public servants (Centre, States, local bodies, public corporations, public authorities)
* **Enabling Act:** Parliament enacted **Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985** (authorises Central Govt to set up CAT, SATs, JATs)

### Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT)
* **Setup & Benches:** Set up in **1985**; Principal Bench at **New Delhi**; currently **19 regular benches** (17 at High Court seats + Jaipur & Lucknow) + circuit sittings
* **Jurisdiction:**
  * **Covered:** All-India Services, Central civil services, civil posts under Centre, civilian employees of defence services
  * **Exempted (Not Covered):** Members of defence forces, officers and servants of Supreme Court, secretarial staff of Parliament
* **Composition:** **1 Chairman** + **69 Members** (*Vice-Chairman post abolished by **Administrative Tribunals (Amendment) Act, 2006***)
  * Drawn from both judicial and administrative streams
* **Qualifications & Term (*Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021*):**
  * **Minimum Age:** Must have completed **50 years of age**
  * **Tenure:** **4 years** or until attaining **70 years** (Chairman) / **67 years** (Members), whichever is earlier
* **Appointment:** Central Government on recommendation of **Search-cum-Selection Committee** chaired by **Chief Justice of India (CJI)** or a nominated SC judge
* **Procedure:**
  * Guided by **principles of natural justice** (NOT bound by Civil Procedure Code, 1908)
  * Nominal application fee = **₹50**; applicant can appear in person or through a lawyer
* **Judicial Review & Appeals (***L. Chandra Kumar Case, 1997***):**
  * SC declared exclusion of High Court jurisdiction (**Art 323A(2)(d)** & **Art 323B(3)(d)**) **unconstitutional** (*judicial review = Basic Structure*)
  * Appeals against CAT orders lie before **Division Bench of concerned High Court** (aggrieved public servant **cannot** approach Supreme Court directly)

### State Administrative Tribunals (SATs)
* **Establishment:** Set up by Central Govt on specific request of concerned state government
* **Jurisdiction:** Original jurisdiction in service matters of state government employees
* **Appointment:** Chairman and Members appointed by **Central Govt** on recommendation of Search-cum-Selection Committee chaired by **Chief Justice of concerned High Court**

### Joint Administrative Tribunals (JATs)
* **Provision:** Administrative Tribunals Act 1985 provides for setting up JAT for 2 or more states; exercises powers for all participating states

---

## TRIBUNALS FOR OTHER MATTERS (ARTICLE 323B)
* **Legislative Authority:** Empowers **both Parliament and State Legislatures** (within their legislative competence) to establish tribunals for specific matters
* **8 Specified Matters:**
  1. Taxation
  2. Foreign exchange, import and export
  3. Industrial and labour disputes
  4. Land reforms (*added by **75th Amendment Act, 1993***)
  5. Ceiling on urban property
  6. Elections to either House of Parliament or State Legislature
  7. Rent and tenancy rights
  8. Foodstuffs (production, procurement, supply, distribution)

---

## COMPARISON: ARTICLE 323A vs ARTICLE 323B

| Feature | Article 323A | Article 323B |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Purpose / Subject Matter** | Service matters of public servants only | Other specified matters (taxation, labour, land reforms, etc.) |
| **2. Establishing Authority** | **Parliament ONLY** | **Both Parliament and State Legislatures** (within competence) |
| **3. Hierarchy** | Only 1 tribunal for Centre and 1 for each State / 2+ States (**No hierarchy**) | **Hierarchy of tribunals** may be created |
| **4. Judicial Review** | Appeals lie to **Division Bench of High Court** (*L. Chandra Kumar 1997*) | Appeals lie to **Division Bench of High Court** (*L. Chandra Kumar 1997*) |


## Articles Related to Tribunals
* **Article 323A:** Administrative tribunals
* **Article 323B:** Tribunals for other matters
