## CONSTITUTIONAL PROVISIONS

### Article 74-Council of Ministers to Aid and Advise President
* **Clause 1:** Council of Ministers (CoM) with Prime Minister at head to aid and advise President (President **shall act** in accordance with advice)
  * *Reconsideration Provision:* President may require CoM to reconsider advice; President **must act** in accordance with advice tendered after reconsideration (*Proviso added by **44th Amendment Act, 1978**; **42nd Amendment Act, 1976** originally made advice binding*)
* **Clause 2:** Nature of advice tendered by ministers to President **cannot be inquired into in any court** (establishes intimate and confidential relationship)

### Article 75-Other Provisions as to Ministers
* **Appointment (75(1)):** PM appointed by President; other ministers appointed by President on **advice of PM**
* **Ceiling on Size (75(1A)):** Total number of ministers (including PM) in CoM **shall not exceed 15%** of total strength of Lok Sabha (*Added by **91st Amendment Act, 2003***)
* **Defection Disqualification (75(1B)):** MP disqualified under 10th Schedule (defection) is also **disqualified to be appointed as minister** (*Added by **91st Amendment Act, 2003***)
* **Tenure (75(2)):** Ministers hold office during **pleasure of the President** (*Individual Responsibility*)
* **Responsibility (75(3)):** CoM is **collectively responsible** to the Lok Sabha (*Collective Responsibility*)
* **Oath (75(4)):** President administers oaths of **office and secrecy**
* **Membership Mandate (75(5)):** Minister who is not an MP for **6 consecutive months** ceases to be a minister
* **Salaries (75(6)):** Salaries and allowances determined by **Parliament** (*Salaries and Allowances of Ministers Act, 1952; amended 2001*)

### Article 77-Conduct of Business of the Government of India
1. Executive actions expressed to be taken in the **name of the President**
2. Orders/instruments authenticated as per rules made by President (validity cannot be questioned on ground that it is not made by President)
3. President makes rules for more convenient transaction of business & allocation among ministers

### Article 78-Duties of Prime Minister
* Communicating CoM decisions, furnishing administration/legislative info to President, and submitting single-minister decisions to CoM upon President's requirement

### Article 88-Rights of Ministers as Respects the Houses
* Right to speak and take part in proceedings of **either House**, joint sitting, and parliamentary committees (where named member)
* **Voting Limitation:** **Cannot vote** in a House of which he/she is not a member

## NATURE OF ADVICE BY MINISTERS
* **Binding Character:** Advice is binding on President (**42nd & 44th Amendment Acts**)
* **Judicial Bar:** Courts barred from inquiring into advice (**Article 74(2)**)
* **Key Supreme Court Rulings:**
  * ***U.N.R. Rao Case (1971):*** Dissolution of Lok Sabha does **NOT** dissolve CoM; Article 74 is **mandatory**; President cannot exercise executive power without CoM aid & advice (unassisted action = unconstitutional violation of Art 74)
  * ***Shamsher Singh Case (1974):*** Constitutional "satisfaction" of President = satisfaction of **Council of Ministers** (not personal satisfaction)

## APPOINTMENT OF MINISTERS
* **Selection:** Solely on recommendation of Prime Minister (President appoints only PM-recommended persons)
* **House Membership:**
  * Usually drawn from Lok Sabha or Rajya Sabha
  * Non-MP can be appointed minister for **max 6 consecutive months**; must secure seat (election/nomination) or cease to be minister
* **Participation:** Minister can participate/speak in both Houses, but **votes ONLY in member House**

## OATH AND SALARY OF MINISTERS

### Oaths Administered by President
1. **Oath of Office:** True faith & allegiance to Constitution; uphold sovereignty & integrity of India; discharge duties faithfully; do right to all without fear, favour, affection or ill will
2. **Oath of Secrecy:** Not reveal/communicate matters brought under consideration directly/indirectly except for due discharge of duties

### Deputy Prime Minister Oath Case
* ***Devi Lal Case (1990):*** Oath as Deputy PM challenged as unconstitutional (Constitution mentions only PM & Ministers)
* **SC Ruling:** Upheld oath as valid; "Deputy Prime Minister" is **descriptive only** (does not confer PM powers); non-mention of sub-categories (Deputy PM, Minister of State, Deputy Minister) in Constitution does **not vitiate oath** so long as substantive part is correct

## RESPONSIBILITY OF MINISTERS

### 1. Collective Responsibility (Article 75(3))
* **Bedrock Principle:** Fundamental principle underlying parliamentary system
* **Joint Responsibility:** CoM jointly responsible to Lok Sabha for all omissions & commissions → Act as a team; **“swim and sink together”**
* **No-Confidence Motion:** Passage by Lok Sabha → Entire CoM resigns (including Ministers from Rajya Sabha)
  * *Alternative:* CoM can advise President to dissolve Lok Sabha for fresh elections
* **Cabinet Decisions Binding:** Decisions bind ALL ministers (duty to defend inside & outside Parliament)
* **Disagreement → Resignation:** Minister unable to defend cabinet decision **must resign**
* **Famous Resignations:**
  * **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (1953):** Differences on Hindu Code Bill
  * **C.D. Deshmukh:** Differences on states reorganisation policy
  * **Arif Mohammed Khan:** Opposition to Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Divorce) Act, 1986

### 2. Individual Responsibility (Article 75(2))
* **Presidential Pleasure:** Ministers hold office during pleasure of President
* **Removal Mechanism:** President removes minister **ONLY on advice of PM** (in case of differences/dissatisfaction)
* **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Quote:** *“Collective responsibility can be achieved only through the instrumentality of the Prime Minister... endowing that office with statutory authority to nominate and dismiss ministers.”*

### 3. No Legal Responsibility
* **British vs Indian System:**
  * **Britain:** Legal responsibility exists → Every royal order countersigned by minister; minister legally liable in court (*“The King can do no wrong”*)
  * **India:** **NO legal responsibility** → Presidential order does **NOT** require ministerial countersignature; courts barred from inquiring into ministerial advice

## COMPOSITION OF THE COUNCIL OF MINISTERS
* **3-Tier Structure:**
  1. **Cabinet Ministers:** Head vital ministries (Home, Defence, Finance, External Affairs); members of Cabinet; attend meetings & dictate policy; broad responsibilities
  2. **Ministers of State (MoS):**
     * **Independent Charge:** Perform same functions/powers as cabinet ministers for their ministry; NOT cabinet members; attend meetings **only when specially invited**
     * **Attached MoS:** Assigned specific departments/work under overall charge & supervision of cabinet minister
  3. **Deputy Ministers:** Attached to cabinet ministers or MoS to assist in administrative, political, parliamentary duties; no independent charge; NOT cabinet members
* **Deputy Prime Minister:** Appointed occasionally for political exigencies
* **Parliamentary Secretaries:** Attached to senior ministers to assist in parliamentary duties; **NOT members of CoM**; appointed by **PM** (not President); no department under control

## COUNCIL OF MINISTERS vs CABINET

| Feature | Council of Ministers | Cabinet |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Size** | Wider body (**60 to 70 ministers**) | Smaller body (**15 to 20 ministers**) |
| **2. Categories** | Includes all 3 categories (Cabinet, MoS, Deputy Ministers) | Includes **Cabinet Ministers only** (part of CoM) |
| **3. Meetings** | Does not meet as a body to transact business; no collective functions | Meets frequently (usually weekly) as a body; has **collective functions** |
| **4. Powers** | Vested with all powers **in theory** | Exercises powers of CoM **in practice** |
| **5. Functions** | Functions determined by Cabinet | Directs CoM via binding policy decisions |
| **6. Implementation** | Implements decisions taken by Cabinet | Supervises implementation of decisions by CoM |
| **7. Constitutional Status** | Constitutional body (**Articles 74 & 75**); max size **15% of Lok Sabha** (*91st Amendment 2003*); 3-tier classification based on British convention (*Salaries & Allowances of Ministers Act 1952* gives legislative sanction) | Inserted in **Article 352** by **44th Amendment Act (1978)** (not in original text); defined as PM + cabinet-rank ministers appointed under Art 75 |
| **8. Responsibility** | Collectively responsible to Lok Sabha | Enforces collective responsibility of CoM to Lok Sabha |

## ROLE OF CABINET
1. Highest decision-making authority in politico-administrative system
2. Chief policy formulating body of Central Government
3. Supreme executive authority of Central Government
4. Chief coordinator of Central administration
5. Advisory body to President (advice is binding)
6. Chief crisis manager (deals with all emergency situations)
7. Deals with major legislative & financial matters
8. Exercises control over higher appointments (constitutional authorities & senior secretariat administrators)
9. Deals with foreign policy & international affairs

## KITCHEN CABINET
* **Concept:** Informal, extra-constitutional inner circle consisting of PM + a few influential trusted colleagues / friends / family members (**‘Inner Cabinet’**)
* **Function:** Real centre of power where crucial political & administrative decisions are "cooked" before formal cabinet placement
* **Prominence:** Particularly powerful during **Indira Gandhi’s era**
* **Merits:** Small unit → highly efficient, rapid decision-making, maintains strict secrecy
* **Demerits:** Reduces authority & status of Cabinet as supreme decision-making body; circumvents legal process by allowing non-legal/outside figures influence
* **Global Prevalence:** Exists in USA and Britain as well
