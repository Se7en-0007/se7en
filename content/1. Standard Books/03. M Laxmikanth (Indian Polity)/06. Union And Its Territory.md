## UNION OF STATES
* **Articles 1 to 4 under Part-I** deal with the Union and its territory
* **Article 1:** Defines India, that is, Bharat as a **‘Union of States’** rather than a ‘Federation of States’
  * **Name of Country:** Constituent Assembly adopted a mix of traditional (**Bharat**) and modern (**India**)
  * **Type of Polity:** Described as **‘Union’** though federal in structure
* **Ambedkar's 2 Reasons for 'Union of States':**
  1. Indian Federation is **not the result of an agreement** among states (unlike American Federation)
  2. States have **no right to secede** from the federation (Indestructible Union)
* **Classification of Territory (Article 1):**
  1. Territories of the states (**28 states** at present)
  2. Union territories (**8 UTs** at present)
  3. Territories that may be acquired by the Government of India at any time
* **First Schedule:** Specifies names and territorial extent of States and UTs
* **Territory of India vs Union of India:**
  * **‘Territory of India’** = Wider expression (includes States + UTs + Acquired Territories)
  * **‘Union of India’** = Narrower expression (includes only States; members sharing power distribution with Centre)
* **Acquisition of Foreign Territories:** Sovereign India can acquire foreign territories under international law:
  * **Modes:** Cession (treaty, purchase, gift, lease, plebiscite), occupation, conquest, or subjugation
  * **Examples acquired:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli; Goa, Daman and Diu; Puducherry; Sikkim

## PARLIAMENT’S POWER TO REORGANISE THE STATES
* **Article 2:** Empowers Parliament to **admit into** the Union of India or **establish** new states on terms & conditions it thinks fit
  * *Admit:* Admission of states already in existence
  * *Establish:* Establishment of states not in existence before (both relate to territories **not** part of Union of India)
* **Article 3:** Relates to formation or changes in **existing states** of Union of India (internal re-adjustment):
  1. Form new state by separation of territory or by uniting states/territories
  2. Increase area of any state
  3. Diminish area of any state
  4. Alter boundaries of any state
  5. Alter name of any state
* **2 Mandatory Conditions under Article 3:**
  1. Bill can be introduced in Parliament **only with prior recommendation of President**
  2. President **must refer bill to concerned State Legislature** for expressing views within a specified period
* **Key Principles of Article 3:**
  * President/Parliament is **not bound by views** of state legislature (may accept or reject)
  * No fresh reference needed for every amendment moved and accepted in Parliament
  * **Union Territories:** No reference needed to UT legislature; Parliament can take any action
  * **"An indestructible union of destructible states":** Territorial integrity of states **not guaranteed** by Constitution
    * *Comparison (USA):* USA = "An indestructible union of indestructible states" (state consent mandatory)
* **Article 4:** Declares that laws made under Article 2 & Article 3 are **not** constitutional amendments under **Article 368**
  * Passed by **simple majority** & ordinary legislative process
* **Cession of Territory & Berubari Union Case (1960):**
  * **Context:** Decision to cede Berubari Union (West Bengal) to Pakistan led to Presidential reference
  * **SC Ruling:** Power of Parliament to diminish area (Article 3) **does not cover cession** of Indian territory to foreign country
  * **Requirement:** Cession requires Constitutional Amendment under **Article 368** → **9th Constitutional Amendment Act (1960)** enacted to transfer territory
  * **1969 SC Ruling:** Settlement of **boundary dispute** does not require constitutional amendment (done by executive action as it involves no cession)
* **Exchange of Territories with Bangladesh (100th Amendment Act, 2015):**
  * Enacted to implement Land Boundary Agreement (1974) & Protocol (2011)
  * **Transfers:** India transferred **111 enclaves** to Bangladesh; Bangladesh transferred **51 enclaves** to India
  * Involved adverse possessions transfer + demarcation of **6.1 km undemarcated border**
  * **First Schedule Modified:** Territories of 4 states modified (**Assam, West Bengal, Meghalaya, Tripura**)
  * **Historical Chain:** Radcliffe Award (1947) → Bagge Award (1950) → Nehru-Noon Agreement (1958) → 9th Amendment (1960, unnotified) → Land Boundary Agreement (1974) → Protocol (2011) → 100th Amendment (2015)

## EVOLUTION OF STATES AND UNION TERRITORIES

### Integration of Princely States
* **1947 Status:** Two political units → British provinces (direct rule) + Princely states (552 in Indian geographical limits under British paramountcy)
* **Independence Options (Indian Independence Act 1947):** Join India, join Pakistan, or remain independent
* **Outcome:** 549 joined India; **3 refused initially**:
  1. **Hyderabad:** Integrated via **Police Action** (*Operation Polo*)
  2. **Junagarh:** Integrated via **Referendum**
  3. **Kashmir:** Integrated via **Instrument of Accession**
* **1950 Four-Fold Classification (29 total):**
  * **Part A (9):** Erstwhile Governor's provinces (*Assam, Bihar, Bombay, Madhya Pradesh, Madras, Orissa, Punjab, United Provinces, West Bengal*)
  * **Part B (9):** Erstwhile Princely states with legislatures (*Hyderabad, Jammu & Kashmir, Madhya Bharat, Mysore, PEPSU, Rajasthan, Saurashtra, Travancore-Cochin*)
  * **Part C (10):** Centrally administered Chief Commissioner's provinces & princely states (*Ajmer, Bhopal, Bilaspur, Cooch-Behar, Coorg, Delhi, Himachal Pradesh, Kutch, Manipur, Tripura*)
  * **Part D (1):** Solitary territory (*Andaman and Nicobar Islands*)

### Dhar Commission and JVP Committee
* **Demand:** Regional agitations (especially South India) for states reorganisation on linguistic basis
* **Dhar Commission (June 1948):** Appointed under **S.K. Dhar** (Linguistic Provinces Commission)
  * *Report (Dec 1948):* Recommended reorganisation based on **administrative convenience**, NOT linguistic factor → Led to resentment
* **JVP Committee (Dec 1948):** Congress committee of **Jawaharlal Nehru**, **Vallabhbhai Patel**, and **Pattabhi Sitaramayya** (no chairman)
  * *Report (April 1949):* Formally **rejected language** as basis for reorganisation
* **First Linguistic State (Oct 1953):**
  * **Andhra State** created for Telugu-speaking people by separating from Madras State
  * **Trigger:** Death of **Potti Sriramulu** after a 56-day hunger strike
  * **Capital:** Kurnool; High Court: Guntur

### Fazl Ali Commission
* **Appointment (Dec 1953):** 3-member States Reorganisation Commission under **Fazl Ali** (Members: **K.M. Panikkar** and **H.N. Kunzru**)
* **Report (Sept 1955):**
  * Broadly **accepted language** as basis of reorganisation
  * **Rejected 'one language–one state'** theory
  * Primary consideration = Preservation & strengthening of national unity & security
* **4 Major Factors Identified:**
  1. Preservation and strengthening of national unity and security
  2. Linguistic and cultural homogeneity
  3. Financial, economic, and administrative considerations
  4. Planning and welfare promotion of people and nation
* **Recommendations:** Abolish four-fold classification; create 16 states + 3 centrally administered territories
* **Implementation:**
  * **States Reorganisation Act (1956)** + **7th Constitutional Amendment Act (1956)**
  * Done away with Part A & B distinction; abolished Part C states
  * **Nov 1, 1956:** Created **14 States** and **6 Union Territories**
* **14 States (1956):** Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Bombay, Jammu and Kashmir, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Madras, Mysore, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal
* **6 Union Territories (1956):** Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Delhi, Himachal Pradesh, Laccadive Minicoy and Amindivi Islands, Manipur, Tripura
* **Key Mergers in 1956:**
  * **Kerala:** Travancore-Cochin + Malabar District (Madras) + Kasargode
  * **Andhra Pradesh:** Telugu areas of Hyderabad + Andhra State (Capital shifted to Hyderabad)
  * **Madhya Pradesh:** Madhya Bharat + Vindhya Pradesh + Bhopal
  * **Bombay:** Saurashtra + Kutch + Bombay
  * **Mysore:** Coorg + Mysore
  * **Punjab:** PEPSU + Punjab
  * **Rajasthan:** Ajmer + Rajasthan

### New States and Union Territories Created After 1956
* **15. Gujarat (1960):** Bombay State divided into **Maharashtra** (Marathi) & **Gujarat** (15th state, Gujarati; capital Ahmedabad) via *Bombay Reorganisation Act, 1960*
* **Dadra and Nagar Haveli (1961):** Liberated from Portuguese in 1954; administered by people's administrator till 1961; made UT by **10th Amendment Act, 1961**
* **Goa, Daman and Diu (1962):** Acquired from Portuguese via police action (1961); made UT by **12th Amendment Act, 1962**. Goa conferred statehood in 1987
* **Puducherry (1962):** French establishments (Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe, Yanam) handed over in 1954; made UT by **14th Amendment Act, 1962**
* **16. Nagaland (1963):** Naga Hills and Tuensang area taken out of Assam to form 16th state (*State of Nagaland Act, 1962*)
* **17. Haryana & Chandigarh (1966):** Akali Dal (Master Tara Singh) demand for 'Punjabi Subha' → **Shah Commission (1966)** → *Punjab Reorganisation Act, 1966* created Punjabi Punjab, Hindi **Haryana** (17th state), and UT **Chandigarh** (common capital)
* **18. Himachal Pradesh (1971):** UT of Himachal Pradesh elevated to statehood (*State of Himachal Pradesh Act, 1970*)
* **19. Manipur, 20. Tripura, 21. Meghalaya (1972):** *North-Eastern Areas (Reorganisation) Act, 1971*:
  * **Manipur** (19th state) & **Tripura** (20th state) elevated from UTs
  * **Meghalaya** (21st state) elevated from sub-state/autonomous state (created by 22nd Amendment, 1969)
  * UTs of **Mizoram** & **Arunachal Pradesh** created out of Assam
* **22. Sikkim (1975):**
  * Ruled by Chogyal; became protectorate in 1947
  * **35th Amendment (1974):** Made 'associate state' (added Art 2A & 10th Schedule)
  * **1975 Referendum:** People voted to abolish Chogyal
  * **36th Amendment (1975):** Full statehood (22nd state); added Art 371-F; repealed Art 2A & 10th Schedule
* **23. Mizoram, 24. Arunachal Pradesh, 25. Goa (1987):**
  * **Mizoram** (23rd state): *Mizoram Peace Accord (1986)* between GoI and Mizo National Front
  * **Arunachal Pradesh** (24th state): Elevated from UT
  * **Goa** (25th state): Separated from UT of Goa, Daman and Diu
* **26. Chhattisgarh, 27. Uttarakhand, 28. Jharkhand (2000):**
  * **Chhattisgarh** (26th state): Carved out of Madhya Pradesh (*MP Reorganisation Act, 2000*)
  * **Uttarakhand** (27th state): Carved out of Uttar Pradesh (*UP Reorganisation Act, 2000*)
  * **Jharkhand** (28th state): Carved out of Bihar (*Bihar Reorganisation Act, 2000*)
* **29. Telangana (2014):** Carved out of Andhra Pradesh (*AP Reorganisation Act, 2014*) as 29th state
* **Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh (2019):**
  * *The Constitution (Application to Jammu and Kashmir) Order, 2019* revoked special status under Article 370
  * *Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019* bifurcated state into **UT of Jammu and Kashmir** and **UT of Ladakh** (Kargil + Leh)
* **Merger of UTs (2020):** *Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu (Merger of Union Territories) Act, 2019* merged them into single UT
* **Current Total:** **28 States** and **8 Union Territories**

### Change of Names
* **United Provinces** → **Uttar Pradesh** (1950)
* **Madras** → **Tamil Nadu** (1969)
* **Mysore** → **Karnataka** (1973)
* **Laccadive, Minicoy and Amindivi Islands** → **Lakshadweep** (1973)
* **Union Territory of Delhi** → **National Capital Territory of Delhi** (69th Amendment Act, 1991)
* **Uttaranchal** → **Uttarakhand** (2006)
* **Pondicherry** → **Puducherry** (2006)
* **Orissa** → **Odisha** (2011)

## ARTICLES RELATED TO UNION AND ITS TERRITORY AT A GLANCE
* **Article 1:** Name and territory of the Union
* **Article 2:** Admission or establishment of new states
* **Article 2A:** Sikkim to be associated with the Union (*Repealed*)
* **Article 3:** Formation of new states and alteration of areas, boundaries or names of existing states
* **Article 4:** Laws made under Articles 2 and 3 to provide for amendment of First & Fourth Schedules and supplemental, incidental, and consequential matters
