## DEMAND FOR A CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY
* **1934: Idea proposed** by **M.N. Roy** (Communist movement pioneer)
* **1935: Indian National Congress (INC)** officially demanded a Constituent Assembly (CA) to frame India's Constitution
* **1938: Jawaharlal Nehru** declared free India's Constitution must be framed without outside interference by a CA elected on **adult franchise**
* **1939:** INC passed a formal resolution reiterating this demand
* **1940: August Offer** → British Gov accepted demand in principle
* **1942: Cripps Mission** → **Sir Stafford Cripps** proposed draft on framing an independent Constitution after World War II
  * **Muslim League (ML) Rejection:** Wanted partition into two autonomous states with two separate Constituent Assemblies
* **1946: Cabinet Mission Plan** (consisting of **Lord Pethick Lawrence**, **Sir Stafford Cripps**, **A.V. Alexander**)
  * Rejected two CAs; proposed a single CA scheme which satisfied the ML

## COMPOSITION OF THE CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY
* **November 1946: CA Constituted** under Cabinet Mission Plan
* **Total Strength = 389**
  * **British India = 296 seats**
    * 292 members from 11 Governors' provinces (*Madras, Bombay, UP, Bihar, Central Provinces, Orissa, Punjab, NWFP, Sindh, Bengal, Assam*)
    * 4 members from 4 Chief Commissioners' provinces (*Delhi, Ajmer-Merwara, Coorg, British Baluchistan*)
  * **Princely States = 93 seats**
* **Seat Allocation Rules:**
  * **Proportional to Population:** ~1 seat per million population
  * **Community Division (British Provinces):** Allocated among **Muslims**, **Sikhs** and **General** (all others) proportional to population
* **Election & Nomination Process:**
  * **British Provinces Seats:** Elected by members of each community in provincial legislative assemblies
  * **Method:** Proportional representation by single transferable vote (**PR-STV**)
  * **Princely States Seats:** Nominated by heads of the princely states
  * **Nature of CA:** Partly elected, partly nominated; members indirectly elected by provincial assemblies under **limited franchise** (*based on tax, property, education under GOI Act of 1935*)
* **Elections to British Provinces (July–August 1946):**
  * **Seats contested: 296**
  * **INC** won **208** seats
  * **Muslim League** won **73** seats
  * Small groups and independents won **15** seats
  * **93 seats of Princely States** remained vacant (decided to stay away)
* **Representation of Society:** Included all sections (Hindus, Muslims, Sikhs, Parsis, Anglo-Indians, Indian Christians, SCs, STs and women)
  * **15 Women members** in total
  * Included all major leaders except **Mahatma Gandhi**

## WORKING OF THE CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY
* **First Meeting: December 9, 1946**
  * Boycotted by **Muslim League** (demanded separate state of Pakistan)
  * Attended by **207 members**
  * **Temporary President:** **Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** (oldest member, following French practice)
* **December 11, 1946: Core Officers Elected**
  * **Permanent President:** **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**
  * **Vice-President:** **H.C. Mukherjee**
* **July 16, 1947: Dual Vice-Presidents**
  * **V.T. Krishnamachari** elected as 2nd Vice-President (CA resolved to have two VPs)

### Objectives Resolution
* **December 13, 1946:** Moved by **Jawaharlal Nehru** (laid down fundamentals and philosophy of constitutional structure)
* **Core Principles:**
  * Proclaim India as an **Independent Sovereign Republic**
  * Form a **Union** of British India, princely states and other willing territories
  * Territories to retain **autonomous unit** status with **residuary powers** (except Union-vested powers)
  * All power and authority derived from the **people**
  * Guarantee and secure **Justice** (social, economic, political), **Equality** of status/opportunity/before law and **Freedom** (thought, expression, belief, faith, worship, vocation, association, action)
  * Provide adequate safeguards for **minorities, backward/tribal areas, depressed/other backward classes**
  * Maintain **territorial integrity** and sovereign rights on land, sea, air
  * Contribute to **world peace** and welfare of mankind
* **Adoption:** Unanimously adopted on **January 22, 1947**; modified version became the **Preamble**

### Changes by The Independence Act
* **Gradual entry of Princely States:**
  * **April 28, 1947:** Reps of 6 states (*Baroda, Bikaner, Jaipur, Patiala, Rewa, Udaipur*) joined
  * Post-June 3, 1947 (Mountbatten Plan partition): Most other princely states and ML members from Indian Dominion joined
* **Three major changes by Indian Independence Act of 1947:**
  1. **Fully Sovereign Body:** CA could frame any constitution and abrogate/alter any British law
  2. **Dual Function Legislative Body:** Assigned two separate roles performed on separate days:
     * **Constitution Making:** Chaired by **Dr. Rajendra Prasad**
     * **Ordinary Law Enactment:** Chaired by **G.V. Mavalankar** (CA became first Parliament of Free India / Dominion Legislature; first met Nov 17, 1947)
     * *Duration:* Dual roles continued till **November 26, 1949**
  3. **Strength Reduction:** Pakistan-bound ML members withdrew → CA strength reduced from **389 to 299**
     * British Provinces: Reduced from **296 to 229**
     * Princely States: Reduced from **93 to 70**

### Other Functions Performed
* **May 1949:** Ratified India’s membership of the **Commonwealth**
* **July 22, 1947:** Adopted the **National Flag**
* **January 24, 1950:** Adopted the **National Anthem**
* **January 24, 1950:** Adopted the **National Song**
* **January 24, 1950:** Elected **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** as first President of India
* **Time, Expenditure & Scope:**
  * Took **2 years, 11 months and 18 days** across **11 sessions**
  * Constitution-makers studied Constitutions of **60 countries**
  * Draft Constitution considered for **114 days**
  * Total expenditure: **₹64 lakh**
* **January 24, 1950:** Final session of CA
* **January 26, 1950 – 1951-52:** CA became **Provisional Parliament of India** until new Parliament formed after first general elections (ceased April 17, 1952; new bicameral Parliament met May 1952)

### Drafting Committee
* **August 29, 1947: Appointed** to prepare a draft of the new Constitution
* **7 Members:**
  1. **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (Chairman)
  2. **N. Gopalaswamy Ayyangar**
  3. **Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar**
  4. **Dr. K.M. Munshi**
  5. **Syed Mohammad Saadullah**
  6. **N. Madhava Rau** (replaced **B.L. Mitter** who resigned due to ill-health)
  7. **T.T. Krishnamachari** (replaced **D.P. Khaitan** who died in 1948)
* **Timeline of Drafts:**
  * **First Draft:** Published **February 1948** (8 months given for public review & amendments)
  * **Second Draft:** Published **October 1948**
  * Total draft preparation time: Less than 6 months (sat for only **141 days**)

## ENACTMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION
* **November 4, 1948:** Final draft introduced by **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (First Reading; 5 days general discussion)
* **November 15, 1948 – October 17, 1949:** Second Reading (clause-by-clause consideration; 7,653 amendments proposed, 2,473 discussed)
* **November 14, 1949:** Third Reading started
* **November 26, 1949: Passed & Enacted**
  * Motion: *"the Constitution as settled by the Assembly be passed"* declared passed
  * Signed by **284 out of 299** members present on that day
* **The Adopted Constitution (Nov 26, 1949):**
  * Vested with **Preamble, 395 Articles, 8 Schedules**
  * **Preamble** was enacted *after* the entire Constitution was already enacted
* **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Law Minister):** Known as **"Father of the Constitution of India"** and **"chief architect of the Constitution of India"** (Brilliant writer, constitutional expert and undisputed SC leader)
* **November 19, 2015 Notification:** Government declared **November 26** as **Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas)** to promote constitutional values

---

## ENFORCEMENT OF THE CONSTITUTION
* **November 26, 1949 (Immediate Enforcement):** Articles **5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 60, 324, 366, 367, 379, 380, 388, 391, 392 and 393** (citizenship, elections, provisional parliament, temporary and transitional provisions, short title)
* **January 26, 1950 (Commencement of remaining provisions):** Major part of Constitution came into force (celebrated as Republic Day)
  * **Why January 26?** Historical Importance → **January 26, 1930: Purna Swaraj day** was celebrated following Lahore Session (December 1929) of the INC
* **Repeals:** Commencement repealed **Indian Independence Act of 1947** and **Government of India Act of 1935** (and amending acts)
  * **Abolition of Privy Council Jurisdiction Act (1949)** was continued

## CRITICISM OF THE CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY
* **1. Not a Representative Body:** Members not directly elected on adult franchise
* **2. Not a Sovereign Body:** Created by British proposals; held sessions with British permission
* **3. Time Consuming:** Took nearly 3 years (USA took < 4 months)
  * **Naziruddin Ahmed** (CA Member) coined **"Drifting Committee"** to mock the Drafting Committee
* **4. Dominated by Congress:** One-party body in a one-party country
  * **Granville Austin:** *"The Assembly was the Congress and the Congress was India"*
* **5. Lawyer–Politician Domination:** Other sections under-represented; led to complex language and bulky document
* **6. Dominated by Hindus:**
  * **Lord Viscount Simon:** Called it *"a body of Hindus"*
  * **Winston Churchill:** Commented it represented *"only one major community in India"*

## HINDI TEXT OF THE CONSTITUTION
* **58th Constitutional Amendment Act of 1987:** Inserted **Article 394-A** in Part XXII (last part of Constitution)
* **Core Provisions:**
  * **Presidential Authority:** President shall publish under his authority:
    1. Translation of the Constitution in **Hindi**, incorporating all amendments
    2. Translation in **Hindi** of every amendment in English
  * **Same Meaning:** Translation and amendments shall have same meaning as English; difficulties in meaning resolved/revised by the President
  * **Authoritative Text:** Published Hindi version deemed for all purposes as authoritative text in Hindi
