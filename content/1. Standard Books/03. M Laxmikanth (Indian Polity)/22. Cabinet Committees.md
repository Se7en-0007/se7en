## FEATURES OF CABINET COMMITTEES
* **Extra-Constitutional:** Not mentioned in Constitution → Established under **Rules of Business**
* **2 Types:**
  * **Standing Committees:** Permanent nature
  * **Ad Hoc Committees:** Temporary nature; constituted for special problems & disbanded after task completion (*e.g., Emergency Committee set up in 1962 post-Chinese invasion*)
* **Creation & Variation:** Set up by **Prime Minister** according to situation requirements → Number, nomenclature & composition vary from time to time
* **Membership:**
  * Varies from committee to committee
  * Mostly **Cabinet Ministers**, but **Non-Cabinet Ministers** can be members or special invitees
  * Includes Ministers in charge of subjects + other senior Ministers
* **Chairmanship:**
  * Mostly headed by **Prime Minister**
  * If PM is a member of a committee, PM **invariably presides** over it
  * Sometimes other senior Cabinet Ministers act as Chairman
* **Functions & Decision Power:** Sort out issues, formulate proposals for Cabinet, & **take decisions** (*Cabinet can review decisions*)
* **Significance & Purpose:**
  * Organisational device to **reduce enormous workload of Cabinet**
  * Facilitates **in-depth policy examination** & **effective coordination**
  * Based on principles of **division of labour** and **effective delegation**

## FUNCTIONS OF CABINET COMMITTEES
1. **Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs (CCPA):**
   * Deals with all policy matters pertaining to **domestic and foreign affairs**
   * **Super-Cabinet:** Most powerful of all Cabinet Committees
2. **Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA):**
   * Directs and coordinates governmental activities in **economic sphere**
3. **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC):**
   * Decides all higher-level appointments in **Central Secretariat, Public Enterprises, Banks and Financial Institutions**
1. **Cabinet Committee on Parliamentary Affairs:**
   * Looks after progress of **government business in Parliament**
5. **Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS):**
   * Deals with issues relating to **defence, law and order, internal security and atomic energy**
1. **Cabinet Committee on Accommodation (CCA):**
   * Allotment of **government accommodation** to MPs, Central Govt employees and other persons/organisations
1. **Cabinet Committee on Investment and Growth (CCIG):**
   * Policy matters aimed at **accelerating capital inflows, export promotion, import substitution and improving ease of doing business**
1. **Cabinet Committee on Employment and Skill Development (CCESD):**
   * Issues related to **developing skills, enhancing employability of workforce and increasing women workforce participation**

## GROUPS OF MINISTERS
* **Nature & Function:** Ad hoc bodies constituted in addition to Cabinet Committees to examine emergent/critical issues; instrument of **inter-ministerial coordination**
* **Types:**
  * **Empowered GoMs (EGoMs):** Empowered to take decisions on behalf of Cabinet
  * **Standard GoMs:** Make recommendations to Cabinet
* **Working Mechanism:** Ministers heading concerned ministries inducted → Advice crystallises → Body disbanded

### Second Administrative Reforms Commission (2ARC) Recommendations
* **Commission Details:** 2nd ARC (2005–2009) headed by **Veerappa Moily**
* **Observations:** Large number of GoMs → Irregular meetings → Significant delays on major issues
* **Key Recommendations:**
  1. **Selective Use:** Selective & effective use of GoMs with clear mandate
  2. **Time Limits:** Prescribe explicit time limits for completing entrusted work
  3. **Decision Delegation:** Empower GoMs to arrive at decisions on behalf of Cabinet to ensure early resolution of issues
